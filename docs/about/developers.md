# Developers & Interviews

## Key people

| Person | Role (as reported) | Notable statements |
|---|---|---|
| **Holly Longdale** | Executive Producer / VP, World of Warcraft | "A new beginning for World of Warcraft". After the Anniversary Edition she and Ion decided to "just do it". The beta was the biggest in Blizzard's history |
| **Ion Hazzikostas** | Senior Game Director | The community gave wildly different definitions of "Classic+". SoD tested how much change is too much. SD vs HD was a hard call. No Steam release planned. Regionally staggered patches are a scheduling choice, not a tech limit. A great starting point for new players |
| **Tim Jones** | Lead Game Designer | Three pillars. Rotations simple but not repetitive. No add-ons needed to play a class correctly |
| **Nora Mills** | Lead Software Engineer | A "completely separate third pillar", not a remake. Grounded stories about everyday Azeroth |
| **Clay Stone** | WoW Classic team | No WoW Token or boosts, because "the journey is the game" |
| **Josh Greenfield** (Aggrend) | Senior Game Designer / producer (sources differ) | Possible Mak'gora arena for Hardcore. Beta numbers "craziest" in his 17 years. On launch night, admitted some zones felt empty |
| Paul Kubit, Kim Flack | Forever developers | BlizzCon interviews with RaiderIO and SoulSoBreezy |
| Satya Nadella | Microsoft CEO | "Largest beta in franchise history" (28 Oct earnings call) |

**Composers** (per Wikipedia): Adam Burgess, Brendon Williams, David Arkenstone, Neal Acree, Jason Hayes.
