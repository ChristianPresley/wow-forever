# Hardcore

!!! info "Status (7 Nov 2026)"
    **Not yet available.** The roadmap lists Hardcore for **Winter 2026/2027**, after the 9 Dec raid unlock. Blizzard has not announced an exact date or patch.

## What we know

- **Permadeath.** Death "carries lasting consequences".
- **Closed ecosystem.** No groups, trade or mail with other rulesets, and **no mixed-ruleset battlegrounds**.
- One account can play **both factions**.
- **Dead characters can transfer out** to Normal, PvP or RP.
- **One-way Legacy:** rewards that you get on Hardcore also apply to other rulesets. Progress from other rulesets does not apply to Hardcore.

## Content made for Hardcore { #designed-with-hardcore-in-mind }

At the BlizzCon Hardcore panel, developers said that they made much of Forever's content for Hardcore:

- Some new **dungeon items with special effects** give new ways to survive when death is permanent.
- The developers intend the **[Legacy system](../characters/legacy.md)** to be especially useful to Hardcore players.
- The new safety check for world refresh started because of Hardcore concerns. It applies on all rulesets.
