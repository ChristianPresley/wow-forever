# Hardcore

!!! info "Status (7 Nov 2026)"
    **Not yet available.** The roadmap lists Hardcore for **Winter 2026/2027**, after the 9 Dec raid unlock. No exact date or patch has been announced.

## What we know

- **Permadeath.** Death "carries lasting consequences".
- **Closed ecosystem.** No grouping, trading or mail with other rulesets, and **no mixed-ruleset battlegrounds**.
- **Both factions** can be played on one account.
- **Dead characters can transfer out** to Normal, PvP or RP.
- **One-way Legacy:** rewards earned on Hardcore carry to other rulesets, but progress from other rulesets does not apply to Hardcore.

## Designed with Hardcore in mind

At the BlizzCon Hardcore panel, developers said much of Forever's content was built with Hardcore in mind:

- Some new **dungeon items with special effects** offer new ways to survive content when death is permanent.
- The **[Legacy system](../characters/legacy.md)** is meant to be especially useful to Hardcore players.
- The new world-refresh safety check came out of Hardcore concerns but applies everywhere.

## Speculation and unconfirmed items

- **Mak'gora:** Josh Greenfield (Aggrend) floated a possible "dedicated space" for duels to the death. Not confirmed.
- **Self-Found:** whether a Classic-style Self-Found option exists is **unconfirmed**.
