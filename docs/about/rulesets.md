# Rulesets & Megaserver

## No realms

!!! forever "Rulesets replace realms"
    Forever has **no realm list**. Before you make a character, you select a **Ruleset**. Each ruleset is one large shared world, a "mega-realm", **per region**. Blizzard calls a ruleset "a broad player ecosystem rather than a single realm".

- **Names:** names have 2 parts (first and last) and are unique **per region**.
- **Language:** set *Preferred Online Language* in *Game Menu → Options → System → Languages and Social*. The setting is account-wide with a 24-hour cooldown. In the open world, the game tries to show you players who use your language. There are no dedicated language servers.
- **Faction balance:** a background system tries to keep the factions balanced in the part of the world that each player sees. On PvP rulesets, Blizzard can temporarily limit new characters for one faction, as in Season of Discovery.
- **Layering:** Blizzard has published no technical details. At launch, some zones felt empty. Josh Greenfield said that this was true. A hotfix on 6 Nov improved the long-range visibility of other players.
- **World refresh safety:** a new system checks that you are in a safe spot before the world refreshes around you. This applies to every ruleset.

!!! conflict "Conflict"
    Some articles (citing Wowhead) describe 1 global megaserver per ruleset. The Warcraft Wiki and Method say that there is 1 per region. Names are unique per region, and this supports that interpretation.

## Cross-ruleset rules

- Groups, guilds, dungeons, raids, the auction house and mail work **only within the same ruleset and faction**. There is no cross-faction play anywhere.
- **Battlegrounds mix players from different rulesets**, except Hardcore.
- Characters cannot move freely between rulesets. **Exception:** a *dead* Hardcore character can transfer to another ruleset.
- **Collections and Legacy Points are account-wide across rulesets.** Hardcore is one-way. Legacy that you get on Hardcore applies on other rulesets. Progress from other rulesets does not go into Hardcore.
