# Rulesets & Megaserver

## No realms

!!! forever "Rulesets replace realms"
    Forever has **no realm list**. Before creating a character you pick a **Ruleset**. Each ruleset is one large shared world, a "mega-realm", **per region**. Blizzard calls a ruleset "a broad player ecosystem rather than a single realm".

- **Names:** two-part names (first and last), unique **per region**.
- **Language:** set *Preferred Online Language* under *Game Menu → Options → System → Languages and Social*. The setting is account-wide with a 24-hour cooldown. The game prefers to show you open-world players who share your language. There are no dedicated language servers.
- **Faction balance:** a background system aims to keep factions balanced within each player's view of the world. On PvP rulesets, creating characters for one faction can be temporarily limited, as in Season of Discovery.
- **Layering:** Blizzard has published no technical details. At launch some zones felt empty. Josh Greenfield acknowledged it, and a 6 Nov hotfix improved long-range visibility of other players.
- **World refresh safety:** a new system checks that you are in a safe spot before the world refreshes around you. This applies to every ruleset.

!!! conflict "Conflict"
    Some coverage (citing Wowhead) describes one global megaserver per ruleset. The Warcraft Wiki and Method say per region, and per-region name uniqueness supports that reading.

    Several outlets (TweakTown, Gfinity, mein-mmo) count three or four rulesets. Blizzard's official article lists **RP-PvP** as a separate fourth launch ruleset, with Hardcore to follow.

## Cross-ruleset rules

- Grouping, guilds, dungeons, raids, the auction house and mail work **only within the same ruleset and faction**. There is no cross-faction play anywhere.
- **Battlegrounds mix players from different rulesets**, except Hardcore.
- Characters cannot move freely between rulesets. **Exception:** a *dead* Hardcore character can transfer to another ruleset.
- **Collections and Legacy Points are account-wide across rulesets.** Hardcore is one-way: Legacy earned on Hardcore applies elsewhere, but progress from other rulesets does not flow back into Hardcore.
