## Baseline changes

- **Forms are no longer a disadvantage.** Potions and most consumables work while you stay in form, including Rage potions and Thistle Tea. Weapon enchants and procs (weightstones, Crusader, Windfury Totem) work. In form, auto-attack has the same DPS as your weapon. Most doors and objects work in form, and so do Shadowmeld, War Stomp and Walk on Air.
- **Cat:** Shred 200% weapon damage (was 155%), Claw 140% (was 110%), Ravage 435% (was 350%). The scaling of Rip, Rake, Ferocious Bite and Pounce increased by ~25%.
- **Bear:** threat **+115%** (was +50%). Swipe hits 3 targets with AP scaling. Growl matches Taunt. Frenzied Regeneration can heal 100% of health.
- New baseline: **Omen of Clarity** (lvl 20), **Revive** (lvl 12, out-of-combat res), **Nature's Grasp** (lvl 10), **Poised** (lvl 32, next Energy ability costs 10 less).
- **Balance:** Barkskin usable in forms, Entangling Roots indoors, no Hurricane cooldown, Faerie Fire usable in forms (free, 6 s cooldown), Thorns scales with spell power, cheaper Wrath. Starfire base damage up ~40%.

## Talents

=== "Balance"

    Genesis · Nature's Majesty · **Nature's Splendor** (11) · **Insect Swarm** (16, moved from Resto) · Overgrowth · **Eclipse** (Wrath speeds up your next Starfires) · improved Nature's Grace · **Moonkin Form** (party crit for spells and attacks, does not stack with Leader of the Pack)

=== "Feral"

    **Feral Charge** (11, now has a Cat version) · Shredding Attacks · **Shifting Power** (16: instant 40 Energy for mana, 16 s cooldown, 8 s when improved) · **Primal Bite** (bear 16, replaces Mangle) · **Leader of the Pack** (21) · Predatory Strikes · Natural Reaction · Rend and Tear · Blood Frenzy · **Lacerate** (now a talent)

    **Furor 5/5** lets Energy regenerate outside Cat Form. Furor plus Shifting Power **replace Powershifting**.

=== "Restoration"

    Naturalist (now also +2–10% damage) · **Gift of the Earthmother** (11: −0.5 s GCD on Rejuvenation, Swiftmend and Wild Growth) · **Swiftmend** (16, requires your own HoT) · Living Spirit · **Wild Growth** (31, 3 s cast) · **Bloom** (added pre-launch)

## Removed

**Berserk** (removed pre-launch) · Mangle · Tiger's Fury (replaced by Shifting Power) · Feral Aggression, Primal Fury, Feral Faerie Fire and Improved Mark of the Wild (now part of baseline)
