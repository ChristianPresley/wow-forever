## Baseline changes

- **Aimed Shot** is baseline at level 20 for all specs. Multi-Shot shares its cooldown. Arcane Shot has its own cooldown.
- **Traps work in combat**, and all traps share a **30 s** cooldown.
- Aspect of the Beast adds melee AP. Scorpid Sting gives −2% hit. Disengage removes more threat.
- Pre-launch numbers: Aimed Shot +110% bonus damage, Arcane Shot +30% base damage, Serpent Sting buffed, Aspect of the Hawk ranks 20/35/50/70/90/110 RAP, Hunter's Mark ranks do not stack.

## Pets

- Pet stats **scale with the Hunter's gear**, and pets no longer get player buffs.
- Every family learns Bite or Claw, and Dive or Dash.
- **New family: Fox** (*Trickster's Dance*: dodge and attack speed for 12 s).

## Talents

The [Hunter talent guide](../../talents/hunter.md) shows all 3 trees and gives a rating to each talent.

=== "Beast Mastery"

    Deadly Aspects · Endurance Training (absorbs Thick Hide) · Focused Fire · **Summon Hawk** (16: up to 2 hawks, shares Arcane Shot's cooldown) · Intimidation · Bestial Discipline (also restores mana)

=== "Marksmanship"

    Lethal Attacks · Improved Stings · **Careful Aim** (20–100% of Intellect as AP) · Rapid Killing · **Lone Wolf** (11: +20% damage with no pet) · **Trueshot Aura** (21) · Rapid Recuperation · Barrage · **Sniper Shot** (31: 4 s cast, 15 s cooldown, 45 yd)

=== "Survival"

    Improved Tracking (merges the Slaying talents) · Entrapment (always procs from traps) · Savage Strikes · Predator's Edge (dual-wield) · **Counterattack** (16) · Resourcefulness · Expose Prey · Survivalist's Discipline · **Strider Kick** (21) · **Lacerating Strikes** (31: Mongoose Bite applies a 21 s bleed)

## Removed

Wyvern Sting · Killer Instinct · Improved Feign Death · Monster/Humanoid Slaying · Improved Hunter's Mark

In the beta, reports said that Hunter was the **fastest leveling class**.
