## Cross-class changes

!!! forever "These apply to every class"

    - **Talent structure:** talents still start at level 10, with a 51-point maximum. Single-point capstone tiers sit at **11, 16 (new), 21 and 31** points. Talents and Legacy talents are changed at class trainers.
    - **Dual Talent Specialization:** **level 40, 50g** at a class trainer. Swap freely outside combat and battlegrounds. Changing talents *within* a spec still costs a fee, and that fee decays to **1g** over time.
    - **Training cost:** about **206–207g** in total for levels 1–60, for every class.
    - **Low-rank spells** no longer reduce proc chance, except Arcane Concentration, Frostbite and Fingers of Frost.
    - **Proc roots** (Frostbite, Entrapment, Improved Wing Clip, Counterattack, Improved Hamstring) now share diminishing returns.
    - **Weapon imbues** (Shaman imbues, Rogue poisons, Warlock stones) **stack** with sharpening stones, weightstones and oils.
    - **Pet scaling:** Hunter and Warlock pets scale from the owner's stats and gear.
    - **Hit from talents:** several talents now grant hit, so players depend less on hit gear.
    - **Anti-farming:** shorter Cone of Cold, Improved Blizzard's slow capped at 40%, and Consecration's full damage capped at 4 targets.
    - Throw has its 0.5 s windup again. New spells are no longer added to action bars automatically. The battleground deserter debuff is now 40 s.
    - Design goal: **simple but not repetitive rotations**, with **every spec viable**, and no add-ons needed to play correctly.

## Changes between the deep dives and launch

The kits shifted noticeably right before launch (build history on [Patches](../../about/patches.md)):

- Druid: **Berserk removed**, **Bloom added**
- Shaman: **Lava Burst removed**, *Lightning Mastery* added
- Priest: **Binding Heal → Holy Binding**
- Paladin: **Salvation removed**, **Unsealed Wrath** added
- Rogue: gained **Shank**, **Poised** and **Death Mark**
- BlizzCon-demo names that were never confirmed later: Mangle, Hot Streak, Drain Hope, Restless Blades
