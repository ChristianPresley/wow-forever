## Baseline changes

- **Holy Strike** (lvl 6): 50% weapon damage as Holy. Cooldown 10 or 12 s (sources conflict).
- **Judgement no longer consumes your Seal.**
- **Seal of Fury** (lvl 10), the tank seal: Holy damage per swing plus an absorb equal to 50% of that damage. Its Judgement **taunts** (10 yd). Judgement of Fury lasts 3 s.
- **Righteous Fury** at level 10 (was 16): +33% threat, +90% Holy threat.
- **Consecration** baseline at lvl 20, with full damage capped at 4 targets.
- **Blessing of Kings** and Improved Blessing of Wisdom are baseline, and blessings last **1 hour**.
- Resistance auras cover the raid within 30 yd. Lay on Hands cooldown is 20 min (was 1 hr).
- Seal twisting is **not** baseline (see Twist of Light).
- **Removed pre-launch:** Salvation, Mass Salvation, Revoke, Blessing of Light.

## Talents

=== "Holy"

    Improved Seals · Unyielding Faith · **Voice of Truth** (11: 6 s silence/interrupt immunity, 3 min cooldown) · Reverence · Purifying Power · Infusion of Light · **Divine Favor** (16) · Divine Precision (4/8% hit) · **Holy Shock** (21, 10 s cooldown) · Consecrated Ground · Holy Power (10–50% crit-heal bonus) · **Light's Vigil** (31) · *new pre-launch:* **Oath of Light**, **Boundless Light**

=== "Protection"

    Redoubt · Anticipation (+20 Defense) · **Improved Seal of Fury** (11, mana return) · Improved Righteous Fury (3/6/9% damage reduction) · Shield Specialization · Sacred Duty · **Swift Judgement** (16) · **Templar's Bulwark** (21: absorb equal to 100% max health for 8 s, 5 min cooldown, Forbearance) · Reckoning (procs on block) · Iron Creed · Holy Shield (3 ranks) · *new pre-launch:* **Stoicism**, **Shieldsworn** (Holy Strike −4 s cooldown)

=== "Retribution"

    Benediction · Holy Conduit · Conviction · Vindication · Sanctified Judgement · Pursuit of Justice · Eye for an Eye · **Sacred Arbiter** (16) · Vengeance (5 stacks) · **Repentance** (21) · **Champion of the Light** (spell damage from Intellect, 12–60% by rank) · Instrument of the Law · **Twist of Light** (31: seal twisting via "Echo") · **Unsealed Wrath** *(pre-launch: stores your Seal for up to 3 stacks)*

!!! unconfirmed "Unsealed Wrath: replacement or addition?"
    The pre-launch notes call Unsealed Wrath "the new 31st talent". Whether it replaced Twist of Light or was added alongside it is unconfirmed. Mobalytics counts **20 brand-new Paladin talents** (Holy 8, Protection 5, Retribution 7).

## Removed

Blessing of Sanctuary · Improved Concentration Aura · Improved Retribution Aura · Sanctity Aura · Salvation (and its variants) · Blessing of Light

## Mount quest

Alliance paladins have the classic *Redemption*-themed charger quest. **Forsaken paladins** get their own level-60 epic mount quest ridden "in the name of **Retribution**". See [Lore](../../world/lore.md).
