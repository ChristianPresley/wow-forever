# Priest

**Races:** Human, Dwarf, Gnome (**new**), Night Elf, Troll, Undead.

## Baseline changes

- **Fear Ward** and **Devouring Plague** for **every race** at level 20.
- **Shadow Word: Death** baseline at lvl 32. It deals 30% backlash if the target survives, and none on a miss.
- **Divine Spirit** baseline at lvl 30.
- Fade threat reduction doubled. Prayer of Healing heals the *target's* party. Renew stacks from multiple Priests.

## Race-locked spells

| Race | Spells |
|---|---|
| Human | **Divine Grace** *(new)*, Feedback |
| Dwarf | Desperate Prayer, **Chastise** *(new: 2 s root on humanoids)* |
| Gnome | **Confounding Flash** *(new: confuses 5 enemies for 3 s)*, **Contingency Plan** *(new: ward that triggers below 35% health)* |
| Night Elf | Starshards (buffed, 30 s cooldown), Elune's Grace |
| Troll | Hex of Weakness, Shadowguard |
| Undead | Touch of Weakness, **Dark Sacrifice** *(new: health to mana)* |

## Talents

=== "Discipline"

    Power in Light · Twin Disciplines · Holy Precision (up to 18% hit) · Mental Agility · Mental Strength (now % Intellect) · **Soul Warding** (16: cheaper Power Word: Shield with a shorter cooldown) · **Penance** (21: 2 s channel, 12 s cooldown, heals or damages) · Renewed Hope · **Divine Aegis** (crit heals shield)

=== "Holy"

    Twilight Focus (pushback protection) · Inspiration · Searing Light (redesigned) · Litany of Light · Spirit of Redemption (15 s) · **Holy Binding** (16: 50% of Flash Heal healing on others also heals you; replaced Binding Heal pre-launch) · **Prayer of Mending** (31: 5 bounces, 10 s cooldown)

=== "Shadow"

    Shadow Focus (now hit) · Shadow Reach · Improved Mind Flay · **Vampiric Embrace** (16: 30 s duration, 1 min cooldown) · Shadow Weaving (self-only) · **Devouring Contagion** (Devouring Plague jumps on death) · Early Demise · **Shadowform** now allows all non-healing spells, halves Shadow spell costs and adds +100% Shadow crit damage, with a lower flat damage bonus

## Removed

Lightwell · Improved Prayer of Healing · Unbreakable Will · Force of Will · Improved Vampiric Embrace · Binding Heal (pre-launch)
