# Rogue

**Races:** Human, Dwarf, Gnome, Night Elf, Orc, Troll, Undead, Skyborne (both).

## Baseline changes

- **Smooth energy regeneration**, with no 2-second ticks.
- **Combo points stay on the original target.**
- **One-handed axes** are usable.
- Poisons have more charges and stack with stones. Deadly and Instant Poison AP coefficients are doubled.
- Feint threat doubled. **Sap no longer breaks stealth.** Distract lowers enemy stealth detection for 10 s. Expose Armor equals 5 Sunders.
- **Shank** (lvl 24, added pre-launch): 5 Energy, 10 s cooldown, 50% off-hand damage. Generates 15 Energy (25 at rank 2, lvl 44).
- **Poised** (lvl 18): your next Energy ability costs 10 less.

## Talents

=== "Assassination"

    Malice (applies to poisons) · Murder (now hits Giants) · Improved Expose Armor · Lethality (2 pts, 10/20% crit damage) · **Cold Blood** (16) · Improved Poisons · Vigor · **Mutilate** (21: 60 Energy, 2 combo points, daggers, 90% weapon damage) · Seal Fate · **Venom** (31-point finisher: +30% poison damage, +10% application chance)

=== "Combat"

    Improved Eviscerate · Puncturing Wounds · Deflection · Precision · Endurance · **Flawless Execution** (16: −15 Eviscerate cost) · **Hack and Slash** (replaces the weapon specializations: axes get the sword extra-attack proc, maces ignore armor) · Weapon Expertise · Aggression · Efficient Strikes

=== "Subtlety"

    Camouflage · Master of Deception · Setup · Dirty Tricks · Initiative · Improved Distract · **Premeditation** (16, 20 s) · Serrated Blades · **Dirty Deeds** (Garrote from the front) · Hemorrhage (+15% Rupture and Garrote damage) · Quietus · Cutthroat · **Thousand Cuts** (31) · **Death Mark** (new active: 25 Energy, 10 s cooldown)

## Removed

Dagger, Mace, Sword and Fist Weapon Specialization (merged into Hack and Slash) · Sleight of Hand · Deadliness

*The BlizzCon demo mentioned "Restless Blades". It is unconfirmed at launch.*
