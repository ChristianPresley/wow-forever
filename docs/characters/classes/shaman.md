## Baseline changes: totems

- Totem GCD is **1.0 s** (was 1.5). Persistent totems last **5 min** (was 2).
- A new **totem tracker UI**: right-click to destroy a totem. Resistance totems affect the whole raid.
- **Call of the Elements / Spirits / Ancestors** (lvl 20/30/40): totem-bar presets that drop all four totems in one 3 s cast.
- **Totemic Recall** (lvl 20) refunds 25% of totem mana. **Totemic Call** (lvl 22, 30 s cooldown) teleports your totems to you.
- **Fire Nova** is now a spell cast at your Fire Totem; Fire Nova Totem is gone.
- Windfury and Flametongue totems are party buffs, and Windfury adds an extra swing.
- **Tranquil Air Totem removed.**

## Other baseline changes

- Imbues last **60 min** and stack with stones and oils.
- Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning cast faster. Max-rank Lightning Bolt does +80% damage, and Chain Lightning is a 2.5 s cast again.
- Two-handed axes and maces are baseline, along with Enhancing Totems, Weapon Mastery and Improved Weapon Totems.

## Talents

=== "Elemental"

    Elemental Devastation · Elemental Alacrity · Improved Fire Nova · Eye of the Storm · **Call of Thunder** (16, +5% crit) · Elemental Reach · **Lightning Overload** (10/20/30%) · **Earthbound** (21: Earthbind roots for 5 s) · Elemental Fury · **Lightning Mastery** (the renamed Elemental Mastery slot; effect *unconfirmed*{ .unconfirmed })

=== "Enhancement"

    Thundering Strikes · Mental Dexterity (50–150% of Intellect as AP) · Improved Ghost Wolf · **Shamanistic Focus** (11) · Toughness · Flurry · **Stormstrike** (16: 150% weapon damage, 6 s cooldown) · **Spirit Weapons** (21) · Mental Quickness · Improved Stormstrike · **Maelstrom Weapon** · **Rage of the Farseer** (31: +30% attack speed for 25 s, 3 min cooldown)

=== "Restoration"

    Totemic Focus · Mindfulness · Natural Grace · Tidal Focus · Improved Reincarnation · **Water Shield** (11) · Restorative Totems · **Mana Tide Totem** (16, moved down from 31) · Healing Way · **Riptide** (31: 12 s cooldown, 40% cheaper, HoT only)

## Removed

**Elemental Mastery** (too strong in PvP) · **Lava Burst** (removed pre-launch) · Tranquil Air Totem · Shield Specialization · Nature's Guidance

Shaman PvP gear now comes in **healer, melee and caster** sets.
