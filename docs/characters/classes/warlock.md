## Baseline changes

- Curse of Agony and Curse of Doom are now **Banes**: one Bane per Warlock per target.
- Curse of the Elements covers all magic schools, and **Curse of Shadows is removed**.
- **Firestone** and **Spellstone** are weapon imbues. Spellstone affects Shadow only.
- Life Tap scales with Spirit. Drain Soul's chance to give a shard rises with each tick.
- Pets scale from your stats, gaining 2 Health per point of your Stamina.
- DoTs can crit.
- Training costs were lowered to offset Grimoire purchases.

## Talents

=== "Affliction"

    Suppression · Improved Corruption · Malediction · Soul Harvest · Improved Drains · Improved Bane of Agony · **Pandemic** (DoT crit damage) · Nightfall · **Curse of Exhaustion** (16) · Malevolence · Soul Siphon · **Wrack** (31: ~86 dps base at level 60)

    *The BlizzCon demo called the capstone "Drain Hope". Later sources say Wrack.*

=== "Demonology"

    Demonic Aegis · Fel Vitality · Demonic Energies · Master Summoner · **Demonic Sacrifice** (11, 2-hour buffs) · **Fel Domination** (16) · Decimation · **Demonic Brand** (pet threat, for pet-tanking) · **Soul Link** (21) · Improved Felhunter · Demonic Knowledge · Master Demonologist · **Demonic Pact** (31: keep your sacrifice buff while another demon is out)

=== "Destruction"

    Destructive Reach · Molten Skin · Aftermath (Conflagrate daze) · Ruin · Agonizing Flames · **Conflagrate** (16) · Fire and Brimstone · **Bane of Havoc** (21: 15% of damage done to others is copied to the cursed target) · Shadow and Flame · **Incinerate** (31)

## Removed (~17 talents)

Improved Healthstone · Fel Intellect · Fel Stamina · Improved Subjugate Demon · Improved Firestone · Improved Spellstone · Improved Curse of Weakness · Improved Drain Life · Improved Drain Mana · Improved Curse of Exhaustion · Grim Reach · Dark Pact · Improved Firebolt · Improved Lash of Pain · Devastation · Improved Immolate · Emberstorm

Warlocks can still **summon** with Ritual of Summoning. It is the only summon in the game, since Meeting Stones don't summon.
