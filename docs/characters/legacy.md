# Legacy System

**Also called:** Legacy · Legacy Points (LP) · Legacy talents
{: .fc-aka }

!!! forever "Forever only"
    The **Legacy System** is Forever's account-wide progression and **replaces achievements**. It arrived in Patch 1.60.1.

## How it works

1. Complete **Legacy Challenges** to earn **Legacy Points** (1 point each). **65 points** are available at launch.
2. Points are **earned account-wide** but **spent per character** in three trees: **Professions**, **Adventure** and **Resourcefulness**.
3. Each character can spend **up to 16 points** at launch. Blizzard says you don't need alts to reach the cap.
4. Respec Legacy talents at a class trainer.

- **No double-dipping:** once one character reaches a class-level or tradeskill threshold, other characters of the same class or profession earn nothing more for it.
- **UI:** hotkey **Y** or the micro-menu button, with Rewards, Challenges and Trees tabs. It unlocks with your first point.
- **Hardcore:** points earned on Hardcore apply on other rulesets, but not the other way around. Hardcore has its own challenges in place of the PvP ones.

## Point sources (65 at launch)

| Category | Points | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Classes** | 27 | Three tiers (Novice, Experienced, Master) for each of the 9 classes, at levels **25 / 45 / 60** |
| **Tradeskills** | 18 | Skill 150 / 225 / 300 in each of the 6 crafting professions (Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Leatherworking, Tailoring). Gathering doesn't count |
| **PvP** | 12 | Honor Rank 3, 7, 10, 13, 14 (5); "Master of" WSG, AB, AV and Darkspear Islands at exalted (4); Field of Honor journey weeks 4, 7, 10 (3) |
| **Dungeons** | 3 | Novice, Experienced and Master Spelunker |
| **Raids** | 3 | Conqueror of the Wilds (Hyjal Summit), of the Deeps (Barrow Deeps), of the Lair (Onyxia) |
| **Adventure** | 2 | The Lord Valthalak / Dungeon Set 2 questline; Explore Azeroth on one character |

!!! conflict "Conflict"
    The Warcraft Wiki's main Forever page lists class tiers at **25/40/60**. Its Legacy page and Warcraft Tavern say **25/45/60**.

## Legacy trees

Each tree has 7 known talents and 2 hidden "upcoming" slots.

### Professions

| Talent | Ranks | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Working Overtime | 5 | +4% skill-up chance per rank (primary, secondary and class tradeskills) |
| Bountiful Harvest | 5 | +20% Scarce materials from Mining, Herbalism and Skinning |
| Luremaster | 2 | 25% chance of an extra fish while a lure is active |
| Performance Bonus | 3 | 5% chance of double Merchant's Favor from crates |
| Bartering | 2 | −5% vendor prices per rank (up to −10% on mounts) |
| Master Chef | 5* | 10% chance of an extra cooking result |
| Dedicated Study | 1 | Daily +1 to your lowest tradeskill. Once all are at 300, gives Elemental Essences instead |
