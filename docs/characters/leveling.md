# Leveling, Mounts & Reputation

## Leveling

- **Cap 60, permanently.** Catch-up comes only from leveling.
- **1,000+ new quests** across new and reworked zones. See [Zones](../world/zones.md).
- **Pace:** developers put levels 1–20 at about **20.5 hours**, compared with 20.4 in Classic Era (fan-reported). Normal mobs take about 10–15 s to kill solo.
- **Dungeons:** mobs give *less* XP and quests give *much more*. Beta tuning halved dungeon quest bonus XP (1 Oct) and raised dungeon mob XP ~20% (8 Oct).
- Crafted food gives a small XP bonus.
- **Fastest classes in beta:** Hunter, then Warlock and Mage. **Slowest:** Warrior and Priest.
- **Rested XP:** camping, the Leatherworking Camp Tent, and the Legacy talents *Well Rested* and *Talented*.

### Carried over from Season of Discovery

- **Cozy Sleeping Bag:** a stacking XP buff and rested XP while logged out, from a scavenger hunt starting at level 14.
- **Chronoboon Displacer:** world buffs work in the open world only and are removed in dungeons and raids.
- **Library books** for every class: turning in 10/20/25 books unlocks quests at level 20/35/45.

## Riding

| Skill | Level | Speed | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Apprentice | 40 | +60% | **100g**, including your first racial mount (extra colors 20g) |
| Journeyman (epic) | 60 | +100% | **1,000g**. Epic mounts reportedly 200g each (*single source*) |
| Flying | — | — | **None, by design** |

- **Discounts:** Honored with your home faction gives −10% on Riding and racial mounts. The Legacy talent *Bartering* gives up to −10% on mounts. Skyborne reputation gives 5–20% off Galestriders.
- Mounts of other races require **Exalted** reputation.
- **Class mounts:** Paladin (*Tome of Nobility*; Forsaken paladins have their own path) and Warlock (Strahad Farsan, Ratchet, lvl 40), plus the hard level-60 epic class quests.
- **New mounts:** Galestriders (Skyborne), Reins of the Spectral Bear (Legacy), Reins of the Pack Kodo (6,000 Merchant's Favor), PvP Rank 11 Black War mounts, and the edition mounts (Cerulean Prideclaw, Veteran Adventurer's Loyal Companion).
- Store and edition mounts still need the Riding skill.

## Reputation

- The Classic home-city factions remain.
- **New:**
    - **High Order** (Alliance Skyborne) and **Windshapers** (Horde Skyborne). At Exalted, other races can buy Galestriders.
    - **Azeroth Commerce Authority** and **Durotar Supply and Logistics** (the profession crate factions).
    - **Darkspear Raiders / Theramore Expeditionary Force** (Darkspear Islands battleground). Exalted gives a tabard and a Legacy Point.
    - **Nightclaw** druids (Zephras Isle, repeatable quest).
    - **Kirin Tor** (Dalaran; not available to the Horde).
    - Mount Hyjal endgame factions (names unconfirmed).
    - Reworked **Gelkis** and **Magram** centaur factions (Desolace).
- The Legacy talent **Diplomat** gives up to +10% reputation gain.
