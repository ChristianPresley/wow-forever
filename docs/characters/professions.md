# Professions

This page gives the rules of professions. For a path from skill 1 to 300 and the full recipe list of each profession, see the [Profession Guides](../professions/index.md).

## Unchanged from Classic

- **9 primary professions:** Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Herbalism, Leatherworking, Mining, Skinning, Tailoring. A character can have **2**.
- **Secondary skills:** Cooking, First Aid, Fishing.
- **Skill cap 300:** Apprentice (75), Journeyman (char lvl 10, up to 150), Expert (lvl 20, up to 225), Artisan (lvl 35, up to 300).
- Jewelcrafting and Inscription are not in the game.

## New in Forever

!!! forever "Professions no longer give stats"
    Professions give **no passive stat bonuses**. Their value comes from crafting, gathering and [camping](../systems/camping.md).

- **600+ new recipes** according to the developers (a datamine counted 884). Armor crafters get many new **BoP** patterns.
- **Healing potions moved from Alchemy to First Aid** (about 6 potions).
- A new **Professions window** (hotkey **K**) with search, filters, recipe tracking and chat links.
- A dedicated **reagent bag** slot: the 6-slot Linen Reagent Bag (Tailoring) or a 4-slot starter gathering bag.
- A starter quest lets you select a gathering profession (+2 skill).
- **Scarce materials** from gathering (the Legacy talent Bountiful Harvest increases them).
- **Profession blueprints** drop from dungeon bosses.
- 6 crafting **titles** (Alchemist, Blacksmith, Enchanter, Engineer, Leatherworker, Tailor). Faction vendors sell them.
- Crafting professions give **[Legacy Points](legacy.md)** at 150, 225 and 300.
- **Mage-only Comprehension** tradeskill (see [Mage](../guides/mage/index.md#professions)).

## Crates & Merchant's Favor

This works like Season of Discovery. Loot **crates** in the world and fill them. Turn them in for silver, reputation and **Merchant's Favor**. Merchant's Favor is a character-bound currency.

| Faction | Side | Location |
|---|---|---|
| **Azeroth Commerce Authority** | **Alliance**{ .pill .pill--alliance } | Three Corners, Redridge Mountains |
| **Durotar Supply and Logistics** | **Horde**{ .pill .pill--horde } | West of the Crossroads, the Barrens |

High-end recipes need Favor **and** reputation. Examples: rare helm recipes for 30 Favor, a tabard for 200, a *Packmule Treat* for 1,500, and **Reins of the Pack Kodo** for **6,000**.

## Racials tied to professions

- **Gnome**, Engineering Specialization: 20% fewer device failures.
- **Tauren**, Cultivation: grows an herb that any player can pick. Herbalists get a bonus yield.
