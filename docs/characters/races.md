**Playable:** Human, Dwarf, Gnome, Night Elf (Alliance) · Orc, Troll, Tauren, Undead (Horde) · **Skyborne** (both factions, as two separate versions).
**Not playable:** Blood Elf, Draenei and every later race.

!!! forever "Racials were rebuilt"
    Every race now has **four racials: two active and two passive**. The old weapon-skill and resistance racials (Sword/Mace/Axe/Bow Specialization and the resistances) were **removed**. Base stats were corrected before launch; Tauren Warriors, for example, now start with +5 Strength over Humans.

### Lore

They descend from exiles of **Eldre'Thalas** (Dire Maul): Highborne magisters and ordinary kaldorei. A rebellion led by the masked **Shal'nan** opposed **Prince Tortheldrin**'s plan to draw power from the Twisting Nether, and drove the exiles west. They made a bargain with **wind spirits**, who lifted **Zephras Isle** into **Skywall**. There they lived for thousands of years under a Council of Elders.

When the wind spirits vanished, the pylons failed and settlements such as **Ban'aethal** were lost. After the **Al'Aketh** cult of Al'Akir was exposed, the Skyborne returned to Azeroth and split by faction:

Other groups include High Elder Talaanis Shadowsong, the Nightclaw Druids, the Peacekeepers and Empyrean Blades militias, and the Rangers of Thendal Grove. Druid forms are tied to **Urs'endris** (the Great Windborne Bear) and **Saeyleenan** (the Great Windborne Cat). Skyborne shamans train all four elements, not just air. Two elves can speak Darnassian to each other across factions.

### Customization

- **HD models only** at launch, with two body types. SD Skyborne models are expected in early 2027.
- Options: face, skin (vivid blue is the "Sky-Touched Blessing"), hair, ears, eyes, eyebrows, **feathers**, tattoos, jewelry and facial hair.
- **Druid form colors** (barbershop): Default (blue/white), Deep Purple and Blue, Umber, Empyrean.

### Galestriders

The Skyborne racial mount. The first one is sold at **Fairweather Stables**, Zephras Isle (53.8, 81.3), in Umber, Stormy and Empyrean, with Swift versions of each. Reputation discounts run 5/10/15/20% from Friendly to Exalted. **Other races** can buy them at **Exalted** with a Skyborne faction, earned through Zephras quests or cloth turn-ins in Dalaran or Mulgore.

During the beta, **1,889,084 Skyborne** were created, about **21%** of all characters.
