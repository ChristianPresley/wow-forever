## What players praise

- A **smooth launch**, especially after the beta queues. PC Gamer called it "buttery smooth"; queues rarely went past ~10 minutes.
- **A living, social world:** campfires, busy zones, community-first design.
- Visuals that "look modern and play vintage", plus the **SD/HD toggle** and opt-in presets.
- **Value:** included in the subscription (PC Gamer: "excellent bargain"), with a fresh, fair starting line for everyone.
- **Questing-first leveling**: dungeon kill XP cut and quest XP raised.
- Class reworks, especially the Paladin.
- **Discovery:** hidden items and secrets.

## What players criticize

- **The Skyborne $30 paywall** ("cash elves"), and the beta requiring the $59.99 pack. An r/wow thread passed 700 comments. Critics also call Skyborne a "Blood Elf palette swap" and Walk on Air mostly fall-damage negation.
- **Layering** splits groups and world PvP. The **PvP faction lock** separates friends.
- **A retail-style UI** with no Classic toggle, and the Midnight add-on restrictions (no WeakAuras).
- No Dungeon Finder bothers some players, while purists welcome its absence. GDKP ban debates.
- **Class balance:** overtuned racials in beta (Troll Warlock), Shaman frustration over Lava Burst's removal, a Thorns exploit, spell-power wands.
- Frame drops in cities. Beta loot and chest bugs.
- Some veterans feel the new visuals hurt nostalgia. Zones felt quieter than past Classic launches.

## Context

Blizzard's legal win against the **Turtle WoW** private server (shutdown 14 May 2026) pushed some of its players toward Forever. Claims that other private servers also shut down are *unconfirmed*{ .unconfirmed }.

## Streamers & guilds

- **Asmongold** (Olympus guild) leaned toward PvE, saying he couldn't play on PvP.
- **Naguura** and **Leon** (Bonjwa guild, 200–250 members) went **Horde** and held a vote on PvE vs PvP.
- An unverified forum list *unconfirmed*{ .unconfirmed } put Venruki, Pika, Soda, Xaryu, Esfand, Ziqo, Payo, Shobek and Summit on "PvP Alliance".
- **Payo** was banned before launch for encouraging gold buying.

## World firsts

### First to level 60

A **five-player group** tied for world first a little over **3 days** after launch (reported 7 Nov). They pulled big AoE packs and leveled in sync:

| Character | Class |
|---|---|
| Cloud Bells | Paladin |
| Lmgd Mmr | Shaman |
| Jokerd Prime | Mage |
| Burning Speed | Mage |
| Vitochie Gofast | Mage |

The next-highest player was level 54. Race, region and ruleset weren't reported.

### Raid race

The raids unlock **9 Dec**. With **no PTRs and hidden item stats**, it will be a blind race. No organized Race to World First broadcast has been announced.
