## What players praise

- A **smooth launch**, especially after the beta queues. PC Gamer called it "buttery smooth". Queues were rarely longer than ~10 minutes.
- **A living, social world:** campfires, busy zones, community-first design.
- Visuals that "look modern and play vintage", plus the **SD/HD toggle** and opt-in presets.
- **Value:** included in the subscription (PC Gamer: "excellent bargain"), with a new, fair start for all players.
- **Questing-first leveling**: dungeon kill XP cut and quest XP raised.
- Class reworks, especially the Paladin.
- **Discovery:** hidden items and secrets.

## What players criticize

- **The Skyborne $30 paywall** ("cash elves"), and the $59.99 pack that the beta needed. An r/wow thread had more than 700 comments. Critics also call Skyborne a "Blood Elf palette swap". They say that Walk on Air is mostly fall-damage negation.
- **Layering** splits groups and world PvP. The **PvP faction lock** separates friends.
- **A retail-style UI** with no Classic toggle, and the Midnight add-on restrictions (no WeakAuras).
- Some players do not like that there is no Dungeon Finder. Purists like that it is not there. Debates about the GDKP ban.
- **Class balance:** overtuned racials in beta (Troll Warlock), Shaman frustration about the removal of Lava Burst, a Thorns exploit, spell-power wands.
- Frame drops in cities. Beta loot and chest bugs.
- Some veterans feel that the new visuals hurt nostalgia. Zones felt quieter than at past Classic launches.

## Context

Blizzard won a legal case against the **Turtle WoW** private server (shutdown 14 May 2026). This caused some of its players to move to Forever. Claims that other private servers also shut down are *unconfirmed*{ .unconfirmed }.

## Streamers & guilds

- **Asmongold** (Olympus guild) preferred PvE. He said that he could not play on PvP.
- **Naguura** and **Leon** (Bonjwa guild, 200–250 members) selected **Horde** and held a vote about PvE vs PvP.
- An unverified forum list *unconfirmed*{ .unconfirmed } put Venruki, Pika, Soda, Xaryu, Esfand, Ziqo, Payo, Shobek and Summit on "PvP Alliance".
- **Payo** got a ban before launch because he encouraged gold buying.

## World firsts

### First to level 60

A **5-player group** tied for world first a little more than **3 days** after launch (reported 7 Nov). They pulled big AoE packs and leveled together:

| Character | Class |
|---|---|
| Cloud Bells | Paladin |
| Lmgd Mmr | Shaman |
| Jokerd Prime | Mage |
| Burning Speed | Mage |
| Vitochie Gofast | Mage |

The next-highest player was level 54. The reports did not give race, region or ruleset.

### Raid race

The raids unlock **9 Dec**. There are **no PTRs and the item stats are hidden**, so it will be a blind race. Nobody has announced an organized Race to World First broadcast.
