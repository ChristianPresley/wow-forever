The furbolg city of the **Blackmaw clan** is behind a gate that was sealed for a long time. The clan fights a corruption that spreads. The dungeon has **multiple routes**, and a hidden path leads "far beneath Azeroth". Reportedly, its area is **larger than Blackrock Depths**.

## Known details

- **Boss:** **Varrox Bleakhoof**.
- **Datamined items:** *Blackmaw Pouch* (possibly reputation-related), *Blackmaw Ritual Totem*.

!!! conflict "Conflict"
    GosuGamers (citing Wowhead) reports "20+ bosses" and no single route. Zockify counts 7. Neither figure is verified.
