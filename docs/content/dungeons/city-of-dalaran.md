The barrier hiding Dalaran's restoration has collapsed, unleashing arcane chaos: backfiring enchantments and runaway constructs. The dungeon has **multiple paths and optional encounters**.

## Attunement: Dalaran Sewer Key

At least one party member must carry the **Dalaran Sewer Key**. It comes from the Image of Archmage Modera in Silverpine Forest.

- **Alliance:** *An Alarming Request* (Emissary Jacques, Southshore) → … → *Heart of Disruption* → *Friend of the Kirin Tor*.
- **Horde:** *Key to the City* and *Prison Break-In* (Magus Wordeen Voidglare, Tarren Mill).
- The chains also have starting points in Stormwind and the Undercity.
