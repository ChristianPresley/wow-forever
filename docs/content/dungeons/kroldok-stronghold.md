An ogre fortress with a connection to the **Twilight's Hammer**. Ogre outrunners kidnap travelers, and people hear strange chanting at night. In the fortress, there are workshops, encampments and guarded passages.

## Known details

- **Boss:** **Overlord Mugg** (a Legacy challenge shows this name). The rest of the roster is *unconfirmed*{ .unconfirmed }.
- **Datamined items:** Krol'dok Compound Key, Krol'dok Ogre Key, and quest-starting *Krol'dok Moulding* items.

!!! conflict "Conflict"
    Zockify lists Krol'dok as a 10-player instance. Blizzard says all 9 new dungeons are 5-player.
