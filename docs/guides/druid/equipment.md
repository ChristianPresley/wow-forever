A Druid uses 3 different sets of items. Feral needs leather with Strength and Agility and a strong two-hand weapon. Balance needs spell damage, and Restoration needs healing power. With dual spec at level 40, you carry 2 of these sets.

About 40% of the items of Forever had hidden stats at launch. The stats of an item become visible when a player first finds it. Thus, no source has a tested list for level 60. The item names on this page come from Timesaver.gg (data of 6 November 2026), Method.gg, wowtbc.gg and Warcraft Tavern. The Codex did not verify the stats.

The crafted leather items have families with a name, for example Brawler's, Totemic, Wisdom's, Skulker's, Skycaller's, Mender's and Warden's. The client data gives no stats. The role of each family on this page comes from the fan lists that use it.

## Item rules in Forever { #rules }

- In form, your auto-attack uses the DPS of your weapon. Shred, Claw, Ravage, Primal Bite and Lacerate do a percentage of weapon damage.
- Weightstones, sharpening stones, weapon enchants and Windfury Totem work in form.
- Potions and most consumables work in form. Potions still have 1 shared cooldown.
- Hit is 1 stat for melee, ranged attacks and spells. A melee attack needs 9% hit against a raid boss (Warcraft Tavern).
- Nature's Reach gives 4% hit. Its tooltip does not limit the hit to spells, and Warcraft Tavern uses it for Cat.
- Expertise is a new stat. It replaces weapon skill, and it decreases the chance that an enemy dodges or parries. No source gives a confirmed cap.
- A tank needs 440 Defense against raid bosses (Warcraft Tavern).
- Weapons have spell damage and healing from about level 10. Healing items also have spell power.
- HoTs can crit in Forever. Haste does not make DoTs or HoTs tick faster (Warcraft Tavern).
- Method.gg says that the Embrace of the Viper set and the Blackened Leather set have new bonuses.
- Many of the best patterns cost Merchant's Favor at Three Corners in Redridge Mountains.
- Each profession gives a camp feature in place of a passive stat.

## Feral: leather and a two-hand weapon { #feral }

The set for Cat and Bear. You wear leather with Strength, Agility and Stamina, and you carry the two-hand weapon with the highest DPS. A Bear replaces some items with armor, Defense and dodge.

**Stat priority:** Leveling: weapon DPS > Strength, Agility, Attack Power > Stamina > Intellect. Cat at 60 (Warcraft Tavern): Hit to 9% > Expertise > Crit > Haste > Agility > Strength. Bear at 60: Defense to 440 > Dodge > Armor > Stamina
