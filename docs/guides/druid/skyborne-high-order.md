# High Order Skyborne Druid

!!! tip "Add-on"
    Read with the [Druid guide](index.md). This page covers only what changes for an Alliance Skyborne.

## Why this combo

**NEW in Forever.** The Skyborne (*shen'dorei*) exist only in this game, and the High Order are their Alliance faction, descended from Highborne magisters of Eldre'Thalas. Skyborne druids draw their forms from the wind spirits **Urs'endris** (the Great Windborne Bear) and **Saeyleenan** (the Great Windborne Cat). Their forms have their own look, with four colors at the barbershop.

You get **haste** (Wind Blessed), a **regeneration cooldown** (Read Ley Line) and **Walk on Air**, which works in forms. Warcraft Tavern calls the gap to Night Elf **marginal**. Your choice is mostly about looks and the Zephras Isle story.

!!! warning "Paid race"
    Skyborne need the **Skyborne Heroic Pack** or a higher edition. See [Editions](../../about/editions.md) and [Skyborne](../../characters/races.md#skyborne).

**Best for:** Balance and Restoration (mana regen), Cat if you value haste. **Compared with Night Elf:** less burst crit and no Shadowmeld; more sustain and mobility. See [Night Elf Druid](night-elf.md).

## Racials in practice

| Racial | Type | PvE | PvP | Rating |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Read Ley Line** | Active: +50% health and mana regen for 15 s; +100% while *Attuned* (1 hr buff from standing at a Ley Line) | Big for Balance leveling downtime and for Resto mana. **Not usable with Innervate** | Short reset between fights | **4/5**{ .stars .s4 } |
| **Walk on Air** | Active: 10 s controlled glide. **Works in forms** | Skip paths, survive falls, reach ledges | Escape off cliffs, flag-carrier tricks. Cooldown 1.5 or 2 min (*conflict*) | **3/5**{ .stars .s3 } |
| **Wind Blessed** | Passive: haste | Faster casts and swings, more Energy use | Same | **4/5**{ .stars .s4 } |
| **Elemental Insight** | Passive: +5% damage vs Elementals | Situational: Hyjal and Skywall elementals, some dungeons | None | **2/5**{ .stars .s2 } |

!!! conflict "Conflict: Wind Blessed"
    Sources give 1%, 2% or 3% haste. Blizzard's latest notes say **2%**.

Swap *Morning Glory Dew* for whatever drink you carry. Read Ley Line and Innervate don't stack, so Resto should use one and save the other.

## Starting zone walkthrough: Zephras Isle (1–~12)

Both Skyborne factions share [Zephras Isle](../../world/zephras-isle.md), phased apart. It takes you to about **12–14**.

**Thendal Village (1–5)**

1. Start at Thendal Village. Quests: *Coming of Age*, *Harmony in Balance*, *The Cirrusfly Queen*, *Harvesting Windstones* (gathering profession voucher), *Falling With Style* (teaches you to use Walk on Air) and *The Anchors of Zephras*.
2. Your faction opener is ***Reading the Leylines***, which shows you how to get Attuned.
3. Caster play as usual: Wrath, Moonfire, Rejuvenation.

**Road to Shen'dar Village (5–9)**

1. Fight Al'Aketh thugs under Malduko Cloudcrash. Ride the *Blessing of Zephras* tornadoes for +40% speed.
2. **The Great Outdoors** (Raan Wildwind) teaches [camping](../../systems/camping.md).
3. In Shen'dar Village, Constable Aonda hands out bandit, hippogryph and Prideclaw quests, ending with "Badwind" Bennic and *WANTED: Vulgara the Insatiable*.

**Falaath Village and Valanaar (9–11)**

1. Infiltrate cult-held **Falaath Village** wearing the Mark of Akir.
2. Return the Shadowsong Family Signet to High Elder Talaanis Shadowsong in **Valanaar** and meet Supreme Magister **Elaadrin Evengale**.
3. **Level 10 Bear Form:** ***The Great Ursera Spirit***. Instead of the Darnassus quest, Skyborne earn Bear Form on the island; the Ursera bear chain from Thendal Village ends with Urs'anah.

**Gustberry Lowlands and Shadowgale Forest (11–13)**

1. High Order chain *The Missing Scholar* (Shriekling Den), then Index Esoteria at the Overlook Standing Stones.
2. **Nightclaw druids** in Shadowgale Forest: the repeatable *Mercy Falls on Deaf Ears* gives Nightclaw reputation; *The Ties That Bind* and *Wounds of Betrayal* lead to Commander Haalien. Recording crystals in Nightclaw Cavern tell the Skyborne origin story.
3. Secret: `/sing` at the frogs in Gustberry Lowlands for the *Musical Gustjumper* pet.
4. Finish with the Shrine of Akir assault and *Confront Lorthuna*.

**Trainers:** Method lists **Xyton Silverwind**, **Naeluna Swiftmend** and **Lotheluum Starbreeze** on Zephras Isle. A 5 Nov hotfix fixed a Zephras druid trainer that wasn't spawning.

**Galestrider:** Fairweather Stables (53.8, 81.3) sells the racial mount for level 40. High Order reputation discounts it 5–20%.

## Route to 60 from Valanaar

The High Order airship leaves the **east Skydock** for **north Dalaran** in the Alterac Mountains, a 30–40 zone. Don't quest there at 12. Head for the Alliance lowbie zones.

| Levels | Zone(s) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1–13 | Zephras Isle | Bear Form at 10 on the island |
| 13 | Dalaran → Hillsbrad → Southshore | Walk south. Take the new **Southshore pier** boat loop to Menethil, or run on to Arathi and the Wetlands |
| 13–20 | Loch Modan, Westfall, Redridge (via Ironforge and the Tram) or Darkshore (via Menethil → Auberdine) | Lessons Anew (14) and Aquatic Form (16): where Skyborne take these is *unconfirmed*; check Valanaar first, then Darnassus |
| 20 | Zephras Isle | ***The Great Windborne Cat Spirit*** (Cat Form, Prowl, Claw, Rip) |
| 20–30 | Ashenvale, Wetlands, Duskwood, Hillsbrad | [Excavation Site](../../content/dungeons/excavation-site.md) |
| 28–40 | **Alterac and [Dalaran](../../world/zones.md#dalaran)**, Arathi, Desolace, Stranglethorn, Dustwallow | Your airship hub becomes your quest hub. [City of Dalaran](../../content/dungeons/city-of-dalaran.md); Kirin Tor reputation |
| 35–45 | **[Riverglades](../../world/zones.md#riverglades)** (Farholde Keep) | |
| 40–60 | Same Alliance route as the [Druid guide](index.md#leveling-walkthrough-160) | Felwood trainer Golhine the Hooded; Moonglade via Teleport |

**Getting home:** the airship runs both ways, and anyone can visit the island. Dalaran reportedly has no flight master, so walk to Southshore or Refuge Pointe for flights. **Alfina Nightgaze** in Dalaran is listed as a druid trainer.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Class quests:** island versions at 10 (*The Great Ursera Spirit*) and 20 (*The Great Windborne Cat Spirit*). The Darnassus quests are for Night Elves.
- **Reputation:** High Order (home) and **Kirin Tor** (Dalaran; Alliance-only). Nightclaw from the Zephras repeatable. Exalted High Order lets other races buy Galestriders.
- **Mount:** Galestrider (Umber, Stormy, Empyrean, plus Swift versions).
- **Form colors:** Default (blue/white), Deep Purple and Blue, Umber, Empyrean, changed at the barbershop.
- **Models:** HD only at launch; SD models expected in early 2027.
- **Languages:** Darnassian and Common. You can talk Darnassian with Windshaper elves across the faction line.
- **Lore:** the High Order chase lost arcane knowledge and lean toward the Kirin Tor. A druid among them follows the older, kaldorei side of Skyborne tradition.

## Verdict

A good, distinctive Alliance druid. **Wind Blessed** and **Read Ley Line** make it the best Alliance pick for **Balance and Restoration**, and it's close to Night Elf for Cat. You lose Shadowmeld and Elune's Light burst. The awkward part is the start: you land in a 30–40 zone at level 12 and must walk to the lowbie zones.
