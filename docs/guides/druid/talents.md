The Druid has 4 roles in 3 trees. **Feral Combat** is the tree for Cat damage and for the Bear tank. **Balance** is the caster damage tree, and **Restoration** is the healer tree. Some of the best talents for a role are in a different tree. For example, Heart of the Wild and Naturalist are in Restoration, but each Feral and Balance build takes them.

## How to read this page

The Druid has 52 talents in 3 trees. You get 1 talent point for each level from 10 to 60, for a total of 51 points.

- **Opens at:** the number of points that you must spend in the same tree before the row opens. Each row needs 5 more points.
- **Max:** the maximum rank of the talent.
- **Leveling:** the value of the talent when you quest and do dungeons from level 10 to 59.
- **PvE:** the value at level 60 in dungeons and raids. This is damage, healing or tank value, for the role of the tree.
- **PvP:** the value in battlegrounds and world PvP.

**S**{ .tier .tier-s } Core. Take it in each build of that type. **A**{ .tier .tier-a } Strong. Take it when you can. **B**{ .tier .tier-b } Good. Use it to fill points. **C**{ .tier .tier-c } Situational or a personal preference. **D**{ .tier .tier-d } Do not take it.

## FAQ

??? question "Where are Shifting Power and Primal Bite in the tree?"
    In the launch client, the 2 talents are in row 7 of Feral Combat and need 30 Feral points. Shifting Power needs Predatory Discipline, and Primal Bite needs Leader of the Pack. Beta-era guides put them at 16 points. The client also gives Shifting Power an 8 s cooldown.

??? question "Where is Gift of the Earthmother?"
    The launch client has no talent with this name. Row 3 of Restoration has Subtlety, Gift of Nature and Bloom. Gift of Nature increases all your healing by 10%.

??? question "Why does each Feral and Balance build put points in Restoration?"
    Heart of the Wild is now in row 1 of Restoration. It gives 20% Stamina to the Bear, 10% Strength to the Cat and 10% Intellect. Naturalist in row 2 increases all your damage by 10%. Thus, 10 Restoration points are good for each build.

??? question "Can I use Feral Charge in Cat Form?"
    This is not confirmed. Beta-era guides gave Feral Charge a Cat version. The tooltip in the launch client lists only Bear Form and Dire Bear Form.

??? question "Do I take Moonkin Form or Leader of the Pack for the raid aura?"
    The 2 auras give 3% crit chance to the party and raid within 45 yd. The earlier Codex class guide says that they do not stack. Thus, a raid needs only 1 of them.
