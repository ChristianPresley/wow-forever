## Why this combo

The Tauren is the original Horde druid. You start at Camp Narache in Mulgore and train in the Elder Rise of Thunder Bluff. Through Hamuul Runetotem, your people are close to the Cenarion Circle. Method recommends Tauren for **Bear tanking and PvP**. **Endurance** (+5% health) is the best tank racial for druids, and **War Stomp** now works in form. It is a Classic combination, not new in Forever.

**Best for:** Bear tank, battleground and world PvP, Herbalism.
**Compared with Windshaper Skyborne:** you get health, an AoE stun and better gathering, but you lose haste. See [Windshaper Skyborne Druid](skyborne-windshaper.md).

## Starting zone walkthrough: Mulgore (1–~12)

**Camp Narache (1–6)**

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  Your first trainer is **Gart Mistrunner** at Camp Narache.
2.  Do the Plainstrider and mountain cougar quests, then the Battleboar quests and the Brambleblade Ravine quilboar quests. Spam Wrath and Moonfire. Heal with Rejuvenation.
3.  At **4**, train Moonfire and Rejuvenation. At **6**, train Thorns.
4.  At about level 5, take **The Great Outdoors** to unlock camping.

</div>

**Bloodhoof Village (6–11)**

1. The druid trainer is **Gennia Runetotem**. Set your hearthstone here.
2. Do the quests around the Venture Co. mine, Palemane gnolls, and the Red Rocks.
3. **Level 8:** Entangling Roots. **Level 10:** Nature's Grasp and your first talent point.
4. **Level 10 Bear Form:** take **Heeding the Call** in Thunder Bluff (Elder Rise, from Turak Runetotem in Classic). It sends you to Moonglade and teaches **Teleport: Moonglade**.

**Leaving (11–12)**

- **Skywatcher Plateau** (northwest Mulgore) is new: the Windshaper airship lands here, and a path leads toward Desolace. You can fly to [Zephras Isle](../../world/zephras-isle.md) to look around.
- At about 11, go east through the Barrens to the **Crossroads**.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Class quests:** Heeding the Call (10), Lessons Anew (14), A Lesson to Learn (16), The Great Cat Spirit (20), all from Thunder Bluff.
- **Mount:** **Kodo** at Bloodhoof Village at 40. Honored with Thunder Bluff gives −10%. The **Reins of the Pack Kodo** (6,000 Merchant's Favor) is a profession reward.
- **Reputation:** Thunder Bluff. Darkspear Raiders from [Darkspear Islands](../../content/pvp/battlegrounds.md#darkspear-islands). You can get Windshaper reputation when you turn in cloth in Mulgore. At Exalted, it gives a Galestrider.
- **Barbershop:** Thunder Bluff got one in Forever.
- **No Kirin Tor:** the Horde cannot get Kirin Tor reputation, and Horde players are attacked in Dalaran.
- **Lore:** because of the Cenarion ties of Hamuul Runetotem, Moonglade feels like home. The Mount Hyjal story is mainly about the Cenarion Circle.

## Verdict

<div class="verdict" markdown>

The best Horde **bear** and the best druid for **PvP**. War Stomp in form is a real interrupt and escape. Endurance always gives value to tanks. Plainsrunning makes levels 1–30 much faster. Select Windshaper Skyborne if you mostly play Cat or Balance in PvE and want haste.
