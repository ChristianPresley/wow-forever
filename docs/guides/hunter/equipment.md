A Hunter wears leather to level 39 and can wear mail from level 40. The most important slot is the ranged weapon, because it sets the damage of Auto Shot and of all shots. For Beast Mastery and Marksmanship, the melee weapon gives stats only. For Survival, the melee weapon does damage.

About 40% of the items of Forever had hidden stats at launch. The stats of an item become visible when a player first finds it. Thus, no source has a tested list for level 60. The item names on this page come from Timesaver.gg (data of 6 November 2026), Warcraft Tavern, Method.gg, wowtbc.gg, Kami Labs and ConquestCapped. Only the Drowned City items have public stats.

## Item rules in Forever { #rules }

- The starting weapon of each race is a Classic-era fact: guns for Dwarves and Tauren, bows for Night Elves, Orcs and Trolls. No source gives it for Humans and Skyborne.
- No race has a weapon skill bonus in Forever. Weapon skill on items is much lower than in Classic.
- Method.gg says that Expertise replaces weapon skill on items. Warcraft Tavern says that the skill itself did not change. The exact rule is not clear.
- Method.gg says that hit is 1 stat for melee, ranged attacks and spells. Crit is 1 stat (Warcraft Tavern).
- Against a level 63 boss you need 9% hit at 300 weapon skill, or 6% at 305 (Warcraft Tavern).
- 1 Agility gives 2 ranged Attack Power and 1 melee Attack Power. 53 Agility gives 1% crit at level 60.
- 14 Attack Power gives 1 damage per second. The pet gets 10% of your Attack Power and 100% of your crit.
- 1 Intellect gives 15 mana. With Careful Aim 5/5, it also gives 1 Attack Power.
- Haste is a stat in Forever. Warcraft Tavern did not see it on items in the beta.
- A quiver or an ammo pouch uses 1 bag slot. Warcraft Tavern says that it often gives ranged attack speed, but gives no values.
- Necklaces can have an enchant in Forever.
- Healing potions come from First Aid, not from Alchemy.
- Each profession gives a camp feature in place of a passive stat. The Camp Chair of Skinning gives 2% crit.

## Melee Survival { #melee }

The set for the Survival path. Your melee weapon now does damage, so its damage and its speed are important. You still carry a ranged weapon for the pull. No source compares a two-hand weapon with 2 one-hand weapons.

**Stat priority:** Leveling: Agility > Attack Power > Crit > Strength > Intellect > Stamina and Spirit (Warcraft Tavern, beta). No source has a ranking for level 60

### Levels 1–9 { #melee-l1 }

Use the melee weapon with the most damage. You fight in melee often before level 10, because you have no pet.

| Slot | Item | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Melee | **Brave's Axe** | Quest: *Rite of Strength*, Mulgore (Horde) |
| Staff | **Apprentice's Spellstaff** | Quest: *Rare Books*, Elwynn Forest (Alliance) |

### Levels 10–19 { #melee-l10 }

A slow two-hand weapon makes Raptor Strike and Mongoose Bite hit harder. Train each new weapon type at a weapon master. A new weapon type starts at low skill.

| Slot | Item | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Melee | **Balanced Fighting Stick** | Quest: *WANTED: "Hogger"*, Elwynn Forest (Alliance) |
| Melee | **Steady Bastard Sword** | Quest: *Skull Rock*, Durotar (Horde) |
| Melee | **Piercing Axe** | Quest: *Red Silk Bandanas*, Deadmines (Alliance) |
| Staff | **Segmented Spider Leg** | [Ruins of Lordaeron](../../content/dungeons/ruins-of-lordaeron.md): Witherfang (Horde) |

### Levels 20–29 { #melee-l20 }

Polearms open at level 20. In Classic, a Hunter learns Dual Wield at level 20, but no source confirms the level for Forever. Predator's Edge from level 26 increases off-hand damage and melee crit damage.

| Staff | **Crescent Staff** | Quest: *Leaders of the Fang*, Wailing Caverns (Horde) |
| Polearm | **Bronze Dory** | [Blacksmithing](../../professions/blacksmithing.md), skill 95 **New**{ .pill .pill--new .pill--sm } |

### Levels 30–39 { #melee-l30 }

Strider Kick at level 30 does 100% of your melee weapon damage. The Drowned City gives a polearm with public stats and a ring with weapon damage.

|---|---|---|
| Melee | **Armor Piercer** | Razorfen Kraul, Razorfen Spearhide (Horde) |
| Polearm | **Felbough Arm** | [City of Dalaran](../../content/dungeons/city-of-dalaran.md): Fel Ancient |

### Levels 40–49 { #melee-l40 }

Mail opens at level 40, and it helps more in melee than at range. Krol'dok Stronghold drops 2 fist weapons, but no source gives their stats. Keep the Felbough Arm if you find no better weapon.
