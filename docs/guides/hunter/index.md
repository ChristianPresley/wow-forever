## Before level 1: Make your Hunter { #start }

A Hunter does physical damage from range and has a pet. From level 10, the pet holds the enemy while you shoot. This is the reason that the Hunter was the fastest class to level in the beta.

The class has many tools to stay alive. Concussive Shot and Wing Clip slow an enemy. Freezing Trap removes 1 enemy from the fight. Feign Death ends a fight that you cannot win.

Forever keeps this base and makes each talent tree a damage spec. **Beast Mastery** makes the pet stronger and adds hawks. **Marksmanship** makes your shots stronger and can play with no pet. **Survival** fights in melee with traps and bleeds.

The class has 1 rule that you must learn early. You cannot shoot a target that is nearer than 8 yards. Players call this the deadzone. Much of the class is about the distance between you and the enemy.

You make 3 decisions before level 1: your race, your talent path and your equipment set. The add-ons below describe each option. You can change the talents and the equipment later. You cannot change the race.

- Long fights use all your mana before you have talents such as Rapid Recuperation

- Ammunition, pet food and stable fees use bag space and gold (Classic-era behavior)

## Levels 1–9: Alone with a bow { #l1 }

You start with Auto Shot and Raptor Strike, and you have no pet. Pull each enemy with Auto Shot from maximum range. When the enemy gets to you, use Raptor Strike and fight in melee.

At level 4 you train Serpent Sting, a damage-over-time effect. Use it on each enemy at the start of the fight. At level 6 you train Arcane Shot, an instant shot, and Hunter's Mark.

Level 8 gives Concussive Shot, which slows the enemy. From this level you can kite. Shoot, use Concussive Shot, walk away, turn, and shoot again. Each step keeps the enemy out of melee range for longer.

You use ammunition with each shot. Buy arrows or bullets at each vendor, and keep a quiver or an ammo pouch in a bag slot. Your ranged weapon is your most important item.

At about level 5, take the quest *The Great Outdoors* near your first village. It teaches you to make a camp. See [Camping](../../systems/camping.md).

| Level | You train | Why it is important |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Auto Shot, Raptor Strike** | Your ranged attack and your first melee attack |
| 4 | **Serpent Sting** | Damage over time. Use it on each enemy |
| 4 | **Aspect of the Monkey** | More dodge when an enemy hits you. Classic-era level |
| 6 | **Arcane Shot, Hunter's Mark** | An instant shot. Hunter's Mark makes ranged attacks against the target stronger |
| 8 | **Concussive Shot** | A slow. It is the base of each kite |

!!! tip "Tip"
    Replace your ranged weapon each time that you find one with more damage per second. It sets the damage of Auto Shot and of all shots.

!!! hardcore "Hardcore"
    Before level 10 you have no pet, so each enemy hits you. Fight 1 enemy at a time. If a second enemy comes, use Concussive Shot and run.

## Levels 10–19: Tame your first pet { #l10 }

Level 10 changes the class. You train Aspect of the Hawk, and your trainer gives the quest *Taming the Beast*. You tame 3 beasts with a Taming Rod. Then *Training the Beast* teaches Tame Beast, Feed Pet, Revive Pet and Beast Training.

From here, the pet fights for you. Send the pet first, and let it use Growl. Then shoot. The enemy stays on the pet, so you take little damage and you stop to eat less often.

You also get your first talent point at level 10, and 1 more point at each level. You have 51 points at level 60. The Talents add-on below gives the talent for each point. The Legacy talent *Talented* can give you the first point up to 5 levels earlier.

The pet needs care. Feed it until it is Happy. A Happy pet does 125% damage, and an Unhappy pet does only 75%. Mend Pet at level 12 heals it during the fight. You can tame only a beast of your level or lower.

Your first dungeons open at level 13. Quests give much more XP than dungeon enemies, so do each dungeon for its quests. In a group, set the pet to Passive or Defensive and turn off Growl. The tank holds the enemies there.

At level 14, start the scavenger hunt for the Cozy Sleeping Bag. It gives an XP buff and rested XP. See [Leveling](../../characters/leveling.md).

| Level | You train | Why it is important |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | **Aspect of the Hawk** | More ranged Attack Power. Your default aspect |
| 10 | **Tame Beast, Call Pet, Feed Pet, Revive Pet** | From the taming quests. The pet learns Growl, Great Stamina and Natural Armor |
| 12 | **Mend Pet** | Heals the pet during the fight |
| 12 | **Wing Clip** | A melee slow. Use it, then walk out of the deadzone. Classic-era level |
| 16 | **Immolation Trap, Mongoose Bite** | Your first trap, and the melee attack of the Survival tree. Classic-era level |
| 18 | **Multi-Shot** | A shot that hits more than 1 target |

## Levels 20–29: Aimed Shot, traps and speed { #l20 }

Before level 20, each fight is the same: the pet tanks and you shoot. At level 20 you get tools for the start of the fight, for a second enemy and for travel. Your trainer teaches Aimed Shot, Freezing Trap and Aspect of the Cheetah.

Aimed Shot is a cast with a large hit, and all specs get it from the trainer in Forever. It changes the start of each fight. Cast it while the pet runs to the target. Remember that Aimed Shot and Multi-Shot have the same cooldown.

Traps are your crowd control. Freezing Trap freezes 1 enemy for 10 seconds, and it ends when the target takes damage. In Forever you can put a trap during combat. All traps have 1 cooldown of 30 seconds, so select the correct trap.

Use Aspect of the Cheetah for travel only. If an enemy hits you while it is active, you become Dazed. Change to Aspect of the Hawk before you fight.

Rapid Fire at 26 is your first damage cooldown. Frost Trap at 28 slows all enemies in an area. Polearms become available at the weapon masters at level 20. Expert professions also open at level 20.

Level 25 gives the *Hunter: Novice* Legacy Point. Only the first Hunter on your account gets it. See [Legacy](../../characters/legacy.md).

| Level | You train | Why it is important |
|---|---|---|
| 20 | **Aimed Shot** | A cast with a large hit. It has the same cooldown as Multi-Shot |
| 20 | **Aspect of the Cheetah, Freezing Trap** | Travel speed, and crowd control for 1 enemy |
| 20 | **Disengage** | Removes threat. It is not a leap. Classic-era level |
| 22 | **Scorpid Sting** | Decreases the hit chance of the target by 2%. Classic-era level |
| 24 | **Track Hidden** | Shows enemies in stealth on the minimap |
| 26 | **Rapid Fire** | Your first damage cooldown |
| 28 | **Frost Trap** | Slows all enemies in an area |

## Levels 30–39: Feign Death and careful pulls { #l30 }

Level 30 gives Feign Death. You fall down, and enemies stop their attack on you. Use it when the pet dies, when you pull too many enemies, or when you have more threat than the tank.

Feign Death is not a guarantee. An enemy can resist it. Always have a second plan: a trap, a slow or a way to run.

Your pet also learns Dash or Dive at level 30. Viper Sting at 36 drains mana from casters. In dungeons, you now have 3 tasks: ranged damage, Freezing Trap on a marked target, and careful pulls.

The new battleground Battle for the Darkspear Islands opens at level 30. It has 15 players on each side, 4 bases and a flag in the center. A Hunter is good at flag chases and at the defense of a base. See [Battlegrounds](../../content/pvp/battlegrounds.md#darkspear-islands).

Start to save gold now. At level 40 you need 100g for riding, and dual spec costs 50g. The new zone [Riverglades](../../world/zones.md#riverglades) opens at level 35. Artisan professions and the centaur quests in Desolace also need level 35.

| Level | You train | Why it is important |
|---|---|---|
| 30 | **Feign Death** | Removes you from combat. An enemy can resist it |
| 30 | **Aspect of the Beast** | The melee aspect of Survival. Classic-era level |
| 34 | **Explosive Trap** | Fire AoE damage. Classic-era level |
| 36 | **Viper Sting** | Drains mana. Warcraft Tavern gives this level |

**Dungeons in this chapter**

- Razorfen Kraul and Gnomeregan **29–38**{ .lvl }
- Scarlet Monastery **30–46**{ .lvl }: 4 wings. Arcanist Doan in the Library drops a crossbow
- [The Drowned City](../../content/dungeons/drowned-city.md) **35–42**{ .lvl }: New. It drops a gun, a polearm and leather with Agility

!!! tip "Tip"
    In a dungeon, pull with Hunter's Mark and Auto Shot, and let the tank get threat first. Nothing shows that Forever has Misdirection.

!!! hardcore "Hardcore"
    Keep Feign Death and a trap ready before each pull. Do not fight with Aspect of the Cheetah or Aspect of the Pack active.

## Levels 40–49: Mail, a mount and a second spec { #l40 }

Level 40 changes much. Your class trainer teaches you to wear mail. You can buy riding for 100g, which includes your first mount. You can buy dual spec for 50g from your class trainer.

Mail gives more armor, but Agility is more important than the armor type. Warcraft Tavern says that leather and mail are both good at level 60. Replace a leather item only when the mail item has better stats.

Each talent path gets its largest talent at level 40. The Talents add-on shows how it changes your fight. The public beta stopped at level 30. Thus, almost every build above level 30 is theory, not tested play.

With dual spec, keep your solo build in 1 spec. A good pair is Beast Mastery for quests, with Marksmanship for dungeons or Survival for PvP. The cost of a respec decreases to 1g with time. See [Leveling](../../characters/leveling.md#dual-specialization).

Change your pet for the task. Use a Wolf in dungeons, because Furious Howl helps the group. Keep a Boar or a Cat for quests. Aspect of the Wild at 46 gives Nature resistance to the group.

Level 45 gives the *Hunter: Experienced* Legacy Point.

## Levels 50–59: Mana, hit and the Onyxia attunement { #l50 }

The last 10 levels have the most dungeons. Reportedly, each boss in the new dungeons drops a blue item. Start to collect your level 60 equipment here.

Your talents are almost complete. Most of the last points go into a second tree. Start to keep items with hit, because you need hit at level 60.

Mana is the limit in long dungeon fights. Use Aspect of the Hawk and Serpent Sting on each boss, but do not use Arcane Shot on cooldown when your mana is low. Auto Shot uses no mana.

The dungeons also ask more of your control tools. If you pull too many enemies, use Feign Death. Keep Freezing Trap ready for a second enemy. Remember the trap cooldown: after Explosive Trap, Freezing Trap is not ready for 30 seconds.

Start the attunement for Onyxia's Lair early. Its quest chain goes through Blackrock Depths and Blackrock Spire. Quest in Azshara for its new quests, and in Winterspring for the route to Mount Hyjal.

## Level 60: Ranged weapon, hit and ammunition { #l60 }

At level 60 you get the *Hunter: Master* Legacy Point. Mount Hyjal opens through Darkwhisper Gorge in Winterspring. The fast mount costs 1,000g.

All 3 launch raids open on 9 December 2026. Use the time before that date for 4 tasks. Get the best ranged weapon that you can. Get hit from items. Collect consumables and ammunition. Complete the attunement for Onyxia's Lair.

The hit number is not certain. Warcraft Tavern gives 9% at 300 weapon skill, and 6% at 305 skill, against a level 63 boss. Its class overview says 9% against bosses at level 60. Only the Survival talent Surefooted gives hit.

No source has a tested item list for level 60. Collect blue items with Agility, hit and crit from the level 60 dungeons. Get reputation from the Battle for the Darkspear Islands for its rewards: trinkets, rings and weapons.

The attunement gives the Drakefire Amulet. It is not account-wide, so each character must do it. If your path changes its build at level 60, respec now. The Talents add-on gives the builds of your path.

**Dungeons in this chapter**

- [Blackmaw Hold](../../content/dungeons/blackmaw-hold.md) **55–60**{ .lvl }

## Raids and PvP: Lone Wolf or the pet { #raid }

**The Barrow Deeps** has 10 players. In a small raid, the abilities of your pet and your traps are more important. A Wolf with Furious Howl helps the physical damage dealers. See [The Barrow Deeps](../../content/raids/barrow-deeps.md).

**Hyjal Summit** has 20 players and 13 bosses. It is possibly the main source of Hunter equipment. See [Hyjal Summit](../../content/raids/hyjal-summit.md).

**Onyxia's Lair** has 40 players and much Fire damage. Blizzard recommends Fire resistance but gives no number. Keep distance from other players in phase 2. See [Onyxia's Lair](../../content/raids/onyxias-lair.md).

Players expect Marksmanship in raids, because it has Trueshot Aura and Sniper Shot. Lone Wolf gives 20% damage with no pet, so some Hunters dismiss the pet. It is not confirmed how much damage a pet does in raids, or how well pets live through boss AoE. Keep a pet in the stable for fights where its abilities help. Pets do not get player buffs, but they get your crit and a part of your Attack Power.

Tranquilizing Shot removes Enrage and Frenzy effects from an enemy. No source says where you learn it in Forever.

In PvP, Survival is the control spec. Each trap roots its targets with Entrapment. Counterattack roots a melee player after a parry, and Strider Kick gives you speed. Lacerating Strikes adds a bleed. Roots from procs share diminishing returns. See [PvP](../../content/pvp/index.md) and [Battlegrounds](../../content/pvp/battlegrounds.md).

| Enemy in PvP | What you do |
|---|---|
| Warrior, Rogue | Keep range with Wing Clip, Frost Trap and Concussive Shot. Scorpid Sting decreases their hit chance |
| Mage | A difficult fight. Viper Sting drains mana. Feign Death makes the Mage lose its target. Keep Scatter Shot for later in the fight (Classic-era) |
| Priest, Shaman, Druid healers | Use a pet with Dismember or Sonic Blast. Use Viper Sting, and keep a sting on the target |
| Hunter | Pet control and trap timing decide the fight. Tranquilizing Shot removes the Enrage of the enemy pet |
| Warlock | Kill the pet, or disarm it with *Mine!*. Tranquilizing Shot also helps |

PvP pets: Cat (fast attacks), Wind Serpent (Lightning Breath ignores armor), Crocolisk (Dismember), Bat (Sonic Blast) and Bird of Prey (*Mine!*).

Battlegrounds: Warsong Gulch from level 10, Arathi Basin from 20, the Darkspear Islands from 30 and Alterac Valley from 51. Honor and ranks have seasons with weekly limits. You can get Rank 14 at about week 10.

In the open world, Feign Death, Freezing Trap and Concussive Shot let you select your fights. Track Humanoids and Track Hidden show enemies before they attack.

!!! hardcore "Hardcore"
    [Hardcore](../../about/hardcore.md) comes in winter 2026/27. The Hunter is one of the safest classes for it. Play Beast Mastery, and keep a defensive pet (Boar, Bear or Turtle) for dangerous zones. In corridors, set the pet to Passive so that it does not pull. Do elite quests and dungeons with a group. Walk on Air and Shadowmeld are more escapes for Skyborne and Night Elves.

## Professions { #professions }

A Hunter wears leather and then mail, and Leatherworking makes both. You can skin while the pet completes a kill. A profession gives no passive stat in Forever. Each profession adds a [camp feature](../../systems/camping.md).

| Rank | Professions | Use them for |
|---|---|---|
| **S**{ .tier .tier-s } | [Leatherworking](../../professions/leatherworking.md) + [Skinning](../../professions/skinning.md) | The selection of Method.gg. New leather and mail from cheap materials, quivers and armor kits. Camp features: Camp Tent (rested XP) and Camp Chair (+2% crit) |
| **A**{ .tier .tier-a } | [Engineering](../../professions/engineering.md) + [Mining](../../professions/mining.md) | The selection of Warcraft Tavern. Guns, scopes, ammunition, explosives and PvP gadgets. Camp features: Reagent Bot and Lodestone (melee Attack Power) |
| **B**{ .tier .tier-b } | [Alchemy](../../professions/alchemy.md) + [Herbalism](../../professions/herbalism.md) | Agility elixirs and flasks for raids. Tauren Herbalists get more herbs from Cultivation. Camp features: Mana Well (mana regeneration) and Incense Candle (Intellect) |

[Cooking](../../professions/cooking.md) makes food with an XP bonus and food for the pet. [First Aid](../../professions/first-aid.md) makes bandages and healing potions. [Fishing](../../professions/fishing.md) gives the Fish Bowl camp feature. Compare all classes in [Best professions by class](../../characters/professions.md#best-professions-by-class).

You can spend a maximum of 16 [Legacy](../../characters/legacy.md) points on 1 character. Crafting professions give Legacy Points at skill 150, 225 and 300.

## Common mistakes

- You use Multi-Shot and expect Aimed Shot to be ready. The 2 abilities have the same cooldown.
- You keep Growl on in a group. The pet takes enemies from the tank.
- You do not feed the pet. An Unhappy pet does only 75% damage.
- You have no ammunition in the middle of a dungeon.
- You fight with Aspect of the Cheetah active and become Dazed.
- You ignore Intellect on a build with Careful Aim. The talent changes Intellect into Attack Power.
- You use Explosive Trap and then need Freezing Trap. All traps have 1 cooldown.
- You use Feign Death as your only plan. An enemy can resist it.

## FAQ

??? question "Which path is best for leveling?"
    Beast Mastery. Method, Mobalytics and Warcraft Tavern recommend it, because the pet tanks and you stop less often. The Hunter was the fastest class in the beta.

??? question "Is it a good idea to level with Lone Wolf and no pet?"
    No. Lone Wolf gives 20% damage, but you lose your tank. It is a raid talent. A Blizzard forum thread criticizes it because it rewards you when you ignore the pet.

??? question "My pet dies often. Is that my mistake?"
    Partly, it is the game. At launch, pets were less durable than in Classic Era. Blizzard announced a fix for 10 November. Use Mend Pet early, take Endurance Training, and use a defensive family for large pulls.

??? question "Why can I not put a Frost Trap after an Explosive Trap?"
    In Forever, all traps have 1 cooldown of 30 seconds.

??? question "Why does my shot not fire?"
    The target is in the deadzone, nearer than 8 yards. Move back, or use Wing Clip first.

??? question "Does Hunter's Mark help the pet?"
    Method says yes, because the pet gets a part of your Attack Power.

??? question "Is Survival a melee spec now?"
    Mostly. You can shoot first and then fight in melee, or stay in melee with 2 weapons and Predator's Edge. It is weaker than Beast Mastery for quests.

??? question "Can I tame a Fox?"
    Yes. Fox is a new family with Trickster's Dance, which gives dodge and attack speed for 12 seconds. Players report foxes on Zephras Isle, but this is not confirmed.

??? question "Can I get the epic bow Rhok'delar?"
    No. The quest needs a drop from Molten Core, and Molten Core is not in Forever at launch.
