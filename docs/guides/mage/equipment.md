A Mage wears cloth for all 60 levels. The important slots are the weapon and the wand. Low levels need spell power, Intellect and Spirit. Level 60 needs spell power and hit.

About 40% of the items of Forever had hidden stats at launch. The stats of an item become visible when a player first finds it. Thus, no source has a tested list for level 60. The item names on this page come from Timesaver.gg (data of 6 November 2026), Method.gg and wowtbc.gg. The Codex did not verify the stats.

## Item rules in Forever { #rules }

- Hit is 1 stat for melee, ranged attacks and spells. Items show hit as a percentage.
- A raid boss needs 17% spell hit (Method.gg). Warcraft Tavern says 16%. Precision gives 6%, so you need about 11% from items.
- Weapons have bonus spell damage from about level 10 (Method.gg).
- Fan reports say that wand damage increases with spell power. Blizzard has not confirmed it.
- Items for one school of magic are almost of no use to a different spec (Method.gg).
- Reportedly, healing items also give spell damage. One source (WowCarry) gives one third of the healing value.
- A staff can have a much larger enchant than a one-hand weapon: +65 spell power against +30 (beta data).
- Necklaces and off-hand items can have enchants. These slots are new.
- Each profession gives a camp feature in place of a passive stat. The Faction Banner of Tailoring gives Spirit.

## Stamina for PvP and Hardcore { #stamina }

A safer set. You replace some Intellect and Spirit with Stamina. Use it for battlegrounds, for world PvP and for Hardcore. No source has an item list for this set.

**Stat priority:** Stamina > Spell Power > Intellect. Keep enough hit for dungeons

### Levels 1–9 { #stamina-l1 }

Take Stamina on each item that offers it. A wand is still your first important item.

### Levels 10–19 { #stamina-l10 }

Take Stamina and Intellect in place of Spirit. More health gives you time to cast Frost Nova and step back.

### Levels 20–29 { #stamina-l20 }

Keep a second set with Stamina for PvP. Ice Block, at level 26 on the Frost path, does not replace health.

### Levels 30–39 { #stamina-l30 }

The Battle for the Darkspear Islands opens at level 30. Use Ice Armor in PvP against melee classes. PvP ranks give necklaces from a vendor.

### Levels 40–49 { #stamina-l40 }

Mana Shield absorbs damage, and the Frost path adds Ice Barrier at level 40. But the shields end quickly, and then only your health protects you. Thus, do not replace Stamina with more Intellect.

### Levels 50–59 { #stamina-l50 }

The battleground factions sell items for reputation. See the [PvP](../../content/pvp/index.md) page for ranks and seasons.

### Level 60 { #stamina-l60 }

PvP has 1 blue honor set and 1 epic honor set. An epic item needs the blue item and an Emboldened Seal, which needs a rank. The Mage items have Magus (Horde) or Silk (Alliance) in the name. In Hardcore, keep the Stamina set for each dungeon.
