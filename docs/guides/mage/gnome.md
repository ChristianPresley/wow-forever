# Gnome Mage

!!! tip "Add-on"
    Read with the [Mage guide](index.md). This page covers only what changes when your Mage is a Gnome.

## Why this combo

Gnome Mage is the original "glass-cannon with an escape hatch". Forever adds **Eureka!**, an 8-second burst window for non-periodic spells, on top of Escape Artist and extra mana. Gnomes are also the smallest target in PvP and the best Engineers. If you want the highest burst on the Alliance side, this is it.

## Racials in practice

| Racial | Type | Rating | Mage use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Escape Artist** | Active | ★★★★★ PvP · ★★ PvE | Removes roots and snares and gives 3 s immunity. Breaks Frost Nova, Hamstring, Entangling Roots and Frostbite, then you Blink away |
| **Eureka!** *(new)* | Active | ★★★★ | For 8 s, non-periodic abilities cost 10% less and deal 10% more. Fits Arcane Blast chains, Fireball/Pyroblast, Ice Lance shatters. It does not boost DoTs such as Ignite or Frostfire Bolt's tick |
| **Expansive Mind** | Passive | ★★★ | +5% maximum mana. More casts per drink and a bigger Arcane burn phase |
| **Engineering Specialization** | Passive | ★★ | 20% fewer device failures. Only matters if you take Engineering, but Engineering is a strong PvP pick |

## Starting zone walkthrough (1–12)

Coldridge Valley and Dun Morogh follow the Classic layout. Quest names are Classic-era.

1. **Coldridge Valley (1–6).** Start at Anvilmar. Your Mage trainer is **Marryk Nurribit** inside Anvilmar (Classic); check your bags for his class letter. Kill wolves, troggs and Frostmane trolls in the valley.
2. **Level 4.** Train Frostbolt and Conjure Water.
3. **Level 5–6.** Head through the tunnel to **Kharanos**. The Thunderbrew Distillery inn is your hub. Magis Sparkmantle trains Mages there (Classic).
4. **Kharanos (6–10).** Do the Brewnall Village, Shimmer Ridge and Gnomeregan-outskirts quests, plus the Frostmane troll cave. Polymorph at 8.
5. **Level 10.** Frost Nova and your first talent point. Ironforge trainers are in the **Mystic Ward**. Tailoring is in the Great Forge area.
6. **10–12.** Finish Dun Morogh and move to Loch Modan via the North Gate pass.

!!! tip "Hall of Thanes"
    Forever's new dungeon [Hall of Thanes](../../content/dungeons/hall-of-thanes.md) (13–20) is in Ironforge itself. It's the easiest first dungeon for a Gnome.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Reputation:** Gnomeregan Exiles Honored for 10% off riding and the Mechanostrider. Other races can only ride Mechanostriders at Exalted.
- **Gnomeregan** (31–37) is where many Alliance quests point you; a Mage's AoE is welcome against the leper gnome packs.
- **Kirin Tor:** Alliance-only reputation in Dalaran.
- **Engineering:** nets, bombs and grenades are classic PvP tools; Engineering Specialization makes them fail less.
- **Lore:** Gnome magi have studied in Dalaran since its founding; your Engineering and arcane background suit the restored city.

## Verdict

The best Alliance PvP Mage after the Human, and arguably the best for burst PvE thanks to Eureka!. Escape Artist turns every root into a wasted global for your opponent. If you like Engineering, take Gnome.
