## Why this combo

Human Mage is the classic Alliance caster. It is still one of the best all-round choices. Forever replaced the old weapon racials with a **stun breaker** and **+1% crit**. A Mage uses both of them all the time. You also start near Stormwind, which now has a harbor with a boat to Darkshore. Thus your early route has the most options on the Alliance side.

## Starting zone walkthrough (1–12)

Northshire and Elwynn are mostly the Classic zones. The quest names below are Classic-era. Forever kept the core chains, but added new NPCs and side quests in some starting zones.

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **1–5**{ .lvl } **Northshire Abbey.** Deputy Willem gives you the first quests: the wolves and kobolds outside the abbey. Your Mage trainer is **Khelden Bremen** on the upper floor of the abbey (Classic location). He also sends a class letter, which you find in your bags.
2.  **2–4**{ .lvl } At 4, train Frostbolt and Conjure Water. Kill kobolds in the vineyard and the Echo Ridge Mine.
3.  **5–6**{ .lvl } Complete *Report to Goldshire* and leave the valley. Fire Blast at 6.
4.  **6–10**{ .lvl } **Goldshire.** The Lion's Pride Inn is your hub. A Mage trainer works in the inn (Classic). Do the Fargodeep Mine, Jerod's Landing and Eastvale Logging Camp quests. At 8, Polymorph makes double pulls safe.
5.  **10**{ .lvl } Frost Nova and your first talent point. Select a gathering or crafting profession now. Tailoring trainers are in the Trade District of Stormwind.
6.  **10–12.** Complete Elwynn (Princess, the Brotherhood of Thieves chain) and do the *Westfall* letters. Or take the new **Stormwind Harbor** boat to Darkshore.

</div>

!!! tip "Stormwind Mage Quarter"
    All trainers and the Mage portal trainer are in the Wizard's Sanctum in the Mage Quarter. The transmogrifier, **Fyrenz Vishonar**, is also in the Mage Quarter (48, 84).

## Route to 60 from Stormwind

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **10–20**{ .lvl } Westfall, then Redridge **or** Darkshore

    <span class="step-label">Travel</span> Fly from Stormwind. The harbor boat goes directly to Auberdine

2.  **18–25**{ .lvl } Loch Modan or the last Darkshore quests, Redridge

    <span class="step-label">Travel</span> Deeprun Tram to Ironforge for Loch Modan

3.  **20–30**{ .lvl } Duskwood, Wetlands, Ashenvale

    <span class="step-label">Travel</span> Wetlands has the new [Excavation Site](../../content/dungeons/excavation-site.md) dungeon

4.  **25–35**{ .lvl } Hillsbrad (new Southshore pier), Alterac Mountains

    <span class="step-label">Travel</span> Menethil → Southshore → Auberdine boat loop

5.  **30–40**{ .lvl } Stranglethorn, Arathi, Desolace, Dustwallow

    <span class="step-label">Travel</span> Dalaran (Alterac) is Alliance-friendly. The [City of Dalaran](../../content/dungeons/city-of-dalaran.md) key chain starts in Southshore or Stormwind

6.  **35–45**{ .lvl } **[Riverglades](../../world/zones.md#riverglades)**

    <span class="step-label">Travel</span> Walk east from Redridge. The Farholde Keep flight connects to Lakeshire

7.  **40–50**{ .lvl } Tanaris, Feralas, Hinterlands, Badlands

    <span class="step-label">Travel</span> Riding at 40

8.  **50–60**{ .lvl } Un'Goro, Azshara, Felwood, Plaguelands, Winterspring, Burning Steppes

    <span class="step-label">Travel</span> Winterspring goes to [Mount Hyjal](../../world/zones.md#mount-hyjal)

</div>

**Teleports (Classic levels):** Stormwind and Ironforge at 20, Darnassus at 30. Ask other Mages for a Dalaran portal. *Teleport: Dalaran* at 50 is unconfirmed.

**Dungeons on the route:** The Deadmines (Westfall) and The Stockade (Stormwind) are very near. [Hall of Thanes](../../content/dungeons/hall-of-thanes.md) is a short tram ride away.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Class quests:** Classic-era Alliance Mage quests go through the Stormwind and Ironforge trainers. All classes can do the library-book quests at 20/35/45. Give the books in your capital.
- **Reputation:** at Honored with Stormwind, you get a 10% discount on riding and the Horse. The **Azeroth Commerce Authority** crate faction is at Three Corners, Redridge. It is near your route at 15–25.
- **Kirin Tor:** reputation in Dalaran, only for the Alliance.
- **PvP:** Champion's Hall in Stormwind has PvP vendors.
- **Lore:** most of the magi of the Kirin Tor are Humans. The restored Dalaran in the Alterac Mountains is truly your city.

## Verdict

<div class="verdict" markdown>

A safe choice. Will to Survive is the best Alliance PvP racial for a Mage. Ruthless helps every spec, and the extra Spirit makes early leveling easy. Select Gnome for more burst and root breaks, or Skyborne for haste and regen.

</div>
