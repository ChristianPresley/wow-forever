A Mage kills enemies from range and controls the fight. Polymorph removes 1 enemy from the fight. Frost Nova holds enemies in place. Blink moves you away from danger. The class also has utility that no other class has: conjured food and water, teleports, portals, Remove Lesser Curse, Counterspell and Slow Fall.

The weak point of the class is defense. A Mage wears cloth and has the lowest armor in the game. Thus, each fight has 1 rule: do not let the enemy stay near you. Each chapter of this page shows how you obey that rule with the spells of that level range.

In Forever, Blizzard rebuilt all 3 talent trees. Each school of magic (Arcane, Fire and Frost) is now a full damage spec. The class also got Frostfire Bolt, a spell that counts as 2 schools, and Comprehension, its own minor tradeskill.

You make 3 decisions before level 1: your race, your talent path and your equipment set. The add-ons below describe each option. You can change the talents and the equipment later. You cannot change the race.

## <span class="fc-ch__lv">Levels 1–9</span> Fireball, Frostbolt and Polymorph { #l1 .fc-ch data-toc-label="1–9 · Fireball" data-label="1–9 · Fireball" }

You start with Fireball and Frost Armor. Keep Frost Armor on you at all times. To pull an enemy is to start a fight with it. Pull each enemy with Fireball from maximum range, and cast until it dies.

At level 4 you train Frostbolt. Frostbolt slows the enemy, so the enemy gets to you later. It becomes your main spell. At level 6 you train Fire Blast, an instant spell. Use it when the enemy has low health.

Level 8 is the first large step. Polymorph changes 1 enemy into a sheep, and damage ends the effect. From this level, a pull of 2 enemies is safe: cast Polymorph on the second enemy, then kill the first enemy.

Mana is your limit. You make your own water from level 4 and your own food from level 6, so a drink costs nothing. Drink to full mana before each pull. A solo kill takes 10 to 15 seconds, so pull 1 enemy at a time. Train each new rank of Conjure Water and Conjure Food.

The game does not put new spells on your action bars. After each visit to a trainer, open the spellbook and move the new spells to your bars.

Warcraft Tavern says that **Comprehension** becomes available at level 6. Keep the scrolls that you loot. *Comprehend Scroll* is in the General tab of the spellbook, and it changes these scrolls into stat scrolls.

| Level | You train | Why it is important |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Fireball, Frost Armor, Arcane Intellect** | Your first damage spell and your first buffs. Kami-Labs says that Arcane Intellect now lasts 1 hour |
| 4 | **Frostbolt, Conjure Water** | Frostbolt slows the target. Conjure Water removes the cost of drinks |
| 6 | **Fire Blast, Conjure Food** | An instant spell to kill a target with low health |
| 8 | **Polymorph, Arcane Missiles** | Polymorph removes 1 enemy from the fight for its duration |

!!! tip fc-inline "Tip"
    Get a wand as soon as you can. A wand kills an enemy with low health and uses no mana. Fan reports say that wand damage increases with spell power in Forever.

!!! hardcore fc-inline "Hardcore"
    Do not fight 2 enemies before level 8. If a second enemy comes, run before your health is low.

## <span class="fc-ch__lv">Levels 10–19</span> First talents, Frost Nova and the first dungeon { #l10 .fc-ch data-toc-label="10–19 · First talents" data-label="10–19 · First talents" }

At level 10 you train Frost Nova, and you get your first talent point. From here, you get 1 talent point at each level, to a total of 51 at level 60. The Talents add-on below gives the talent for each point.

Frost Nova changes each fight. Before level 10, an enemy that got to you stayed near you. Now you cast Frost Nova, step back, and continue to cast. The enemy cannot hit you while it is Frozen. To kite is to do damage to an enemy while you stay out of its range. Frost Nova is the first step of each kite.

Arcane Explosion at 14 and Flamestrike at 16 are your first AoE spells. Use them on groups of weak enemies only, because each cast uses much mana.

Your first dungeons open at level 13. Quests give much more XP than dungeon enemies, so do each dungeon for its quests. In a group, your jobs are damage and Polymorph on the target that the tank marks. At 18 you get a third job: Remove Lesser Curse.

Each dungeon in Forever has 1 difficulty and no summoning stone. Only the ritual of a Warlock can summon a player. Bring water for the group, because you are the only class that makes it.

At level 14, start the scavenger hunt for the Cozy Sleeping Bag. It gives an XP buff and rested XP. See [Leveling](../../characters/leveling.md).

| Level | You train | Why it is important |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | **Frost Nova** | Holds all enemies near you in place. The first step of each kite |
| 12 | **Slow Fall** | Prevents fall damage |
| 14 | **Arcane Explosion** | Instant AoE around you |

## <span class="fc-ch__lv">Levels 20–29</span> Blink, Counterspell and teleports { #l20 .fc-ch data-toc-label="20–29 · Blink" data-label="20–29 · Blink" }

Level 20 gives you more than any level before it. You train Blink, Blizzard, Evocation and Mana Shield. You also learn the teleports to 2 capital cities of your faction.

Blink moves you 20 yards forward and removes stuns and roots. It is your escape, and it is the second step of a kite: Frost Nova, then Blink. Evocation refills your mana when no drink is possible. Mana Shield makes damage use your mana in place of your health.

Your 11th talent point also comes at level 20. On the Frost path it gives Ice Lance. On the Arcane path it gives Arcane Blast. The Talents add-on shows how each of them changes your fight.

Counterspell at 24 gives you a new job in dungeons and in PvP. It stops an enemy cast and locks that school of magic. Use it on healers and on dangerous casters. Cone of Cold at 26 is an instant slow for enemies in front of you.

The teleports change how you travel. Teleport to a capital for training and for the Auction House. All classes have 3 book quests, at levels 20, 35 and 45. At 20, turn in 10 library books in your capital for the first book quest.

Level 25 gives the *Mage: Novice* Legacy Point. Only the first Mage on your account gets it. See [Legacy](../../characters/legacy.md).

| Level | You train | Why it is important |
|---|---|---|
| 20 | **Blink, Blizzard, Evocation, Mana Shield, Fire Ward** | Blink is your escape. Evocation refills your mana |
| 20 | **Teleports to 2 capital cities** | Fast travel to trainers and the Auction House |
| 22 | **Scorch, Frost Ward** | Scorch is a fast Fire spell |
| 24 | **Counterspell** | Stops a cast. Use it on healers and on dangerous casters |
| 26 | **Cone of Cold** | An instant slow in front of you |
| 28 | **Conjure Mana Agate** | An emergency mana refill |

!!! warning fc-inline "Caution"
    The spell Blizzard makes AoE leveling possible: you pull many enemies, slow them and kill them together. The world-first level 60 group had 3 Mages that used large AoE pulls. But for a solo Mage, AoE pulls are more dangerous than in Classic. Improved Blizzard slows less, and Cone of Cold is shorter. Dungeon enemies also give less XP. Learn single-target questing first.

!!! tip fc-inline "Tip"
    When a dungeon pull goes wrong, cast Frost Nova on the enemies that attack the healer. Then use Blink to get away from them.

!!! hardcore fc-inline "Hardcore"

## <span class="fc-ch__lv">Levels 30–39</span> Ice Armor, Mage Armor and gold for level 40 { #l30 .fc-ch data-toc-label="30–39 · Ice Armor" data-label="30–39 · Ice Armor" }

These levels add few new spells. They make your Mage safer, and they prepare you for level 40. At 30 you train Ice Armor and the teleport to the third capital of your faction.

Mage Armor comes at level 34. In Forever it gives much more mana regeneration than in Classic. From this level, you select an armor spell for each situation. Use Mage Armor when you quest alone, because you drink less. Use Ice Armor when enemies hit you often.

The new battleground Battle for the Darkspear Islands opens at level 30, with 15 players on each side. Fan sites say that Warsong Gulch and Arathi Basin are also in the game, from levels 10 and 20. A Mage is a strong defender of flags and nodes.

Start to save gold now. At level 40 you need 100g for riding, and dual spec costs 50g. Training also costs gold: all spells from level 1 to 60 cost about 206g.

At 35, the second book quest needs 20 library books. Artisan professions also become available at level 35.

| Level | You train | Why it is important |
|---|---|---|
| 30 | **Ice Armor, teleport to the third capital** | More armor and a slow on enemies that hit you |
| 34 | **Mage Armor** | Mana regeneration while you cast. Much stronger in Forever |

**Dungeons in this chapter**

- **29–38**{ .lvl } Razorfen Kraul and Gnomeregan
- **30–46**{ .lvl } Scarlet Monastery: 4 wings. It gives a one-hand weapon and an off-hand item for casters

## <span class="fc-ch__lv">Levels 40–49</span> Frostfire Bolt, a mount and a second spec { #l40 .fc-ch data-toc-label="40–49 · Frostfire Bolt" data-label="40–49 · Frostfire Bolt" }

Level 40 gives you 4 new things. You train **Frostfire Bolt**, the new spell of Forever. You can buy riding for 100g. You can buy dual spec for 50g from all Mage trainers. You also learn your first portals, which take your group to a capital city.

Frostfire Bolt has a 3 second cast. It does a large hit, damage over time and a slow. It counts as Fire and Frost, so talents in the 2 trees increase it. It uses the better of your Fire and Frost spell power, against the lower of the 2 resistances of the target. Use it against targets that resist Frost, and to slow an enemy that runs.

With dual spec, keep your solo build in 1 spec. Use the second spec for dungeons or for PvP. The usual pair is 1 damage build for PvE and 1 Frost build for PvP.

The new zone [Riverglades](../../world/zones.md#riverglades) is for levels 35 to 45. At 45, the third book quest needs 25 library books. Level 45 also gives the *Mage: Experienced* Legacy Point.

| Level | You train | Why it is important |
|---|---|---|
| 40 | **Frostfire Bolt** | New. A slow, a large hit and damage over time. It counts as Fire and Frost |
| 40 | **Portals** | You can take your group to a capital city |

**Dungeons in this chapter**

- **40–47**{ .lvl } [Krol'dok Stronghold](../../content/dungeons/kroldok-stronghold.md): New. It is in Riverglades
- **37–54**{ .lvl } Razorfen Downs, Uldaman and Zul'Farrak

!!! warning fc-inline "Caution"
    Buy riding before dual spec if you do not have 150g. A mount saves more time than a second spec.

!!! hardcore fc-inline "Hardcore"
    Cast Mana Shield before a pull that you are not sure about. Do not let an enemy run away. Slow it with Frostfire Bolt.

## <span class="fc-ch__lv">Levels 50–59</span> Spell hit and the last dungeons { #l50 .fc-ch data-toc-label="50–59 · Spell hit" data-label="50–59 · Spell hit" }

The last 10 levels have the most dungeons. Reportedly, each boss in the new dungeons drops a blue item. Thus, you start to collect your level 60 equipment here.

Your main talents are complete. The last points go into support talents, and the Talents add-on gives them for your path. The fight itself changes little, so use these levels to prepare for level 60.

Hit becomes important now. A dungeon boss is 2 or 3 levels above you, so more of your spells miss. Items with hit start at about level 50. Keep them, also when an item with more spell power replaces them for quests.

At 58 you train Conjure Mana Ruby, the largest mana gem. A fan source (Kami-Labs) reports that Mages learn Teleport: Dalaran at level 50. No other source confirms this.

## <span class="fc-ch__lv">Level 60</span> Spell power, hit and the Onyxia attunement { #l60 .fc-ch data-toc-label="60 · Spell power" data-label="60 · Spell power" }

At level 60 you get the *Mage: Master* Legacy Point. [Mount Hyjal](../../world/zones.md#mount-hyjal) opens through Darkwhisper Gorge in Winterspring. The fast mount costs 1,000g.

All 3 launch raids open on 9 December 2026. Use the time before that date for 4 tasks: equipment, hit, consumables and the attunement for Onyxia's Lair.

For equipment, get spell power first, then hit. The new dungeons for level 55 and above and the Classic level 60 dungeons give cloth with spell power. A Tailor can also look for the new cloth patterns of Forever.

A raid boss needs 17% spell hit (Method.gg). Warcraft Tavern says 16%. No source has a test at level 60. Precision gives 6%, so you need about 11% from items.

The attunement for Onyxia's Lair gives the Drakefire Amulet. It is not account-wide, so each character must do it. The [Equipment add-on](equipment.md) lists the consumables.

Respec to a level 60 build now. Respec costs decrease to 1g over time. The Talents add-on gives the builds of your path. No source has published tested raid builds, so read each tooltip.

## <span class="fc-ch__lv">Raids and PvP</span> Raid jobs, and Frost in PvP { #raid .fc-ch data-toc-label="Raids · Raid jobs" data-label="Raids · Raid jobs" }

In a raid you do damage, and you keep the jobs that you learned in dungeons: water, Polymorph, Counterspell and curse removal. The size of the raid changes how much of each job is yours.

**The Barrow Deeps** has 10 players. You are probably the only Mage, so you give all the food, the water and the curse removal. See [The Barrow Deeps](../../content/raids/barrow-deeps.md).

**Hyjal Summit** has 20 players and 13 bosses on routes that branch. Expect Polymorph and Counterspell jobs. See [Hyjal Summit](../../content/raids/hyjal-summit.md).

**Onyxia's Lair** has 40 players and much Fire damage. Fire Mages are less useful there, so Frost or Arcane is the safer selection. See [Onyxia's Lair](../../content/raids/onyxias-lair.md).

In PvP, Frost is the usual spec. Ice Lance does 300% more damage to a Frozen target, and Ice Block with Cold Snap gives 2 escapes. Polymorph removes a healer from the fight. Counterspell stops heals and Fear. Roots from procs now share diminishing returns, so chains of freezes are shorter than in Classic.

Fan sites list 4 battlegrounds: Warsong Gulch, Arathi Basin, Alterac Valley and the new [Battle for the Darkspear Islands](../../content/pvp/battlegrounds.md#darkspear-islands). See [PvP](../../content/pvp/index.md) for ranks and seasons.

| Enemy in PvP | What you do |
|---|---|
| Rogue | Stay out of stealth range. Use Frost Nova, Blink and Ice Block |
| Warrior | Kite with Frost Nova, Cone of Cold and Blink. After you use Blink, do not let the Warrior Charge to you |
| Warlock | Counterspell the Fear |
| Priest | Counterspell heals and Mind Control. Do burst damage after the shield ends |
| Hunter | Go into the dead zone (Classic mechanic) or Blink away. Use Frost Nova on the pet |
| Mage | The Mage that uses Counterspell first has the advantage. Keep Ice Block for Polymorph and Pyroblast |

!!! hardcore fc-inline "Hardcore"
    [Hardcore](../../about/hardcore.md) comes in winter 2026/27. A Mage dies when enemies get to it, so play Frost. Frost has many slows, and Cold Snap gives a second Ice Block. Before each pull, make sure that Blink, Frost Nova and Ice Block are not on cooldown. Cast Mana Shield and Ice Barrier before the pull.

## Common mistakes

- You forget that the game no longer adds new spells to your action bars.
- You cast low-rank spells for Arcane Concentration, Frostbite or Fingers of Frost procs. Ranks that are more than 12 levels below you have a lower proc chance.
- You buy dual spec without gold for riding at level 40. Plan for 150g or more.
- You cast only Arcane Blast outside a burst phase. Each stack increases its mana cost by 100%.
- You break Polymorph with Arcane Explosion, Flamestrike or Blizzard in a dungeon.

## FAQ

??? question "Which path is best for leveling: Frost, Arcane or Fire?"
    Frost is the safest, and most guides recommend it. Arcane is strong solo when you have Arcane Blast and Missile Barrage. Fire is the weakest at low levels because it needs crit.

??? question "Why does Arcane Blast use so much mana?"
    Each stack increases its mana cost by 100%. Cast other spells between 2 casts, and let the stacks expire outside a burst phase.

??? question "Why do guides give different numbers for Arcane Blast?"
    Blizzard's deep dive from the beta gave +175% mana cost and 15% of Intellect for each stack. The launch client gives +100% mana cost and 12% of Intellect, with a maximum of 4 stacks for 18 seconds.

??? question "Why does Ice Lance not cast?"
    Ice Lance has the same 8 second cooldown as Fire Blast.

??? question "Is Hot Streak a different talent from Heating Up?"
    No. The BlizzCon demo and some guides call the proc Hot Streak. Blizzard's deep dive and the client call it Heating Up. It is 1 talent.

??? question "Can Horde Mages go to Dalaran?"
    NPCs in Dalaran attack Horde characters on sight, and Horde characters cannot get Kirin Tor reputation. They can go into the City of Dalaran dungeon through the sewer.

??? question "Is AoE farming still possible?"
    Yes, but it is weaker. Improved Blizzard slows by a maximum of 40%, and Cone of Cold is shorter.
