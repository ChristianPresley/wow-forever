# Orc Mage

!!! tip "Add-on"
    Read with the [Mage guide](index.md). This page covers only what changes when your Mage is an Orc.

## Why this combo

**New in Forever.** Orcs couldn't be Mages in Classic. In Forever they can, and they arrive with arguably the strongest Horde caster racial kit: **Blood Fury** now boosts spell power on a 2-minute cooldown, **Shatter Curse** cleanses Warlock curses and Banes, and **Hardiness** shortens stuns. Warcraft Tavern and Method.gg both name Orc as a top Horde Mage.

## Racials in practice

| Racial | Type | Rating | Mage use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Blood Fury** | Active | ★★★★★ | +10% attack power **and spell power** for 15 s on a **2 min** cooldown (was 3). Lines up with Arcane Power, Combustion or an Ice Lance shatter window |
| **Shatter Curse** *(new)* | Active | ★★★★ PvP · ★★★ PvE | Removes curses and Warlock **Banes**, then −5% magic damage taken for 8 s. Mages already have Remove Lesser Curse, but this also clears Banes and is an off-GCD-style self-cleanse plus damage reduction (*GCD behavior unverified*) |
| **Command** | Passive | ☆ | +5% pet damage. Only Hunters and Warlocks benefit; wasted on a Mage |
| **Hardiness** | Passive | ★★★★ PvP | −20% stun duration. Shorter Cheap Shots, Kidney Shots and Hammer of Justice |

## Starting zone walkthrough (1–12)

Forever added new NPCs, quests and "small moments" to the Valley of Trials. The Classic chains below are still the backbone; exact quest text may differ.

1. **Valley of Trials (1–6).** Start at the Den. Your Mage trainer in the valley is **Mai'ah**, the Troll trainer who teaches all Horde Mages here (Classic location; check the Den area). Kill boars and scorpids, then the Vile Familiars in the cave and Sarkoth.
2. **Level 4.** Frostbolt and Conjure Water. Polymorph at 8.
3. **Razor Hill (6–10).** Hand in *Report to Sen'jin Village* / *Report to Orgnil*. Kill Kul Tiran sailors at Tiragarde Keep and quilboar in the Razormane camp. **Sen'jin Village** to the south has a Mage trainer (Classic).
4. **Level 10.** Frost Nova and first talent point. Orgrimmar's Mage trainers are in the Valley of Spirits area (Classic).
5. **10–12.** Burning Blade cultists in Skull Rock and Dustwind Cave, then cross into the Barrens at the Crossroads.

!!! tip "Darkspear Islands"
    The new [Battle for the Darkspear Islands](../../content/pvp/battlegrounds.md#darkspear-islands) battleground opens at 30. Your side is the **Darkspear Raiders**.

## Route to 60 from Orgrimmar

| Levels | Zones | Travel |
|---|---|---|
| 10–20 | The Barrens (Crossroads), with Silverpine or Durotar as alternatives | Zeppelin to Undercity for Silverpine |
| 15–25 | Stonetalon, Barrens; [Ragefire Chasm](../../content/dungeons/index.md) and Wailing Caverns | Durotar Supply and Logistics crate camp west of the Crossroads |
| 20–30 | Hillsbrad (Tarren Mill), Ashenvale, Thousand Needles | Tarren Mill also starts the Dalaran Sewer Key chain |
| 30–40 | Arathi, Stranglethorn, Desolace, Dustwallow | Grom'gol zeppelin for STV |
| 35–45 | **[Riverglades](../../world/zones.md#riverglades)** (Rog'mar) | Through the Badlands or Swamp of Sorrows; Rog'mar flights connect to Stonard, Hammerfall, Kargath |

| 50–60 | Un'Goro, Azshara, Felwood, Plaguelands, Winterspring, Burning Steppes | [Mount Hyjal](../../world/zones.md#mount-hyjal) at 60 |

**Teleports (Classic levels):** Orgrimmar and Undercity at 20, Thunder Bluff at 30.

!!! warning "Dalaran"
    Horde players are **attacked on sight** in Dalaran and can't earn Kirin Tor reputation. You can still run the [City of Dalaran](../../content/dungeons/city-of-dalaran.md) dungeon through the sewer with the key from Tarren Mill.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Reputation:** Orgrimmar Honored for 10% off riding and the Wolf.
- **PvP vendors:** Hall of Legends in Orgrimmar.
- **Transmog:** Mon'ye in the Cleft of Shadows, Orgrimmar (46, 54).
- **Lore:** Orc spellcasters have traditionally been shamans and (in the Old Horde) warlocks. Orc arcanists were rare in Classic-era lore, so how your character came to study arcane magic is largely yours to role-play.

## Verdict

The strongest Horde Mage on paper: a spell-power cooldown every 2 minutes, a curse/Bane cleanse and stun resistance. Command is a wasted slot, but nothing else is. Roll Troll instead if you want haste and better solo survival.
