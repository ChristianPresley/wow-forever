## Why this combo

**New in Forever.** The [Skyborne](../../characters/races.md#skyborne) are only in Forever. The Mage is the central class of their Alliance branch. The **High Order** comes from the Highborne magisters of Eldre'Thalas and is an ally of the **Kirin Tor**. The Skyborne Mage is **Alliance-only**. The Horde Windshapers cannot be Mages.

For mechanics, you get permanent **haste**, a strong cooldown for **mana regeneration** and a **glide**. Warcraft Tavern names Skyborne as the best race for an Alliance Mage.

!!! conflict "Conflicts"
    - **Wind Blessed:** sources give 1%, 2% or 3% haste. Blizzard's latest notes give **2%**.
    - **Walk on Air** cooldown: 1.5 or 2 minutes. It is different in different sources.
    - You cannot use Read Ley Line together with a Druid's Innervate.

## Starting zone walkthrough: Zephras Isle (1–12)

You start at **Thendal Village** on [Zephras Isle](../../world/zephras-isle.md), a floating island in Skywall. Both factions use the island, but phasing keeps them apart. Tornadoes on the roads give *Blessing of Zephras* (+40% speed).

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **1–5**{ .lvl } **Thendal Village.** Do *Coming of Age*, *Harmony in Balance*, *The Cirrusfly Queen*, *Harvesting Windstones*, *Falling With Style* (teaches Walk on Air) and *The Anchors of Zephras*. *Harvesting Windstones* gives a voucher for a gathering profession. Choose Herbalism, or do not take a gathering profession if you plan Tailoring + Enchanting.
2.  **High Order first quest:** *Reading the Leylines*. This quest teaches Read Ley Line and the *Attuned* buff.
3.  **5–8**{ .lvl } **Road to Shen'dar Village.** Fight the Al'Aketh thugs of Malduko Cloudcrash. *The Great Outdoors* teaches [camping](../../systems/camping.md). Shen'dar Village has bandit, hippogryph and Prideclaw quests. At 8, Polymorph makes the bandit camps easy.
4.  **8–10**{ .lvl } **Falaath Village.** In *Secret Agent Elf*, go into the cult in a disguise. Kill Commander Cyclas and Commander Aanders. In *A Last Request*, get back the Shadowsong Family Signet.
5.  **10**{ .lvl } **Valanaar.** Give the signet to High Elder Talaanis Shadowsong and meet **Supreme Magister Elaadrin Evengale**. Valanaar has trainers, a bank and the Skydocks. Spend your first talent point.
6.  **10–11**{ .lvl } **Gustberry Lowlands.** Do the High Order chain *The Missing Scholar* (Shriekling Den). Use `/sing` at the frogs to get the *Musical Gustjumper* pet.
7.  **11–12**{ .lvl } **Shadowgale Forest.** The High Order chain goes to Index Esoteria at the Overlook Standing Stones. A gnome hermit trains Engineering.
8.  **12–14**{ .lvl } **Finale.** Attack the Shrine of Akir and do *Confront Lorthuna*. Archmage **Ansirem Runeweaver** of the Kirin Tor comes with Alliance reinforcements. *What Comes Next* sends you off the island.

</div>

Reportedly, the island quests take you to about level **12–14**. You can leave at any level.

## Route to 60 from Valanaar

To leave, take the **airship from the east Skydock to north Dalaran** in the Alterac Mountains. Dalaran is a level 30–40 area, and reportedly it has **no flight master**. Thus, your first task is to go to a starter zone.

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **12–20**{ .lvl } Walk south through Alterac to **Southshore** (Hillsbrad). Take the new boat from the pier to **Auberdine**, and do the Darkshore quests. Or continue on the boat to **Menethil** for Loch Modan and Westfall

    <span class="step-label">Travel</span> The Southshore flight point has flights to Ironforge and Stormwind

2.  **18–25**{ .lvl } Westfall, Redridge, Loch Modan, Darkshore

    <span class="step-label">Travel</span> Stormwind Harbor ↔ Auberdine boat

3.  **20–30**{ .lvl } Wetlands, Duskwood, Ashenvale

    <span class="step-label">Travel</span> [Excavation Site](../../content/dungeons/excavation-site.md)

4.  **25–35**{ .lvl } Hillsbrad, Alterac Mountains

    <span class="step-label">Travel</span> You already know the route. The [City of Dalaran](../../content/dungeons/city-of-dalaran.md) dungeon is here

5.  **30–40**{ .lvl } Arathi, Stranglethorn, Desolace (new path to [Shen'dralas](../../world/zones.md#shendralas)), Dustwallow

    <span class="step-label">Travel</span> Shen'dralas has a connection to Skyborne ancestry, but no source confirms a story connection

6.  **35–45**{ .lvl } **[Riverglades](../../world/zones.md#riverglades)**

    <span class="step-label">Travel</span> Through Redridge

7.  **40–50**{ .lvl } Tanaris, Feralas, Hinterlands, Badlands

    <span class="step-label">Travel</span> Riding at 40

8.  **50–60**{ .lvl } Un'Goro, Azshara, Felwood, Plaguelands, Winterspring

    <span class="step-label">Travel</span> [Mount Hyjal](../../world/zones.md#mount-hyjal) at 60. Elemental Insight helps here

</div>

**Trainers:** Valanaar has trainers for Skyborne. The Mage trainers in all Alliance capitals (Stormwind Mage Quarter, Ironforge Mystic Ward, Darnassus) also train you. Skyborne Mages use the Alliance list for teleport cities and levels (*Classic levels: Stormwind and Ironforge at 20, Darnassus at 30*). No source reports a Valanaar teleport.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Reputation:** High Order is your home faction. Galestrider discounts are 5/10/15/20% from Friendly to Exalted.
- **Galestrider:** buy your first at **Fairweather Stables** (53.8, 81.3) on Zephras Isle, in Umber, Stormy or Empyrean colors. Swift versions are also available. Come back at 40. The island is one airship trip from Dalaran.
- **Kirin Tor:** the ally of your faction. Kirin Tor reputation is Alliance-only.
- **Nightclaw** druids on Zephras Isle give a repeatable reputation quest.
- **Cosmetics:** feathers, tattoos and the bright blue "Sky-Touched Blessing" skin. Some editions include the *Shen'dorei Skyseer's Garb* ensemble.
- **Lore:** the High Order looks for lost arcane knowledge. Thus, the Mage is the "lore" class for them. Their leader, Supreme Magister Elaadrin Evengale, is a Mage. Two Skyborne of different factions can talk to each other in Darnassian.

## Verdict

<div class="verdict" markdown>

This is the best choice for lore and a strong choice for mechanics. Permanent haste and Read Ley Line help when you level and when you raid. You lose the PvP escapes of Human and Gnome (no racial that breaks stuns or roots), and the race costs money. If you have the pack and play mostly PvE, choose this Alliance Mage.

</div>
