# Skyborne Mage

!!! tip "Add-on"
    Read with the [Mage guide](index.md). This page covers only what changes when your Mage is a Skyborne of the High Order.

## Why this combo

**New in Forever.** The [Skyborne](../../characters/races.md#skyborne) exist only in Forever, and the Mage is the class their Alliance branch is built around: the **High Order** descends from the Highborne magisters of Eldre'Thalas and is allied with the **Kirin Tor**. Skyborne Mage is **Alliance-only**; the Horde Windshapers can't be Mages.

Mechanically you get permanent **haste**, a strong **mana-regen** cooldown and a **glide**. Warcraft Tavern names Skyborne as the best Alliance Mage race.

|---|---|
| **Status** | **NEW** combination, Alliance only |
| **Faction** | Alliance |
| **Start / capital** | Zephras Isle / Valanaar |

!!! warning "Paid race"
    Skyborne require the **Skyborne Heroic Pack** ($29.99) or a higher edition. See [Editions](../../about/editions.md). Skyborne use **HD models only** at launch.

## Racials in practice

| Racial | Type | Rating | Mage use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Walk on Air** | Active | ★★★ PvP · ★★ PvE | 10 s controlled glide. Step off a ledge and float to safety, or cross gaps in Alterac Valley and the Darkspear Islands. Also a fall-damage negator — but Mages already have Slow Fall, so this matters less for you than for other classes |
| **Read Ley Line** | Active | ★★★★ leveling · ★★★ raid | +50% health and mana regen for 15 s, or +100% while *Attuned* (1 hour, gained at a Ley Line). Pop it while drinking to cut downtime; in raids it stretches your mana between Evocations |
| **Wind Blessed** | Passive | ★★★★ | Permanent haste. Every cast is faster, which helps Arcane Blast chains and Frostbolt kiting |
| **Elemental Insight** | Passive | ★★ | +5% damage to Elementals. Useful in Hyjal, Un'Goro and Silithus quests and in some new dungeons; note Fire Mages still hit fire-immune elementals poorly |

!!! note "Conflicts"
    - **Wind Blessed:** sources say 1%, 2% or 3% haste. Blizzard's latest notes say **2%**.
    - **Walk on Air** cooldown: 1.5 or 2 minutes depending on source.
    - Read Ley Line can't be used together with a Druid's Innervate.

### Macros

Drink and regen in one press:

```
#showtooltip Read Ley Line
/cast Read Ley Line
/use Conjured Water
```

Replace *Conjured Water* with your current rank's item name (e.g. *Conjured Crystal Water*).

## Starting zone walkthrough: Zephras Isle (1–12)

You start at **Thendal Village** on [Zephras Isle](../../world/zephras-isle.md), a floating island in Skywall. Both factions share it but are phased apart. Tornadoes along the roads give *Blessing of Zephras* (+40% speed).

1. **Thendal Village (1–5).** *Coming of Age*, *Harmony in Balance*, *The Cirrusfly Queen*, *Harvesting Windstones* (gives a gathering-profession voucher — pick Herbalism or skip it if you plan Tailoring + Enchanting), *Falling With Style* (teaches Walk on Air), *The Anchors of Zephras*.
2. **High Order opener:** *Reading the Leylines*. This introduces Read Ley Line and the *Attuned* buff.
3. **Road to Shen'dar Village (5–8).** Al'Aketh thugs under Malduko Cloudcrash. *The Great Outdoors* teaches [camping](../../systems/camping.md). Shen'dar Village has bandit, hippogryph and Prideclaw quests. Polymorph at 8 makes the bandit camps easy.
4. **Falaath Village (8–10).** *Secret Agent Elf*: infiltrate the cult in disguise. Kill Commander Cyclas and Commander Aanders. *A Last Request* recovers the Shadowsong Family Signet.
5. **Valanaar (10).** Hand the signet to High Elder Talaanis Shadowsong and meet **Supreme Magister Elaadrin Evengale**. Valanaar has trainers, a bank and the Skydocks. Spend your first talent point.
6. **Gustberry Lowlands (10–11).** High Order chain *The Missing Scholar* (Shriekling Den). `/sing` at the frogs for the *Musical Gustjumper* pet.
7. **Shadowgale Forest (11–12).** The High Order chain leads to Index Esoteria at the Overlook Standing Stones. A gnome hermit trains Engineering.
8. **Finale (12–14).** Assault the Shrine of Akir and *Confront Lorthuna*. Archmage **Ansirem Runeweaver** of the Kirin Tor arrives with Alliance reinforcements. *What Comes Next* sends you off the island.

The island reportedly levels you to about **12–14**. You can leave at any level.

## Route to 60 from Valanaar

You leave by **airship from the east Skydock to north Dalaran** in the Alterac Mountains. Dalaran is a 30–40 area, and it reportedly has **no flight master**, so your first job is to reach a starter zone.

| Levels | Zones | Travel |
|---|---|---|
| 12–20 | Walk south through Alterac to **Southshore** (Hillsbrad), take the new pier boat to **Auberdine**, and quest Darkshore. Or take the loop on to **Menethil** for Loch Modan and Westfall | Southshore flight point links to Ironforge and Stormwind |
| 18–25 | Westfall, Redridge, Loch Modan, Darkshore | Stormwind Harbor ↔ Auberdine boat |
| 20–30 | Wetlands, Duskwood, Ashenvale | [Excavation Site](../../content/dungeons/excavation-site.md) |
| 25–35 | Hillsbrad, Alterac Mountains | You already know the way; the [City of Dalaran](../../content/dungeons/city-of-dalaran.md) dungeon is here |
| 30–40 | Arathi, Stranglethorn, Desolace (new path to [Shen'dralas](../../world/zones.md#shendralas)), Dustwallow | Shen'dralas ties into Skyborne ancestry, though no story crossover is confirmed |
| 35–45 | **[Riverglades](../../world/zones.md#riverglades)** | Via Redridge |
| 40–50 | Tanaris, Feralas, Hinterlands, Badlands | Riding at 40 |
| 50–60 | Un'Goro, Azshara, Felwood, Plaguelands, Winterspring | [Mount Hyjal](../../world/zones.md#mount-hyjal) at 60; Elemental Insight helps here |

**Trainers:** Valanaar has trainers for Skyborne; any Alliance capital Mage trainer (Stormwind Mage Quarter, Ironforge Mystic Ward, Darnassus) also works. Which capitals Skyborne Mages can teleport to, and at what level, follows the Alliance list (*Classic levels: Stormwind and Ironforge at 20, Darnassus at 30*). A Valanaar teleport has not been reported.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Reputation:** High Order is your home faction. Galestrider discounts are 5/10/15/20% from Friendly to Exalted.
- **Galestrider:** buy your first at **Fairweather Stables** (53.8, 81.3) on Zephras Isle in Umber, Stormy or Empyrean, with Swift versions. Come back at 40 — the island is an airship ride from Dalaran.
- **Kirin Tor:** your faction's ally. Kirin Tor reputation is Alliance-only.
- **Nightclaw** druids on Zephras Isle offer a repeatable reputation quest.
- **Cosmetics:** feathers, tattoos and the vivid-blue "Sky-Touched Blessing" skin. The *Shen'dorei Skyseer's Garb* ensemble comes with some editions.
- **Lore:** the High Order seek lost arcane knowledge, which makes the Mage the "lore" class for them. Their leader, Supreme Magister Elaadrin Evengale, is a Mage figure. Two Skyborne can talk to each other across factions in Darnassian.

## Verdict

The best-fitting lore choice and a strong mechanical one: permanent haste and Read Ley Line help both leveling and raiding. You give up Human/Gnome's PvP escapes (no stun or root breaker), and the race costs money. If you own the pack and mostly PvE, this is the Alliance Mage to roll.
