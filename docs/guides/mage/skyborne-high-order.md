## Why this combo

**New in Forever.** The [Skyborne](../../characters/races.md#skyborne) exist only in Forever, and the Mage is the class their Alliance branch is built around: the **High Order** descends from the Highborne magisters of Eldre'Thalas and is allied with the **Kirin Tor**. Skyborne Mage is **Alliance-only**; the Horde Windshapers can't be Mages.

Mechanically you get permanent **haste**, a strong **mana-regen** cooldown and a **glide**. Warcraft Tavern names Skyborne as the best Alliance Mage race.

## Starting zone walkthrough: Zephras Isle (1–12)

You start at **Thendal Village** on [Zephras Isle](../../world/zephras-isle.md), a floating island in Skywall. Both factions share it but are phased apart. Tornadoes along the roads give *Blessing of Zephras* (+40% speed).

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **1–5**{ .lvl } **Thendal Village.** *Coming of Age*, *Harmony in Balance*, *The Cirrusfly Queen*, *Harvesting Windstones* (gives a gathering-profession voucher — pick Herbalism or skip it if you plan Tailoring + Enchanting), *Falling With Style* (teaches Walk on Air), *The Anchors of Zephras*.
2.  **High Order opener:** *Reading the Leylines*. This introduces Read Ley Line and the *Attuned* buff.
3.  **5–8**{ .lvl } **Road to Shen'dar Village.** Al'Aketh thugs under Malduko Cloudcrash. *The Great Outdoors* teaches [camping](../../systems/camping.md). Shen'dar Village has bandit, hippogryph and Prideclaw quests. Polymorph at 8 makes the bandit camps easy.
4.  **8–10**{ .lvl } **Falaath Village.** *Secret Agent Elf*: infiltrate the cult in disguise. Kill Commander Cyclas and Commander Aanders. *A Last Request* recovers the Shadowsong Family Signet.
5.  **10**{ .lvl } **Valanaar.** Hand the signet to High Elder Talaanis Shadowsong and meet **Supreme Magister Elaadrin Evengale**. Valanaar has trainers, a bank and the Skydocks. Spend your first talent point.
6.  **10–11**{ .lvl } **Gustberry Lowlands.** High Order chain *The Missing Scholar* (Shriekling Den). `/sing` at the frogs for the *Musical Gustjumper* pet.
7.  **11–12**{ .lvl } **Shadowgale Forest.** The High Order chain leads to Index Esoteria at the Overlook Standing Stones. A gnome hermit trains Engineering.
8.  **12–14**{ .lvl } **Finale.** Assault the Shrine of Akir and *Confront Lorthuna*. Archmage **Ansirem Runeweaver** of the Kirin Tor arrives with Alliance reinforcements. *What Comes Next* sends you off the island.

</div>

The island reportedly levels you to about **12–14**. You can leave at any level.

## Route to 60 from Valanaar

You leave by **airship from the east Skydock to north Dalaran** in the Alterac Mountains. Dalaran is a 30–40 area, and it reportedly has **no flight master**, so your first job is to reach a starter zone.

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **12–20**{ .lvl } Walk south through Alterac to **Southshore** (Hillsbrad), take the new pier boat to **Auberdine**, and quest Darkshore. Or take the loop on to **Menethil** for Loch Modan and Westfall

    <span class="step-label">Travel</span> Southshore flight point links to Ironforge and Stormwind

2.  **18–25**{ .lvl } Westfall, Redridge, Loch Modan, Darkshore

    <span class="step-label">Travel</span> Stormwind Harbor ↔ Auberdine boat

3.  **20–30**{ .lvl } Wetlands, Duskwood, Ashenvale

    <span class="step-label">Travel</span> [Excavation Site](../../content/dungeons/excavation-site.md)

4.  **25–35**{ .lvl } Hillsbrad, Alterac Mountains

    <span class="step-label">Travel</span> You already know the way; the [City of Dalaran](../../content/dungeons/city-of-dalaran.md) dungeon is here

5.  **30–40**{ .lvl } Arathi, Stranglethorn, Desolace (new path to [Shen'dralas](../../world/zones.md#shendralas)), Dustwallow

    <span class="step-label">Travel</span> Shen'dralas ties into Skyborne ancestry, though no story crossover is confirmed

6.  **35–45**{ .lvl } **[Riverglades](../../world/zones.md#riverglades)**

    <span class="step-label">Travel</span> Via Redridge

7.  **40–50**{ .lvl } Tanaris, Feralas, Hinterlands, Badlands

    <span class="step-label">Travel</span> Riding at 40

8.  **50–60**{ .lvl } Un'Goro, Azshara, Felwood, Plaguelands, Winterspring

    <span class="step-label">Travel</span> [Mount Hyjal](../../world/zones.md#mount-hyjal) at 60; Elemental Insight helps here

</div>

**Trainers:** Valanaar has trainers for Skyborne; any Alliance capital Mage trainer (Stormwind Mage Quarter, Ironforge Mystic Ward, Darnassus) also works. Which capitals Skyborne Mages can teleport to, and at what level, follows the Alliance list (*Classic levels: Stormwind and Ironforge at 20, Darnassus at 30*). A Valanaar teleport has not been reported.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Reputation:** High Order is your home faction. Galestrider discounts are 5/10/15/20% from Friendly to Exalted.
- **Galestrider:** buy your first at **Fairweather Stables** (53.8, 81.3) on Zephras Isle in Umber, Stormy or Empyrean, with Swift versions. Come back at 40 — the island is an airship ride from Dalaran.
- **Kirin Tor:** your faction's ally. Kirin Tor reputation is Alliance-only.
- **Nightclaw** druids on Zephras Isle offer a repeatable reputation quest.
- **Cosmetics:** feathers, tattoos and the vivid-blue "Sky-Touched Blessing" skin. The *Shen'dorei Skyseer's Garb* ensemble comes with some editions.
- **Lore:** the High Order seek lost arcane knowledge, which makes the Mage the "lore" class for them. Their leader, Supreme Magister Elaadrin Evengale, is a Mage figure. Two Skyborne can talk to each other across factions in Darnassian.

## Verdict

<div class="verdict" markdown>

The best-fitting lore choice and a strong mechanical one: permanent haste and Read Ley Line help both leveling and raiding. You give up Human/Gnome's PvP escapes (no stun or root breaker), and the race costs money. If you own the pack and mostly PvE, this is the Alliance Mage to roll.

</div>
