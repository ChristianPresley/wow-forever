# Undead Mage

!!! tip "Add-on"
    Read with the [Mage guide](index.md). This page covers only what changes when your Mage is Undead.

## Why this combo

The Forsaken Mage is the Horde's PvP classic. **Will of the Forsaken** breaks fear, charm and sleep — the main tools Warlocks and Priests use against you — and **Cannibalize** now restores mana as well as health, which makes it a genuine leveling tool for a caster. Forever also filled western Tirisfal with new content and added a new dungeon on your doorstep.

## Racials in practice

| Racial | Type | Rating | Mage use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Will of the Forsaken** | Active | ★★★★★ PvP · ★★ PvE | Instantly removes charm, fear and sleep (no lasting immunity in Forever). Your answer to Fear, Psychic Scream, Seduction and Mind Control |
| **Cannibalize** | Active | ★★★ leveling | 7% health and mana per 2 s tick, up to 35%, from a humanoid or undead corpse. Use it instead of a drink after humanoid pulls. Now usable while immune to physical damage |
| **Underwater Breathing** | Passive | ★ | +300% breath. Handy in the Drowned City and underwater quests |
| **Touch of the Grave** *(new)* | Passive | ★★ | 30% chance for casters to drain up to 5% of max health, 15 s internal cooldown. A little free healing |

!!! note "Cannibalize and mana"
    The research notes give Cannibalize as health **and** mana per tick. Classic Cannibalize restored health only. If it only heals in your client, treat it as a food substitute.

### Macros

```
#showtooltip Will of the Forsaken
/cast Will of the Forsaken
/cast Blink
```

Save Will of the Forsaken for fears. Against stuns, use Blink or Ice Block — you have no stun breaker.

## Starting zone walkthrough (1–12)

1. **Deathknell (1–6).** You rise in the crypt. Your Mage trainer is **Isabella** in Deathknell's church (Classic); she sends a class letter. Kill mindless zombies, Scarlet Convert initiates and the wolves around the village.
2. **Level 4.** Frostbolt and Conjure Water. **8:** Polymorph.
3. **Brill (6–10).** The Gallows' End Tavern is your hub; a Mage trainer works in Brill (Classic). Do the Agamand Mills, Scarlet Crusade, Darkhound and Gnoll quests.
4. **New western Tirisfal.** Forever filled the empty western strip with **Shadowvale** and **Bandarion Keep** in the Whispering Wood. It's home to the Forsaken Paladin storyline; expect extra quests for every class around 8–15. Details per class are *unconfirmed*.
5. **Level 10.** Frost Nova and first talent point. Undercity's Mage trainers are in the **Magic Quarter**.
6. **10–12.** Finish Tirisfal, then Silverpine Forest to the south.

!!! tip "Ruins of Lordaeron"
    The new [Ruins of Lordaeron](../../content/dungeons/ruins-of-lordaeron.md) dungeon (15–22) is in Tirisfal Glades — your first new dungeon, right next to home.

!!! warning "Dalaran"
    Horde Mages are attacked on sight in Dalaran and can't earn Kirin Tor reputation. The [City of Dalaran](../../content/dungeons/city-of-dalaran.md) dungeon is still open to you through the sewer.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Reputation:** Undercity Honored for 10% off riding and the Skeletal Horse.
- **The Undercity** is a common target of world PvP raids on PvP rulesets; keep a hearthstone or teleport ready.
- **Scarlet Monastery** (30–46) is a short walk from Undercity, ideal for Mage AoE runs within the new XP rules.
- **Lore:** many Forsaken Mages were once Dalaran magi; the restored city that now attacks you on sight is a nice piece of role-play tension.

## PvP and Hardcore notes

- **Versus Warlocks:** keep Will of the Forsaken for Fear or Seduction, then Counterspell the next Fear cast. You have no curse/Bane cleanse beyond Remove Lesser Curse.
- **Versus Priests:** Will of the Forsaken breaks Psychic Scream and Mind Control; Polymorph the Priest while you reset.
- **Versus Rogues and Warriors:** your racials don't help with stuns. Pre-shield (Mana Shield, Ice Barrier) and keep Blink and Ice Block ready.
- **Hardcore:** Cannibalize after a humanoid fight is a free top-up that saves your conjured food for emergencies.

## Verdict

The best Horde Mage for PvP against casters, and a solid leveler if Cannibalize restores mana for you. Pick Orc for raw burst and stun resistance, Troll for PvE haste.
