## Why this combo

The Forsaken Mage is the traditional Horde PvP choice. **Will of the Forsaken** breaks fear, charm and sleep. These are the main tools that Warlocks and Priests use against you. **Cannibalize** now gives back mana and health, so it is a real leveling tool for a caster. Forever also added new content to western Tirisfal and a new dungeon near your home.

!!! note "Cannibalize and mana"
    The research notes say that Cannibalize gives health **and** mana per tick. In Classic, Cannibalize gave back only health. If it gives only health in your client, use it as a replacement for food.

## Starting zone walkthrough (1–12)

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **1–6**{ .lvl } **Deathknell.** You start in the crypt. Your Mage trainer is **Isabella** in the Deathknell church (Classic). She sends a class letter. Kill mindless zombies, Scarlet Convert initiates and the wolves near the village.
2.  **4**{ .lvl } Train Frostbolt and Conjure Water. **8:** Train Polymorph.
3.  **6–10**{ .lvl } **Brill.** The Gallows' End Tavern is your hub. Brill has a Mage trainer (Classic). Do the Agamand Mills, Scarlet Crusade, Darkhound and Gnoll quests.
4.  **New western Tirisfal.** The western part of the zone was empty. Forever added **Shadowvale** and **Bandarion Keep** in the Whispering Wood. The Forsaken Paladin story is there. Expect more quests for all classes at about level 8–15. Details for each class are *unconfirmed*.
5.  **10**{ .lvl } Train Frost Nova and spend your first talent point. The Undercity Mage trainers are in the **Magic Quarter**.
6.  **10–12.** Complete Tirisfal. Then go south to Silverpine Forest.

</div>

!!! tip "Ruins of Lordaeron"
    The new [Ruins of Lordaeron](../../content/dungeons/ruins-of-lordaeron.md) dungeon (15–22) is in Tirisfal Glades. It is your first new dungeon, and it is near your home.

**Teleports (Classic levels):** Undercity and Orgrimmar at 20, Thunder Bluff at 30.

!!! warning "Dalaran"
    NPCs in Dalaran attack Horde Mages on sight, and Horde Mages cannot get Kirin Tor reputation. You can still go into the [City of Dalaran](../../content/dungeons/city-of-dalaran.md) dungeon through the sewer.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Reputation:** Undercity Honored gives 10% off riding and the Skeletal Horse.
- **The Undercity:** on PvP rulesets, world PvP raids often attack the Undercity. Keep a hearthstone or teleport ready.
- **Scarlet Monastery** (30–46) is a short walk from Undercity. It is a good place for Mage AoE runs under the new XP rules.
- **Lore:** many Forsaken Mages were magi of Dalaran before. Now the restored city attacks you on sight. This gives good material for role-play.

## PvP and Hardcore notes

- **Against Warlocks:** keep Will of the Forsaken for Fear or Seduction. Then use Counterspell on the next Fear cast. You have no curse/Bane cleanse other than Remove Lesser Curse.
- **Against Priests:** Will of the Forsaken breaks Psychic Scream and Mind Control. Use Polymorph on the Priest while you reset.
- **Against Rogues and Warriors:** your racials do not help against stuns. Cast shields before the fight (Mana Shield, Ice Barrier). Keep Blink and Ice Block ready.

!!! hardcore "Hardcore"
    After a fight with humanoids, use Cannibalize to get back health for free. This saves your conjured food for emergencies.

## Verdict

<div class="verdict" markdown>

This is the best Horde Mage for PvP against casters. It is also a good race for leveling if Cannibalize gives you mana. Choose Orc for more burst and stun resistance, or Troll for PvE haste.

</div>
