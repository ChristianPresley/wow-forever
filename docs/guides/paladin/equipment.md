A Paladin wears mail to level 39 and plate from level 40. The weapon decides the role: a slow two-hand weapon for Retribution, a one-hand weapon and a shield for Protection and Holy. After dual spec at level 40 you carry 2 sets.

About 12,600 items of Forever had hidden stats at launch. The stats of an item become visible when a player first finds it. Thus, no source has a tested list for level 60. The item names on this page come from Timesaver.gg (data of 6 November 2026), wowtbc.gg, Method.gg, Warcraft Tavern and ConquestCapped. The Codex did not verify the stats.

The 3 Timesaver.gg lists give different items for each spec. The tool computes them from stat weights. It leaves out world drops, reputation items and raid items.

## Item rules in Forever { #rules }

- Hit is 1 stat for melee, ranged attacks and spells. Crit is also 1 stat (Method.gg).
- No source confirms a hit cap for Forever. Leprestore says that no exact value is confirmed. The Classic caps assume Weapon Skill, and Expertise replaces it in Forever. Thus, do not use a Classic cap.
- Hit comes from Precision (3%), from Divine Precision (8%, Holy spells only) and from the Dwarf racial Artisan's Precision (1%).
- Blizzard has not published a Defense target for Forever. The Classic target of 440 Defense is not confirmed.
- Healing items also give spell power (Method.gg). Weapons can have spell power or healing power from about level 10.
- Champion of the Light changes Intellect into spell damage. Thus, Intellect is a damage stat for Retribution from level 35.
- Seal of Command needs a slow weapon. ConquestCapped says 3.2 seconds or slower. Warcraft Tavern recommends a faster weapon for Seal of Fury.
- You can use a weapon stone or an oil together with a Seal (Classic rule).
- For leveling, ConquestCapped puts Stamina, Spirit and Intellect before Strength, because most damage comes from abilities. Leprestore puts weapon damage, Strength and crit first. The 2 sources agree that the weapon is the most important item.
- Transmog is account-wide, and it works only for the same armor type.
- Necklaces and off-hand items can have enchants. These slots are new.
- Professions give no passive stats. Camp buffs do not stack with the equivalent class buff: the Lodestone does not stack with Blessing of Might.
- The rare crafted items of Blacksmithing have 5 versions for each slot. The client data gives no stats for them, so read the tooltip.
