Read this page with the [Paladin guide](index.md). This page gives the differences for a Human paladin.

## Why this combo

The Human paladin is the traditional Knight of the Silver Hand. It trains at the Cathedral of Light in Stormwind. It is also available in Classic, so this is **not** a new combination. The racial changes in Forever changed what Humans give:

- **Will to Survive** is a racial that breaks stuns. It is the best PvP racial that a paladin can have.
- **Ruthless** (+1% crit) helps all specs.
- **The Human Spirit** (+10% Spirit) helps mana between fights. Mana is the main paladin problem while you level.
- The old Sword and Mace Specialization racials are **removed**, so Humans no longer have an advantage in weapon skill. Diplomacy is now the Legacy talent *Diplomat*.

Stormwind is also near the new Alliance content: the harbor with a boat to Auberdine, the Azeroth Commerce Authority camp in Redridge, and the road east from Redridge to Riverglades.

## Starting zone walkthrough (1–12)

You start at **Northshire Abbey** in Elwynn Forest. The Classic quests are mostly the same, with new NPCs and quests between them.

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **1–5**{ .lvl } Northshire Valley

    **Brother Sammuel** (Northshire Abbey, 50.4, 42.0) trains you until level 6. Do the kobold, wolf and Defias quests

2.  **4**{ .lvl } Northshire

    Train **Judgement** and **Blessing of Might**

3.  **5**{ .lvl } Goldshire

    Get *The Great Outdoors* outside the hub to unlock camping, and learn Cooking

4.  **5–10**{ .lvl } Goldshire, Eastvale, Jerod's Landing

    Your trainer is **Brother Wilhelm** in Goldshire (41.0, 66.0). Train **Holy Strike** at 6 and Hammer of Justice at 8

5.  **10**{ .lvl } Goldshire

    Train **Seal of Fury, Righteous Fury, Lay on Hands**. First talent: Benediction (Ret) or Redoubt (Prot)

6.  **10–12**{ .lvl } Stormwind, Westfall

    Go to the **Cathedral of Light** (trainers Arthur the Faithful, Katherine the Pure, Lord Grayson Shadowbreaker, near 49, 50)

7.  **12**{ .lvl } Stormwind

    **Redemption** class quest (Classic: *The Tome of Divinity*, from the Cathedral of Light). Expect a short chain with a cloth turn-in and a resurrection task

</div>

!!! note "Classic-era detail"
    The sources that we examined do not document the exact Redemption chain for Alliance paladins in Forever. The Classic version starts in the Cathedral of Light at level 12. The Forsaken version (*A Lesson in Divinity*) has the same structure.

## Route to 60

Your trainers are in **Stormwind** (Cathedral of Light) and Goldshire, and also in Ironforge and Theramore.

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **10–20**{ .lvl } Westfall, Loch Modan, Redridge

    The Deadmines (18–24) is your first large dungeon. **Rhahk'Zor's Hammer** is a very good slow two-hand weapon. [Hall of Thanes](../../content/dungeons/hall-of-thanes.md) (13–18) is a short trip away, in Ironforge

2.  **20**{ .lvl } Stormwind / Ironforge

    **Verigan's Fist** class quest (Classic: *The Tome of Valor*). The reward is a two-hand mace. The quest needs materials from the Deadmines, Shadowfang Keep and Blackfathom Deeps. Make a group for the dungeon parts

3.  **20–30**{ .lvl } Redridge, Duskwood, Wetlands, Ashenvale

    [Excavation Site: Wetlands](../../content/dungeons/excavation-site.md), the Stockade (in Stormwind), Blackfathom Deeps. Turn in crates at the **Azeroth Commerce Authority** in Three Corners, Redridge

4.  **28–35**{ .lvl } Hillsbrad, Alterac Mountains

    [City of Dalaran](../../content/dungeons/city-of-dalaran.md) and the Kirin Tor quests (Alliance only). Start with Emissary Jacques in Southshore (*fan source*)

5.  **30–40**{ .lvl } Arathi Highlands, Stranglethorn, Desolace, Dustwallow

    Scarlet Monastery (Tirisfal Glades, a long trip through Horde land); [The Drowned City](../../content/dungeons/drowned-city.md) (35–40)

6.  **35–45**{ .lvl } [Riverglades](../../world/zones.md#riverglades)

    Walk east from Redridge to **Farholde Keep** and the Brotherhood of the Horse. This content has a traditional knight theme that is good for a paladin

7.  **45–60**{ .lvl } Feralas, Tanaris, Hinterlands, Azshara, Un'Goro, Plaguelands, Winterspring

    Fly back to Stormwind or Ironforge to train

</div>

**Travel tips**

- The **Stormwind harbor** now has a boat to Auberdine. Thus, the Kalimdor zones (Darkshore, Ashenvale, Desolace, Feralas) are a short trip.
- The Deeprun Tram connects Stormwind and Ironforge. The trainers in both cities can train you.
- A new pier at Southshore is part of the Menethil–Southshore–Auberdine boat route.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Mounts.** At 40, Alliance paladins get the **Warhorse** (*The Tome of Nobility* in Classic, from the Stormwind or Ironforge trainer). At 60, the epic **Charger** chain has a Redemption theme and goes through Stormwind NPCs (Lord Grayson Shadowbreaker, Duthorian Rall and others in Classic). Details for Forever are not confirmed. Your racial mount is a **horse** (100g with Apprentice Riding at 40).
- **Reputation.** Stormwind is your home faction. Honored gives −10% on Riding and racial mounts. The Kirin Tor (Dalaran) is Alliance-only. The Brotherhood of the Horse quests in Riverglades have lore that is good for paladins.
- **Library books:** turn them in to Garion Wendell in the Wizard's Sanctum in Stormwind.
- **Transmog:** Fyrenz Vishonar in the Mage District (48, 84).
- **Lore.** Human paladins continue the Order of the Silver Hand. In the Forever timeline, the Forsaken now have their own paladins. This gives interesting material for roleplay.

## Verdict

<div class="verdict" markdown>

**The best PvP paladin, and a good choice for all roles.** Will to Survive is the strongest racial against stuns that a paladin can have. Ruthless and The Human Spirit help all specs. Dwarves are a little better than Humans in raid DPS (hit) and in fights with many poisons. Humans are better in battlegrounds and world PvP.

</div>
