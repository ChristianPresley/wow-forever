## <span class="fc-ch__lv">Before level 1</span> Make your Paladin { #start .fc-ch data-toc-label="Start · Make your Paladin" data-label="Start · Make your Paladin" }

<aside class="infobox infobox--row" data-class="paladin" markdown>
<p class="infobox-title">Paladin at a glance</p>

| | |
|---|---|
| **Roles** | Melee damage (Retribution), tank (Protection), healer (Holy) |
| **Armor** | Mail, then **Plate**{ .pill .pill--plate } at level 40. Shields |

| **Also called** | Pally · Pala · Holy, Prot or Ret pally · Shockadin (Holy with melee damage) |

</aside>

A Paladin fights in melee with a Seal on the weapon. Each swing triggers the Seal, and Judgement releases its power on the target. Blessings and auras make the whole group stronger. Heals, a shield and 3 emergency spells make the class very difficult to kill.

Forever gives the class the tools that it did not have in Classic. Holy Strike is a melee strike with a short cooldown. Seal of Fury and its taunt make Protection a full tank from level 10. Judgement no longer removes your Seal.

Each talent tree is 1 role. Retribution does melee damage with a two-hand weapon. Protection tanks with a one-hand weapon and a shield. Holy heals. You can do all 3 roles on 1 character, and dual spec at level 40 lets you keep 2 of them.

You make 3 decisions before level 1: your race, your talent path and your equipment set. The add-ons below describe each option. You can change the talents and the equipment later. You cannot change the race, and the race also decides your faction.

- Blessings last 1 hour, Blessing of Kings is baseline, and resistance auras protect the raid
- Protection is a full tank from level 10, with a taunt on Judgement of Fury
- Strong AoE threat and damage in dungeons with Consecration from level 20

    - **Holy Strike** (level 6) is an instant melee strike that does weapon damage and Holy damage. Its cooldown is 10 seconds at launch.
    - **Judgement keeps your Seal.** Judgement is not on the global cooldown, and its debuffs last 40 seconds.
    - **Seal of Fury** (level 10) is the tank Seal. It adds Holy damage and a small absorb to each swing. Judgement of Fury taunts at 10 yards.
    - **Righteous Fury** comes at level 10, not 16. It gives +33% threat and +90% Holy threat.
    - **Consecration** (level 20) is baseline for all specs. It does full damage to only the first 4 targets.
    - **Blessings** last 1 hour. Blessing of Kings and the improved Blessing of Wisdom are baseline.
    - **Lay on Hands** has a 20 minute cooldown. In Classic it was 1 hour.
    - **Voice of Truth** is a trained spell: 6 seconds of immunity to silence and interrupt, with a 3 minute cooldown.
    - **Unsealed Wrath** is the last Retribution talent. The talent Twist of Light from the beta is not in the launch client.
    - Without Blessing of Salvation, damage classes do not get a 30% threat decrease. Tanks must make more threat.
    - **Undead Paladin** is the new race combination. Players made 612,096 of them in the beta.

## <span class="fc-ch__lv">Levels 1–9</span> Seals, Judgement and Holy Strike { #l1 .fc-ch data-toc-label="1–9 · Seals" data-label="1–9 · Seals" }

You start with Seal of Righteousness, Holy Light and Devotion Aura. Cast the Seal before each fight and keep the aura active. Then attack in melee. The Seal adds Holy damage to each swing and costs little mana.

At level 4 you train Judgement and Blessing of Might. Judgement releases the Seal on the target for instant damage. In Forever the Seal stays on you, so you do not cast it again. Use Judgement each time that it is available.

Level 6 gives you Holy Strike, the new melee strike of Forever. From this level your fight has a fixed sequence: Seal, Judgement, Holy Strike, and white swings between them. Divine Protection, also at 6, is your first emergency spell.

At level 8 you train Hammer of Justice, a stun. Use it to stop a cast or to stop an enemy that runs. It is your only tool for those 2 problems, because the class has no interrupt and no slow. Against a strong enemy, use Hammer of Justice first. Then use Judgement and Holy Strike while the enemy is stunned.

Mana is the limit at these levels. Drink between pulls, and use Holy Light before the pull, not during the fight. At about level 5, learn Cooking and get the camping quest *The Great Outdoors*. See [Camping](../../systems/camping.md).

| Level | You train | Why it is important |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Seal of Righteousness, Holy Light, Devotion Aura** | Your first Seal, your heal and your first aura |
| 4 | **Judgement, Blessing of Might** | Judgement releases the Seal for instant damage. The Seal stays active |
| 6 | **Holy Strike, Divine Protection, Seal of the Crusader** | Holy Strike is new: an instant strike with a 10 second cooldown |
| 8 | **Hammer of Justice, Purify** | A stun. Purify removes poisons and diseases |

!!! tip fc-inline "Tip"
    The game does not put new spells on your action bars. Open the spellbook after each visit to a trainer.

!!! hardcore fc-inline "Hardcore"
    Divine Protection at level 6 is your first emergency spell. If a pull goes wrong, use it early and move away from the enemies.

## <span class="fc-ch__lv">Levels 10–19</span> Seal of Fury and your first dungeon { #l10 .fc-ch data-toc-label="10–19 · Seal of Fury" data-label="10–19 · Seal of Fury" }

At level 10 your class changes. You train Seal of Fury, Righteous Fury, Lay on Hands and Blessing of Protection. You also get your first talent point. From here, you get 1 talent point at each level. The Talents add-on below gives the talent for each point.

Seal of Fury and Righteous Fury make you a tank. Righteous Fury increases your threat. Judgement of Fury is a taunt with a 10 yard range. With these 2 spells and a shield, each Paladin can tank the first dungeons, also without Protection talents.

Lay on Hands heals you or an ally to full health. Its cooldown is now 20 minutes, so use it in each bad fight. Blessing of Protection makes a cloth player immune to physical attacks.

At level 12, do the class quest for Redemption, your resurrection spell. Blessing of Wisdom at 14 gives mana regeneration. Blessing of Freedom at 18 removes roots and slows. In a group, ask each player which Blessing they want.

Your first dungeons open at level 13. Dungeon enemies give less XP in Forever, and dungeon quests give much more. Thus, get all the quests before you go in. Meeting stones cannot summon in Forever, so go to the entrance in time. At level 14, start the scavenger hunt for the Cozy Sleeping Bag. See [Leveling](../../characters/leveling.md).

| Level | You train | Why it is important |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | **Seal of Fury, Righteous Fury** | The tank Seal and more threat. Judgement of Fury taunts |
| 10 | **Lay on Hands, Blessing of Protection** | A full heal each 20 minutes. Immunity to physical attacks for 1 ally |
| 12 | **Redemption** | Class quest. It brings a dead player back to life |
| 14 | **Blessing of Wisdom** | Mana regeneration for you and for casters |
| 16 | **Retribution Aura** | Enemies that hit you take Holy damage |
| 18 | **Blessing of Freedom** | Removes roots and slows. Your answer to kiting |

**Dungeons in this chapter**

- **13–20**{ .lvl } [Hall of Thanes](../../content/dungeons/hall-of-thanes.md): New. It is in Ironforge
- **13–18**{ .lvl } Ragefire Chasm: It is in Orgrimmar
- **15–22**{ .lvl } [Ruins of Lordaeron](../../content/dungeons/ruins-of-lordaeron.md): New. It has many undead, and it is in Tirisfal Glades
- **15–24**{ .lvl } Wailing Caverns: Purify removes the poisons there
- **17–26**{ .lvl } The Deadmines: Rhahk'Zor drops a slow two-hand mace

## <span class="fc-ch__lv">Levels 20–29</span> Consecration and your class weapon { #l20 .fc-ch data-toc-label="20–29 · Consecration" data-label="20–29 · Consecration" }

At level 20 you train Consecration, Blessing of Kings, Exorcism and Flash of Light. Your 11th talent point also comes at level 20. For Retribution and Protection, it gives the first key talent.

Consecration burns the ground around you. It is your first AoE spell, and it is your main threat tool on a group of enemies. It does full damage to only the first 4 targets, and it costs much mana. Use it when 2 or more enemies attack you.

Flash of Light is a fast heal that costs little mana. It makes you a useful healer in dungeons. Blessing of Kings increases all stats, and most players want it.

Your class weapon quest also starts in this chapter. Alliance Paladins get Verigan's Fist. Undead Paladins get Wolfsbane. The 2 chains need items from dungeons, so make a group. You can also train polearms from level 20.

At 20, give 10 library books to the librarian for the first book quest. He is Garion Wendell in Stormwind or Owen Thadd in the Undercity. Level 25 gives your first Paladin Legacy Point. See [Legacy](../../characters/legacy.md).

| Level | You train | Why it is important |
|---|---|---|
| 20 | **Consecration** | AoE Holy damage around you. Full damage to the first 4 targets |
| 20 | **Blessing of Kings, Flash of Light, Exorcism** | Kings is baseline in Forever. Exorcism hits undead and demons |
| 22 | **Concentration Aura, Seal of Justice** | The aura protects your casts when enemies hit you |
| 24 | **Turn Undead** | A fear for 1 undead enemy |
| 28 | **Shadow Resistance Aura** | The first of 3 resistance auras. They cover the raid in 30 yards |

**Dungeons in this chapter**

- **20–30**{ .lvl } Shadowfang Keep: It has a step of the 2 class weapon quests
- **24–32**{ .lvl } Blackfathom Deeps
- **25–29**{ .lvl } The Stockade: It is in Stormwind
- **26–33**{ .lvl } [Excavation Site: Wetlands](../../content/dungeons/excavation-site.md): New. It is short, with 3 bosses
- **28–35**{ .lvl } [City of Dalaran](../../content/dungeons/city-of-dalaran.md): New. NPCs in Dalaran attack Horde characters

## <span class="fc-ch__lv">Levels 30–39</span> Divine Shield and Forbearance { #l30 .fc-ch data-toc-label="30–39 · Divine Shield" data-label="30–39 · Divine Shield" }

These levels add few new spells, but 1 of them changes how safe you are. Divine Shield at level 34 makes you immune to all damage. It is your best escape. While it is active, you can heal yourself or use your hearthstone.

Divine Shield causes Forbearance. During Forbearance you cannot use Divine Shield, Divine Protection or Blessing of Protection again (Classic rule). Thus, each emergency spell is a decision. Do not use 2 of them in 1 fight.

Divine Intervention at 30 saves 1 player when the group dies. The Frost and Fire resistance auras come at 32 and 36. Seal of Wisdom at 38 gives back mana with each swing.

Battlegrounds are a change from quests. Fan sites say that Warsong Gulch opens at level 10 and Arathi Basin at level 20. The new Battle for the Darkspear Islands opens at level 30. A Paladin is a strong flag carrier and node defender.

Start to save gold now. At level 40 you need 100g for riding and 50g for dual spec. The second book quest at 35 needs 20 library books. Blizzard said that a larger balance update comes when players get to levels 30 to 39.

| Level | You train | Why it is important |
|---|---|---|
| 30 | **Divine Intervention** | You die, and 1 ally becomes immune and leaves the fight (Classic effect) |
| 32 | **Frost Resistance Aura** | For fights with Frost damage |
| 34 | **Divine Shield** | Full immunity. It causes Forbearance |
| 36 | **Fire Resistance Aura** | Important for Onyxia's Lair at level 60 |
| 38 | **Seal of Wisdom** | Each swing can give back mana |

**Dungeons in this chapter**

- **29–38**{ .lvl } Razorfen Kraul and Gnomeregan
- **30–46**{ .lvl } Scarlet Monastery: 4 wings with a weapon or a shield for each spec
- **35–42**{ .lvl } [The Drowned City](../../content/dungeons/drowned-city.md): New. It is on the coast of Stranglethorn Vale

!!! hardcore fc-inline "Hardcore"
    Divine Shield at level 34 is your best escape. Cast it, then use your hearthstone while it is active. Keep Blessing of Freedom for roots and slows.

## <span class="fc-ch__lv">Levels 40–49</span> Plate, a mount and a second spec { #l40 .fc-ch data-toc-label="40–49 · Plate" data-label="40–49 · Plate" }

Level 40 changes much. You can wear plate armor. You can buy riding for 100g. You can buy dual spec for 50g from your Paladin trainer. Your 31st talent point gives the last talent of your tree.

Paladins also get a class mount, the Warhorse. Sources do not agree if it is a trainer spell, a quest, or the two together. In all cases, keep 100g for the riding skill.

Dual spec is important for a hybrid class. Keep your quest build in 1 spec and a group role in the other. You change specs for free outside combat and outside battlegrounds. A change of talents in 1 spec costs gold, and this cost decreases to 1g with time.

Retribution with Protection is the most popular pair. Retribution with Holy is for players who want to heal. Protection with Holy is for players who play mostly in groups. Each spec needs its own equipment from now on. Keep a one-hand weapon and a shield in your bags if you tank. Keep the items with Intellect and healing power if you heal.

Cleanse at 42 removes magic effects, poisons and diseases. Hammer of Wrath at 44 hits a target with low health from range. It helps against enemies that run. The new zone Riverglades is for levels 35 to 45, and it has more than 150 quests. At 45, the third book quest needs 25 library books. Level 45 also gives your second Paladin Legacy Point.

| Level | You train | Why it is important |
|---|---|---|
| 40 | **Summon Warhorse** | The class mount. The exact source is not confirmed |
| 42 | **Cleanse** | Removes magic, poisons and diseases. A group job in dungeons and raids |
| 44 | **Hammer of Wrath** | A ranged hit on a target with low health |
| 46 | **Blessing of Sacrifice** | You take a part of the damage of 1 ally |

**Dungeons in this chapter**

- **37–46**{ .lvl } Razorfen Downs: Many undead. Exorcism and Turn Undead are useful
- **40–47**{ .lvl } [Krol'dok Stronghold](../../content/dungeons/kroldok-stronghold.md): New. It is in Riverglades
- **41–54**{ .lvl } Uldaman and Zul'Farrak
- **46–55**{ .lvl } Maraudon
- **48–55**{ .lvl } [Alcaz Prison](../../content/dungeons/alcaz-prison.md): New. It is in Dustwallow Marsh

!!! conflict "Conflict: Twist of Light"
    Older guides and the beta deep dive show Twist of Light as the last Retribution talent. The launch client does not have it. Unsealed Wrath is the last Retribution talent, and it replaces the seal twisting of Classic.

!!! warning fc-inline "Caution"
    Buy riding before dual spec if you do not have 150g. A mount saves more time than a second spec.

## <span class="fc-ch__lv">Levels 50–59</span> Greater Blessings and undead dungeons { #l50 .fc-ch data-toc-label="50–59 · Greater Blessings" data-label="50–59 · Greater Blessings" }

The last 10 levels have the most dungeons. Reportedly, each boss in the new dungeons drops a blue item. Start to collect your level 60 equipment here.

Holy Wrath at 50 is an AoE spell against undead and demons. The Greater Blessings start at 52. A Greater Blessing buffs all players of 1 class in the raid for 1 hour. It needs a Symbol of Kings, which is a vendor reagent.

You have the last talent of your tree, so most of your last points go into the other 2 trees. They give Strength, crit, hit or mana. No source confirms a hit cap for Forever.

Scholomance and Stratholme have many undead. Exorcism, Holy Wrath and Turn Undead work on most of their enemies, so these 2 dungeons are good for a Paladin. Cleanse is also important there.

| Level | You train | Why it is important |
|---|---|---|
| 50 | **Holy Wrath** | AoE Holy damage against undead and demons |
| 52 | **Greater Blessing of Might** | A Blessing for all players of 1 class. It needs a Symbol of Kings |
| 54 | **Greater Blessing of Wisdom** | The same for casters and healers |

**Dungeons in this chapter**

- **50–60**{ .lvl } Sunken Temple and Blackrock Depths
- **54–60**{ .lvl } Dire Maul and Lower Blackrock Spire
- **55–60**{ .lvl } [Blackmaw Hold](../../content/dungeons/blackmaw-hold.md): New. It is in Azshara
- **58–60**{ .lvl } Scholomance and Stratholme: Many undead. Good dungeons for a Paladin
- **58–60**{ .lvl } [Shaper's Terrace](../../content/dungeons/shapers-terrace.md): New. It is in Un'Goro Crater

    Do not fight humanoids that run near other groups of enemies. You have no slow, and only Hammer of Justice and Hammer of Wrath can stop them.

## <span class="fc-ch__lv">Level 60</span> Charger, attunement and raid role { #l60 .fc-ch data-toc-label="60 · Charger" data-label="60 · Charger" }

At level 60 you train Greater Blessing of Kings, and you get your third Paladin Legacy Point. [Mount Hyjal](../../world/zones.md#mount-hyjal) opens. The fast mount needs Journeyman Riding for 1,000g.

Your epic class mount is a quest chain. Alliance Paladins get the Charger. Undead Paladins get the Forsaken Charger, and they ride in the name of Retribution. No source confirms the steps of the 2 chains for Forever.

All 3 launch raids open on 9 December 2026. Use the time before that date for 4 tasks. Decide your raid role. Collect the equipment for that role. Collect consumables. Do the attunement for Onyxia's Lair.

The attunement gives the Drakefire Amulet. It must be in your bags, and it is not account-wide. Each character must do the chain.

Dungeon loot has new stats in Forever, so the Classic lists of items before the raids do not apply. Do the dungeons for levels 55 to 60, mostly the new ones. The Equipment add-on gives the items that the public lists name.

For consumables, collect mana potions, elixirs, stat food and weapon stones or oils. The Legacy talent Gourmand makes food buffs last 33% longer. Camp buffs do not stack with the equivalent class buff.

## <span class="fc-ch__lv">Raids and PvP</span> Raid buffs and battlegrounds { #raid .fc-ch data-toc-label="Raids · Raid buffs" data-label="Raids · Raid buffs" }

**The Barrow Deeps** has 10 players, and **Hyjal Summit** has 20. In raids of this size, each class buff is important. Expect to give Blessing of Kings, a resistance aura and Cleanse. See [The Barrow Deeps](../../content/raids/barrow-deeps.md) and [Hyjal Summit](../../content/raids/hyjal-summit.md).

**Onyxia's Lair** has 40 players and much Fire damage. Use Fire Resistance Aura. It covers the raid in 30 yards. Fire resistance equipment is recommended, but no source gives a number. See [Onyxia's Lair](../../content/raids/onyxias-lair.md).

Blessing of Salvation is not in the game. Thus, the tank must make more threat, and damage classes must monitor their threat. A Retribution Paladin has less threat because of Two-Handed Weapon Specialization.

Holy is the most probable raid role. Protection is a good main tank or off-tank in the 2 smaller raids. Retribution gives constant damage and the same buffs. No source has tested the 3 specs in raids.

In battlegrounds, a Paladin is a very good flag carrier and node defender. Blessing of Freedom, Divine Shield and your heals keep you alive. You cannot catch enemies that run. See [PvP](../../content/pvp/index.md) and [Battlegrounds](../../content/pvp/battlegrounds.md).

The battlegrounds are Warsong Gulch, Arathi Basin, Alterac Valley and the new Battle for the Darkspear Islands. The new battleground has 15 players in each team. In world PvP, Divine Shield and Lay on Hands make it difficult for enemies to kill you. The Legacy talent *For Great Honor* gives up to +10% Honor.

The table below is Classic-era knowledge, with the changes of Forever.

| Enemy in PvP | What you do |
|---|---|
| Warrior | You survive longer. Keep Blessing of Freedom for Hamstring and Hammer of Justice for the burst |
| Rogue | Stuns are the danger. Humans have Will to Survive. Dwarves remove poisons with Stoneform |
| Mage, Warlock | They kite and use Fear. Undead have Will of the Forsaken. Other races need Blessing of Freedom |
| Priest | A dispel removes your buffs. Use Voice of Truth when they try to stop your heals |
| Hunter | Get near them with Pursuit of Justice and Blessing of Freedom. Scatter Shot blocks Will of the Forsaken |
| Druid, Shaman | Long fights. Keep Hammer of Justice for their heal |

!!! hardcore fc-inline "Hardcore"
    The roadmap lists [Hardcore](../../about/hardcore.md) for winter 2026/27. A Paladin is one of the safest classes for it. Templar's Bulwark and Divine Shield both cause Forbearance, so plan to use 1 of them, not the 2. An Undead removes Fear with Will of the Forsaken, and a Human breaks stuns with Will to Survive.

## Professions { #professions }

<div class="fc-stack" markdown>

A Paladin wears plate from level 40, and **Blacksmithing** is good for all 3 specs. It has many new recipes, and it makes mail with caster stats before level 40. Warcraft Tavern strongly recommends it for a spell power build.

| Rank | Professions | Use them for | Codex rating: leveling, raids, PvP, gold |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S**{ .tier .tier-s } | [Mining](../../professions/mining.md) + [Blacksmithing](../../professions/blacksmithing.md) | Weapons, mail and plate for each level range, weapon stones and shield spikes. The standard selection | 4, 4, 3, 3 of 5 |
| **A**{ .tier .tier-a } | [Mining](../../professions/mining.md) + [Engineering](../../professions/engineering.md) | PvP gadgets | 2, 3, 5, 2 of 5 |
| **B**{ .tier .tier-b } | [Herbalism](../../professions/herbalism.md) + [Alchemy](../../professions/alchemy.md) | Elixirs and mana potions for Holy healers in raids | 3, 5, 3, 4 of 5 |

[Enchanting](../../professions/enchanting.md) makes the crafted Librams. Take [First Aid](../../professions/first-aid.md) on each character, because it now makes healing potions. [Cooking](../../professions/cooking.md) and [Fishing](../../professions/fishing.md) supply the camp. Compare all classes in [Best professions by class](../../characters/professions.md#best-professions-by-class).

Each crafting profession adds a [camp feature](../../systems/camping.md). Blacksmithing gives the Sharpening Wheel (Strength), and Mining gives the Lodestone (melee attack power). Engineering gives the Reagent Bot. The pair Herbalism and Alchemy gives the Incense Candle (Intellect) and the Mana Well (mana regeneration). Crafting professions also give Legacy Points at skill 150, 225 and 300. The 3 Paladin races have no profession racial.

## Legacy talents { #legacy }

You can spend a maximum of 16 [Legacy](../../characters/legacy.md) points on 1 character. A Paladin gets class Legacy Points at levels 25, 45 and 60.

| Tree | Picks for a Paladin |
|---|---|
| Adventure | **Thrill of Adventure** gives back health and mana when you kill an enemy in the open world. It is the best pick for a class that often has no mana |
| Adventure | **Well Rested** 5/5, then **Talented** 5/5. Talented gives your first talent point up to 5 levels earlier |
| Resourcefulness | **Reagent Economy** removes the Symbol of Kings and the Symbol of Divinity. **Permanence** makes class buffs last longer |
| Other | *For Great Honor* gives up to +10% Honor. *Diplomat* helps reputation |

## Common mistakes

- You cast your Seal again after each Judgement. In Forever the Seal stays active.
- You keep Righteous Fury active when you are not the tank.
- You plan Templar's Bulwark and Divine Shield in 1 fight. They share Forbearance.
- You use Seal of Command with a fast weapon. Use Seal of Righteousness until you have a slow two-hand weapon.
- You pull more than 4 enemies and expect full Consecration damage on all of them.
- You forget that the game no longer adds new spells to your action bars.
- You buy dual spec without gold for riding at level 40.

## FAQ

??? question "Does Judgement still remove my Seal?"
    No. In Forever the Seal stays active after Judgement, so you do not cast it again.

??? question "Where is Blessing of Salvation?"
    Blizzard removed it before launch, together with Blessing of Light and Blessing of Sanctuary.

??? question "Can I seal-twist?"
    Not as in Classic. The last Retribution talent, Unsealed Wrath, replaces it. Swing with one Seal to store copies, then change Seals.

??? question "Can I take Holy Shock and Unsealed Wrath in one build?"
    No. Holy Shock needs 21 Holy points and Unsealed Wrath needs 31 Retribution points. This is 52 points, and you have 51.

??? question "Why is my Consecration weaker on large pulls?"
    It does full damage to only the first 4 targets.

??? question "Where do Horde Paladins train?"
    At Bandarion Keep, Brill or Deathknell in Tirisfal Glades. No source confirms a trainer in the Undercity or Orgrimmar.

??? question "Which spec is best for leveling?"
    Retribution is the usual selection. Protection is good if you level mostly in dungeons. Holy is for players who heal dungeons from the first levels.

??? question "How do I get the Warhorse at level 40?"
    Sources do not agree. In Classic it is the quest *The Tome of Nobility* from an Alliance trainer. The Forsaken quest has no confirmed quest giver.
