A Priest wears cloth for all 60 levels and cannot use shields or relics. The wand is the main weapon until Penance at level 30 or Shadowform at level 40. From level 40, dual spec lets you play 2 roles, so carry a healer set and a damage set.

About 40% of the items of Forever had hidden stats at launch. The stats of an item become visible when a player first finds it. Most item names for each level come from 1 tool, Timesaver.gg (data of 6 November 2026). It shows no stats and no levels. The level 60 lists come from the Kami-Labs tool of 9 November. Use each list as candidates, not as tested lists. The Codex did not verify the stats.

No source compares a staff with a one-hand weapon and an off-hand item in numbers.

## Item rules in Forever { #rules }

- Bonus healing on an item also gives one third of its value as spell damage. Caster weapons give spell damage and healing.
- Early wands have spell power as a stat, from about level 5 (Warcraft Tavern).
- In the beta, wand damage increased with your spell power. RankedBoost reports that Blizzard removed this on 24 September 2026. No source tested it after launch.
- Hit is 1 stat for melee, ranged attacks and spells. Heals cannot miss, so a healer needs no hit.
- A spell misses a raid boss 16% of the time (Warcraft Tavern). A search result from Icy Veins gives 17%. Blizzard has published no cap.
- Shadow Focus gives 5% Shadow hit. Thus a Shadow Priest needs 11% to 12% hit from items at level 60.
- Holy Precision gives 18% Holy hit with 3 points. This is more than the boss miss chance of 16% or 17% that the sources give. Thus a Smite build probably needs no hit on items.
- Intellect gives mana and spell crit. Mental Strength increases your total Intellect by a maximum of 10%.
- Spirit is a real healer stat. Meditation keeps 50% of the regeneration while you cast, and Spiritual Guidance changes Spirit into healing.
- The Warcraft Tavern Priest guide says that Forever has haste as a stat. Its stats guide says that no known item has haste.
- Necklaces and off-hand items can have enchants. These slots are new.
- Each profession gives a camp feature in place of a passive stat. The Faction Banner of Tailoring gives Spirit and does not stack with Divine Spirit.
- The 20 library books give the Philanthropist's Ring with 10 spell power (Warcraft Tavern).
