# Night Elf Priest

!!! tip "Add-on"
    Read with the [Priest guide](index.md).

## Why this combo

Night Elf Priest is a Classic combination and **not new**. Forever made it much stronger: **Starshards** now does far more damage on a 30 s cooldown, **Elune's Grace** became a strong anti-physical defensive, and the new **Elune's Light** racial gives +10% crit for 15 s. Warcraft Tavern calls Night Elf "very strong" for Priests.

**Best for:** Shadow and Discipline damage (Starshards, Elune's Light), PvP (Shadowmeld, Elune's Grace), and healers who want a crit cooldown.

## Racials in practice

| Racial | Type | PvE | PvP | Rating |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Elune's Light** *(new)* | Active | **+10% crit for 15 s.** Burst for Shadow; a healer crit window for Inspiration, Divine Aegis and Renewed Hope | Burst for a Mind Blast + SW:Death kill | ★★★★★ |
| **Shadowmeld** | Active | Drop out of combat to drink, or skip a pack while waiting for the group | Ambushes, flag guarding, losing a chaser | ★★★ |
| **Quickness** | Passive | +1% dodge, +2% run speed | Kiting | ★★★ |
| **Wisp Spirit** | Passive | Faster corpse runs. Great for a slow-leveling cloth class | — | ★★ (★★★★ leveling) |

**Priest spells unique to Night Elves**

| Spell | Level | Effect | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Starshards** (buffed) | 10 | Arcane damage, much stronger than in Classic, on a **30 s cooldown** | Put it in every rotation: opener for Discipline and Holy leveling, extra burst for Shadow. It's Arcane, so Shadowform's discounts don't apply |
| **Elune's Grace** (redesigned) | 20 | Melee and ranged attacks against you are **50% less likely to hit** for 15 s or until 3 attacks miss. 5-minute cooldown | Rogue or Warrior on you; a loose mob on the healer; boss melee if you're tanking briefly |

**Macros**

Elune's Light into burst (Shadow):

```
#showtooltip Elune's Light
/cast Elune's Light
/cast Mind Blast
```

Shadowmeld to drink (mobs must lose you first):

```
#showtooltip Shadowmeld
/cast Shadowmeld
/use [nocombat] Refreshing Spring Water
```

Swap the water name for what you carry. Whether Shadowmeld still drops combat in Forever as in Classic isn't documented.

## Starting zone walkthrough (1–~12)

Night Elves start in **Shadowglen**, Teldrassil. Blizzard says Shadowglen got the same refresh as the Valley of Trials: **new NPCs, quests, profession hooks and "small moments"**. Quest names below are Classic-era and may differ.

1. **Level 1–2:** take the opening quests at **Aldrassil**, the great tree, and cull young nightsabers and thistle boars. The Priest trainer is inside **Aldrassil** (Classic: Shanda).
2. **Level 2–5:** grell and Fel Moss quests, the moonwell, and the Shadowthread Cave spiders. Train **Shadow Word: Pain** at 4.
3. **Level 5:** take **The Great Outdoors**, learn Cooking, and plan your wand profession.
4. **Level 6:** **Power Word: Shield**. Leave Shadowglen down the ramp for **Dolanaar**.
5. **Level 6–10:** the Dolanaar hub: Ban'ethil Barrow Den, the Fel Rock, Lake Al'Ameth and the Starbreeze Village quests. Dolanaar has early class training (Classic-era).
6. **Level 10:** learn **Starshards**, your first talent, and the class quest. Full training is in **Darnassus**, at the **Temple of the Moon**.
7. **Level 10–12:** finish Teldrassil (the Oracle Glade, the Gnarlpine furbolgs), then take the boat from **Rut'theran Village** to **Auberdine** in Darkshore.

- **Class trainers:** Temple of the Moon, Darnassus.
- Kalimdor is your home turf. Azshara (new quests and [Blackmaw Hold](../../content/dungeons/blackmaw-hold.md)), Felwood, Winterspring and Mount Hyjal are all night elf story zones.
- The new Stormwind Harbor boat makes Eastern Kingdoms zones much easier to reach than in Classic.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Mount:** Frostsabers at 40 (100g). Honored with **Darnassus** gives 10% off.
- **Reputation:** Darnassus. Mount Hyjal's endgame factions (names unconfirmed) and the Cenarion Circle fit your story.
- **Barbershop:** Darnassus gained a barbershop (beta notes).
- **Lore:** the Sisterhood of Elune leads night elf priesthood. The Forever raids under Hyjal include the **Barrow Deeps**, where Illidan was imprisoned, a natural fit for a Priestess of the Moon.
- **Elves and Skyborne:** Skyborne speak Darnassian, so Night Elves can talk with Horde Skyborne in Darnassian across factions.

## Verdict

**One of the strongest Priest races in Forever.** Elune's Light and the buffed Starshards add real damage, Elune's Grace and Shadowmeld make you hard to kill in PvP, and Wisp Spirit eases a slow level. Choose Night Elf for Shadow or a damage-heavy Discipline; Human or Dwarf if you want stun or poison protection instead.
