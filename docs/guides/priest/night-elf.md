## Why this combo

Night Elf Priest is a Classic combination and **not new**. Forever made it much stronger. **Starshards** now does much more damage on a 30 s cooldown. **Elune's Grace** is now a strong defensive spell against physical attacks. The new **Elune's Light** racial gives +10% crit for 15 s. Warcraft Tavern calls Night Elf "very strong" for Priests.

**Best for:** Shadow and Discipline damage (Starshards, Elune's Light), PvP (Shadowmeld, Elune's Grace), and healers who want a crit cooldown.

**Priest spells unique to Night Elves**

| Spell | Level | Effect | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Starshards** (buffed) | 10 | Arcane damage, much stronger than in Classic, on a **30 s cooldown** | Use it in each rotation: first attack for Discipline and Holy when you level, more burst for Shadow. It is Arcane damage. Thus the Shadowform cost decrease does not apply |
| **Elune's Grace** (redesigned) | 20 | For 15 s or until 3 attacks miss, melee and ranged attacks against you have a **50% lower chance to hit**. 5-minute cooldown | A Rogue or Warrior attacks you. A mob attacks the healer. Boss melee attacks if you must tank for a short time |

## Starting zone walkthrough (1–~12)

Night Elves start in **Shadowglen**, Teldrassil. Blizzard says that Shadowglen got the same refresh as the Valley of Trials: **new NPCs, quests, profession hooks and "small moments"**. The quest names below are Classic-era and can be different.

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **1–2**{ .lvl } Get the first quests at **Aldrassil**, the great tree. Kill young nightsabers and thistle boars. The Priest trainer is in **Aldrassil** (Classic: Shanda).
2.  **2–5**{ .lvl } Do the grell and Fel Moss quests, the moonwell, and the Shadowthread Cave spiders. At 4, train **Shadow Word: Pain**.
3.  **5**{ .lvl } Get **The Great Outdoors** and learn Cooking. Plan your wand profession.
4.  **6**{ .lvl } Train **Power Word: Shield**. Go down the ramp out of Shadowglen to **Dolanaar**.
5.  **6–10**{ .lvl } Do the quests at the Dolanaar hub: Ban'ethil Barrow Den, the Fel Rock, Lake Al'Ameth and Starbreeze Village. Dolanaar has early class training (Classic-era).
6.  **10**{ .lvl } Learn **Starshards** and your first talent, and get the class quest. Full training is in **Darnassus**, at the **Temple of the Moon**.
7.  **10–12**{ .lvl } Complete Teldrassil (the Oracle Glade, the Gnarlpine furbolgs). Then take the boat from **Rut'theran Village** to **Auberdine** in Darkshore.

</div>

- **Class trainers:** Temple of the Moon, Darnassus.
- Kalimdor is your home continent. Azshara (new quests and [Blackmaw Hold](../../content/dungeons/blackmaw-hold.md)), Felwood, Winterspring and Mount Hyjal are all zones with night elf stories.
- With the new Stormwind Harbor boat, it is much easier to go to Eastern Kingdoms zones than in Classic.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Mount:** Frostsabers at 40 (100g). Honored with **Darnassus** gives 10% off.
- **Reputation:** Darnassus. The endgame factions of Mount Hyjal (names unconfirmed) and the Cenarion Circle fit your story.
- **Barbershop:** Darnassus now has a barbershop (beta notes).
- **Lore:** The Sisterhood of Elune leads the night elf priests. The Forever raids under Hyjal include the **Barrow Deeps**, where Illidan was a prisoner. This is a good fit for a Priestess of the Moon.
- **Elves and Skyborne:** Skyborne speak Darnassian. Thus Night Elves can talk in Darnassian with Horde Skyborne, across factions.

## Verdict

<div class="verdict" markdown>

**One of the strongest Priest races in Forever.** Elune's Light and the buffed Starshards add real damage. Elune's Grace and Shadowmeld make you difficult to kill in PvP. Wisp Spirit makes slow leveling easier. Select Night Elf for Shadow or for a Discipline build with much damage. Select Human or Dwarf if you want protection from stuns or poisons.

</div>
