The Priest has 2 healer trees and 1 damage tree in Forever. **Discipline** gives shields, Penance and mana talents. **Holy** gives the largest heals and Prayer of Mending. **Shadow Magic** is a full damage spec with Shadowform at 31 points. Each level 60 build puts about 20 points in a second tree, and Discipline is the usual second tree.

## How to read this page

The Priest has 52 talents in 3 trees. You get 1 talent point for each level from 10 to 60, for a total of 51 points.

- **Opens at:** the number of points that you must spend in the same tree before the row opens. Each row needs 5 more points.
- **Max:** the maximum rank of the talent.
- **Leveling:** the value of the talent when you quest and do dungeons from level 10 to 59.
- **PvE:** the value at level 60 in dungeons and raids. This is damage, healing or tank value, for the role of the tree.
- **PvP:** the value in battlegrounds and world PvP.

**S**{ .tier .tier-s } Core. Take it in each build of that type. **A**{ .tier .tier-a } Strong. Take it when you can. **B**{ .tier .tier-b } Good. Use it to fill points. **C**{ .tier .tier-c } Situational or a personal preference. **D**{ .tier .tier-d } Do not take it.

## FAQ

??? question "Is Power Infusion in the game?"
    Yes. In the launch client, Power Infusion is in Discipline row 7. It needs 30 Discipline points and Penance. The Blizzard deep dive did not mention it.

??? question "Where is Shadow Affinity?"
    The launch client has no Shadow Affinity talent. The only threat talent is Silent Resolve in Discipline row 2, which decreases the threat of all spells by 30%.

??? question "What is the cooldown of Vampiric Embrace?"
    The launch client shows a duration of 30 s and a cooldown of 30 s. Beta information gave a cooldown of 1 minute. Thus, you can keep it active on 1 target all the time.

??? question "Does Shadowform give crit chance or crit damage?"
    Crit damage. The client tooltip says that the crit damage bonus of Shadow spells increases by 100%. It also gives +10% Shadow damage and decreases the mana cost of Shadow spells by 50%.

??? question "Does a healer need Holy Precision?"
    No. Heals cannot miss. Holy Precision helps only Smite, Holy Fire, Holy Nova and Penance as damage spells.
