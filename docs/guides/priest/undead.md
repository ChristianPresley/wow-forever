## Why this combo

Undead Priest is a Classic combination and **not new**. It's the classic Horde PvP Priest: **Will of the Forsaken** breaks fears, charms and sleeps. Forever adds **Touch of the Grave**, a passive health drain, and **Dark Sacrifice**, a new Priest spell that turns your health into mana. Warcraft Tavern calls Undead strong, mostly because of Touch of the Grave; it rates Touch of Weakness as weak.

**Best for:** Shadow Priests, PvP of any spec, and leveling (Cannibalize plus Dark Sacrifice means less drinking).

**Priest spells unique to Undead**

| Spell | Level | Effect | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Touch of Weakness** | (Classic spell) | Classic-era: a self-buff that deals Shadow damage and weakens the next melee attacker | Minor; better while leveling than in raids |
| **Dark Sacrifice** *(new)* | 20 | Converts your own health into mana over time. The mana scales with **Spirit** | Pair with Cannibalize or a Renew on yourself to keep going without drinking. In raids, a mana cooldown when a healer can top you up |

## Starting zone walkthrough (1–~12)

The Undead start in **Deathknell**, Tirisfal Glades. Tirisfal got the biggest starting-zone change of the Horde: the empty western strip is now filled with **Shadowvale** and **Bandarion Keep** in the Whispering Wood, home of the new Forsaken Paladin journey. Quest names below are Classic-era and may differ.

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **1–2**{ .lvl } rise from the crypt and take the opening quests in Deathknell (*Rude Awakening* in Classic). Kill mindless zombies and wretched ghouls. The Priest trainer is in **Deathknell's chapel** (Classic: Dark Cleric Duesten).
2.  **2–5**{ .lvl } *Rattling the Rattlecages*, the Night Web's Hollow spiders, the Scarlet convert camp and *Marla's Last Wish*. Train **Shadow Word: Pain** at 4.
3.  **5**{ .lvl } take **The Great Outdoors** and learn Cooking. Tirisfal's new content includes profession hooks.
4.  **6**{ .lvl } **Power Word: Shield**. Kill *Samuel Fipps* and head east to **Brill**.
5.  **6–10**{ .lvl } Brill's quests: Agamand Mills, Garren's Haunt, the Scarlet Crusade, the murlocs of Brightwater Lake, and Cold Hearth Manor. Brill has early class trainers (Classic-era). Explore the new **Shadowvale** content in the west.
6.  **10**{ .lvl } your first talent and the class quest. Full training is in the **Undercity**.
7.  **10–12**{ .lvl } finish Tirisfal, then go south into **Silverpine Forest**.

</div>

## Faction-specific notes

- **Mount:** skeletal horses at 40 (100g). Honored with **Undercity** gives 10% off.
- **Reputation:** Undercity is your home faction.
- **Forsaken Paladins:** Forever adds Undead Paladins with their own journey at Bandarion Keep. Undead Priests don't share it, but you'll see their story around Tirisfal.
- **Lore:** Forsaken priests largely turned from the Light to the shadow; Shadow is the on-theme spec, but Holy and Discipline Forsaken priests exist too.
- **World PvP:** beta raids on the Undercity were common. On PvP rulesets, expect Alliance raids near Brill and the city.

## Verdict

<div class="verdict" markdown>

**The best Horde PvP Priest and a strong Shadow pick.** Will of the Forsaken counters the most dangerous CC in the game, Touch of the Grave adds free sustain, and Dark Sacrifice plus Cannibalize cuts drinking time while leveling. Troll has the better PvE throughput cooldown (Berserking); Undead wins everywhere fear is a threat.

</div>
