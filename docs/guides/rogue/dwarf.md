## Why this combo

Dwarf Rogue is a Classic combination. Forever made it much stronger. **Stoneform** now removes and prevents bleeds, poisons and diseases, and gives 10% physical damage reduction. This is a direct counter to other Rogues and Warriors. **Artisan's Precision** gives +1% hit. At low levels, hit is the stat that Rogues have the most problems with. Dwarves start in Dun Morogh, near the new **Hall of Thanes** dungeon.

**Best for:** PvE players who want a hit bonus, and PvP players who fight other melee players.

## Starting zone: Coldridge Valley and Dun Morogh (1–12)

The quest names below are from Classic. Blizzard named new content in Dun Morogh (Hall of Thanes), but it has not listed changes to Coldridge Valley.

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **1**{ .lvl } **Anvilmar**, Coldridge Valley

    Get *Dwarven Outfitters*. Take the Rogue letter (*Encrypted Rune*) to **Solm Hargrin**, in Anvilmar

2.  **1–5**{ .lvl } Coldridge Valley

    Wolves, troggs and Frostmane trolls: *A New Threat*, *Coldridge Valley Mail Delivery*, *The Troll Cave*, *Scalding Mornbrew Delivery*

3.  **4**{ .lvl } Solm Hargrin

    **Backstab**, **Pick Pocket**

4.  **5–6**{ .lvl } Go through the tunnel to **Kharanos**

    The Kharanos rogue trainer, **Hogral Bakkan**, is in the Thunderbrew Distillery

5.  **6–10**{ .lvl } Dun Morogh

    *Beer Basted Boar Ribs*, *Tools for Steelgrill*, *The Grizzled Den*, *Senir's Observations*, Frostmane Hold, Shimmer Ridge, Brewnall Village

6.  **10**{ .lvl } Hogral Bakkan

    **Slice and Dice**, **Sap**, **Sprint**. First talent point

7.  **10–12**{ .lvl } Gnomeregan outskirts, Helm's Bed Lake

    Complete Dun Morogh. Then go east through the gate to Loch Modan

</div>

- With **Big Game Hunter**, the wolves, boars and bears of Dun Morogh die 5% faster.
- Frostmane trolls are humanoids. Use **Pick Pocket** on them before each kill.
- The troggs in Coldridge Valley come in pairs. Use Sap or Gouge on one of them.

## Faction notes

- **Poison quest (20):** It starts with Master Mathias Shaw in Old Town, Stormwind, not in Ironforge. Take the Deeprun Tram. See the [main guide](index.md#the-poison-quest-level-20).
- **Library books:** Garion Wendell in the Mage Quarter of Stormwind.
- **Mount:** rams from Amberstill Ranch in east Dun Morogh. Honored with Ironforge gives 10% off riding and rams.
- **Reputation:** Ironforge.
- **Uldaman** (Badlands) and **Hall of Thanes** are dungeons about dwarven history. Both are good for Find Treasure players who look for chests.
- **PvP tips:**
    - Against Rogues, keep Stoneform for Rupture or Garrote with poisons. Do not use it against the opener.
    - Against Warriors, Stoneform removes Rend and Deep Wounds and decreases Mortal Strike damage for 8 s.
    - You have no stun or snare breaker. Thus use Stoneform together with Evasion, Vanish and your PvP trinket.
- **Lore:** The rogues of Ironforge meet in the Forlorn Cavern, the less honest part of the city.

## Verdict

<div class="verdict" markdown>

**A strong choice that many players underestimate.** Stoneform is the best racial in the game against Rogues and Warriors. +1% hit is the PvE stat that you want most. Select Dwarf if you expect to fight many melee enemies, or if you want a PvE character that is easy to play. Human is the stronger all-rounder for PvP. Dwarf is the better choice against bleed and poison builds.

</div>
