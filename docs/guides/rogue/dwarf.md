# Dwarf Rogue

!!! tip "Add-on"
    Read with the [Rogue guide](index.md). This page covers only what changes for a Dwarf.

## Why this combo

Dwarf Rogue is a Classic combination. Forever made it much stronger. **Stoneform** now cleanses and blocks bleeds, poisons and diseases and gives 10% physical damage reduction, a direct counter to other Rogues and Warriors. **Artisan's Precision** gives +1% hit, the stat Rogues struggle with most early. Dwarves start in Dun Morogh, close to the new **Hall of Thanes** dungeon.

**Best for:** PvE players who want a hit bonus and PvP players fighting other melee.

## Racials in practice

| Racial | Type | Rogue rating | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stoneform** *(changed)* | Active | **5/5**{ .stars .s5 } PvP · **3/5**{ .stars .s3 } PvE | Removes bleeds, poisons and diseases, grants immunity to them, and reduces physical damage taken by 10% for 8 s. Kills enemy Rogue poisons and Rupture/Garrote, and Warrior Rend and Deep Wounds. In PvE it is a short defensive cooldown and a cleanse for poison-heavy dungeons |
| **Find Treasure** | Active | **2/5**{ .stars .s2 } | Tracks treasure chests. Pairs well with Lockpicking: open locked chests for coin and items |
| **Artisan's Precision** *(new)* | Passive | **4/5**{ .stars .s4 } | +1% hit with attacks and spells. Every Rogue wants hit until misses are rare |
| **Big Game Hunter** *(new)* | Passive | **3/5**{ .stars .s3 } leveling · **1/5**{ .stars .s1 } raids | +5% damage against Beasts. A lot of leveling content is beasts; launch raid bosses mostly aren't |

### Macros

```text
#showtooltip Stoneform
/cast Stoneform
/cast Evasion
```

The "I'm being hit by a Warrior or Rogue" button: physical damage reduction, bleed and poison cleanse, and dodge in one press. Use it carefully, because it spends two cooldowns.

## Starting zone: Coldridge Valley and Dun Morogh (1–12)

Quest names below are from Classic. Blizzard named new content in Dun Morogh (Hall of Thanes) but has not listed changes to Coldridge Valley.

| Level | Where | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Anvilmar**, Coldridge Valley | Take *Dwarven Outfitters* and the Rogue letter (*Encrypted Rune*) to **Solm Hargrin**, inside Anvilmar |
| 1–5 | Coldridge Valley | Wolves, troggs and Frostmane trolls: *A New Threat*, *Coldridge Valley Mail Delivery*, *The Troll Cave*, *Scalding Mornbrew Delivery* |
| 4 | Solm Hargrin | **Backstab**, **Pick Pocket** |
| 5–6 | Through the tunnel to **Kharanos** | The Kharanos rogue trainer, **Hogral Bakkan**, is in the Thunderbrew Distillery |
| 6–10 | Dun Morogh | *Beer Basted Boar Ribs*, *Tools for Steelgrill*, *The Grizzled Den*, *Senir's Observations*, Frostmane Hold, Shimmer Ridge, Brewnall Village |
| 10 | Hogral Bakkan | **Slice and Dice**, **Sap**, **Sprint**; first talent point |
| 10–12 | Gnomeregan outskirts, Helm's Bed Lake | Finish Dun Morogh, then go east through the gate to Loch Modan |

- **Big Game Hunter** makes the wolves, boars and bears of Dun Morogh die 5% faster.
- Frostmane trolls are humanoids: **Pick Pocket** them before each kill.
- Coldridge Valley troggs come in pairs; Sap one or Gouge one.

## Faction notes

- **Poison quest (20):** starts with Master Mathias Shaw in Stormwind's Old Town, not in Ironforge. Take the Deeprun Tram. See the [main guide](index.md#the-poison-quest-level-20).
- **Library books:** Garion Wendell in Stormwind's Mage Quarter.
- **Mount:** rams from Amberstill Ranch in eastern Dun Morogh. Honored with Ironforge gives 10% off riding and rams.
- **Reputation:** Ironforge.
- **Uldaman** (Badlands) and **Hall of Thanes** are dwarven-history dungeons, and both suit Find Treasure players hunting chests.
- **PvP tips:**
    - Against Rogues, save Stoneform for Rupture or Garrote plus poisons, not for the opener.
    - Against Warriors, Stoneform clears Rend and Deep Wounds and blunts Mortal Strike damage for 8 s.
    - You have no stun or snare breaker, so pair Stoneform with Evasion, Vanish and your PvP trinket.
- **Lore:** Ironforge's rogues gather in the Forlorn Cavern, the city's shadier corner.

## Verdict

**A strong, underrated pick.** Stoneform is the best anti-Rogue and anti-Warrior racial in the game, and +1% hit is the PvE stat you want most. Pick Dwarf if you expect to fight a lot of melee or want a forgiving PvE character. Human is the stronger all-rounder for PvP; Dwarf is the better choice against bleed and poison builds.
