A Rogue wears leather for all 60 levels. The weapons are the most important slots, because most of your attacks use weapon damage. Your talent path decides the weapon type. The armor is the same for the 3 talent trees.

About 40% of the items of Forever had hidden stats at launch. The stats of an item become visible when a player first finds it. Thus, no source has a tested list for level 60. The item names on this page come from Timesaver.gg (data of 6 November 2026), Method.gg, Kami-Labs and wowtbc.gg. The Codex did not verify the stats, so this page gives no item stats.

## Item rules in Forever { #rules }

- Method.gg says that hit is 1 stat for melee, ranged attacks and spells. The sources give hit as a percentage.
- Warcraft Tavern gives a miss chance of 5% against an enemy of your level and 9% against a raid boss. A search result from Icy Veins gives 8% for special attacks. No source has tested the value.
- Precision gives 3% hit. Weapon Expertise decreases dodges and parries by 2%. It no longer gives weapon skill.
- Racial weapon skills are gone. Select a weapon for its stats, not for your race.
- Hack and Slash gives the bonus of the weapon that makes the attack. Swords and axes: extra attacks. Daggers and fist weapons: crit. Maces: armor.
- Use a slow main-hand weapon for Sinister Strike. Method says that Combat does less damage with daggers.
- Warcraft Tavern says that slow weapons are best with Instant Poison and fast weapons with Deadly Poison.
- A poison, a sharpening stone or weightstone, and Windfury Totem all work on the same weapon in Forever.
- Poisons increase with Attack Power, and they can crit. Malice also gives crit to poisons.
- 29 Agility gives 1% crit, and 1 Agility gives 1 Attack Power (Warcraft Tavern).
- Warcraft Tavern puts crit first for leveling, but Agility and hit first in its general guide. After you have enough hit, Agility and crit have about the same value.
- Necklaces can have enchants. This slot is new.
- Each profession gives a camp feature in place of a passive stat. The Camp Chair of Skinning gives 2% crit at a camp.
- When you loot an item, you get its appearance. Loot items that are too low for you.

## Slow main hand { #slow }

The standard set for Combat. You use a slow sword, mace, axe or fist weapon in the main hand, with quest rewards and dungeon drops. The item names come from Timesaver.gg, Method.gg and Kami-Labs.

**Stat priority:** Leveling: Crit > Agility > Attack Power > Strength > Stamina (Warcraft Tavern). Level 60: hit, then Agility and crit

## PvP and Hardcore { #pvp }

A safer set. You take Stamina in place of some Attack Power, and you carry potions and bombs. Use it for battlegrounds, for world PvP and for Hardcore.

**Stat priority:** Agility > Stamina > crit > Attack Power. Hit is less important against players

### Levels 1–9 { #pvp-l1 }

Take Stamina on each item that offers it. You have no healing, so more health is more time to use Gouge and a bandage.

### Levels 10–19 { #pvp-l10 }

Use the weapons of your talent path. Keep bandages and food in your bags at all times. Sprint from level 10 lets you leave a fight that you cannot win.

### Levels 20–29 { #pvp-l20 }

Put Crippling Poison on 1 weapon when you fight players or enemies that run away. Carry Thistle Tea and Healing Potions. First Aid makes the Healing Potions in Forever.

### Levels 30–39 { #pvp-l30 }

The Battle for the Darkspear Islands opens at level 30. Use Wound Poison against healers and Mind-numbing Poison against casters. PvP ranks give rewards. See [PvP](../../content/pvp/index.md).
