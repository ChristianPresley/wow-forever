The Rogue is a melee damage class that wears leather. It uses Energy and Combo Points, and it starts most fights from Stealth. This page follows 1 Rogue from character creation to the raids, level by level. ## Before level 1: Make your Rogue { #start }

A Rogue fights with 2 resources. Attacks such as Sinister Strike use Energy and add Combo Points to the target. A finishing move such as Eviscerate uses all the Combo Points. More Combo Points make the finishing move stronger.

The second part of the class is control. Stealth lets you select the enemy and the moment. Sap, Gouge, Kick and the stuns stop enemies. Vanish, Sprint and Evasion end a fight that goes wrong. No other class decides the start of a fight as well as a Rogue.

In Forever, each of the 3 talent trees is a damage spec. Combat gives constant damage with each weapon type. Assassination uses daggers, crit and poisons. Subtlety uses openers from Stealth and bleeds. The new ability Shank has a talent in each tree.

You make 3 decisions before level 1: your race, your talent path and your equipment set. The add-ons below describe each option. You can change the talents and the equipment later. You cannot change the race.

## Levels 1–9: Sinister Strike, Stealth and Gouge { #l1 }

You start with Sinister Strike, Eviscerate and Stealth. The fight is simple at first. Use Sinister Strike until the enemy has 3 or more Combo Points, then use Eviscerate.

At level 4 you train Backstab and Pick Pocket. Backstab needs a dagger, and you must be behind the target. Use Pick Pocket on each humanoid from Stealth before you attack. The coins and the lockboxes pay for your training.

Gouge at level 6 is your first control ability. It stops the enemy for a few seconds. Use that time to get behind the enemy, or to use a bandage. Evasion at level 8 is your first emergency ability. It lets you dodge most melee attacks for a short time.

You have no healing ability. Learn First Aid in your first town and eat between pulls. Fight 1 enemy at a time.

Replace the starting dagger as soon as a quest gives you a better weapon. The Equipment add-on gives the first weapon for your set.

| Level | You train | Why it is important |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sinister Strike, Eviscerate, Stealth** | Your first attack, your first finishing move and the base of each opener |
| 4 | **Backstab, Pick Pocket** | Backstab needs a dagger and a position behind the target. Pick Pocket gives coins |
| 6 | **Gouge** | Stops the enemy for a few seconds. It ends when the enemy takes damage |
| 8 | **Evasion** | A large dodge bonus for a short time. Use it when 2 enemies attack you |

!!! tip "Tip"
    Thieves, cultists and other humanoids carry coins. Open each fight against a humanoid with Stealth and Pick Pocket.

!!! hardcore "Hardcore"
    Do not fight 2 enemies before you have Gouge and Evasion. In Stealth, do not go into caves that you do not know.

## Levels 10–19: Sap, Slice and Dice and Dual Wield { #l10 }

Level 10 changes the class more than any level before it. You train Slice and Dice, Sap and Sprint, and you get your first talent point. Method says that you also learn Dual Wield at level 10. From here, you get 1 talent point at each level.

Slice and Dice is a finishing move that increases your attack speed. Use it early in each fight, with 1 or 2 Combo Points. Then use the next Combo Points for Eviscerate.

Sap makes pulls of 2 enemies safe. Sap the second humanoid from Stealth, kill the first enemy, then fight the Sapped enemy. Sap does not end Stealth in Forever, so you can also Sap an enemy and go past it.

The next abilities give you more ways to start and control a fight. Kick at 12 stops a cast, and Gouge also stops one. Garrote at 14 and Ambush at 18 are openers from Stealth. Feint at 16 decreases your threat in a group. Level 18 also gives you **Poised**, a new ability of Forever. Use it before your most expensive ability, because it makes that ability cost 10 less.

Your first dungeons open at level 13. Quests give much more XP than dungeon enemies, so do each dungeon for its quests. In a group, your job is damage, Sap on the target that the tank marks, and Kick on casters. Sap first, then let the tank pull.

The developers say that levels 1 to 20 take about 20 hours. At level 14, start the scavenger hunt for the Cozy Sleeping Bag. It gives an XP buff and rested XP. See [Leveling](../../characters/leveling.md) and [Camping](../../systems/camping.md).

| Level | You train | Why it is important |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | **Slice and Dice, Sap, Sprint** | More attack speed, your crowd control and your escape |
| 10 | **Dual Wield** | A weapon in each hand. Method gives level 10 |
| 12 | **Kick** | Stops a cast. The fastest interrupt in a group |
| 14 | **Garrote, Expose Armor** | A bleed opener. Expose Armor is equal to 5 Sunder Armors in Forever |
| 16 | **Feint, Lockpicking** | Feint decreases your threat. Lockpicking opens lockboxes, chests and some doors |
| 18 | **Ambush** | A large opener from Stealth. It needs a dagger |
| 18 | **Poised** | New. Your next Energy ability costs 10 less |

**Dungeons in this chapter**

- [Hall of Thanes](../../content/dungeons/hall-of-thanes.md) **13–20**{ .lvl }: New. It is in Ironforge
- Ragefire Chasm **13–18**{ .lvl }: It is in Orgrimmar
- [Ruins of Lordaeron](../../content/dungeons/ruins-of-lordaeron.md) **15–22**{ .lvl }: New. It is in Tirisfal Glades
- Wailing Caverns **15–24**{ .lvl }
- The Deadmines **17–26**{ .lvl }: Many humanoids for Sap and Pick Pocket

??? example "Macros for Sap, Kick and Ambush"

    ```
    #showtooltip Sap
    /cleartarget
    /targetenemy
    /cast Sap
    ```

    ```
    #showtooltip Kick
    /cast [@focus,exists,harm] Kick; Kick
    ```

    ```
    #showtooltip Ambush
    /cast Pick Pocket
    /cast Ambush
    ```

    The third macro uses Pick Pocket on the target and then opens the fight. Sap does not end Stealth. Thus you can Sap 1 enemy, then use Pick Pocket and Ambush on a different enemy. Forever uses a modern client, so conditions such as `@focus` probably work. Blizzard has not published a list of macro limits.

!!! warning "Caution"
    In a dungeon, do not attack before the tank. Use Feint before your threat becomes a problem, not after.

!!! tip "Tip"
    Lockpicking starts at level 16. Practice it on the lockboxes from Pick Pocket. Warcraft Tavern recommends 80 Lockpicking for the poison quest at level 20.

## Levels 20–29: Poisons, Vanish and Shank { #l20 }

Level 20 starts the **poison quest**. It teaches the Poisons skill and the recipe for Thistle Tea. The steps for each faction are below. Warcraft Tavern recommends that you wait until level 22 and have 80 Lockpicking. At 22 you have Vanish and Distract, and the quest is much easier with them.

Poisons change your equipment more than your rotation. You put 1 poison on each weapon. In Forever, a poison stays on the weapon together with a sharpening stone or a weightstone. Instant Poison comes from the quest. Crippling Poison comes at 20 and Mind-numbing Poison at 24. Buy the reagents from a Shady Dealer.

Rupture at level 20 is a bleed finishing move for enemies that live a long time. Vanish at level 22 is your best escape. It puts you back in Stealth in the middle of a fight. Carry Flash Powder for it (Classic-era reagent). Cheap Shot at level 26 is a stun opener. It gives you some seconds of free damage at the start of each fight.

At level 24 you train **Shank**, the second new ability of Forever. Shank costs 5 Energy, hits with your off-hand weapon and gives 15 Energy back. Its cooldown is 10 seconds. It gives more Energy than it costs, so use it each time it is ready, in each spec.

2 rewards start in these levels. Give your library books to the book collector of your faction. The Leveling page says that 10 books open a quest at level 20. Level 25 gives the *Rogue: Novice* Legacy Point. Only the first Rogue on your account gets it. See [Legacy](../../characters/legacy.md).

| Level | You train | Why it is important |
|---|---|---|
| 20 | **Rupture** | A bleed finishing move. Use it on enemies that live longer than 15 seconds |
| 20 | **Poisons: Instant Poison, Crippling Poison** | Instant Poison comes from the poison quest. Crippling Poison slows the target |
| 22 | **Vanish, Distract** | Vanish puts you back in Stealth. Distract turns enemies away from you |
| 24 | **Shank** | New. 5 Energy, a 10 second cooldown, and 15 Energy back |
| 24 | **Mind-numbing Poison** | Makes the casts of the target slower |

    3. Turn in the quest. You learn **Poisons** and the recipe for **Thistle Tea**.
    4. The chest gives you a debuff. The quest *The Touch of Zanzil* removes it. Anti-Venom also removes it.

    3. Go to Serge Hinott in Hillsbrad Foothills for *Hinott's Assistance*. You learn **Poisons** and the recipe for **Thistle Tea**.
    4. Warcraft Tavern says that the Horde quest is much more difficult than the Alliance quest.

## Levels 30–39: Kidney Shot, Blind and Deadly Poison { #l30 }

These levels complete your control. Kidney Shot at level 30 is a stun that uses Combo Points. It also stops a cast when Kick is on cooldown. Blind at level 34 removes 1 enemy from the fight for some seconds. With Sap, Gouge, Kidney Shot and Blind, a pull of 3 enemies is possible but not easy.

Deadly Poison comes at level 30 and Wound Poison at level 32. The usual setup is Instant Poison on the main hand and Deadly Poison on the off hand. Warcraft Tavern says that Deadly Poison has a 30% chance to apply at each rank. Put new poisons on your weapons before each dungeon.

Your 21st talent point comes at level 30. It gives Blade Flurry, Venom or Hemorrhage on the 3 leveling paths. The Talents add-on tells you how the fight changes on your path.

The new battleground Battle for the Darkspear Islands opens at level 30. A Rogue takes bases that have no defender and stops enemy flag carriers.

Start to save gold now. At level 40 you need 100g for riding, and dual spec costs 50g. All ranks of your abilities from level 1 to 60 cost about 206g. Lockpicking and Pick Pocket help you pay for them. The Leveling page says that 20 library books open the second book quest at level 35.

## Levels 40–49: Adrenaline Rush, Mutilate or Death Mark { #l40 }

Level 40 gives few new abilities, but it changes how you play. You can buy riding for 100g, and the price includes your first mount. You can buy dual spec for 50g from your class trainer. You train Safe Fall, which decreases fall damage.

Level 40 is also your 31st talent point. Each leveling build takes the last talent of its tree here: Adrenaline Rush, Mutilate or Death Mark. The Talents add-on describes the change for your path.

With dual spec, keep your solo build in 1 spec. The usual second spec is Subtlety for PvP, or Combat for dungeons if you level with a different tree. You can change the spec outside of combat at no cost. To respec, go to a class trainer in a capital. The Leveling page says that respec fees decrease to 1g over time.

Shank rank 2 comes at level 44. It gives 25 Energy back in place of 15. From this level, Shank is a large part of your Energy.

The new zone Riverglades is for levels 35 to 45. Both factions quest there, so expect world PvP. Level 45 gives the *Rogue: Experienced* Legacy Point. The Leveling page says that 25 library books open the third book quest at level 45.

| Level | You train | Why it is important |
|---|---|---|
| 40 | **Safe Fall** | Decreases fall damage |
| 44 | **Shank (rank 2)** | 25 Energy back in place of 15 |

**Dungeons in this chapter**

- [Krol'dok Stronghold](../../content/dungeons/kroldok-stronghold.md) **40–47**{ .lvl }: New. It is in Riverglades. It drops a fist weapon with Agility
- Uldaman **41–51**{ .lvl }

## Levels 50–59: Hit, Lockpicking and the last dungeons { #l50 }

The last 10 levels have the most dungeons. Items from the new dungeons have stats for a specific class and spec. Reportedly, each boss in the new dungeons drops a blue item. Start to collect your level 60 equipment here.

You have the last talent of your main tree. Most of the points from 50 to 60 go into a second or a third tree. The Combat and Subtlety builds take Ruthlessness and Lethality from Assassination. The Assassination build takes Puncturing Wounds and Precision from Combat.

Hit becomes important, because dungeon bosses are 2 or 3 levels above you. A miss costs Energy and Combo Points. Keep items with hit from this level.

Lockpicking is useful in Blackrock Depths and Dire Maul. You can open boxes and some doors for your group. Keep your skill near the maximum for your level.

No source lists new abilities for these levels. You train higher ranks of the abilities that you have.

## Level 60: Hit, weapons and the Onyxia attunement { #l60 }

At level 60 you get the *Rogue: Master* Legacy Point. The riding skill for a fast mount costs 1,000g. Mount Hyjal opens through Darkwhisper Gorge in Winterspring. Get reputation there, because it gives the patterns for the crafted Tier 1 items. Fan sources name the faction Guardians of Hyjal, and the name is not confirmed.

All 3 launch raids open on 9 December 2026. Use the time before that date for 4 tasks. Decide your spec and get the weapons for it. Get hit on your items. Collect poisons and consumables. Do the attunement for Onyxia's Lair.

No source has a tested hit value for Forever. Warcraft Tavern gives a 9% miss chance against a raid boss. A search result from Icy Veins gives 8% for special attacks, but the Codex was not able to read the page. Precision gives 3%, so a build with Precision needs 5% to 6% from items.

The attunement gives the Drakefire Amulet. Start the chain at level 58 to 60. It is not account-wide, so each character must do it. The chain needs the blood of General Drakkisath in Upper Blackrock Spire.

Respec to a level 60 build now. The Talents add-on gives the builds of your path. Tier lists from before the launch put Assassination and Combat in the highest damage tier. No player has tested the specs in the raids.

The Equipment add-on gives the items to look for. The lists of level 60 items are not final, because many items still have hidden stats. Dungeons also drop a currency, Tarnished Undermine Real. Pix Xizzix in Stranglethorn Vale sells level 60 items for it. Honor buys blue PvP items.

## Raids and PvP: The 3 launch raids and battlegrounds { #raid }

**The Barrow Deeps** has 10 players. It is the old prison of Illidan. A small raid needs your Kick and your stuns on the enemies before the bosses. Many enemies are night elves and Druids of the Claw. See [The Barrow Deeps](../../content/raids/barrow-deeps.md).

**Hyjal Summit** has 20 players and 13 bosses on routes that branch. Expect many groups of enemies between the bosses. Blade Flurry is very strong there. See [Hyjal Summit](../../content/raids/hyjal-summit.md).

**Onyxia's Lair** has 40 players. Stay behind her, and move away from the tail and from Deep Breath. Fire resistance helps. See [Onyxia's Lair](../../content/raids/onyxias-lair.md).

In each raid, your jobs are damage and threat control. Use Feint before you need it. Use Expose Armor only if no Warrior applies Sunder Armor, and agree on it with your raid. Put new poisons on your weapons after each wipe. World buffs do not work in raids. The Chronoboon Displacer removes them in instances.

In PvP, each other class prepares for a fight against a Rogue. Subtlety is the usual spec. Sap a defender from Stealth and take the base, or prepare a full opener. Distract tells players that a Rogue is near. Human Perception lets players see you earlier. The Legacy talent High Alert does the same, but not in battlegrounds.

See [PvP](../../content/pvp/index.md) and [Battlegrounds](../../content/pvp/battlegrounds.md) for the rules and the rewards.

| Battleground | Your job |
|---|---|
| Warsong Gulch (10 against 10) | Kill the enemy flag carrier and defend your flag. Sap the escort and use Kidney Shot on the carrier |
| Arathi Basin (15 against 15) | Take bases that have no defender. Sap a defender who is alone and click the flag |
| [Darkspear Islands](../../content/pvp/battlegrounds.md#darkspear-islands) (15 against 15, levels 30 to 60) | The same job on 4 bases. Also stop the carrier of the central flag |
| Alterac Valley (40 against 40) | Kill reinforcements and take towers from Stealth |

| Enemy in PvP | What you do |
|---|---|
| Warrior | Kite with Crippling Poison. Keep Evasion and Gouge for the time when the Warrior has Rage |
| Mage | Open with Cheap Shot, then Kidney Shot. Kick Polymorph or Frostbolt. Use a trinket or a racial against Frost Nova |
| Priest | Use Mind-numbing Poison. Keep Vanish or Will of the Forsaken for Psychic Scream |
| Warlock | Open on the Warlock, not on the pet. Kick the Fear |
| Hunter | Get to the Hunter quickly. Use Vanish to get away from a Freezing Trap |
| Paladin | Use Wound Poison against heals. Do burst damage when Divine Shield is on cooldown. Paladins can tank in Forever, so look at the spec first |
| Druid | Kick heals and Entangling Roots. Use Crippling Poison when the Druid changes to Cat Form or Travel Form |
| Shaman | Continue to attack through Grounding Totem. Kick heals |
| Rogue | The Rogue who opens first usually wins. Use Distract to find the enemy |

!!! hardcore "Hardcore"
    [Hardcore](../../about/hardcore.md) comes in winter 2026/27. The Rogue is one of the safest classes there, because Vanish, Sprint, Evasion, Gouge and Blind stop a bad pull. Do not fight 2 enemies if you cannot use Sap or Gouge on one of them. Use Stealth to go past enemies that you do not need to kill.
    
    Always carry bandages and Healing Potions. Carry Flash Powder and Blinding Powder also, unless you have the Legacy talent Reagent Economy. The Legacy talents Thrill of Adventure and Field Medicine give more healing. Stoneform of the Dwarf and Escape Artist of the Gnome are strong racials for Hardcore.

<p class="fc-addon__label" markdown>:material-star-four-points: <span class="fc-addon__kind">Talents add-on</span> <strong>Combat</strong> [Rogue talents](talents.md#level-60-builds){ .fc-addon__more } <a class="fc-addon__change" href="#picks">Change</a></p>

Combat is the default raid spec, and it works with swords, axes, maces and fist weapons. No source has tested which weapon type is best with Hack and Slash. Blade Flurry is very strong on the groups of enemies in Hyjal Summit.

<div class="priority" markdown>

1. Open from Stealth with **Cheap Shot** or **Garrote**. On a boss, open with **Sinister Strike**.
2. Use **Slice and Dice** at 1 or 2 Combo Points. Keep it active.
3. Use **Shank** each time it is ready. With Flash of Steel, it is a large source of Energy.
4. Use **Sinister Strike** to add Combo Points. Use **Poised** before an ability with a high cost.
5. Use **Eviscerate** at 5 Combo Points.
6. Use **Blade Flurry** when 2 or more targets are near you. Use **Adrenaline Rush** for burst.

</div>

<div class="fc-addon" data-pick="talents" data-value="assassination" markdown>

<p class="fc-addon__label" markdown>:material-star-four-points: <span class="fc-addon__kind">Talents add-on</span> <strong>Assassination (daggers)</strong> [Rogue talents](talents.md#level-60-builds){ .fc-addon__more } <a class="fc-addon__change" href="#picks">Change</a></p>

A Mutilate crit gives 3 Combo Points with Seal Fate. Use Shank each time it is ready, because it starts Venom. The build has no cooldown for 2 targets, so Combat is better in dungeons.

<div class="priority" markdown>

1. Open with **Garrote** if the target lives a long time. If not, open with **Ambush**.
2. Use **Slice and Dice** and keep it active.
3. Use **Shank** each time it is ready. It starts **Venom**.
4. Use **Mutilate** to add Combo Points. A Sinister Strike can add the last point for less Energy.
5. Use **Eviscerate** at 4 or 5 Combo Points. Use **Cold Blood** on an Eviscerate with 5 Combo Points.
6. On a boss, use **Expose Armor** first if no Warrior applies Sunder Armor.

</div>


</div>

<div class="fc-addon" data-pick="talents" data-value="subtlety" markdown>

<p class="fc-addon__label" markdown>:material-star-four-points: <span class="fc-addon__kind">Talents add-on</span> <strong>Subtlety (openers and PvP)</strong> [Rogue talents](talents.md#level-60-builds){ .fc-addon__more } <a class="fc-addon__change" href="#picks">Change</a></p>

In PvP, the opener decides the fight. Preparation gives a second Vanish, Blind and Cold Blood. In raids, Icy Veins says that Subtlety can possibly become the strongest spec. It is not confirmed that each boss can bleed.

<div class="priority" markdown>

1. Use **Premeditation**, then open with **Ambush**. With Dirty Deeds, you can open with **Garrote** from the front.
2. Use **Slice and Dice**. In a long fight, also keep **Rupture** active.
3. Use **Hemorrhage** to add Combo Points. It increases your Rupture and Garrote damage.
4. Use **Ghostly Strike** each time it is ready, then **Ambush** or **Garrote**.
5. Use **Shank** each time it is ready. With Boot Knife, it adds 2 Combo Points.
6. Use **Death Mark** for burst while Slice and Dice is active.
7. For more burst in PvP: Gouge, then Vanish, then Ambush. Then use **Preparation**.

</div>

## Professions { #professions }

A Rogue needs leather armor and armor kits for PvE, or explosives for PvP. Method recommends Skinning and Mining for gold while you level. Change Mining to Leatherworking or Blacksmithing when you have enough gold.

| Rank | Professions | Use them for |
|---|---|---|
| **S**{ .tier .tier-s } | [Skinning](../../professions/skinning.md) + [Leatherworking](../../professions/leatherworking.md) | Leather armor, armor kits and many new rare patterns. The Camp Chair gives 2% crit at a camp |
| **A**{ .tier .tier-a } | [Engineering](../../professions/engineering.md) + [Mining](../../professions/mining.md) | Bombs, grenades and nets for PvP. Gnomes have 20% fewer device failures |
| **B**{ .tier .tier-b } | [Herbalism](../../professions/herbalism.md) + [Alchemy](../../professions/alchemy.md) | Gold, Agility elixirs and Free Action Potions |

[Blacksmithing](../../professions/blacksmithing.md) makes weapons, sharpening stones and the new fist weapons. [First Aid](../../professions/first-aid.md) is the most important secondary skill, because you have no healing. It now also makes Healing Potions. [Cooking](../../professions/cooking.md) makes Thistle Tea. Compare all classes in [Best professions by class](../../characters/professions.md#best-professions-by-class).

Professions give no passive stats in Forever. Each profession gives a [camp feature](../../systems/camping.md). Skinning and Leatherworking give the Camp Chair and the Camp Tent (rested XP). Engineering and Mining give the Reagent Bot and the Lodestone (melee Attack Power). Crafting professions give Legacy Points at skill 150, 225 and 300.

**Lockpicking** and **Poisons** are class skills. Lockpicking starts at level 16, and it is a good source of gold. Poisons start with the quest at level 20.

## Common mistakes

- You use the starting dagger too long on the Combat path.
- You do not put new poisons on your weapons after a long dungeon.
- You open on an elite enemy when Vanish and Sprint are on cooldown.
- You let Slice and Dice end.
- You do not use Shank each time it is ready.
- You do not increase Lockpicking, which is a good source of gold.
- You use Blade Flurry near a Sapped or Polymorphed enemy.

## FAQ

??? question "Which path is best for leveling: Combat, Assassination or Subtlety?"
    Combat. It works with each weapon type, and it needs no position behind the target. Assassination is good if you have 2 good daggers. Subtlety is best in a group.

??? question "Which race is best?"
    All races are good. For Alliance PvP, guides recommend Human or Gnome. For Alliance PvE, they recommend Night Elf or Human. For the Horde, they recommend Orc for PvE and PvP. Undead is the usual Horde PvP selection.

??? question "Is Venom a finishing move?"
    No. In the launch client, Venom is a passive talent that starts when you use Shank. The deep dive of Blizzard from October described a finishing move for 25 Energy.

??? question "Must I still time my abilities to the Energy tick?"
    No. Energy regenerates smoothly in Forever.

??? question "Do I lose my Combo Points when I change targets?"
    No. They stay on the first target, and you can go back and use them.

??? question "Why does Mutilate not work?"
    It needs a dagger in each hand.

??? question "Does a poison replace my sharpening stone?"
    No. In Forever, a poison and a stone work on the same weapon.

??? question "Is Shank useful?"
    Yes. It costs 5 Energy and gives 15 Energy back, or 25 at rank 2. Each tree also has a talent that makes it stronger.

??? question "Can I Sap an enemy and stay in Stealth?"
    Yes. This is new in Forever.

??? question "Is Expose Armor useful?"
    Only if your group has no Warrior who applies Sunder Armor. It is equal to 5 Sunder Armors, so it is good for groups without a Warrior.

??? question "Is the last Subtlety talent Thousand Cuts or Death Mark?"
    It is Death Mark. The launch client has no Thousand Cuts. The deep dive of Blizzard from October described Thousand Cuts, and older guides still name it.
