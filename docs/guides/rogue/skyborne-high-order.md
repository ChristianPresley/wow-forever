# Skyborne Rogue (High Order)

!!! tip "Add-on"
    Read with the [Rogue guide](index.md). This page covers only what changes for an Alliance Skyborne.

!!! warning "Paid race"
    Skyborne need the **Skyborne Heroic Pack** or a higher edition. See [Editions](../../about/editions.md).

## Why this combo

**NEW in Forever.** The Skyborne are a Forever-only elven race descended from exiles of Eldre'Thalas, and the High Order is their Alliance branch. Rogue is one of five High Order classes. You get **Wind Blessed** (permanent haste), **Walk on Air** (a 10 s controlled glide) and a starting zone, [Zephras Isle](../../world/zephras-isle.md), that only Skyborne play through.

**Best for:** players who want something new, steady haste for poison and Combat builds, and the Galestrider mount from day one of riding.

## Racials in practice

| Racial | Type | Rogue rating | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Walk on Air** | Active | ★★★ | A 10 s controlled glide. Cross gaps, drop off cliffs safely, take shortcuts while leveling. Rogues get Safe Fall at 40, but gliding is also a route-finder and escape in world PvP |
| **Read Ley Line** | Active | ★★ | +50% regen for 15 s, +100% while *Attuned* at a Ley Line. It is unclear whether it affects Energy; for health regen it shortens downtime |
| **Wind Blessed** | Passive | ★★★★ | Permanent haste. More swings means more poison and Hack and Slash procs |
| **Elemental Insight** | Passive | ★★ | +5% damage against Elementals. Zephras Isle is full of them, and some dungeons too |

!!! note "Conflicts"
    - **Wind Blessed:** sources give 1%, 2% or 3% haste. The latest official notes say **2%**.
    - **Walk on Air** cooldown: 1.5 or 2 minutes, depending on the source.
    - Whether **Read Ley Line** boosts Energy regen is *unconfirmed*.

## Starting zone: Zephras Isle (1–12)

Every Skyborne starts in **Thendal Village** in the northwest of Zephras Isle, a floating island in Skywall. Both factions share the zone but are phased apart. The story takes you to about level 12–14. Full story: [Zephras Isle](../../world/zephras-isle.md).

| Level | Where | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | **Thendal Village** | *Coming of Age*, *Harmony in Balance*, *The Cirrusfly Queen*, *Harvesting Windstones* (gathering profession voucher), *Falling With Style* (your first Walk on Air), *The Anchors of Zephras* |
| 3–4 | Thendal Village | High Order opener *Reading the Leylines* |
| 4–7 | Road to **Shen'dar Village** | Al'Aketh thugs under Malduko Cloudcrash. Ride the **Blessing of Zephras** tornadoes for +40% speed. **The Great Outdoors** teaches [camping](../../systems/camping.md). Bandits, hippogryphs and Prideclaws; *WANTED: Vulgara the Insatiable* |
| 7–8 | **Falaath Village** | *Secret Agent Elf*: infiltrate the cult in disguise. Perfect Rogue flavor. Kill Commander Cyclas and Commander Aanders |
| 8–9 | **Valanaar** | Hand the Shadowsong signet to High Elder Talaanis Shadowsong and meet **Supreme Magister Elaadrin Evengale**. Valanaar has the class trainers; train here |
| 9–11 | Gustberry Lowlands | High Order chain *The Missing Scholar* (Shriekling Den), Windfield Orchard. `/sing` at the frogs for the *Musical Gustjumper* pet |
| 10 | Valanaar | **Slice and Dice**, **Sap**, **Sprint**; first talent point |
| 10–12 | Shadowgale Forest | High Order chain to Index Esoteria. Nightclaw druid quests (*Mercy Falls on Deaf Ears* is a repeatable reputation quest) |
| 12–14 | Shrine of Akir | Assault the cult, *Confront Lorthuna*, help defeat **Rohash**. Close with *The Fate of Zephras* and *What Comes Next* |

- Lots of Al'Aketh cultists are humanoids: **Pick Pocket** them.
- **Elemental Insight** gives +5% damage on the island's elementals.
- Exact trainer NPC names in Valanaar haven't been documented. Ask a guard.

## Route to 60 from Dalaran

The High Order airship docks at the east Skydock and flies to north **Dalaran** in the Alterac Mountains. Dalaran is a level 30–40 zone, so don't quest there at 12.

| Level | Zone | Hub / flight path |
|---|---|---|
| 12 | Dalaran → walk south to **Southshore** (Hillsbrad) | Careful: Alterac and Hillsbrad are higher level and contested |
| 12–20 | Take the new **Southshore–Auberdine** boat to **Darkshore**, or Southshore–Menethil to reach Loch Modan or Westfall | Auberdine · Thelsamar · Sentinel Hill |
| 20–22 | Poison quest at **SI:7**, Stormwind | Stormwind harbor (boat from Auberdine) |
| 20–30 | Redridge, Duskwood, Wetlands, Ashenvale | Lakeshire · Darkshire · Menethil · Astranaar |
| 26–35 | Hillsbrad and the [City of Dalaran](../../content/dungeons/city-of-dalaran.md) | Southshore. High Order Skyborne are allied with the **Kirin Tor** |
| 30–40 | Arathi, Stranglethorn, Desolace, Dustwallow | Refuge Pointe · Booty Bay · Theramore |
| 35–45 | **Riverglades** | Farholde Keep |
| 40–50 | Badlands, Swamp, Tanaris, Feralas, Hinterlands | Gadgetzan · Feathermoon · Aerie Peak |
| 50–60 | Searing Gorge, Burning Steppes, Azshara, Un'Goro, Felwood, Plaguelands, Winterspring | Everlook, then [Mount Hyjal](../../world/zones.md#mount-hyjal) |

**Class trainers:** Valanaar on Zephras Isle, then **Stormwind's Old Town** on the mainland. Whether Dalaran has a rogue trainer is *unconfirmed*.

## Faction notes

- **Poison quest (20):** Master Mathias Shaw, SI:7, Stormwind. See the [main guide](index.md#the-poison-quest-level-20).
- **Library books:** Garion Wendell, Mage Quarter, Stormwind.
- **Mount:** **Galestriders** at **Fairweather Stables**, Zephras Isle (53.8, 81.3), in Umber, Stormy and Empyrean, with Swift versions. High Order reputation gives 5/10/15/20% off at Friendly → Exalted. Riding still costs 100g at 40.
- **Reputation:** High Order. Other Alliance races can earn Galestriders at Exalted with it. The Nightclaw druids have a separate reputation.
- **Kirin Tor:** the High Order are allies of Dalaran. Kirin Tor reputation is Alliance-only.
- **Language:** Darnassian and Common. You can talk to Windshaper Skyborne in Darnassian across factions.
- **Looks:** HD models only at launch, with two body types; feathers, tattoos and the vivid-blue "Sky-Touched Blessing" skin. See [Races](../../characters/races.md#skyborne).

## Verdict

**A fresh, solid Rogue with steady haste.** Wind Blessed is a good passive for poison and Combat builds, Walk on Air is fun and useful, and the Zephras story (including a spy chapter) suits a Rogue. Human, Gnome and Night Elf have stronger PvP or burst racials; pick High Order for the new race, the starting experience and the Galestrider.
