## Why this combo

!!! success "New in Forever"
    Dwarf Shaman is a **new race/class combination** and the **only way to play a Shaman on the Alliance**. Blizzard lists it with Undead Paladin as the combos that let both factions play all 9 classes.

Dwarves bring Shamans into Alliance groups for the first time in a Classic-style game. Thus Windfury Totem, Strength of Earth and the raid-wide resistance totems are now available to Alliance raids. Every Alliance guild will want some Shamans. There is only one Alliance option, so the question is not *which race*. The question is if Dwarf racials are good for your spec. They are: **+1% hit** helps every spec, and **Stoneform** is one of the best PvP racials in the game.

## Starting zone: Coldridge Valley to Kharanos (1–12)

!!! note "Trainer names"
    Trainer names below come from Warcraft Tavern's guides. The trainer list is partly from an automated summary of that page, so check the names in game.

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **1–6**{ .lvl } Coldridge Valley

    - You start at **Anvilmar**. Your Shaman trainer, **Teo Hammerstorm**, is inside Anvilmar (about 28.8, 66.2).
    - Kill troll whelps and wolves. Keep Rockbiter Weapon active. Open with Lightning Bolt and finish with melee.
    - **Level 4: *Call of Earth*.** Teo asks for two Iceclaw Bear Pendants from Frostmane Troll Whelps (near 27.3, 79.6). He gives you **Earth Sapta**. Follow the path with rubble on it (from about 25.8, 69.3) to the Earth Spirit Stone (25.0, 62.4). Drink the Sapta and defeat the Minor Manifestation of Earth. Return the Rough Quartz to Teo for the **Earth Totem** and Stoneskin Totem.

2.  **6–12**{ .lvl } Kharanos

    - Reports say that a second trainer, **Ingrid Dunwald**, is in Kharanos.
    - Train Earthbind (6) and Stoneclaw and Lightning Shield (8).
    - Do quests near Kharanos. Then do quests at the Frostmane trolls and the Gnomeregan outskirts.
    - **Level 10: *Call of Fire*.** A trainer sends you to **Bruegs Kindleborn**. His path starts behind a farm near the Loch Modan tunnel in Dun Morogh (about 86.7, 47.0). He gives you a Torch of the Dormant Flame for **Braldir Ashmantle** in Loch Modan (32.0, 66.3).
        - Braldir wants Fire Tar from Tunnel Rat Geomancers in Silver Stream Mine and a Reagent Pouch from Stonesplinter Seers in Stonesplinter Valley. These troggs are up to level 14 and are in groups. Get some more levels first, or bring a friend.
        - Drink the **Fire Sapta** at the Fire Spirit Stone above Braldir (31.9, 64.5). Kill the Minor Manifestation of Fire. Light your torch at the Brazier of the Dormant Flame, and bring it back to Bruegs. Reward: **Fire Totem** and Searing Totem.
    - **First talent point** at 10. Enhancement starts with Thundering Strikes. Elemental starts with its first-row damage talents.

</div>

The Fire quest sends you to Loch Modan, so plan to finish it at 11–12 when you do quests there.

## Route to 60 from Ironforge

**Class trainer city:** Ironforge. Warcraft Tavern lists **Eldrun Stormbreaker** in the "Forlorn Cavern" area of Ironforge. It lists **no Shaman trainer in Stormwind or Darnassus**, so you must return to Ironforge (or Kharanos and Coldridge) to train.

!!! warning "Plan your training trips"
    This is the largest practical difference from Horde Shamans, who have about 15 trainers in Kalimdor and Stonard. You can go to Ironforge from Stormwind through the Deeprun Tram. On even levels, remember this when you set your hearthstone.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Mount:** ram. At 40, train riding from **Ultham Ironhorn** and buy your first ram from **Veron Amberstill**. Both are near Amberstill Ranch in Dun Morogh (Classic locations). At Honored with Ironforge, you get a 10% discount on riding and racial mounts.
- **Reputation:** Ironforge. The mounts of other races need Exalted.
- **Dalaran is friendly to you.** You can earn Kirin Tor reputation and do the City of Dalaran dungeon. Horde Shamans cannot do this.
- **Darkspear Islands:** you fight for the **Theramore Expeditionary Force**. Queue at Northwatch or in the capitals.
- **Alliance PvP vendors** are in Champion's Hall, Stormwind.
- **Group value:** Alliance has never had Shaman totems before. Expect raid leaders to ask for Windfury in melee groups and Mana Spring for healers. At first, few Alliance players know how totems work. Tell your group which totems you put down.
- **Lore:** Forever has not published a story about how Dwarves started to use shamanism. Neither Warcraft Tavern guide mentions the Earthen Ring or any Dwarf shaman lore. Treat all lore claims as *unconfirmed*.

## Verdict

<div class="verdict" markdown>

**The best Alliance Shaman, because it is the only one, and it is also a good one.** Artisan's Precision helps every spec get enough hit sooner. Big Game Hunter makes leveling faster. Stoneform stops Rogues and Warriors in PvP. The main disadvantage is that most Shaman trainers are in Dun Morogh and Ironforge, so plan your training trips. In raids, you are one of a small number of Alliance players who can give totems. This makes you very valuable.

</div>
