## Item rules in Forever { #rules }

- Hit is 1 stat for melee, ranged attacks and spells (Method.gg). Crit is also 1 stat.
- Tidal Focus gives 5% hit, and the tooltip does not limit it to spells or to melee. All damage builds take it.
- A raid boss needs 16% spell hit (Warcraft Tavern stats guide) or 17% (Warcraft Tavern Shaman guide). Elemental thus needs about 11% to 12% from items.
- Warcraft Tavern gives 8% hit and 6.5% Expertise as the Enhancement targets. Only 1 source gives the Expertise value. Expertise replaces weapon skill.
- Strength gives a Shaman 2 attack power for each point. Mental Dexterity gives attack power equal to 150% of your Intellect at 3/3.
- In launch week, Intellect and spell power gave Enhancement more than Strength. Blizzard planned tuning for 10 November to make Strength the best stat. Keep your Strength items.
- Weapon imbues last 60 minutes, and they stack with sharpening stones, weightstones and oils.
- No source found haste on items in the beta. The stat lists of Warcraft Tavern still name haste, so read that entry as theory.
- Healing items also give spell damage (Method.gg).
- World buffs do not work in dungeons and raids. Consumables are thus more important than in Classic.
- For an emergency tank role, Method.gg says that you need a one-hand weapon and a shield. Spirit Weapons gives +30% threat while Rockbiter Weapon is active.
- Resistance totems cover the full raid. A resistance of 315 gives the maximum of 75% (Warcraft Tavern).
