## <span class="fc-ch__lv">Before level 1</span> Make your Shaman { #start .fc-ch data-toc-label="Start · Make your Shaman" data-label="Start · Make your Shaman" }

<aside class="infobox infobox--row" data-class="shaman" markdown>
<p class="infobox-title">Shaman at a glance</p>

| | |
|---|---|
| **Roles** | Melee damage (Enhancement), ranged damage (Elemental), healer (Restoration) |
| **Armor** | **Leather**{ .pill .pill--leather }, then **Mail**{ .pill .pill--mail } from level 40. Shields |

| **Totems** | 4 elements. 1 totem of each element at a time |
| **Also called** | Sham · Shammy · Ele (Elemental) · Enh or Enhance (Enhancement) · Resto shaman |

A Shaman can do 3 jobs. Enhancement hits with a slow two-hand weapon and adds Shocks. Elemental casts Lightning Bolt from range. Restoration heals with Healing Wave and Chain Heal. The class has no tank spec. In Forever, Blizzard made Elemental and Enhancement full damage specs.

Totems are the most important tools of the class. You have 4 elements: Earth, Fire, Water and Air. You can have 1 totem of each element at a time. A totem gives a buff to your group, heals it, cleanses it or attacks enemies.

In Forever, totems need much less work than in Classic. They cost less time to put down, they last longer, and 1 cast puts down a full set. The Alliance can also play the class now, as a Dwarf.

You make 3 decisions before level 1: your race, your talent path and your equipment set. The add-ons below describe each option. The race decides your faction and your start zone, and you cannot change it. You can change the talents and the equipment later, so select the path that you want for leveling.

<div class="proscons" markdown>
<div class="pros" markdown>

Strengths

- Group buffs that each group wants: Windfury, Flametongue, Strength of Earth and Mana Spring Totem
- Resistance totems now cover the full raid
- Much utility: Purge, Earth Shock interrupts, Grounding Totem, Tremor Totem and Earthbind Totem
- Each spec can heal in an emergency, and Reincarnation can save a group after a wipe
- Enhancement levels quickly and uses little mana

</div>
<div class="cons" markdown>

Weaknesses

- High mana use, most of all for Elemental during leveling
- Totems do not move with you. Totemic Call at level 22 brings them to you
- No tank spec. Blizzard removed Shield Specialization
- No dual wield in the launch client
- You need 4 class quests before you can use all totems

</div>

## <span class="fc-ch__lv">Levels 1–9</span> Rockbiter and the Earth totem { #l1 .fc-ch data-toc-label="1–9 · Rockbiter" data-label="1–9 · Rockbiter" }

You start with Lightning Bolt and Rockbiter Weapon. Rockbiter Weapon is a weapon imbue: a buff on your weapon that increases your melee damage. Put it on your weapon and renew it each hour. Start each fight with Lightning Bolt from maximum range, then hit the enemy with your weapon.

At level 4 you train Earth Shock, an instant spell that also stops a cast. At level 4 your trainer also gives the class quest *Call of Earth*. You drink Earth Sapta at a spirit stone and defeat a Minor Manifestation of Earth. The reward is the Earth Totem and your first totem spell, Stoneskin Totem.

You need 1 class quest for each element, at levels 4, 10, 20 and 30. In Classic, each quest gave a totem item that you kept in your bags. Warcraft Tavern confirms only the quests and their levels for Forever.

Earthbind Totem at level 6 slows all enemies near it. Stoneclaw Totem at level 8 pulls enemies away from you for some seconds. These 2 totems are your first escape tools. Lightning Shield at level 8 does damage to enemies that hit you.

A solo kill takes 10 to 15 seconds. All 3 specs fight in the same way before level 10: Lightning Bolt, Earth Shock, then melee. The developers say that levels 1 to 20 take about 20 hours.

| Level | You train | Why it is important |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Lightning Bolt, Rockbiter Weapon** | Your first damage spell and your first weapon imbue |
| 4 | **Earth Shock** | Instant damage. It also stops a cast |
| 4 | **Stoneskin Totem** | The reward of the Earth totem quest. Skyborne get the quest at level 3 (Warcraft Tavern) |
| 6 | **Earthbind Totem** | Slows enemies near the totem. Use it to run away |
| 8 | **Stoneclaw Totem, Lightning Shield** | Stoneclaw Totem takes enemies off you for some seconds |

!!! tip fc-inline "Tip"
    The game does not put new spells on your action bars. Open your spellbook after each visit to the trainer.

!!! hardcore fc-inline "Hardcore"
    Keep Stoneclaw Totem and Earthbind Totem on keys that you can reach quickly. If a second enemy comes, put down Stoneclaw Totem and run.

## <span class="fc-ch__lv">Levels 10–19</span> The Fire totem and your first talents { #l10 .fc-ch data-toc-label="10–19 · The Fire totem" data-label="10–19 · The Fire totem" }

At level 10 you get your first talent point, and your trainer gives the class quest *Call of Fire*. The quest has many steps. Its reward is the Fire Totem and Searing Totem. Do it immediately. Searing Totem attacks your target for you, and it is a larger gain than any item at this level.

Level 10 also gives Flame Shock, Flametongue Weapon and Strength of Earth Totem. Your pull now has a plan. Put down Searing Totem and Strength of Earth Totem, pull the enemy to the totems, and fight there.

Level 12 adds Purge, Ancestral Spirit and Fire Nova. Purge removes a buff from an enemy. Fire Nova is a burst of area damage (AoE) that comes from your Fire totem, so stand near the totem when you use it.

Your first dungeons open at level 13. Quests give much more XP than dungeon enemies, so do each dungeon for its quests. In a group, your totems are your most important job, so ask the other players which totems they need. Interrupt casters with Earth Shock, and Purge enemy buffs. Cure Poison at 16 and Tremor Totem at 18 are more group tools.

At level 14, start the scavenger hunt for the Cozy Sleeping Bag. It gives an XP buff and rested XP. See [Leveling](../../characters/leveling.md).

|---|---|---|

| 10 | **Flame Shock, Flametongue Weapon, Strength of Earth Totem** | A damage over time Shock, a second imbue and your first buff totem |
| 12 | **Fire Nova, Purge, Ancestral Spirit** | AoE from your Fire totem, buff removal and a resurrection |
| 16 | **Cure Poison** | A group job in dungeons |
| 18 | **Tremor Totem** | Removes Fear, charm and sleep from your group |

## <span class="fc-ch__lv">Levels 20–29</span> Ghost Wolf and the Water totem { #l20 .fc-ch data-toc-label="20–29 · Ghost Wolf" data-label="20–29 · Ghost Wolf" }

Level 20 is the most important level before 40. You train Ghost Wolf, Call of the Elements, Totemic Recall, Frost Shock, Frostbrand Weapon and Lesser Healing Wave. Your trainer also gives the class quest *Call of Water*.

Ghost Wolf is your travel form until you have a mount. Call of the Elements puts down a set of 4 totems with one 3 second cast. Totemic Recall removes your totems and refunds 25% of their mana. Use the 2 spells before and after each long fight.

*Call of Water* is the longest totem quest. Warcraft Tavern says that the Alliance version needs about 2 hours of travel. The reward is the Water Totem and Healing Stream Totem. Mana Spring Totem follows at level 26.

Totemic Call at level 22 moves your totems to you. In a dungeon, use it when the tank moves the enemies, and you do not put down the totems again. Cure Disease and Poison Cleansing Totem also come at 22. Use Poison Cleansing Totem against spiders and snakes. Magma Totem at 26 is your AoE Fire totem.

At 20, you can give 10 library books for the first book quest. No source gives the Shaman rewards. Reports name Garion Wendell in Stormwind and Owen Thadd in the Undercity as the quest NPCs. No source confirms that Shamans use these NPCs. Level 25 gives the *Shaman: Novice* Legacy Point to the first Shaman on your account. See [Legacy](../../characters/legacy.md).

| Level | You train | Why it is important |
|---|---|---|
| 20 | **Ghost Wolf** | Faster travel. An escape outdoors |
| 20 | **Call of the Elements, Totemic Recall** | A set of 4 totems in 1 cast, and a mana refund when you remove them |
| 20 | **Frost Shock, Frostbrand Weapon, Lesser Healing Wave** | A slow for kiting and a fast heal |
| 20 | **Healing Stream Totem** | The reward of the Water totem quest |
| 22 | **Totemic Call, Cure Disease, Poison Cleansing Totem** | Totemic Call moves your totems to you. It has a 30 second cooldown |
| 26 | **Magma Totem, Mana Spring Totem** | An AoE Fire totem, and mana for your group |

**Dungeons in this chapter**

- **20–30**{ .lvl } Shadowfang Keep
- **24–32**{ .lvl } Blackfathom Deeps
- **25–29**{ .lvl } The Stockade
- **26–33**{ .lvl } [Excavation Site: Wetlands](../../content/dungeons/excavation-site.md): New. It is short, with 3 bosses
- **28–35**{ .lvl } [City of Dalaran](../../content/dungeons/city-of-dalaran.md): New. Dalaran is hostile to the Horde

??? example "Macros for Earth Shock, mouseover heals and burst"

    ```
    #showtooltip Earth Shock
    /stopcasting
    /cast Earth Shock
    ```

    ```
    #showtooltip Lesser Healing Wave
    /cast [@mouseover,help,nodead][] Lesser Healing Wave
    ```

    ```
    #showtooltip Stormstrike
    /cast Blood Fury
    /cast Stormstrike
    ```

    The first macro stops your cast and interrupts the enemy. The second macro heals the player under your mouse pointer. The third macro is for an Orc with Stormstrike, which the Enhancement path gets at level 25. A Troll uses Berserking in its place.

    The client blocks add-ons such as WeakAuras, but these macros work. See [Add-ons & UI](../../systems/addons.md). Give separate keys to your Call spells, Totemic Call, Earth Shock, Purge, Grounding Totem, Tremor Totem, Earthbind Totem and Ghost Wolf.

!!! tip fc-inline "Tip"
    The totem GCD is 1 second. Thus, it costs little to change 1 totem during a fight.

!!! hardcore fc-inline "Hardcore"
    Earthbind Totem, Frost Shock and Ghost Wolf are your escape tools. Before a dangerous pull, make sure that Ghost Wolf is not on cooldown. Keep a rank of Lesser Healing Wave on your bar in each spec.

## <span class="fc-ch__lv">Levels 30–39</span> The Air totem and Windfury { #l30 .fc-ch data-toc-label="30–39 · The Air totem" data-label="30–39 · The Air totem" }

At level 30 you get the last of the 4 elements, and Enhancement damage increases much. You train Windfury Weapon, Grounding Totem, Reincarnation and Call of the Ancestors. Your trainer gives the last totem quest, *Call of Air*.

*Call of Air* is new at this level in Forever. It gives the Air Totem. Its quest chain also gives a rare weapon for your spec and a shield. The Equipment add-on below describes the 3 weapons. Method.gg compares the Enhancement weapon with the Whirlwind Axe of the Warrior.

Windfury Weapon gives extra attacks, so a slow two-hand weapon hits very hard with it. Grounding Totem absorbs 1 harmful spell. Reincarnation lets you come back to life after a wipe, and then you can resurrect the group. It needs an Ankh, unless you have the Legacy talent Reagent Economy.

At level 32 you train Chain Lightning and Windfury Totem. Each group with melee players wants Windfury Totem, so you find dungeon groups more easily from this level. Use Grounding Totem in dungeons against dangerous casters.

The new battleground Battle for the Darkspear Islands opens at level 30. Start to save gold now. At level 40 you need 100g for riding. Dual spec gives you a second talent set, and it costs 50g. The second book quest at 35 needs 20 library books.

| 30 | **Windfury Weapon** | Extra attacks. The main imbue for Enhancement from this level |
| 30 | **Grounding Totem, Reincarnation, Call of the Ancestors** | A spell absorb, a self-resurrection and a second totem set |
| 30 | **Air Totem (class quest)** | It opens the Air totems. The chain also gives a spec weapon |
| 32 | **Chain Lightning, Windfury Totem** | A spell that hits more than 1 target, and the melee group buff |

## <span class="fc-ch__lv">Levels 40–49</span> Mail armor and a healer spec { #l40 .fc-ch data-toc-label="40–49 · Mail armor" data-label="40–49 · Mail armor" }

Level 40 changes much. You can train mail armor at your class trainer (Warcraft Tavern). You can buy riding for 100g, and the price includes your first mount. You can buy dual spec for 50g from your trainer. You also train Call of the Spirits, your third totem set.

Dual spec is very useful for a hybrid. Keep your quest spec in 1 spec, and make the second spec a Restoration spec. Groups always need healers. The group finder is only a list, so a healer spec gets you into groups more quickly. You then need a second set of items, so keep each healer item that you find.

You change talents at a class trainer in a capital city. A talent change in 1 spec still costs a respec fee. The fee for each spec decreases to 1g with time.

With 3 Call spells, you can keep 1 set for solo fights, 1 for melee groups and 1 for caster groups. Windfury Totem and Strength of Earth Totem are for melee groups. Mana Spring Totem and Flametongue Totem are for casters.

The new zone Riverglades is for levels 35 to 45. It is about as large as Stranglethorn Vale, and it has 150 to 200 quests. A new ferry goes from Steamwheedle Port in Tanaris to Powderfuse Port in Riverglades. At 45, the third book quest needs 25 library books. Level 45 also gives the *Shaman: Experienced* Legacy Point.

| 40 | **Call of the Spirits** | Your third totem set |

## <span class="fc-ch__lv">Levels 50–59</span> Hit, mana and the last dungeons { #l50 .fc-ch data-toc-label="50–59 · Hit" data-label="50–59 · Hit" }

From level 50 you train only higher ranks of your spells. The change in these levels comes from your talents and your items. The main tree of your build is complete, so the last points go into the other 2 trees. The hit and mana talents are there.

Hit becomes important near level 60. Tidal Focus gives 5% hit to the 2 damage paths, so you need less hit from items.

These levels have the most dungeons. Reportedly, each boss in the new dungeons drops a blue item. Thus, you start to collect your level 60 equipment in these dungeons. Heal them in your second spec if a group has no healer. Before each boss, ask which totems the group needs, and move them with Totemic Call during the fight.

In the open world, Azshara has many new quests. Many enemies in Un'Goro Crater, Azshara and Silithus are elementals. Training is a large cost in these levels. For all classes, all spells from 1 to 60 cost about 206g to 207g.

The world-first group to reach level 60 had a Shaman. The group used large AoE pulls, about 3 days after launch.

## <span class="fc-ch__lv">Level 60</span> Select your raid role { #l60 .fc-ch data-toc-label="60 · Select your raid role" data-label="60 · Select your raid role" }

At level 60 you get the *Shaman: Master* Legacy Point. Mount Hyjal opens through Darkwhisper Gorge in Winterspring, and the 2 new raids are there. The fast mount costs 1,000g. The game has no flying mounts.

All 3 launch raids open on 9 December 2026. The raids have no difficulty modes. Blizzard says that the difficulty comes from preparation: equipment, consumables and strategy.

No source has a tested item list for a level 60 Shaman. Do the level 60 dungeons, most of all the new dungeons. Upper Blackrock Spire has 10 players, so it is the nearest thing to a raid before 9 December.

Decide your raid role now. Restoration always gets a raid position. Elemental and Enhancement are full damage specs in Forever, but no source has published simulations. Respec to a level 60 build of your path.

Do the attunement for Onyxia's Lair. It gives the Drakefire Amulet. It is not account-wide, so each character must do it. Keep items with Fire resistance for this raid.

If you play PvP, collect the honor set for your role. A blue part and an Emboldened Seal make an epic part.

**Dungeons in this chapter**

## <span class="fc-ch__lv">Raids and PvP</span> Totems for the raid and for PvP { #raid .fc-ch data-toc-label="Raids · Totems for the raid" data-label="Raids · Totems for the raid" }

**The Barrow Deeps** has 10 players. With so few players, the totems of 1 Shaman can possibly cover most of the raid. The raid will ask for your strongest buff totem. See [The Barrow Deeps](../../content/raids/barrow-deeps.md).

**Hyjal Summit** has 20 players and 13 bosses. Plan the totems for each group together with the other Shamans. See [Hyjal Summit](../../content/raids/hyjal-summit.md).

**Onyxia's Lair** has 40 players and much Fire damage. Fire Resistance Totem now covers the full raid, so the raid leader will ask for it. See [Onyxia's Lair](../../content/raids/onyxias-lair.md).

In each raid, ask your group which totems it needs. Use Totemic Call when the fight moves. Imbues now stack with stones and oils, so use the 2 together.

The new Battle for the Darkspear Islands is a 15 against 15 battleground with 4 bases and a central flag. Fan sites say that Warsong Gulch, Arathi Basin and Alterac Valley are also live. Ghost Wolf and Earthbind Totem make you a good flag escort and a good base defender. Purge removes enemy buffs, and Grounding Totem absorbs 1 spell. Shamans have 3 honor sets: healer, melee and caster. See [PvP](../../content/pvp/index.md) and [Battlegrounds](../../content/pvp/battlegrounds.md).

| Enemy in PvP | What you do |
|---|---|
| Warlock | Use Tremor Totem against Fear. Use Grounding Totem against Death Coil or Fear. Purge the buffs |
| Mage | Use Grounding Totem against Polymorph. Use Frost Shock against kiting. Interrupt with Earth Shock |
| Rogue | Stoneclaw Totem absorbs hits |
| Warrior | Kite with Frost Shock and Earthbind Totem. As Elemental, do not fight in melee |
| Priest | Use Tremor Totem against Psychic Scream. Purge the shields |
| Paladin | Purge the blessings. Holy Strike and Seal of Fury make them more dangerous than in Classic |
| Druid | Purge the heals over time. Use Earth Shock on the heal casts |
| Hunter | Use Ghost Wolf to get near. Grounding Totem absorbs 1 spell |

!!! hardcore fc-inline "Hardcore"
    [Hardcore](../../about/hardcore.md) comes in winter 2026/27, and no date is known. On a PvP ruleset, Earthbind Totem and Ghost Wolf help you to escape in the open world. Legacy Points from Hardcore apply to other rulesets, but not the reverse.

1. Keep **Windfury Weapon** on your weapon, and **Lightning Shield** or **Water Shield** on you.
2. Put down Strength of Earth Totem, Searing Totem and Windfury Totem with a Call spell.
3. Pull with **Lightning Bolt**, then use **Stormstrike** on cooldown (6 seconds).
4. Use **Flame Shock** on a target that lives long. On other targets, use **Earth Shock** after Stormstrike.
5. Cast **Lightning Bolt** at 5 **Maelstrom Weapon** stacks.
6. Use **Rage of the Farseer** on elites and bosses, and for burst in PvP.

</div>

<div class="fc-addon" data-pick="talents" data-value="elemental" markdown>

<p class="fc-addon__label"><span class="fc-addon__kind">Talents</span> <strong>Elemental</strong></p>

Tidal Focus gives 5% hit. A raid boss needs 16% or 17% spell hit, so you need about 11% to 12% from items. For Onyxia's Lair, you can move the Eye of the Storm points to Elemental Warding. In PvP, your burst is lower than in Classic, because Elemental Mastery is not in the game. The PvP build keeps Earthbound for its 5 second root.

<div class="priority" markdown>

1. Keep **Water Shield** active.
2. Put down a Fire totem with a Call spell: Searing Totem for 1 target, Magma Totem for groups. Add **Mana Spring Totem**.
3. Use **Flame Shock** on a target that lives long.
4. Cast **Lightning Bolt** as your main spell. Lightning Overload gives free second bolts.
5. Cast **Chain Lightning** on 2 or more targets.
6. Use **Lightning Mastery** on cooldown in a raid. Cast Chain Lightning without pause during it.
7. Use **Earth Shock** to interrupt, or on a target that runs away. All Shocks have the same cooldown.
8. Use **Fire Nova** on groups that stand near your Fire totem.

Healing Wave with Healing Way and Purification is your large heal on the tank. Chain Heal is your group heal. In the client data, Mana Tide Totem restores 88 mana each 3 seconds for 12 seconds. No source confirms a higher amount at level 60. In battlegrounds, Riptide and Chain Heal make Restoration a strong healer.

<div class="priority" markdown>

2. Use **Riptide** on the tank, then cast **Chain Heal** directly on the tank for 25% more healing.
3. Use **Healing Wave** for large, planned heals. Use a lower rank to save mana.
4. Use **Lesser Healing Wave** in an emergency. Use **Nature's Swiftness** for an instant Healing Wave.
5. Use **Mana Tide Totem** when the mana of your group is low.
6. Use the Cure spells, Poison Cleansing Totem or Disease Cleansing Totem where a fight needs them.

## Professions { #professions }

<div class="fc-stack" markdown>

Mana potions are the largest raid cost of a Shaman. Enhancement and leveling Shamans also have good options.

| Rank | Professions | Use them for |
|---|---|---|
| **S**{ .tier .tier-s } | [Herbalism](../../professions/herbalism.md) + [Alchemy](../../professions/alchemy.md) | Elemental and Restoration. Mana potions and elixirs for raids. Tauren Cultivation gives extra herbs. Camp features: Incense Candle (Intellect) and Mana Well (mana regeneration) |
| **A**{ .tier .tier-a } | [Mining](../../professions/mining.md) + [Blacksmithing](../../professions/blacksmithing.md) | Enhancement. Two-hand weapons, and stones that stack with your imbue. Camp features: Lodestone (melee attack power) and Sharpening Wheel (Strength) |
| **A**{ .tier .tier-a } | [Skinning](../../professions/skinning.md) + [Leatherworking](../../professions/leatherworking.md) | Leveling. Leather and mail armor, and the crafted parts of the raid set. Camp features: Camp Chair (+2% crit) and Camp Tent (rested XP) |
| **B**{ .tier .tier-b } | [Mining](../../professions/mining.md) + [Engineering](../../professions/engineering.md) | PvP gadgets. Camp features: Lodestone and Reagent Bot |

2 gathering professions give gold most quickly during leveling, so you can buy your mounts earlier. [First Aid](../../professions/first-aid.md) now makes healing potions. [Cooking](../../professions/cooking.md) makes campfires, and [Fishing](../../professions/fishing.md) gives the Fish Bowl camp feature. [Enchanting](../../professions/enchanting.md) makes 4 totem relics. Warcraft Tavern says that Polished Driftwood Icon binds when you make it.

Crafting professions give [Legacy](../../characters/legacy.md) points at skill 150, 225 and 300. Each profession adds a [camp feature](../../systems/camping.md). Professions give no passive stats in Forever. Compare all classes in [Best professions by class](../../characters/professions.md#best-professions-by-class).

## Common mistakes

- You do not do a totem quest, and then your totem spells are grey. The quests are at levels 4, 10, 20 and 30.
- You forget that the game no longer adds new spells to your action bars.
- You twist totems as in Classic. Twisting is not necessary in Forever.
- You leave your totems behind when the fight moves. Use Totemic Call.
- You use a fast weapon with Windfury Weapon. A slow two-hand weapon does more damage with it.
- You sell your Strength items because Intellect was stronger in launch week. Blizzard planned tuning for 10 November that makes Strength the best Enhancement stat.
- You use Lightning Shield and Water Shield together. Only 1 Elemental Shield can be active.
- You buy dual spec without gold for riding at level 40.

## FAQ

??? question "Which path is best for leveling?"
    Enhancement is the most common recommendation. It uses little mana and kills single enemies quickly. Elemental kills from range, but it drinks often. Restoration is weak for solo quests.

??? question "Can a Shaman tank?"
    The client has no full tank tree. Spirit Weapons gives +30% threat with Rockbiter Weapon, and Anticipation and Toughness add survival. No source confirms the Shaman as a raid tank.

??? question "Can a Shaman dual wield?"
    No. None of the 51 talents in the launch client gives dual wield. Enhancement uses a two-hand weapon.

??? question "Where is Lava Burst?"
    Blizzard removed it before launch. Old beta guides still list it. Lightning Mastery is the last talent of the Elemental Combat tree.

??? question "Can the Alliance play a Shaman?"
    Yes, but only as a Dwarf. Skyborne Shamans are Horde only.

??? question "Elemental does not have sufficient mana during leveling. What can I do?"
    Use a lower rank of Lightning Bolt, Mana Spring Totem and the Legacy talent Thrill of Adventure. Water Shield comes at level 51 on the Elemental path. You can also level as Enhancement and change at 40 with dual spec.

??? question "Why was a Tauren Shaman not available on launch night?"
    Blizzard limited the creation of popular combinations on launch night. Blizzard also locked Horde creation on PvP rulesets for some time because of faction imbalance.
