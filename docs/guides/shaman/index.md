# Shaman Guide

!!! info "Current as of 7 November 2026"
    Forever launched on 4 November. This guide follows the latest pre-launch notes (builds 70753 and 70755) and launch-week hotfixes. Talent builds and stat priorities were published during the beta and are likely to move, especially after the **planned 10 November class tuning**. For reference data, see the [Shaman class page](../../characters/classes/shaman.md).

## Overview

The Shaman is a hybrid that can fill **any non-tank role**: ranged caster (Elemental), melee (Enhancement) or healer (Restoration). Your toolkit is your **totems**: four elements, one totem of each at a time. They buff your group, heal it, cleanse it or hurt enemies.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Roles** | Ranged DPS, melee DPS, healer |
| **Armor** | Leather, then mail from level 40 (Classic rule; not re-confirmed for Forever) |
| **Weapons** | Maces, staves, fist weapons, daggers, axes; **two-handed axes and maces are now baseline** |
| **Resource** | Mana |
| **Races** | [Dwarf](dwarf.md) (Alliance, **new**), [Orc](orc.md), [Troll](troll.md), [Tauren](tauren.md), [Skyborne Windshaper](skyborne-windshaper.md) (Horde only, **new**) |

**Strengths**

- Party-wide buffs that every group wants: Windfury, Flametongue, Strength of Earth, Mana Spring and the resistance totems, which now cover the **whole raid**.
- Strong utility in PvP and dungeons: Purge, Grounding Totem, Tremor Totem, Earth Shock interrupts and Earthbind.
- Every spec can heal in an emergency, and Reincarnation saves wipes.
- Enhancement levels quickly and solos well.

**Weaknesses**

- Heavy mana use, especially for Elemental while leveling.
- Totems are stationary. Moving fights pull you away from your buffs, although Totemic Call (level 22) now brings them to you.
- No tank spec. Shield Specialization was removed.
- Totems need **totem items in your bags**, earned through four class quests.

### What's new in Forever

- **Totems are much less of a chore.** The totem GCD is **1.0 s** (was 1.5), and persistent totems last **5 minutes** (was 2). A totem tracker lets you right-click to destroy a totem.
- **Totem bar presets:** *Call of the Elements* (20), *Call of the Ancestors* (30) and *Call of the Spirits* (40) drop all four totems with one 3 s cast.
- **Totemic Recall** (20) refunds 25% of a totem's mana. **Totemic Call** (22; it was called Totemic Projection in the beta) teleports your totems to you on a 30 s cooldown.
- **Fire Nova is now a spell** that bursts from your active Fire Totem. Fire Nova Totem is gone.
- Windfury and Flametongue Totem are party buffs, and Windfury adds an extra swing. **No more totem twisting**: Grace of Air no longer needs it, and Tranquil Air Totem is removed.
- **Imbues last 60 minutes** and stack with sharpening stones, weightstones and oils.
- Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning cast faster. The top rank of Lightning Bolt does **+80%** damage, and Chain Lightning is a 2.5 s cast again.
- Enhancing Totems, Weapon Mastery and Improved Weapon Totems are now **baseline**.
- **Removed:** Elemental Mastery (too strong in PvP), **Lava Burst** (removed shortly before launch), Tranquil Air Totem, Shield Specialization, Nature's Guidance.
- PvP gear has separate **healer, melee and caster** sets.
- **Alliance gets Shamans for the first time**, as Dwarves.

!!! warning "Planned 10 November tuning"
    Blizzard plans to make **Strength the strongest stat for Enhancement**. During launch week Enhancement Shamans found Intellect and spell power worth more than Strength and attack power. Until the change lands, keep any Strength and attack power gear you find.

## Specs & talents

Talents start at **level 10** and give **51 points** by level 60. The single-point "gold medal" tiers sit at **11, 16, 21 and 31** points. You change talents, and Legacy talents, at a class trainer in a major city. **Dual spec** unlocks at **level 40** for **50g** from your trainer. Changing talents inside a spec still costs a respec fee, but each spec's fee decays to 1g over time.

!!! note "Conflict"
    Warcraft Tavern's builds were made before the **3 November** launch build, which removed Lava Burst, added Call of Thunder and renamed Elemental Mastery to Lightning Mastery. Its Elemental build may still assume the old tree. Check the in-game talent pane before you commit points.

**Leveling milestones (general guidance)**

- **Enhancement:** fill Thundering Strikes and Ancestral Knowledge, take Shamanistic Focus at 11 points, Flurry next, Stormstrike at 16 points (around level 25), Spirit Weapons at 21. Then go deep for Maelstrom Weapon and Rage of the Farseer. Icy Veins notes these last talents need a heavy investment, so they come late.
- **Elemental:** fill the damage and crit talents first, Call of Thunder at 16 points, Earthbound at 21 for safe kiting, then Lightning Overload. Expect to drink often until you have Water Shield or gear.
- **Restoration:** weak for solo leveling. Level as Enhancement or Elemental and pick up a Restoration spec with dual spec at 40.

The **Legacy talent *Talented*** brings your first talent point earlier, down to level 5 at 5/5. See [Professions & Legacy](#professions-legacy).

### Rotations

=== "Elemental"

    1. Drop totems with your Call preset: a Fire totem (Searing for single target, Magma for packs), **Mana Spring**, and Earth and Air for the situation.
    2. **Flame Shock** if the target will live long enough.
    3. **Lightning Bolt** as your filler. Lightning Overload gives free extra bolts.
    4. **Chain Lightning** on two or more targets.
    5. **Earth Shock** to interrupt, or to finish a fleeing target. Shocks share a cooldown.
    6. **Fire Nova** on packs gathered around your Fire Totem.

    Use your racial (Blood Fury, Berserking, Wind Blessed passively) with your opening bolts.

=== "Enhancement"

    1. Keep **Windfury Weapon** on your main hand (Rockbiter or Flametongue before level 30) and **Lightning Shield**, or Water Shield if you have it and mana is short.
    2. Drop Strength of Earth, Searing Totem and, in groups, Windfury Totem.
    3. Pull with a **Lightning Bolt**, or simply run in.
    4. **Stormstrike** on cooldown (6 s).
    5. **Flame Shock** on targets that live long, otherwise **Earth Shock** as a filler and interrupt.
    6. Spend **Maelstrom Weapon** stacks on Lightning Bolt.
    7. Save **Rage of the Farseer** for elites, bosses and PvP bursts.

=== "Restoration"

    1. Keep **Water Shield** up and drop **Mana Spring** or **Healing Stream**.
    2. **Riptide** on the tank, then **Chain Heal** onto them for the bonus (if confirmed).
    3. **Lesser Healing Wave** for emergencies, **Healing Wave** (downranked) for efficiency.
    4. **Mana Tide Totem** when mana dips in long fights.
    5. Use Poison Cleansing or Disease Cleansing Totem, or Cure spells, on the right fights.

### Macros and keybinds

The Forever client has Midnight-style add-on restrictions (no WeakAuras; see [Add-ons & UI](../../systems/addons.md)), but ordinary macros work. Useful ones:

```text
#showtooltip Earth Shock
/stopcasting
/cast Earth Shock
```

Interrupts even while you are casting.

```text
#showtooltip Lesser Healing Wave
/cast [@mouseover,help,nodead][] Lesser Healing Wave
```

Mouseover healing for Restoration or emergency off-heals.

```text
#showtooltip Stormstrike
/cast Blood Fury
/cast Stormstrike
```

Burst for Orc Enhancement. Swap in Berserking for Trolls.

**Keybind priorities:** your three Call presets, Totemic Call, Earth Shock, Purge, Grounding Totem, Tremor Totem, Earthbind Totem, and Ghost Wolf. With the 1.0 s totem GCD, swapping a single totem mid-fight is cheap, so bind Grounding and Tremor directly.

## Leveling walkthrough 1–60

!!! info "Zone ranges"
    The Forever client doesn't assign level ranges to zones. The ranges below are Classic ranges or Forever source estimates. See [Zones](../../world/zones.md) and [Leveling](../../characters/leveling.md).

**General plan:** level as **Enhancement** (the most common recommendation) or Elemental. Questing gives much more XP than dungeon mobs in Forever, and dungeon *quests* give a lot. Normal mobs take about 10–15 s to kill solo. Developers put levels 1–20 at about 20 hours.

### 1–10: starting zone and the Earth totem

| Faction | Zones |
|---|---|
| Alliance | Coldridge Valley → Kharanos (Dun Morogh). See [Dwarf](dwarf.md) |
| Horde | Valley of Trials → Razor Hill (Durotar) for [Orc](orc.md) and [Troll](troll.md); Camp Narache → Bloodhoof Village (Mulgore) for [Tauren](tauren.md); Zephras Isle for [Windshapers](skyborne-windshaper.md), who stay there until about 12 |

- **Spells:** Rockbiter Weapon, Lightning Bolt, Earth Shock (4), Earthbind (6), Stoneclaw and Lightning Shield (8).
- **Class quest, about level 4: *Call of Earth*.** Your first trainer sends you to an Earth Spirit, gives you Earth Sapta to drink, and you defeat a Minor Manifestation of Earth. Reward: the **Earth Totem** and Stoneskin Totem.
- **Fighting style:** open with a Lightning Bolt, Earth Shock as they close, then melee with Rockbiter. Keep a Stoneclaw Totem for adds.
- **Level 10:** your first talent point, and the **Fire totem quest**.

### 10–20: Fire totem and the first dungeons

| Faction | Zones | Dungeons |
|---|---|---|
| Alliance | Loch Modan, then Westfall or Darkshore (Stormwind Harbor now sails to Auberdine) | [Hall of Thanes](../../content/dungeons/hall-of-thanes.md) (13–18), Deadmines (18–24) |
| Horde | The Barrens, Silverpine Forest | Ragefire Chasm (13–18), [Ruins of Lordaeron](../../content/dungeons/ruins-of-lordaeron.md) (15–22), Wailing Caverns |

- **Class quest, level 10: *Call of Fire*.** A multi-step quest that ends with the **Fire Totem** and Searing Totem. Do it straight away; Searing Totem is a big damage boost.
- **Spells:** Flame Shock, Flametongue Weapon, Strength of Earth (10); Fire Nova, Purge, Ancestral Spirit (12); Cure Poison (16); Tremor (18).
- **Talents:** Enhancement takes Shamanistic Focus at 11 points (around level 20 without Legacy).
- **Weapon:** a slow two-handed mace or axe hits hardest with Rockbiter and later Windfury. Two-handed axes and maces no longer need training.

### 20–30: Water totem, Ghost Wolf, totem bar

| Faction | Zones | Dungeons |
|---|---|---|
| Alliance | Wetlands, Redridge, Duskwood, Ashenvale | Deadmines, [Excavation Site](../../content/dungeons/excavation-site.md) (24–29), The Stockade, Blackfathom Deeps |
| Horde | Stonetalon, Hillsbrad Foothills, Ashenvale, Thousand Needles | Shadowfang Keep, Blackfathom Deeps |

- **Level 20 is the big one:** Ghost Wolf, Call of the Elements, Totemic Recall, Frost Shock, Frostbrand Weapon and Lesser Healing Wave.
- **Class quest, level 20: *Call of Water*.** The longest totem quest; Warcraft Tavern puts the Alliance version at about two hours of travel. Reward: the **Water Totem** and Healing Stream Totem.
- **Library books, level 20.** Every class can turn in library books: 10/20/25 books unlock quests at levels 20/35/45. The Shaman rewards are not documented yet. Book quests are reported at Garion Wendell (Wizard's Sanctum, Stormwind) and Owen Thadd (Magic Quarter, Undercity); it is *unconfirmed* whether Shamans use those NPCs.
- **Level 22:** Totemic Call, Cure Disease and Poison Cleansing Totem. **Level 26:** Magma and Mana Spring Totem.
- **Talents:** Stormstrike at 16 points (Enhancement), Call of Thunder at 16 points (Elemental).
- **Dungeon:** the **[City of Dalaran](../../content/dungeons/city-of-dalaran.md)** (28–33) is Alliance-friendly. Horde players are attacked on sight in Dalaran.

## Dungeons

**Your job in a group:**

- **Totems are your main contribution.** Ask the group what they need. Melee groups want Windfury and Strength of Earth. Caster groups want Mana Spring and Flametongue.
- **Interrupt** with Earth Shock, **Purge** enemy buffs, and drop **Tremor** on fear-heavy fights, **Poison Cleansing** against spiders and snakes, and **Grounding** against dangerous casters.
- **Reincarnation** (30) lets you come back after a wipe and resurrect everyone. It needs an Ankh, unless you take the Legacy talent *Reagent Economy*.
- **Totemic Call** saves re-casting totems when the tank moves the pack.

## Raid preparation

All three launch raids open on **9 December 2026**. There are no difficulty modes. Blizzard says difficulty comes from **preparation**: gear, consumables and strategy. See [Raids](../../content/raids/index.md).

| Raid | Size | Shaman notes |
|---|---|---|
| **[The Barrow Deeps](../../content/raids/barrow-deeps.md)** | 10 | With few players, one Shaman's totems may cover most of the raid. Expect to be asked for your strongest buff totem |
| **[Hyjal Summit](../../content/raids/hyjal-summit.md)** | 20 | 13 bosses. Plan totems by group with your other Shamans |
| **[Onyxia's Lair](../../content/raids/onyxias-lair.md)** | 40 | Heavy fire damage. **Fire Resistance Totem now affects the whole raid**, which makes it very valuable. Needs the Drakefire Amulet attunement |

**Pre-raid gear approach.** Forever loot is new and partly undocumented, so no community best-in-slot list exists yet. Instead:

- Run the level-60 dungeons, especially the new ones: each new-dungeon boss reportedly drops a guaranteed blue item. Upper Blackrock Spire (10-player) is the closest thing to a raid before 9 December.
- Collect the PvP set for your role (healer, melee or caster) if you play PvP. Epic PvP pieces are upgraded from blue pieces.
- Keep a fire-resistance set for Onyxia.

**Consumables (Classic-era guidance, not re-checked for Forever):**

| Spec | Weapon | Elixirs and food | Other |
|---|---|---|---|
| Elemental | Brilliant Wizard Oil | Greater Arcane Elixir, spell-power food | Mana potions |
| Enhancement | Weightstone or sharpening stone, plus Windfury | Strength and agility elixirs | Mana potions, Juju items |
| Restoration | Brilliant Mana Oil | Mana regeneration elixirs | Mana potions, Dark Runes |

Because **imbues now stack with stones and oils**, you get both your imbue and an oil or stone. Crafted food also gives a small XP bonus while leveling, and the Legacy talent *Gourmand* makes food buffs last longer.

## PvP

See [PvP](../../content/pvp/index.md) and [Battlegrounds](../../content/pvp/battlegrounds.md).

- **Battlegrounds:** Warsong Gulch, Arathi Basin, Alterac Valley and the new **[Battle for the Darkspear Islands](../../content/pvp/battlegrounds.md#darkspear-islands)** (15v15, levels 30–60). Darkspear Islands has four bases and a central flag. Ghost Wolf and Earthbind make Shamans good flag escorts and base defenders.
- **Gear:** Shamans have three PvP sets: **healer, melee and caster**.
- **World PvP:** on PvP rulesets you are almost always at risk. Earthbind with Earthbound (a 5 s root) and Ghost Wolf help you escape.

**Matchups**

| Enemy | Tips |
|---|---|
| Warlock | **Tremor Totem** against fear, **Grounding Totem** against Death Coil or Fear, Purge their buffs. Orc Shatter Curse removes curses and Banes |
| Mage | Grounding against Polymorph, Frost Shock to stop kiting, interrupt with Earth Shock |
| Rogue | Stoneclaw Totem soaks hits, and Dwarf Stoneform clears bleeds and poisons |
| Warrior | Frost Shock and Earthbind to kite. Don't trade melee hits as Elemental |
| Priest | Tremor against Psychic Scream, Purge their shields |
| Paladin | Purge their blessings. Holy Strike and Seal of Fury make them more dangerous than in Classic |
| Druid | Purge HoTs, Earth Shock their heals |
| Hunter | Close the gap with Ghost Wolf. Grounding absorbs one spell |

Enhancement bursts with Stormstrike plus Rage of the Farseer. Elemental lost Elemental Mastery, so its burst is lower than in Classic. Restoration is a strong battleground healer with Riptide and Chain Heal.

## Gear, stats & enchants

Warcraft Tavern's stat priorities (beta; the author expects them to change at level cap):

| Spec | Priority |
|---|---|
| **Elemental** | Hit (to the 17% spell cap) > Haste > Spell Penetration > Spell Power > Intellect > Crit |
| **Enhancement** | Weapon damage > Hit (at least 8%) > Intellect / Strength > Spell Penetration / Spell Power > Crit > Agility |
| **Restoration** | Healing Power > Intellect > Haste > MP5 > Spirit > Crit |

!!! note "Conflict"
    The Enhancement order above values Intellect as much as Strength, and lists an expertise target (6.5%) that no other source mentions. Blizzard's **planned 10 November tuning** will make **Strength** the top Enhancement stat. Plan for Strength and attack power, with some Intellect.

- **Weapons:** a slow two-hander for Enhancement (Windfury rewards slow hits). A one-hand caster weapon plus shield or off-hand for Elemental and Restoration. The Level 30 Air totem quest weapon carries you a long way.
- **Armor:** leather until 40, then mail (Classic rule). Don't skip good leather with the right stats.
- **Enchants (Classic options):** Crusader or Strength for Enhancement weapons, spell power for Elemental, healing power for Restoration. Forever enchants are not fully documented.
- **Hit from talents:** several classes now get hit from talents. If you take hit talents, adjust your gear goal down.

## Hardcore notes

The **Hardcore** ruleset arrives in winter 2026/27 with no date yet. See [Hardcore](../../about/hardcore.md).

- **Reincarnation** (30) is the obvious question. How it works on Hardcore has **not been confirmed**; don't plan around it.
- **Earthbind**, **Frost Shock** and **Ghost Wolf** (20) are your escape kit. Always keep Ghost Wolf off cooldown before a risky pull.
- **Stoneclaw Totem** draws an add for a few seconds while you run.
- Keep **Lesser Healing Wave** ranks on a bar in every spec.
- **Grounding Totem** stops one deadly spell, such as an elite caster's Polymorph or Fear.
- The Fire totem quest for Dwarves sends you among level-14 troggs in groups. Pull carefully.
- Hardcore Legacy points apply to other rulesets, but not the other way around.

## Common mistakes / FAQ

??? question "My totem spells are greyed out."
    You need the totem item for that element in your bags. Do the Earth (4), Fire (10), Water (20) and Air (30) totem quests.

??? question "Do I still need to twist totems?"
    No. Twisting is gone. Grace of Air works without it, and Windfury is a party buff that adds an extra swing.

??? question "Where is Lava Burst?"
    It was in the September deep dive as Elemental's 31-point talent but was removed before launch. Older beta guides still list it.

??? question "Can Alliance play Shaman?"
    Yes, as **Dwarves only**. See [Dwarf Shaman](dwarf.md). Skyborne Shamans are Horde only.

??? question "Enhancement feels weak with Strength gear."
    In launch week Intellect and spell power were worth more. The 10 November tuning is meant to fix this. Keep Strength gear.

??? question "Elemental runs out of mana while leveling."
    Use downranked Lightning Bolts, Mana Spring, Water Shield when you have it, and the Legacy talent *Thrill of Adventure*. Or level as Enhancement and switch at 40 with dual spec.

??? question "My new spells aren't on my bars."
    Forever doesn't add them automatically any more. Drag them from your spellbook.

??? question "Why couldn't I create a Tauren Shaman at launch?"
    Creation of popular combos, including Tauren Shaman, was throttled on launch night. Horde creation on PvP rulesets was also locked for a time because of faction imbalance.
