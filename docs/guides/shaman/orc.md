## Why this combo

Orc Shaman is the original Horde Shaman. Thrall is an Orc Shaman, and the class is at the center of Orgrimmar. It is a Classic combination and it is not new in Forever. But the orc racials changed a lot. **Blood Fury** now increases both attack power **and** spell power, and it has a **2-minute** cooldown. The new **Shatter Curse** is a counter to Warlocks. Thus, Orc is a strong burst choice for Enhancement and Elemental, and a good PvP choice for all specs.

## Starting zone: Valley of Trials to Razor Hill (1–12)

!!! note "Classic layout"
    Forever added new NPCs, quests and profession hooks to the Valley of Trials. The trainer names and totem quest steps below are from Classic. The Forever versions can be different.

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **1–6**{ .lvl } Valley of Trials

    - The Shaman trainer is in the Den, the cave building at the center of the valley (Classic trainer: Shikrik).
    - Quest on boars, scorpids and the Vile Familiars. Start each fight with Lightning Bolt. Then attack in melee with Rockbiter.
    - **Level 4: *Call of Earth*.** Your trainer sends you to an earth spirit on the hills of the valley. Drink the Earth Sapta and defeat the Minor Manifestation of Earth. Then go back to get the **Earth Totem** and Stoneskin Totem.

2.  **6–12**{ .lvl } Razor Hill and Sen'jin Village

    - Razor Hill has a Shaman trainer (Classic: Swart).
    - Quest around Razor Hill, the Kolkar camps, Tiragarde Keep and the Echo Isles.
    - **Level 10: *Call of Fire*.** Your trainer sends you on the Fire quest. In Classic, the route went through Durotar and the Barrens. Our sources do not document the Forever route. Reward: **Fire Totem** and Searing Totem.
    - **First talent point** at 10.
    - When you get to **Orgrimmar**, train there. The Shaman trainers are in the **Valley of Wisdom** (Classic location).

</div>

**Ragefire Chasm** (13–18), below Orgrimmar, is your first dungeon.

**Totem quests:** Earth (4) and Fire (10) as above. In Classic, **Water (20)** was a quest chain in the Barrens and Southern Barrens. **Air (30)** is new in Forever and gives a spec weapon. Our sources do not yet document its Horde quest giver. Your trainer gives the quest when you reach level 30.

**Dungeons on the way:** Ragefire Chasm, Wailing Caverns, Shadowfang Keep, Razorfen Kraul, Scarlet Monastery, [The Drowned City](../../content/dungeons/drowned-city.md) (near Grom'gol), [Krol'dok Stronghold](../../content/dungeons/kroldok-stronghold.md) (Riverglades), [Alcaz Prison](../../content/dungeons/alcaz-prison.md) (Dustwallow), [Blackmaw Hold](../../content/dungeons/blackmaw-hold.md) (Azshara). The City of Dalaran is hostile to Horde.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Mount:** wolf, from Orgrimmar's kennels. At level 40, Riding costs 100g, and this price includes your first wolf. At Honored with Orgrimmar, you get a 10% discount.
- **Reputation:** Orgrimmar.
- **Darkspear Islands:** you fight for the **Darkspear Raiders**. Queue at Merchant Coast or a battlemaster.
- **PvP vendors:** Hall of Legends, Orgrimmar. On launch night, these vendors were on the list of known issues.
- **PvP ruleset:** For a time in NA and EU, Horde character creation on PvP rulesets was locked because of population imbalance.
- **Lore:** the Earthen Ring's **Muln Earthfury** has a role in Mulgore and appears in the Zephras Isle finale.

## Verdict

<div class="verdict" markdown>

**Best for burst and PvP.** Blood Fury is now one of the best offensive racials for both Shaman damage specs. Hardiness gives you protection from stun-locks. Shatter Curse gives the class a tool that it did not have. Command gives you no benefit. For pure Restoration, a Troll or Tauren can give slightly more, but Orc is never a bad choice.

</div>
