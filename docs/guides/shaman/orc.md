## Why this combo

Orc Shaman is the original Horde Shaman. Thrall is one, and Orgrimmar is built around the class. It's a Classic combination, not new in Forever, but the orc racials changed a lot. **Blood Fury** now boosts both attack power **and** spell power with a **2-minute** cooldown, and the new **Shatter Curse** answers Warlocks. That makes Orc a strong burst pick for Enhancement and Elemental, and a solid PvP choice for any spec.

## Starting zone: Valley of Trials to Razor Hill (1–12)

!!! note "Classic layout"
    Forever added new NPCs, quests and profession hooks to the Valley of Trials. The trainer names and totem quest steps below are Classic ones; Forever's versions may differ.

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **1–6**{ .lvl } Valley of Trials

    - The Shaman trainer is inside the Den, the cave-building at the centre of the valley (Classic trainer: Shikrik).
    - Quest on boars, scorpids and the Vile Familiars. Open with Lightning Bolt, then melee with Rockbiter.
    - **Level 4: *Call of Earth*.** Your trainer sends you to an earth spirit on the hills of the valley. Drink the Earth Sapta, defeat the Minor Manifestation of Earth, and return for the **Earth Totem** and Stoneskin Totem.

2.  **6–12**{ .lvl } Razor Hill and Sen'jin Village

    - Razor Hill has a Shaman trainer (Classic: Swart).
    - Quest around Razor Hill, the Kolkar camps, Tiragarde Keep and the Echo Isles.
    - **Level 10: *Call of Fire*.** Your trainer sends you on the Fire quest. In Classic the route ran through Durotar and the Barrens. The Forever route isn't documented in our sources. Reward: **Fire Totem** and Searing Totem.
    - **First talent point** at 10.
    - Train in **Orgrimmar** once you reach it: the Shaman trainers are in the **Valley of Wisdom** (Classic location).

</div>

**Ragefire Chasm** (13–18), beneath Orgrimmar, is your first dungeon.

**Class trainer city:** Orgrimmar (Valley of Wisdom). Warcraft Tavern counts about **15 Horde Shaman trainers** across Durotar, Mulgore, Orgrimmar, Thunder Bluff, the Swamp of Sorrows (Stonard) and Zephras Isle, so training is easy wherever you quest.

**Totem quests:** Earth (4) and Fire (10) as above. **Water (20)** in Classic was a Barrens and Southern Barrens chain. **Air (30)** is new in Forever and comes with a spec weapon. Its Horde quest giver isn't documented in our sources yet; your trainer gives the quest when you reach 30.

**Dungeons on the way:** Ragefire Chasm, Wailing Caverns, Shadowfang Keep, Razorfen Kraul, Scarlet Monastery, [The Drowned City](../../content/dungeons/drowned-city.md) (near Grom'gol), [Krol'dok Stronghold](../../content/dungeons/kroldok-stronghold.md) (Riverglades), [Alcaz Prison](../../content/dungeons/alcaz-prison.md) (Dustwallow), [Blackmaw Hold](../../content/dungeons/blackmaw-hold.md) (Azshara). The City of Dalaran is hostile to Horde.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Mount:** wolf, from Orgrimmar's kennels. Riding costs 100g at 40 including your first wolf. Honored with Orgrimmar gives 10% off.
- **Reputation:** Orgrimmar.
- **Darkspear Islands:** you fight for the **Darkspear Raiders**. Queue at Merchant Coast or a battlemaster.
- **PvP vendors:** Hall of Legends, Orgrimmar. On launch night these vendors were listed as a known issue.
- **PvP ruleset:** Horde character creation on PvP rulesets was locked for a time in NA and EU because of population imbalance.
- **Lore:** the Earthen Ring's **Muln Earthfury** has a role in Mulgore and appears in the Zephras Isle finale.

## Verdict

<div class="verdict" markdown>

**Best for burst and PvP.** Blood Fury is now one of the best offensive racials for both Shaman damage specs, Hardiness keeps you out of stun-locks, and Shatter Curse fixes a hole in the class kit. Command is wasted on you. For pure Restoration, a Troll or Tauren may give slightly more, but Orc is never a wrong choice.

</div>
