## Why this combo

!!! success "New in Forever"
    The **Skyborne** are a new race that exists only in Forever. The **Windshapers** are the Horde Skyborne. They are a shamanic people, and **Shaman is their signature class**. Skyborne Shamans are **Horde only**. Alliance Skyborne (the High Order) cannot be Shamans.

!!! warning "Paid race"
    To play Skyborne, you must have the **Skyborne Heroic Pack** ($29.99) or a higher edition. See [Editions](../../about/editions.md).

The Windshapers feel that they owe a debt to the air spirits that disappeared from Zephras Isle. They are in contact with the **Earthen Ring**. Skyborne Shamans train **all 4 elements**, not only air. Warcraft Tavern rates Skyborne the **strongest Shaman race** because of a permanent haste bonus and a situational 5% damage bonus.

## Starting zone: Zephras Isle (1–12)

The Skyborne start on **[Zephras Isle](../../world/zephras-isle.md)**, a floating island in Skywall, the Elemental Plane of Air. The 2 factions share the zone, but each faction is in a different phase. On the island, you get to about level 12–14. Tornadoes give *Blessing of Zephras* (+40% movement speed).

    Al'Aketh thugs. *The Great Outdoors* teaches [camping](../../systems/camping.md)

    Meet **Grand Skyseer Ayessa Dawnsinger** (*The Grand Skyseer*). Trainers, bank, Skydocks

    Windshaper chain *The Broken Construct*

6.  **6**{ .lvl } Shadowgale Forest

    Windshaper chain *In Service of Zephras* → *Blood for Blood*. You can repeat the Nightclaw reputation quests

7.  **7**{ .lvl } Shrine of Akir

    The finale against Lorthuna and **Rohash, Lord of the East Wind**. Horde reinforcements come with **Muln Earthfury**

**Shaman specifics**

- **Trainers:** Warcraft Tavern lists Shaman trainers on **Zephras Isle**, and Valanaar has trainers. Our sources do not give the exact names and locations.
- **Totems:** Warcraft Tavern says Skyborne get the **Totem of Charged Flames** from the quest *Meddlesome Mages* on the island (*single source*). Our sources do not document how Skyborne get their Earth totem at level 4. The island probably has its own totem quests. Ask your trainer.
- **Water and Air totems:** it is *unconfirmed* if Skyborne use the standard Horde quests at 20 and 30 after they leave the island.
- **First talent point** at 10, when you are still on the island.
- **Secret:** `/sing` at the frogs in Gustberry Lowlands for the *Musical Gustjumper* pet.

## Route to 60 from Valanaar

**Leaving the island:** take the airship from the **west Skydock** in Valanaar to **Skywatcher Plateau** in Mulgore, near Thunder Bluff. Reportedly, it leaves about every 5 minutes. You can leave at any level. All players can use it to go back and visit the island.

**Class trainer cities:** Valanaar on the island, then **Thunder Bluff** and **Orgrimmar**. Horde has about 15 Shaman trainers in total.

**Lore stop:** **Shen'dralas** has a connection to the Shen'dralar Highborne of Dire Maul. The Skyborne come from Shen'dralar rebels. But Blizzard has not confirmed a direct story link. Also, there is little documentation for the quests and levels of the zone. Dire Maul itself (55–60) is the Skyborne's ancestral home of Eldre'Thalas.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Mount:** the **Galestrider**, bought at **Fairweather Stables** on Zephras Isle (53.8, 81.3), in Umber, Stormy and Empyrean colors, with swift versions. Windshaper reputation gives a discount of 5/10/15/20% from Friendly to Exalted. You must also have the Riding skill at 40.
- **Reputation:** **Windshapers**. Other Horde races can buy Galestriders at Exalted. Cloth turn-ins in Mulgore also give Skyborne reputation.
- **Languages:** Darnassian and Orcish. 2 elves from different factions can speak Darnassian to each other.
- **Customization:** Only HD models at launch, with feathers, tattoos and bright "Sky-Touched" skin. SD models are expected in early 2027.
- **Popularity:** about 21% of beta characters were Skyborne. Thus, you will see many Windshaper Shamans.
- **Story:** your off-world allies are the **Earthen Ring** and **Muln Earthfury** in Mulgore.

## Verdict

<div class="verdict" markdown>

**The strongest Shaman race in theory, but you must pay for it.** Permanent haste from Wind Blessed helps all 3 specs. Elemental Insight is useful against the many elementals at level cap. Skysight and Walk on Air give you mobility. But you have no stun break, curse removal or stun resistance, so in PvP an Orc is stronger. If you have the Heroic Pack and play Horde, this is the most thematic Shaman. It is probably also the best for PvE.

</div>
