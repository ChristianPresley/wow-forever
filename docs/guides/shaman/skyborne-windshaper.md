# Skyborne (Windshaper) Shaman

!!! tip "Add-on"
    Read with the [Shaman guide](index.md).

## Why this combo

!!! success "New in Forever"
    The **Skyborne** are a new race that exists only in Forever. The **Windshapers** are the Horde Skyborne, a shamanic people, and **Shaman is their signature class**. Skyborne Shamans are **Horde only**; Alliance Skyborne (the High Order) can't be Shamans.

!!! warning "Paid race"
    Skyborne require the **Skyborne Heroic Pack** ($29.99) or a higher edition. See [Editions](../../about/editions.md).

The Windshapers feel indebted to the air spirits that vanished from Zephras Isle, and they have contacted the **Earthen Ring**. Skyborne Shamans train **all four elements**, not just air. Warcraft Tavern rates Skyborne the **strongest Shaman race** because of a permanent haste bonus and a situational 5% damage bonus.

## Racials in practice

| Racial | Type | Rating | PvE | PvP |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Wind Blessed** | Passive | ★★★★★ | Permanent haste for every spec. More casts, more swings, more Windfury and Flurry chances | Always on |
| **Elemental Insight** | Passive | ★★★ | +5% damage to Elementals. Useful against the many elementals in Silithus, Un'Goro, Azshara and (probably) Hyjal | — |
| **Walk on Air** | Active | ★★★ | A 10 s controlled glide. Cancels fall damage and opens shortcuts off cliffs | Escape route off high ground, for example in Arathi Basin |
| **Skysight** *(Windshaper only)* | Active | ★★★ | +15% movement speed for 15 s. At an **elemental convergence** it grants *Charged* for 1 hour, raising the bonus to +30% | Chases or escapes; stacks with Ghost Wolf *(unconfirmed)* |

!!! note "Conflict"
    Sources give **Wind Blessed** as 1%, 2% or 3% haste. The latest official notes say **2%**. **Walk on Air** has a cooldown of 1.5 or 2 minutes depending on the source.

**Macro**

```text
#showtooltip Ghost Wolf
/cast Skysight
/cast Ghost Wolf
```

Skysight plus Ghost Wolf for a fast escape. If Skysight triggers the GCD, press them separately.

## Starting zone: Zephras Isle (1–12)

The Skyborne start on **[Zephras Isle](../../world/zephras-isle.md)**, a floating island in Skywall, the Elemental Plane of Air. Both factions share the zone but are phased apart. The island takes you to about level 12–14. Tornadoes give *Blessing of Zephras* (+40% movement speed).

| Chapter | Where | Shaman notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Thendal Village** | Starting quests. The Windshaper opener is *The Gift of Skysight* |
| 2 | Road to **Shen'dar Village** | Al'Aketh thugs. *The Great Outdoors* teaches [camping](../../systems/camping.md) |
| 3 | **Falaath Village** | Infiltrate the cult and recover the Shadowsong Family Signet |
| 4 | **Valanaar** (capital) | Meet **Grand Skyseer Ayessa Dawnsinger** (*The Grand Skyseer*). Trainers, bank, Skydocks |
| 5 | Gustberry Lowlands | Windshaper chain *The Broken Construct* |
| 6 | Shadowgale Forest | Windshaper chain *In Service of Zephras* → *Blood for Blood*. Nightclaw reputation is repeatable |
| 7 | Shrine of Akir | The finale against Lorthuna and **Rohash, Lord of the East Wind**. Horde reinforcements arrive with **Muln Earthfury** |

**Shaman specifics**

- **Trainers:** Warcraft Tavern lists Shaman trainers on **Zephras Isle**, and Valanaar has trainers. Exact names and locations aren't in our sources.
- **Totems:** Warcraft Tavern says Skyborne get the **Totem of Charged Flames** from the quest *Meddlesome Mages* on the island (*single source*). How Skyborne get their Earth totem at level 4 is not documented in our sources. Assume the island has its own totem quests and check with your trainer.
- **Water and Air totems:** whether Skyborne use the standard Horde quests at 20 and 30 once they leave the island is *unconfirmed*.
- **First talent point** at 10, while still on the island.
- **Secret:** `/sing` at the frogs in Gustberry Lowlands for the *Musical Gustjumper* pet.

## Route to 60 from Valanaar

**Leaving the island:** take the airship from the **west Skydock** in Valanaar to **Skywatcher Plateau** in Mulgore, just outside Thunder Bluff. It reportedly leaves about every five minutes. You can leave at any level, and anyone can ride it back to visit.

**Class trainer cities:** Valanaar on the island, then **Thunder Bluff** and **Orgrimmar**. Horde has about 15 Shaman trainers in total.

**Lore stop:** **Shen'dralas** is tied to the Shen'dralar Highborne of Dire Maul. The Skyborne descend from Shen'dralar rebels, but Blizzard hasn't confirmed a direct story link, and the zone's quests and levels are thinly documented. Dire Maul itself (55–60) is the Skyborne's ancestral home of Eldre'Thalas.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Mount:** the **Galestrider**, bought at **Fairweather Stables** on Zephras Isle (53.8, 81.3), in Umber, Stormy and Empyrean colors, with swift versions. Windshaper reputation gives 5/10/15/20% off from Friendly to Exalted. You still need the Riding skill at 40.
- **Reputation:** **Windshapers**. Other Horde races can buy Galestriders at Exalted. Cloth turn-ins in Mulgore also give Skyborne reputation.
- **Languages:** Darnassian and Orcish. Two elves can speak Darnassian to each other across factions.
- **Customization:** HD models only at launch, with feathers, tattoos and vivid "Sky-Touched" skin. SD models are expected in early 2027.
- **Popularity:** about 21% of beta characters were Skyborne, so expect plenty of Windshaper Shamans.
- **Story:** your off-world allies are the **Earthen Ring** and **Muln Earthfury** in Mulgore.

## Verdict

**The strongest Shaman race on paper, at a price.** Permanent haste from Wind Blessed helps all three specs, and Elemental Insight pays off against the many elementals at level cap. Skysight and Walk on Air give you mobility, but you have no stun break, curse removal or stun resistance, so in PvP an Orc is sturdier. If you own the Heroic Pack and play Horde, this is the most thematic Shaman and probably the best for PvE.
