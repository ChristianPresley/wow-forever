The Shaman has 3 roles in Forever. **Elemental Combat** is the ranged damage tree, **Enhancement** is the melee damage tree, and **Restoration** is the healer tree. Each damage build also puts points in the first rows of the other trees, because Thundering Strikes, Tidal Focus, Mindfulness and Water Shield help all specs. The client has no full tank tree, but some Enhancement talents add threat and avoidance.

## How to read this page

The Shaman has 50 talents in 3 trees. You get 1 talent point for each level from 10 to 60, for a total of 51 points.

- **Points at the left of the grid:** the number of points that you must spend in the same tree before the row opens. Each row needs 5 more points.
- **Max:** the maximum rank of the talent.
- **L (leveling):** the value of the talent when you quest and do dungeons from level 10 to 59.
- **PvE:** the value at level 60 in dungeons and raids. This is damage, healing or tank value, for the role of the tree.
- **PvP:** the value in battlegrounds and world PvP.

**S**{ .tier .tier-s } Core. Take it in each build of that type. **A**{ .tier .tier-a } Strong. Take it when you can. **B**{ .tier .tier-b } Good. Use it to fill points. **C**{ .tier .tier-c } Situational or a personal preference. **D**{ .tier .tier-d } Do not take it.

## Enhancement

Enhancement is the melee tree. **Mental Dexterity** changes Intellect into attack power, and **Stormstrike** opens at 15 points. **Maelstrom Weapon** and **Rage of the Farseer** are deep in the tree. **Thundering Strikes** and **Ancestral Knowledge** in row 1 are useful for all 3 specs. Spirit Weapons, Anticipation and Toughness give threat and survival, but no source confirms the Shaman as a raid tank.

## FAQ

??? question "Can a Shaman tank?"
    The client has no full tank tree. Spirit Weapons gives +30% threat with Rockbiter Weapon, and Anticipation and Toughness add survival. No source confirms the Shaman as a raid tank. Use these talents to hold a mob in an emergency.

??? question "Where are Call of Thunder and Earthbound in the tree?"
    In the launch client, Earthbound is in row 4 and needs 15 points. Call of Thunder is in row 5 and needs 20 points. Guides from the beta gave Call of Thunder at 16 points and Earthbound at 21 points.

??? question "What does Lightning Mastery do?"
    It is a 3 min cooldown. For 15 s, Lightning Bolt casts 0.5 s faster and Chain Lightning has no cooldown. It replaces Elemental Mastery. The launch client has no Lava Burst.

??? question "Does Riptide help Chain Heal?"
    Yes. The client tooltip says that a Chain Heal that you cast directly on the Riptide target heals for 25% more. Riptide heals during 15 s and has no instant heal.

??? question "Which talents from other trees does each build take?"
    Thundering Strikes gives 5% crit for all spells and attacks. Tidal Focus gives 5% hit. Mindfulness and Water Shield give mana. Thus, most builds put 8 to 11 points in a second or third tree.
