## Why this combo

The Darkspear trolls are an old shamanic people. Their witch doctors and shamans are an important part of Sen'jin Village. Troll Shaman is a Classic combination. In Forever, **Berserking** is a flat 15-second haste cooldown that is good for all specs. The new **Rapid Regeneration** gives you a free heal between fights. Choose Troll if you want haste for Elemental or Restoration, and less downtime when you level.

## Starting zone: Valley of Trials to Sen'jin Village (1–12)

!!! note "Classic layout"
    As in Classic, Forever shows Durotar as the Troll starting zone and Orgrimmar as the capital. The Valley of Trials has new NPCs and quests. The trainer names and totem quest steps below are from Classic.

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **1–6**{ .lvl } Valley of Trials

    - The Shaman trainer is in the Den at the center of the valley (Classic trainer: Shikrik).
    - Quest on boars, scorpids and imps.
    - **Level 4: *Call of Earth*.** Drink the Earth Sapta at the earth spirit and defeat the Minor Manifestation of Earth. Then go back to get the **Earth Totem** and Stoneskin Totem.

2.  **6–12**{ .lvl } Sen'jin Village and Razor Hill

    - **Sen'jin Village** is the Darkspear home in Durotar. Razor Hill, a short distance to the north, has a Shaman trainer (Classic: Swart).
    - Quest on the Echo Isles, at the Kolkar camps and around Tiragarde Keep.
    - **Level 10: *Call of Fire*.** Your trainer starts the Fire quest. Our sources do not document the Forever route. In Classic, it went through Durotar and the Barrens. Reward: **Fire Totem** and Searing Totem.
    - **First talent point** at 10. After fights, use **Rapid Regeneration** and do not drink.

</div>

**Totem quests:** Earth (4), Fire (10), Water (20, a Barrens chain in Classic), and **Air (30, new in Forever)**, which gives a spec weapon. Your trainer gives each quest.

**Troll-themed stops:** Stranglethorn's jungle trolls and **[The Drowned City](../../content/dungeons/drowned-city.md)** (sunken troll ruins west of Grom'gol, 35–40), the Revantusk trolls in the Hinterlands, and Zul'Farrak in Tanaris.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Mount:** raptor, from Sen'jin Village. At level 40, Riding costs 100g, and this price includes your first raptor. At Honored with Darkspear Trolls, you get a 10% discount.
- **Reputation:** **Darkspear Trolls**. At Exalted, other Horde races can ride raptors.
- **Battle for the Darkspear Islands:** the new battleground is a fight for Darkspear territory near the coast of Dustwallow Marsh. You fight for the **Darkspear Raiders**. This is a good match for a Troll character. Exalted gives a tabard and a Legacy Point.
- **PvP vendors:** Hall of Legends, Orgrimmar.

## Verdict

<div class="verdict" markdown>

**The most flexible Horde Shaman.** Berserking helps all 3 specs equally. Its 15-second duration is now longer than in the beta. Rapid Regeneration and Beast Slaying decrease your downtime when you level. In PvP, you do not get Orc Hardiness or Tauren War Stomp. Thus, Trolls are slightly better in PvE than in PvP.

</div>
