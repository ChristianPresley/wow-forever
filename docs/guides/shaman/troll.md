## Why this combo

The Darkspear trolls are an old shamanic people; their witch doctors and shamans are part of Sen'jin Village's identity. Troll Shaman is a Classic combination. In Forever, **Berserking** is a flat 15-second haste cooldown that suits every spec, and the new **Rapid Regeneration** gives you a free heal between fights. Trolls are the pick if you want haste for Elemental or Restoration, and less downtime while leveling.

## Starting zone: Valley of Trials to Sen'jin Village (1–12)

!!! note "Classic layout"
    Forever lists Trolls as starting in Durotar with Orgrimmar as the capital, as in Classic. The Valley of Trials has new NPCs and quests. The trainer names and totem quest steps below are Classic ones.

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **1–6**{ .lvl } Valley of Trials

    - The Shaman trainer is in the Den at the centre of the valley (Classic trainer: Shikrik).
    - Quest on boars, scorpids and imps.
    - **Level 4: *Call of Earth*.** Drink the Earth Sapta at the earth spirit, defeat the Minor Manifestation of Earth, and return for the **Earth Totem** and Stoneskin Totem.

2.  **6–12**{ .lvl } Sen'jin Village and Razor Hill

    - **Sen'jin Village** is the Darkspear home in Durotar. Razor Hill, just north, has a Shaman trainer (Classic: Swart).
    - Quest on the Echo Isles, at the Kolkar camps and around Tiragarde Keep.
    - **Level 10: *Call of Fire*.** Your trainer starts the Fire quest. The Forever route isn't documented in our sources (in Classic it ran through Durotar and the Barrens). Reward: **Fire Totem** and Searing Totem.
    - **First talent point** at 10. Use **Rapid Regeneration** instead of drinking after fights.

</div>

**Totem quests:** Earth (4), Fire (10), Water (20, a Barrens chain in Classic), and **Air (30, new in Forever)** with a spec weapon. Your trainer gives each quest.

**Troll-themed stops:** Stranglethorn's jungle trolls and **[The Drowned City](../../content/dungeons/drowned-city.md)** (sunken troll ruins west of Grom'gol, 35–40), the Revantusk trolls in the Hinterlands, and Zul'Farrak in Tanaris.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Mount:** raptor, from Sen'jin Village. Riding is 100g at 40 including your first raptor. Honored with Darkspear Trolls gives 10% off.
- **Reputation:** **Darkspear Trolls**. Exalted lets other Horde races ride raptors.
- **Battle for the Darkspear Islands:** the new battleground is fought over Darkspear territory off Dustwallow Marsh, and you fight for the **Darkspear Raiders**. A good fit for a Troll character. Exalted gives a tabard and a Legacy Point.
- **PvP vendors:** Hall of Legends, Orgrimmar.

## Verdict

<div class="verdict" markdown>

**The most flexible Horde Shaman.** Berserking helps all three specs equally, and its 15-second duration is now longer than in the beta. Rapid Regeneration and Beast Slaying cut leveling downtime. In PvP you give up Orc Hardiness and Tauren War Stomp, so Trolls are slightly better in PvE than in PvP.

</div>
