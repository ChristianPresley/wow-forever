A Warlock wears cloth for all 60 levels. To about level 30 the wand is your most important slot, because you use it while your DoTs do damage. After that, spell power for your school is the most important stat.

About 40% of the items of Forever had hidden stats at launch. The stats of an item become visible when a player first finds it. Thus, no source has a tested list for level 60. The item names on this page come from Method.gg, Warcraft Tavern, Timesaver.gg, Kami-Labs, wowtbc.gg and the Warcraft Wiki. The Codex did not verify the stats.

## Item rules in Forever { #rules }

- Hit is 1 stat for melee, ranged attacks and spells. Your demon uses your hit.
- A raid boss needs 17% spell hit (Warcraft Tavern spec guides). The Warcraft Tavern stats guide says 16%. Suppression gives 5%.
- DoTs can crit, so crit is also a stat for Affliction.
- Life Tap increases with Spirit. Warcraft Tavern says that the maximum rank changes 424 health plus your Spirit into mana. Thus, Spirit is more important than in Classic.
- Demons get stats from your stats. Pre-launch notes give 2 demon health for each point of your Stamina.
- Spell Pierce is a new stat on items. It decreases the resistance of the target. It is weak for DoTs.
- Haste makes only casts and the global cooldown faster. Thus, it is weak for Wrack. Warcraft Tavern saw no haste on items in the beta.
- A beta report says that wand damage increases with spell power. No source gives the formula.
- One report says that Firestone and Spellstone stack with Wizard Oil. Warcraft Tavern names the stones a weapon oil. Test this in the game.
- Sources disagree on staff against one-hand weapon with off-hand item. Method.gg prefers the staff, and Timesaver.gg uses a dagger from level 30.
- Warcraft Tavern says that only the crafter can wear the rare Tailoring lines and the Enchanting staves and off-hand items.
- Necklaces and off-hand items can have enchants. These slots are new.
- No guide publishes an enchant list for Warlocks. The enchants in the table come from the Kami-Labs tool or from the recipe names.
- Each profession gives a camp feature in place of a passive stat. The Faction Banner of Tailoring gives Spirit.

## Stamina for pet tanking, PvP and Hardcore { #stamina }

A safer set. You replace some Intellect and Spirit with Stamina. Your demon gets health from your Stamina and uses your hit. No source publishes an item list for this set.

**Stat priority:** Stamina > Spell Power > Hit > Intellect

### Levels 1–9 { #stamina-l1 }

Take Stamina on each item that offers it. A wand is still your first important item.

### Levels 10–19 { #stamina-l10 }

More Stamina gives more health to you and to the Voidwalker. Take Stamina and Intellect in place of Spirit.

### Levels 20–29 { #stamina-l20 }

The Staff of Soran'ruk has 14 Stamina (Warcraft Tavern). The Staff of the Shade is a very rare drop in Razorfen Kraul with +37 Shadow damage and 15 Stamina.

| Slot | Item | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Staff | **Staff of Soran'ruk** | Class quest: *The Orb of Soran'ruk*, level 20 |
| Staff | **Staff of the Shade** | Razorfen Kraul, a very rare drop, level 22 or higher |

### Levels 30–39 { #stamina-l30 }

With Soul Link at level 30 on the Demonology path, your demon takes 30% of the damage that you take. Thus, the health of the demon protects you, and your Stamina gives the demon health. Timesaver.gg lists a PvP necklace for each faction.

| Slot | Item | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Neck | **Master Sergeant's Insignia** | PvP: PvP vendor (Alliance) |
| Neck | **Senior Sergeant's Insignia** | PvP: PvP vendor (Horde) |

### Levels 40–49 { #stamina-l40 }

Demonic Embrace in the Demonology tree is the Stamina talent. Take it for PvP and Hardcore. Hit on your items also helps your demon to hold the enemy, because the demon uses your hit.

### Levels 50–59 { #stamina-l50 }

The battleground factions sell items for reputation. See the [PvP](../../content/pvp/index.md) page for ranks and seasons.

### Level 60 { #stamina-l60 }

Honor buys 1 blue set and 1 epic set for each class. An epic part needs the blue part and an Emboldened Seal. The wowtbc.gg list names the blue Warlock parts Premier Felweave. No source gives the costs.
