# Human Warlock

!!! tip "Add-on"
    Read with the [Warlock guide](index.md). This page covers only what changes when your Warlock is a Human.

## Why this combo

A Classic combination and, in Forever, arguably the best Alliance Warlock. Method.gg ranks Human at the top of its Warlock tier list, and Warcraft Tavern calls Human strong in both PvE and PvP. Two passives do real work for a Warlock: **Ruthless** gives a flat +1% crit, and **The Human Spirit** now gives **+10% Spirit**, which matters because Life Tap scales with Spirit in Forever. **Will to Survive** is a free stun break, which many players call the best PvP racial in the game.

## Racials in practice

| Racial | Type | Rating | Warlock use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Will to Survive** *(new)* | Active | ★★★★★ PvP · ★★ PvE | Instantly removes stuns. Breaks Cheap Shot, Kidney Shot, Hammer of Justice and Charge stuns so you can Fear or Death Coil back. Cooldown not published |
| **Perception** | Active | ★★★ PvP | Detect stealth for 20 s. Spot the Rogue before the opener, then Fear or put a DoT on them. Paired with your Felhunter's Paranoia (if trained) it shuts down stealth openers |
| **Ruthless** *(new)* | Passive | ★★★★ | +1% crit with every school. Worth about 9 spell power for Destruction by Warcraft Tavern's numbers, and DoTs can crit now, so Affliction gains too (more so with Pandemic) |
| **The Human Spirit** | Passive | ★★★ | **+10% Spirit** (was 5%). More Spirit means more mana from Life Tap and better out-of-combat regen while leveling |

!!! note "Diplomacy is gone"
    The old Human reputation racial became the account-wide Legacy talent *Diplomat*. Any race can take it.

### Macros

Stun break into Fear (PvP):

```
#showtooltip Fear
/cast Will to Survive
/cast Fear
```

Stun break into Death Coil:

```
#showtooltip Death Coil
/cast Will to Survive
/cast Death Coil
```

If the client puts Will to Survive on the global cooldown, the second line won't fire on the same press; press it twice. *Whether it shares the GCD is unverified.*

## Starting zone walkthrough (1–12)

You start at **Northshire Abbey** in Elwynn Forest. Warlock trainers and NPC names below follow Classic; Blizzard hasn't announced changes to Northshire, but check in game.

1. **Level 1:** sell your starting gear to the vendor and buy **Immolate** from the warlock trainer, *Drusilla La Salle*, behind the abbey. Do her **Imp** quest.
2. **Levels 1–5:** the standard Northshire chain (kobolds, wolves, the Defias in the vineyard). Imp in front, Shadow Bolt and Immolate. At 4 learn **Corruption** and **Curse of Weakness**, and buy the **Blood Pact** grimoire from the demon trainer.
3. **Level ~5:** head down to **Goldshire**. Take **The Great Outdoors** for [camping](../../systems/camping.md) and the gathering-profession starter quest. The Goldshire warlock trainer (Classic: *Maximillian Crowe*, Lion's Pride Inn basement) handles levels 6–10.
4. **Levels 6–10:** Life Tap at 6, **Bane of Agony** and Fear at 8. Clear Elwynn: Fargodeep Mine, Jasperlode Mine, the Stonefield and Maclure farms, Hogger. Wand as soon as you can (Lesser Magic Wand at 5 if anyone enchants).
5. **Level 10:** first talent point (**Improved Corruption**), Drain Soul and Create Healthstone. Go to the warlock trainers in the **Mage Quarter, Stormwind** (Classic: *Gakin the Darkbinder*, The Slaughtered Lamb basement) for the **Voidwalker** quest. Method.gg rates the Stormwind version as the quickest Alliance Voidwalker quest.
6. **Levels 10–12:** finish Elwynn (Eastvale, Brackwell), then move into **Westfall** with the Voidwalker tanking.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Class quests:** Imp (1, Northshire), Voidwalker (10, Stormwind), Sayaad (20), Felhunter (30), Felsteed (40, via Ratchet), Dreadsteed (60). Forever changes to the Alliance chains aren't documented.
- **Reputation:** Stormwind. Reaching Honored gives 10% off Riding and racial mounts, though the Felsteed makes the 40 mount less urgent.
- **Raids:** the Alliance Onyxia attunement runs through Burning Steppes, Blackrock Depths and Highlord Bolvar in Stormwind. See [Onyxia's Lair](../../content/raids/onyxias-lair.md).
- **PvP:** Theramore Expeditionary Force reputation from Darkspear Islands; the Champion's Hall PvP vendors are in Stormwind.
- **Lore:** human warlocks in Stormwind work out of the Slaughtered Lamb, tolerated but distrusted. Expect guards and quest NPCs to remark on it.

## Verdict

**The safest strong pick for an Alliance Warlock.** Ruthless and the Spirit bonus are small but always on, and Will to Survive is the best defensive racial a Warlock can have in PvP. Choose Gnome only if you prefer its burst window and escape from roots.
