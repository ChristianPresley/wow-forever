## Why this combo

A Classic combination. In Forever, it is possibly the best Alliance Warlock. Method.gg ranks Human at the top of its Warlock tier list. Warcraft Tavern says that Human is strong in both PvE and PvP. 2 passives are very useful for a Warlock. **Ruthless** gives a flat +1% crit. **The Human Spirit** now gives **+10% Spirit**, which is important because Life Tap scales with Spirit in Forever. **Will to Survive** is a free stun break, and many players say that it is the best PvP racial in the game.

## Starting zone walkthrough (1–12)

You start at **Northshire Abbey** in Elwynn Forest. The warlock trainers and NPC names below are from Classic. Blizzard has not announced changes to Northshire, but make sure in the game.

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **1**{ .lvl } Sell your starting gear to the vendor. Buy **Immolate** from the warlock trainer, *Drusilla La Salle*, behind the abbey. Do her **Imp** quest.
2.  **1–5**{ .lvl } The standard Northshire chain (kobolds, wolves, the Defias in the vineyard). Put the Imp in front and use Shadow Bolt and Immolate. At 4, learn **Corruption** and **Curse of Weakness**. Buy the **Blood Pact** grimoire from the demon trainer.
3.  **~5**{ .lvl } Go to **Goldshire**. Take **The Great Outdoors** for [camping](../../systems/camping.md) and the gathering-profession starter quest. The Goldshire warlock trainer (Classic: *Maximillian Crowe*, Lion's Pride Inn basement) trains levels 6–10.
4.  **6–10**{ .lvl } Life Tap at 6, **Bane of Agony** and Fear at 8. Clear Elwynn: Fargodeep Mine, Jasperlode Mine, the Stonefield and Maclure farms, Hogger. Get a wand quickly (Lesser Magic Wand at 5, if a player can enchant).
5.  **10**{ .lvl } First talent point (**Improved Corruption**), Drain Soul and Create Healthstone. Go to the warlock trainers in the **Mage Quarter, Stormwind** (Classic: *Gakin the Darkbinder*, The Slaughtered Lamb basement) for the **Voidwalker** quest. Method.gg rates the Stormwind version as the fastest Alliance Voidwalker quest.
6.  **10–12**{ .lvl } Complete Elwynn (Eastvale, Brackwell). Then go to **Westfall** and use the Voidwalker to tank.

</div>

## Route to 60 from Stormwind

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **10–20**{ .lvl } Westfall, Loch Modan, **Darkshore**

    The new **Stormwind Harbor ↔ Auberdine** boat takes you to Darkshore. You do not go through Menethil. Deadmines at ~18. Sayaad quest at 20 from your Stormwind trainers

2.  **20–30**{ .lvl } Redridge, Duskwood, Wetlands, Ashenvale

    Three Corners in Redridge has the Azeroth Commerce Authority crate camp. Stockade is in the city. New **[Excavation Site](../../content/dungeons/excavation-site.md)** (Wetlands)

3.  **28–35**{ .lvl } Alterac/Hillsbrad, Stranglethorn

    **[City of Dalaran](../../content/dungeons/city-of-dalaran.md)** dungeon. Alliance can get Kirin Tor reputation. Kirin Tor quests start with Emissary Jacques in Southshore (*fan source*)

4.  **30–40**{ .lvl } Arathi, Desolace, Stranglethorn, Dustwallow (Theramore)

    Felhunter quest at 30. **[Drowned City](../../content/dungeons/drowned-city.md)** near the Stranglethorn coast

5.  **35–45**{ .lvl } **[Riverglades](../../world/zones.md#riverglades)**

    Walk in from Redridge through the widened east side. **Farholde Keep** is the Alliance hub (flight master to Lakeshire, Thelsamar, Morgan's Vigil). At 40, do the Felsteed quest to Strahad Farsan in Ratchet

6.  **40–50**{ .lvl } Tanaris, Feralas, Hinterlands, Badlands

    Krol'dok Stronghold (Riverglades), Uldaman, Zul'Farrak. Tanaris ferry to Powderfuse Port

7.  **50–60**{ .lvl } Azshara, Un'Goro, Felwood, Burning Steppes, Plaguelands, Winterspring

    Blackmaw Hold, BRD, Scholomance, Stratholme. Then Darkwhisper Gorge into Mount Hyjal

</div>

**Class trainer city:** Stormwind (Mage Quarter). You turn in library-book quests to **Garion Wendell** in the Wizard's Sanctum, Stormwind.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Class quests:** Imp (1, Northshire), Voidwalker (10, Stormwind), Sayaad (20), Felhunter (30), Felsteed (40, via Ratchet), Dreadsteed (60). There is no documentation of Forever changes to the Alliance chains.
- **Reputation:** Stormwind. At Honored, you get a 10% discount on Riding and racial mounts. But with the Felsteed, the level 40 mount is less urgent.
- **Raids:** the Alliance Onyxia attunement goes through Burning Steppes, Blackrock Depths and Highlord Bolvar in Stormwind. See [Onyxia's Lair](../../content/raids/onyxias-lair.md).
- **PvP:** Theramore Expeditionary Force reputation from Darkspear Islands. The Champion's Hall PvP vendors are in Stormwind.
- **Lore:** human warlocks in Stormwind work from the Slaughtered Lamb. The city tolerates them but does not trust them. Guards and quest NPCs talk about it.

## Verdict

<div class="verdict" markdown>

**The safest strong choice for an Alliance Warlock.** Ruthless and the Spirit bonus are small, but they are always on. Will to Survive is the best defensive racial that a Warlock can have in PvP. Choose Gnome only if you prefer its burst window and escape from roots.

</div>
