## Why this combo

A Classic combination and, in Forever, arguably the best Alliance Warlock. Method.gg ranks Human at the top of its Warlock tier list, and Warcraft Tavern calls Human strong in both PvE and PvP. Two passives do real work for a Warlock: **Ruthless** gives a flat +1% crit, and **The Human Spirit** now gives **+10% Spirit**, which matters because Life Tap scales with Spirit in Forever. **Will to Survive** is a free stun break, which many players call the best PvP racial in the game.

## Starting zone walkthrough (1–12)

You start at **Northshire Abbey** in Elwynn Forest. Warlock trainers and NPC names below follow Classic; Blizzard hasn't announced changes to Northshire, but check in game.

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **1**{ .lvl } sell your starting gear to the vendor and buy **Immolate** from the warlock trainer, *Drusilla La Salle*, behind the abbey. Do her **Imp** quest.
2.  **1–5**{ .lvl } the standard Northshire chain (kobolds, wolves, the Defias in the vineyard). Imp in front, Shadow Bolt and Immolate. At 4 learn **Corruption** and **Curse of Weakness**, and buy the **Blood Pact** grimoire from the demon trainer.
3.  **~5**{ .lvl } head down to **Goldshire**. Take **The Great Outdoors** for [camping](../../systems/camping.md) and the gathering-profession starter quest. The Goldshire warlock trainer (Classic: *Maximillian Crowe*, Lion's Pride Inn basement) handles levels 6–10.
4.  **6–10**{ .lvl } Life Tap at 6, **Bane of Agony** and Fear at 8. Clear Elwynn: Fargodeep Mine, Jasperlode Mine, the Stonefield and Maclure farms, Hogger. Wand as soon as you can (Lesser Magic Wand at 5 if anyone enchants).
5.  **10**{ .lvl } first talent point (**Improved Corruption**), Drain Soul and Create Healthstone. Go to the warlock trainers in the **Mage Quarter, Stormwind** (Classic: *Gakin the Darkbinder*, The Slaughtered Lamb basement) for the **Voidwalker** quest. Method.gg rates the Stormwind version as the quickest Alliance Voidwalker quest.
6.  **10–12**{ .lvl } finish Elwynn (Eastvale, Brackwell), then move into **Westfall** with the Voidwalker tanking.

</div>

## Route to 60 from Stormwind

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **10–20**{ .lvl } Westfall, Loch Modan, **Darkshore**

    New **Stormwind Harbor ↔ Auberdine** boat gets you to Darkshore without going through Menethil. Deadmines at ~18. Sayaad quest at 20 from your Stormwind trainers

2.  **20–30**{ .lvl } Redridge, Duskwood, Wetlands, Ashenvale

    Redridge's Three Corners has the Azeroth Commerce Authority crate camp. Stockade is in the city. New **[Excavation Site](../../content/dungeons/excavation-site.md)** (Wetlands)

3.  **28–35**{ .lvl } Alterac/Hillsbrad, Stranglethorn

    **[City of Dalaran](../../content/dungeons/city-of-dalaran.md)** dungeon; Alliance can earn Kirin Tor reputation. Kirin Tor quests start with Emissary Jacques in Southshore (*fan source*)

4.  **30–40**{ .lvl } Arathi, Desolace, Stranglethorn, Dustwallow (Theramore)

    Felhunter quest at 30. **[Drowned City](../../content/dungeons/drowned-city.md)** off the Stranglethorn coast

5.  **35–45**{ .lvl } **[Riverglades](../../world/zones.md#riverglades)**

    Walk in from Redridge through the widened east side. **Farholde Keep** is the Alliance hub (flight master to Lakeshire, Thelsamar, Morgan's Vigil). At 40, Felsteed quest to Strahad Farsan in Ratchet

6.  **40–50**{ .lvl } Tanaris, Feralas, Hinterlands, Badlands

    Krol'dok Stronghold (Riverglades), Uldaman, Zul'Farrak. Tanaris ferry to Powderfuse Port

7.  **50–60**{ .lvl } Azshara, Un'Goro, Felwood, Burning Steppes, Plaguelands, Winterspring

    Blackmaw Hold, BRD, Scholomance, Stratholme. Then Darkwhisper Gorge into Mount Hyjal

</div>

**Class trainer city:** Stormwind (Mage Quarter). Library-book quests are turned in to **Garion Wendell** in the Wizard's Sanctum, Stormwind.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Class quests:** Imp (1, Northshire), Voidwalker (10, Stormwind), Sayaad (20), Felhunter (30), Felsteed (40, via Ratchet), Dreadsteed (60). Forever changes to the Alliance chains aren't documented.
- **Reputation:** Stormwind. Reaching Honored gives 10% off Riding and racial mounts, though the Felsteed makes the 40 mount less urgent.
- **Raids:** the Alliance Onyxia attunement runs through Burning Steppes, Blackrock Depths and Highlord Bolvar in Stormwind. See [Onyxia's Lair](../../content/raids/onyxias-lair.md).
- **PvP:** Theramore Expeditionary Force reputation from Darkspear Islands; the Champion's Hall PvP vendors are in Stormwind.
- **Lore:** human warlocks in Stormwind work out of the Slaughtered Lamb, tolerated but distrusted. Expect guards and quest NPCs to remark on it.

## Verdict

<div class="verdict" markdown>

**The safest strong pick for an Alliance Warlock.** Ruthless and the Spirit bonus are small but always on, and Will to Survive is the best defensive racial a Warlock can have in PvP. Choose Gnome only if you prefer its burst window and escape from roots.

</div>
