## Why this combo

The original Horde Warlock, and the race most guides call the best fit for the class. Warcraft Tavern's Demonology guide singles out Orc because its racials help both you and your demon, and Method.gg ranks it A tier, just behind Human. **Blood Fury** now boosts spell power on a 2-minute cooldown, **Command** (re-added just before launch) gives your demon +5% damage, **Shatter Curse** strips enemy curses and Banes, and **Hardiness** shortens stuns.

## Starting zone walkthrough (1–12)

You start in the **Valley of Trials**, Durotar, which Blizzard says gained new NPCs, quests and profession hooks in Forever. Trainer names follow Classic.

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **1**{ .lvl } sell your gear, buy **Immolate** from the warlock trainer in the Den (Classic: *Nartok*) and do the **Imp** quest.
2.  **1–5**{ .lvl } boars, scorpids, Sarkoth, the Burning Blade cultists in the cave. Learn **Corruption**, **Curse of Weakness** and the **Blood Pact** grimoire at 4.
3.  **~5**{ .lvl } take **The Great Outdoors** ([camping](../../systems/camping.md)) and the gathering-profession starter. Move to **Razor Hill**; its warlock trainer (Classic: *Dhugru Gorelust*) covers 6–10.
4.  **6–10**{ .lvl } Life Tap at 6, Bane of Agony and Fear at 8. Quest around Razor Hill, Sen'jin Village, the Echo Isles, Tiragarde Keep and Thunder Ridge.
5.  **10**{ .lvl } first talent point (**Improved Corruption**), Drain Soul and Healthstone. The **Voidwalker** quest: the Orgrimmar version (Cleft of Shadow and Skull Rock) works, but both Warcraft Tavern and Method.gg say the **Undercity** version is easier. If you're heading to Undercity anyway, do it there.
6.  **10–13**{ .lvl } **Ragefire Chasm** (13–18) is under Orgrimmar. Then cross into the Barrens.

</div>

## Route to 60 from Orgrimmar

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **10–20**{ .lvl } Barrens, Silverpine (via zeppelin)

    Durotar Supply and Logistics crate camp west of the Crossroads. Ragefire Chasm, Wailing Caverns, Ruins of Lordaeron (Tirisfal). Sayaad quest at 20: Warcraft Tavern describes the Horde version as an **eight-quest chain** across the Barrens and Stonetalon

2.  **20–30**{ .lvl } Stonetalon, Ashenvale, Hillsbrad

    Shadowfang Keep, Blackfathom Deeps. Avoid **[City of Dalaran](../../content/dungeons/city-of-dalaran.md)** guards: Horde are attacked on sight (the dungeon itself has Horde quests)

3.  **30–40**{ .lvl } Thousand Needles, Desolace, Stranglethorn, Arathi

    Felhunter at 30. Shen'dralas via the Valley of Bones in southern Desolace. Darkspear Islands battleground from 30

4.  **35–45**{ .lvl } **[Riverglades](../../world/zones.md#riverglades)**

    Enter from the Badlands or the Swamp of Sorrows. **Rog'mar** is the Horde hub (flights to Stonard, Hammerfall, Kargath, Flame Crest). The **Felsteed** quest at 40 takes you to Strahad Farsan in **Ratchet**, a short trip from Orgrimmar

5.  **40–50**{ .lvl } Tanaris, Feralas, Badlands, Dustwallow

    Krol'dok Stronghold, Zul'Farrak, **[Alcaz Prison](../../content/dungeons/alcaz-prison.md)**

6.  **50–60**{ .lvl } Azshara (right next door; **[Blackmaw Hold](../../content/dungeons/blackmaw-hold.md)**), Un'Goro, Felwood, Burning Steppes, Plaguelands, Winterspring

    Then Mount Hyjal

</div>

**Class trainer city:** Orgrimmar, Cleft of Shadow. Library-book quests go to **Owen Thadd** in the Magic Quarter, Undercity.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Class quests:** Imp (1), Voidwalker (10, Undercity recommended), Sayaad (20, long Horde chain), Felhunter (30), Felsteed (40, Ratchet), Dreadsteed (60).
- **Reputation:** Orgrimmar. Honored gives 10% off Riding and wolves.
- **Raids:** the Horde Onyxia attunement runs through the Badlands, LBRS, Thrall, Rexxar and the UBRS blood. See [Onyxia's Lair](../../content/raids/onyxias-lair.md).
- **PvP:** Darkspear Raiders reputation from Darkspear Islands (portal at Merchant Coast). Hall of Legends vendors are in Orgrimmar.
- **Lore:** orc warlocks carry the shadow of Gul'dan and the Shadow Council. The Burning Blade cultists in the Valley of Trials and Ragefire Chasm are exactly what Thrall's Horde fears your class becoming, and the Cleft of Shadow keeps you out of sight.

## Verdict

<div class="verdict" markdown>

**The best all-round Horde Warlock.** Blood Fury is the strongest damage racial a Warlock can get, Command adds steady pet damage, and Hardiness plus Shatter Curse make you hard to lock down in PvP. Choose Undead for PvP fear immunity or Troll for Berserking, but Orc is never a wrong pick.

</div>
