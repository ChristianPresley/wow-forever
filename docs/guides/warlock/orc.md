## Why this combo

The original Horde Warlock. Most guides say that it is the best race for the class. The Warcraft Tavern Demonology guide names Orc specially, because its racials help both you and your demon. Method.gg ranks it A tier, immediately after Human. The Orc racials:

- **Blood Fury** now increases spell power, with a 2-minute cooldown.
- **Command** (added again shortly before launch) gives your demon +5% damage.
- **Shatter Curse** removes enemy curses and Banes.
- **Hardiness** makes stuns shorter.

## Starting zone walkthrough (1–12)

You start in the **Valley of Trials**, Durotar. Blizzard says that Forever added new NPCs, quests and profession hooks to this zone. The trainer names are from Classic.

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **1**{ .lvl } Sell your gear. Buy **Immolate** from the warlock trainer in the Den (Classic: *Nartok*). Do the **Imp** quest.
2.  **1–5**{ .lvl } Boars, scorpids, Sarkoth, the Burning Blade cultists in the cave. At 4, learn **Corruption**, **Curse of Weakness** and the **Blood Pact** grimoire.
3.  **~5**{ .lvl } Take **The Great Outdoors** ([camping](../../systems/camping.md)) and the gathering-profession starter. Go to **Razor Hill**. Its warlock trainer (Classic: *Dhugru Gorelust*) trains levels 6–10.
4.  **6–10**{ .lvl } Life Tap at 6, Bane of Agony and Fear at 8. Quest near Razor Hill, Sen'jin Village, the Echo Isles, Tiragarde Keep and Thunder Ridge.
5.  **10**{ .lvl } First talent point (**Improved Corruption**), Drain Soul and Healthstone. For the **Voidwalker** quest, you can do the Orgrimmar version (Cleft of Shadow and Skull Rock). But Warcraft Tavern and Method.gg both say that the **Undercity** version is easier. If you go to Undercity for other reasons, do it there.
6.  **10–13**{ .lvl } **Ragefire Chasm** (13–18) is under Orgrimmar. Then go into the Barrens.

</div>

## Route to 60 from Orgrimmar

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **10–20**{ .lvl } Barrens, Silverpine (via zeppelin)

    Durotar Supply and Logistics crate camp west of the Crossroads. Ragefire Chasm, Wailing Caverns, Ruins of Lordaeron (Tirisfal). Sayaad quest at 20: Warcraft Tavern says that the Horde version is an **8-quest chain** in the Barrens and Stonetalon

2.  **20–30**{ .lvl } Stonetalon, Ashenvale, Hillsbrad

    Shadowfang Keep, Blackfathom Deeps. Stay away from **[City of Dalaran](../../content/dungeons/city-of-dalaran.md)** guards: they attack Horde players when they see them (the dungeon has Horde quests)

3.  **30–40**{ .lvl } Thousand Needles, Desolace, Stranglethorn, Arathi

    Felhunter at 30. Go to Shen'dralas through the Valley of Bones in southern Desolace. Darkspear Islands battleground from 30

4.  **35–45**{ .lvl } **[Riverglades](../../world/zones.md#riverglades)**

    Enter from the Badlands or the Swamp of Sorrows. **Rog'mar** is the Horde hub (flights to Stonard, Hammerfall, Kargath, Flame Crest). The **Felsteed** quest at 40 sends you to Strahad Farsan in **Ratchet**, a short distance from Orgrimmar

5.  **40–50**{ .lvl } Tanaris, Feralas, Badlands, Dustwallow

    Krol'dok Stronghold, Zul'Farrak, **[Alcaz Prison](../../content/dungeons/alcaz-prison.md)**

6.  **50–60**{ .lvl } Azshara (immediately next to Orgrimmar, **[Blackmaw Hold](../../content/dungeons/blackmaw-hold.md)**), Un'Goro, Felwood, Burning Steppes, Plaguelands, Winterspring

    Then Mount Hyjal

</div>

**Class trainer city:** Orgrimmar, Cleft of Shadow. You turn in library-book quests to **Owen Thadd** in the Magic Quarter, Undercity.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Class quests:** Imp (1), Voidwalker (10, Undercity recommended), Sayaad (20, long Horde chain), Felhunter (30), Felsteed (40, Ratchet), Dreadsteed (60).
- **Reputation:** Orgrimmar. Honored gives a 10% discount on Riding and wolves.
- **Raids:** the Horde Onyxia attunement goes through the Badlands, LBRS, Thrall, Rexxar and the UBRS blood. See [Onyxia's Lair](../../content/raids/onyxias-lair.md).
- **PvP:** Darkspear Raiders reputation from Darkspear Islands (portal at Merchant Coast). Hall of Legends vendors are in Orgrimmar.
- **Lore:** orc warlocks have the dark history of Gul'dan and the Shadow Council. The Horde of Thrall is afraid that your class will become like the Burning Blade cultists in the Valley of Trials and Ragefire Chasm. The Cleft of Shadow keeps you hidden.

## Verdict

<div class="verdict" markdown>

**The best all-round Horde Warlock.** Blood Fury is the strongest damage racial that a Warlock can get. Command adds constant pet damage. Hardiness and Shatter Curse make it difficult for enemies to lock you down in PvP. Choose Undead for PvP fear immunity or Troll for Berserking, but Orc is never a bad choice.

</div>
