Read with the [Warlock progression](index.md). This page covers only what changes when your Warlock is a Troll.

## Why this combo

**New in Forever.** In Classic, Trolls were not able to be Warlocks. Forever is the first Classic-era version that permits it. It was also a frequent topic in the beta. Reviewers complained that the revamped racials were overtuned, and they named Troll Warlock as an example. Then Blizzard tuned the Troll and Orc racials before launch.

Trolls get:

- **Berserking** for a cast-speed burst.
- The new **Rapid Regeneration** for fast recovery between pulls.
- **Beast Slaying** against beasts.

Warcraft Tavern says that Troll is good in content with many beasts. Method.gg ranks it the lowest of the 5 (C tier). Thus, it is the choice for flavor, not the min-max choice.

!!! conflict "Conflict: Berserking"
    Blizzard's 26 Oct notes and Icy Veins give a 15-second duration. Games.gg still gives 10 seconds with a 3-minute cooldown. An earlier beta guide described a nerf to a flat 10%. This page uses the Blizzard value.

## Starting zone walkthrough (1–12)

Trolls share the **Valley of Trials** in Durotar with orcs. Blizzard says that Forever added new NPCs, quests and profession hooks to this zone. The trainer names are from Classic.

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **1**{ .lvl } Sell your gear. Buy **Immolate** from the warlock trainer in the Den (Classic: *Nartok*). Do the **Imp** quest.
2.  **1–5**{ .lvl } Boars, scorpids, Sarkoth and the Burning Blade cave. **Corruption**, **Curse of Weakness** and the **Blood Pact** grimoire at 4.
3.  **~5**{ .lvl } **The Great Outdoors** ([camping](../../systems/camping.md)) and the gathering starter. Go to **Razor Hill** (trainer, Classic: *Dhugru Gorelust*).
4.  **6–10**{ .lvl } Life Tap at 6, Bane of Agony and Fear at 8. Do the quests in **Sen'jin Village** and the **Echo Isles**. They show the Darkspear home and the story of the trolls that Zalazane hexed. Rapid Regeneration makes your first Life Taps easy.
5.  **10**{ .lvl } First talent point (**Improved Corruption**), Drain Soul, Healthstone. For the **Voidwalker**, Warcraft Tavern and Method.gg both prefer the **Undercity** quest to the Orgrimmar Skull Rock version.
6.  **10–13**{ .lvl } Ragefire Chasm, then the Barrens.

</div>

## Route to 60 from Orgrimmar

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **10–20**{ .lvl } Barrens, Silverpine

    Beast Slaying is very good against the Barrens beasts. Wailing Caverns, Ragefire Chasm, **[Ruins of Lordaeron](../../content/dungeons/ruins-of-lordaeron.md)**. Sayaad at 20 (8-quest Horde chain)

    Shadowfang Keep, Blackfathom Deeps. Arathi Basin opens at 20

3.  **30–40**{ .lvl } Thousand Needles, Desolace, Stranglethorn, Arathi, Dustwallow

    Felhunter at 30. The tigers, panthers and raptors of Stranglethorn are very good Beast Slaying targets. **[Battle for the Darkspear Islands](../../content/pvp/battlegrounds.md#darkspear-islands)** opens at 30

4.  **35–45**{ .lvl } **[Riverglades](../../world/zones.md#riverglades)**

5.  **40–50**{ .lvl } Feralas, Tanaris, Badlands

    Krol'dok Stronghold, Zul'Farrak (troll-themed), Alcaz Prison

6.  **50–60**{ .lvl } Azshara, Un'Goro (many beasts and devilsaurs), Felwood, Plaguelands, Winterspring

    Blackmaw Hold, Shaper's Terrace, Sunken Temple (troll-themed). Then Mount Hyjal

**Class trainer city:** Orgrimmar, Cleft of Shadow. You turn in library-book quests to **Owen Thadd** in the Magic Quarter, Undercity.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Class quests:** the same Horde chains as orcs: Imp (1), Voidwalker (10), Sayaad (20), Felhunter (30), Felsteed (40), Dreadsteed (60). There is no documentation of troll-specific Warlock quests.
- **Reputation:** Darkspear Trolls. Honored gives a 10% discount on Riding and raptors. **Darkspear Raiders** is a separate PvP reputation from the Darkspear Islands battleground. Its theme is a good match for a troll.
- **Lore:** the Darkspear do not trust demonology. Their shadow hunters and witch doctors work with loa, not demons. A troll warlock is an outsider among the Darkspear. The new combinations in Forever let you role-play this. Blizzard has not announced troll-specific Warlock lore.
- **Raids:** the Horde Onyxia attunement is the same as for orcs.

## Verdict

<div class="verdict" markdown>

**The fun, new option, and good in practice.** Berserking gives Destruction a good burst window. Rapid Regeneration and Regeneration make leveling easy. For maximum damage, choose Orc. For PvP, choose Undead. Choose Troll because you want a Darkspear Warlock.

</div>
