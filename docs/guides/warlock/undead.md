## Why this combo

The classic PvP Warlock. **Will of the Forsaken** breaks and blocks the fears, charms and sleeps that decide caster fights, including enemy Warlocks' Fear and Seduction. Warcraft Tavern names Undead as its PvP pick in every Warlock spec guide; Method.gg ranks it B tier overall. Forever adds **Touch of the Grave**, a passive health drain that fits a class built around drains, and **Cannibalize** now restores mana as well as health.

!!! conflict "Conflict: best PvP racial"
    Warcraft Tavern calls Will of the Forsaken the strongest PvP racial for a Warlock. Method.gg gives that title to the Human's new *Will to Survive*. Both are top tier; which matters more depends on whether you fear stuns or fears more.

??? example "Macros"

    Break fear, then Fear back:

    ```
    #showtooltip Fear
    /cast Will of the Forsaken
    /cast Fear
    ```

    Break fear, then Death Coil:

    ```
    #showtooltip Death Coil
    /cast Will of the Forsaken
    /cast Death Coil
    ```

    *Whether Will of the Forsaken triggers the global cooldown is unverified; if it does, press the macro twice.*

## Starting zone walkthrough (1–12)

You rise in **Deathknell**, Tirisfal Glades. Forever filled Tirisfal's empty western strip with **Shadowvale** and **Bandarion Keep**, and added the [Ruins of Lordaeron](../../content/dungeons/ruins-of-lordaeron.md) dungeon. Trainer names follow Classic.

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **1**{ .lvl } sell your gear and buy **Immolate** from the warlock trainer in Deathknell (Classic: *Maximillion*). Do the **Imp** quest.
2.  **1–5**{ .lvl } mindless zombies, Scarlet Crusade initiates, the Night Web Hollow spiders. **Corruption**, **Curse of Weakness** and the **Blood Pact** grimoire at 4. Cannibalize the Scarlets to save on food.
3.  **~5**{ .lvl } take **The Great Outdoors** ([camping](../../systems/camping.md)) and the gathering starter. Move to **Brill**; its trainer (Classic: *Rupert Boch*, Gallows' End Tavern) covers 6–10.
4.  **6–10**{ .lvl } Life Tap at 6, Bane of Agony and Fear at 8. Brill quests, Agamand Mills, the farms. The new **Shadowvale** quests (including *That Shadowvale Green Elixir*) sit in the west.
5.  **10**{ .lvl } first talent point (**Improved Corruption**), Drain Soul and Healthstone. Do the **Voidwalker** quest in the **Magic Quarter, Undercity** (Classic: *Carendin Halgar*). Both Warcraft Tavern and Method.gg rate this the easiest Horde Voidwalker quest; orc and troll Warlocks come here for it too.
6.  **10–15**{ .lvl } finish Tirisfal, then the **Ruins of Lordaeron** dungeon (15–22) right outside Undercity.

</div>

## Route to 60 from Undercity

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **10–20**{ .lvl } Tirisfal, **Silverpine Forest**, then the Barrens (zeppelin)

    Ruins of Lordaeron, Shadowfang Keep later. Silverpine's Greymane Wall reportedly shows Gilneas (*unconfirmed*). Sayaad at 20

2.  **20–30**{ .lvl } Hillsbrad, Stonetalon, Ashenvale

    Tarren Mill hub. Dalaran is close, but its guards attack Horde on sight; the [City of Dalaran](../../content/dungeons/city-of-dalaran.md) dungeon has Horde quests starting with Magus Wordeen Voidglare in Tarren Mill (*fan source*)

3.  **30–40**{ .lvl } Arathi, Thousand Needles, Desolace, Stranglethorn

    Felhunter at 30. **Scarlet Monastery** is in your home zone

4.  **35–45**{ .lvl } **[Riverglades](../../world/zones.md#riverglades)**

    Zeppelin to Grom'gol, then up through the Swamp of Sorrows to **Rog'mar**. Felsteed at 40 via Strahad Farsan in Ratchet (zeppelin to Orgrimmar first)

5.  **40–50**{ .lvl } Badlands, Tanaris, Feralas, Hinterlands

    Krol'dok Stronghold, Uldaman, Zul'Farrak

6.  **50–60**{ .lvl } Western and Eastern Plaguelands (close to home), Burning Steppes, Azshara, Un'Goro, Felwood, Winterspring

    Scholomance and Stratholme are on your doorstep. Then Mount Hyjal

</div>

**Class trainer city:** Undercity, Magic Quarter. Library-book quests are turned in to **Owen Thadd**, also in the Magic Quarter.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Class quests:** Imp (1), Voidwalker (10, Undercity: the easy one), Sayaad (20), Felhunter (30), Felsteed (40), Dreadsteed (60).
- **Reputation:** Undercity. Honored gives 10% off Riding and skeletal horses.
- **Forsaken neighbors:** Bandarion Keep in Tirisfal is home to the new **Forsaken Paladin** journey. It isn't for you, but its storyline about the Light among the Forsaken is part of your capital's new lore.
- **PvP:** Undercity and Southshore raids were common world-PvP targets in the beta; keep a Soulstone handy on a PvP ruleset.
- **Lore:** Undercity's warlocks work openly in the Magic Quarter beside the mages, a sign of how little the Forsaken worry about where power comes from.

## Verdict

<div class="verdict" markdown>

**The PvP Warlock.** Will of the Forsaken turns the class's hardest matchups, other Warlocks and Priests, in your favor, and Touch of the Grave and Cannibalize make leveling smoother. For raid damage, Orc edges ahead, but the gap is small.

</div>
