Read with the [Warlock progression](index.md). This page covers only what changes when your Warlock is Undead.

## Why this combo

The classic PvP Warlock. **Will of the Forsaken** breaks and blocks fears, charms and sleeps, which often decide caster fights. This includes the Fear and Seduction of enemy Warlocks. Warcraft Tavern names Undead as its PvP choice in each Warlock spec guide. Method.gg ranks it B tier overall. Forever adds **Touch of the Grave**, a passive health drain that is a good match for a class that uses drains. **Cannibalize** now restores mana and health.

!!! conflict "Conflict: best PvP racial"
    Warcraft Tavern calls Will of the Forsaken the strongest PvP racial for a Warlock. Method.gg gives that title to the Human's new *Will to Survive*. The 2 racials are top tier. The better one for you depends on which you fear more: stuns or fears.

## Starting zone walkthrough (1–12)

You start in **Deathknell**, Tirisfal Glades. Forever put **Shadowvale** and **Bandarion Keep** in the empty western strip of Tirisfal. It also added the [Ruins of Lordaeron](../../content/dungeons/ruins-of-lordaeron.md) dungeon. The trainer names are from Classic.

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **1**{ .lvl } Sell your gear and buy **Immolate** from the warlock trainer in Deathknell (Classic: *Maximillion*). Do the **Imp** quest.
2.  **1–5**{ .lvl } Mindless zombies, Scarlet Crusade initiates, the Night Web Hollow spiders. **Corruption**, **Curse of Weakness** and the **Blood Pact** grimoire at 4. Use Cannibalize on the Scarlets to use less food.
3.  **~5**{ .lvl } Take **The Great Outdoors** ([camping](../../systems/camping.md)) and the gathering starter. Go to **Brill**. Its trainer (Classic: *Rupert Boch*, Gallows' End Tavern) trains levels 6–10.
4.  **6–10**{ .lvl } Life Tap at 6, Bane of Agony and Fear at 8. Brill quests, Agamand Mills, the farms. The new **Shadowvale** quests (including *That Shadowvale Green Elixir*) are in the west.
5.  **10**{ .lvl } First talent point (**Improved Corruption**), Drain Soul and Healthstone. Do the **Voidwalker** quest in the **Magic Quarter, Undercity** (Classic: *Carendin Halgar*). Warcraft Tavern and Method.gg both rate this as the easiest Horde Voidwalker quest. Orc and troll Warlocks also come here for it.
6.  **10–15**{ .lvl } Complete Tirisfal. Then do the **Ruins of Lordaeron** dungeon (15–22), immediately outside Undercity.

</div>

## Route to 60 from Undercity

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **10–20**{ .lvl } Tirisfal, **Silverpine Forest**, then the Barrens (zeppelin)

    Ruins of Lordaeron, and later Shadowfang Keep. Reportedly, you can see Gilneas from the Greymane Wall in Silverpine (*unconfirmed*). Sayaad at 20

2.  **20–30**{ .lvl } Hillsbrad, Stonetalon, Ashenvale

    Tarren Mill hub. Dalaran is near, but its guards attack Horde players when they see them. The [City of Dalaran](../../content/dungeons/city-of-dalaran.md) dungeon has Horde quests that start with Magus Wordeen Voidglare in Tarren Mill (*fan source*)

3.  **30–40**{ .lvl } Arathi, Thousand Needles, Desolace, Stranglethorn

    Felhunter at 30. **Scarlet Monastery** is in your home zone

4.  **35–45**{ .lvl } **[Riverglades](../../world/zones.md#riverglades)**

    Take the zeppelin to Grom'gol. Then go north through the Swamp of Sorrows to **Rog'mar**. Felsteed at 40 through Strahad Farsan in Ratchet (take the zeppelin to Orgrimmar first)

5.  **40–50**{ .lvl } Badlands, Tanaris, Feralas, Hinterlands

    Krol'dok Stronghold, Uldaman, Zul'Farrak

6.  **50–60**{ .lvl } Western and Eastern Plaguelands (close to home), Burning Steppes, Azshara, Un'Goro, Felwood, Winterspring

    Scholomance and Stratholme are very near. Then Mount Hyjal

</div>

**Class trainer city:** Undercity, Magic Quarter. You turn in library-book quests to **Owen Thadd**, also in the Magic Quarter.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Class quests:** Imp (1), Voidwalker (10, Undercity: the easy one), Sayaad (20), Felhunter (30), Felsteed (40), Dreadsteed (60).
- **Reputation:** Undercity. Honored gives a 10% discount on Riding and skeletal horses.
- **Forsaken neighbors:** Bandarion Keep in Tirisfal is the home of the new **Forsaken Paladin** journey. It is not for you. But its story about the Light among the Forsaken is part of the new lore of your capital.
- **PvP:** in the beta, Undercity and Southshore were frequent targets for world-PvP raids. On a PvP ruleset, keep a Soulstone ready.
- **Lore:** the warlocks of Undercity work openly in the Magic Quarter next to the mages. This shows that the Forsaken do not care much about the source of power.

## Verdict

<div class="verdict" markdown>

**The PvP Warlock.** Will of the Forsaken gives you the advantage in the most difficult matchups of the class: other Warlocks and Priests. Touch of the Grave and Cannibalize make leveling easier. For raid damage, Orc is slightly better, but the difference is small.

</div>
