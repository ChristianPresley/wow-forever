## Why this combo

The Dwarf is the Alliance's tank race in Forever. **Stoneform** now adds **10% physical damage reduction** for 8 s on top of cleansing bleeds, poisons and diseases. That's a short Shield Wall on a racial. **Artisan's Precision** (+1% hit) helps every spec reach the hit cap.

- **Best at:** tanking, and PvP against Rogues and Warriors.
- **Status:** available in Classic; not a new combination.

## Starting zone walkthrough (1–12)

Dwarves start in **Coldridge Valley**, **Dun Morogh**. *Quest and NPC names are Classic-era; check in game.*

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **1–5**{ .lvl } **Coldridge Valley.** Speak to the Warrior trainer at Anvilmar (Classic: Thran Khorman). Kill wolves and troggs and clear the troll cave. Learn **Charge** and **Rend** at 4.
2.  **~5**{ .lvl } **The Great Outdoors.** Pick up the camping intro and learn Cooking.
3.  **5–10**{ .lvl } **Kharanos.** Take the quests around the inn and Gnomeregan's refugees; there's a Warrior trainer in Kharanos. **Hamstring** at 8.
4.  **10**{ .lvl } Do the **Defensive Stance** quest from your trainer. Pick a gathering profession; Mining is easy to level in Dun Morogh.
5.  **First talent at 10.** Protection: **Shield Specialization**. Arms: **Improved Rend**.
6.  **10–13.** Finish Dun Morogh's east side, and queue for the new **[Hall of Thanes](../../content/dungeons/hall-of-thanes.md)** in Ironforge (13–18) as soon as you can.

</div>

**Class trainers:** Ironforge's Warrior trainers are in the **Hall of Arms** (Military Ward). The Deeprun Tram links Ironforge to Stormwind.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Mount:** Ram, from Amberstill Ranch in Dun Morogh. **Honored with Ironforge** gives 10% off.
- **Reputation:** Ironforge. Your nearest crate camp is Three Corners in Redridge.
- **Dungeons:** Hall of Thanes is right in your capital and is one of the best early weapon sources.
- **Lore:** the Dwarven Warrior tradition centers on the Hall of Arms and the Explorers' League. Uldaman and Hall of Thanes are the Dwarf-flavored dungeons.

## Spec fit

| Spec | Fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Protection** | **5/5**{ .stars .s5 } | Stoneform is a free 8 s damage reduction every few minutes and removes bleeds and poisons that tank-killing bosses apply |
| **Arms** | **4/5**{ .stars .s4 } | +1% hit and +5% vs Beasts make leveling smooth; Stoneform wins Rogue and Warrior duels |
| **Fury** | **4/5**{ .stars .s4 } | Dual-wielders miss a lot, so +1% hit is worth more than for any other spec |

**Leveling tip:** Big Game Hunter makes beast-heavy zones (Loch Modan, Wetlands, Arathi, Stranglethorn's tigers and panthers) noticeably faster.

### Leveling checklist

- [ ] Defensive Stance quest at **10**, Berserker Stance quest at **30**
- [ ] Cooking and *The Great Outdoors* for camping by level 5–6
- [ ] First Aid trained early: it now makes health potions as well as bandages
- [ ] Collect class library books for the quests at 20, 35 and 45
- [ ] **Ram** and Apprentice Riding at 40 (100g); plate and dual spec (50g) at the same time
- [ ] Warrior Legacy Points at 25, 45 and 60 (account-wide, first Warrior only)

## Verdict

<div class="verdict" markdown>

The best Alliance race for a **main tank**, and strong for Arms and Fury too thanks to +1% hit. Stoneform also makes Rogues miserable in PvP. Pick Human instead only if you want the stun break.

</div>
