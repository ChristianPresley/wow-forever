## Item rules in Forever { #rules }

- Rage from your hits depends on weapon speed. A slow weapon gives more rage for each swing.
- A crit gives 75% more rage, so crit has more value than in Classic.
- Armor does not decrease the rage from hits on you. A tank does not lose rage with better armor.
- Method.gg says that hit is 1 stat for melee, ranged attacks and spells. A miss gives no damage and no rage.
- Against a raid boss you need 8% hit with 1 weapon and 29% with 2 weapons (Method.gg). Warcraft Tavern gives 9% for 1 weapon.
- Furious Precision gives 10% hit only to off-hand attacks. The launch client has no general hit talent for Warriors.
- Weapon skills are still in the game. A weapon with low skill misses much more frequently.
- The old weapon racials of Humans, Orcs and Trolls are gone. Weaponmaster decides your weapon type.
- Weaponmaster: axes and polearms get crit, maces and staves ignore armor, swords get extra attacks.
- 440 Defense makes you immune to crits from raid bosses (Warcraft Tavern).
- Strength gives 2 attack power for each point, and 1 Block Value for each 20 points. Shield Slam scales with Block Value.
- Warcraft Tavern found no haste on items in the beta.
- Dungeon items have stats with a theme of class, spec, biome or enemy type. When you loot an item, you unlock its transmog.
- Sharpening stones and weightstones now work together with Shaman imbues and Rogue poisons.
- Many Blacksmithing recipes are Bind on Pickup. No source says if the crafted items are also Bind on Pickup.

## Dual wield { #dual }

The Fury set from about level 40, and the raid damage set. You look for a slow main-hand weapon with high damage and a second good one-hand weapon. It needs much hit.

**Stat priority:** Leveling: weapon damage > Strength > Stamina > Agility. Level 60: hit (29% against raid bosses), crit and Strength. No source ranks them

### Levels 1–9 { #dual-l1 }

You cannot use 2 weapons at these levels. Use the items of the Two-hander set.

### Levels 10–19 { #dual-l10 }

Continue with a two-hander. A miss gives no rage, and 2 weapons miss much more frequently than 1 weapon. Against an enemy of your level you need 24% hit with 2 weapons (Method.gg).

### Levels 20–29 { #dual-l20 }

Warcraft Tavern says that you train Dual Wield at level 20. Shadowfang Keep gives 2 one-hand weapons from 1 boss. Change to 2 weapons only when the pair is better than your two-hander.
