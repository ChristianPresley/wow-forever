# Gnome Warrior

!!! tip "Add-on"
    Read with the [Warrior guide](index.md). This page covers only what changes for a Gnome.

## Why this combo

Gnome Warriors used to be a meme pick. Forever gives them a real damage cooldown: **Eureka!** makes non-periodic abilities cost 10% less and hit 10% harder for 8 s. Add **Escape Artist** against snares and you have a solid PvP race with a small burst window.

- **Best at:** PvP (Escape Artist), burst windows with Mortal Strike or Bloodthirst.
- **Status:** available in Classic; not a new combination.

## Racials in practice

| Racial | Type | Rating | PvE | PvP |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Escape Artist** | Active | **4/5**{ .stars .s4 } | Breaks boss roots and snares now and then | Removes Frost Nova, Hamstring, Entangling Roots and Wing Clip, plus 3 s immunity. Use it, then Charge or Intercept |
| **Eureka!** *(new)* | Active | **4/5**{ .stars .s4 } | 8 s of +10% damage and −10% cost on Mortal Strike, Bloodthirst, Whirlwind, Shield Slam and Execute. Line it up with Death Wish or Recklessness | A burst window for a Mortal Strike kill |
| **Expansive Mind** | Passive | **2/5**{ .stars .s2 } | +5% maximum rage. Helps you pool a little more | Minor |
| **Engineering Specialization** | Passive | **2/5**{ .stars .s2 } | 20% fewer device failures; useful only if you're an engineer | Same, for nets and bombs |

!!! note "Not periodic"
    Eureka! applies to **non-periodic** abilities, so Rend and Deep Wounds ticks won't benefit. *How it treats Heroic Strike's on-swing hit is unconfirmed.*

## Starting zone walkthrough (1–12)

Gnomes start in **Coldridge Valley**, **Dun Morogh**, alongside Dwarves. *Quest and NPC names are Classic-era; check in game.*

1. **Coldridge Valley (1–5).** Speak to the Warrior trainer at Anvilmar. Kill wolves and troggs; Gnome refugees from Gnomeregan give extra quests. **Charge** and **Rend** at 4.
2. **The Great Outdoors (~5).** Camping intro and Cooking.
3. **Kharanos and Brewnall (5–10).** Do the Kharanos inn quests and the Gnomeregan refugee chain. **Hamstring** at 8.
4. **Level 10.** **Defensive Stance** quest from your trainer. If you plan to be an engineer, train it in Ironforge's Tinker Town.
5. **First talent at 10.** Protection: **Shield Specialization**. Arms: **Improved Rend**.
6. **10–13.** Finish Dun Morogh and queue **[Hall of Thanes](../../content/dungeons/hall-of-thanes.md)** in Ironforge.

**Class trainers:** Ironforge's **Hall of Arms**. Tinker Town holds the Gnomish engineering trainers.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Mount:** Mechanostrider, from Steelgrill's Depot in Dun Morogh. Your home reputation is the **Gnomeregan Exiles**; check whether its Honored discount applies to Riding as Stormwind's and Ironforge's do (*unconfirmed*).
- **Engineering:** Gnome Warriors are the natural engineers. Nets, bombs and the Gnomish gadgets fit PvP, and the racial cuts failures.
- **Size:** the small model makes you harder to see and target in battlegrounds. It's a cosmetic advantage, but it's real.

## Spec fit

| Spec | Fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Arms** | **4/5**{ .stars .s4 } | Eureka! plus Mortal Strike is a real kill window in PvP, and Escape Artist keeps you on target |
| **Fury** | **3/5**{ .stars .s3 } | Eureka! boosts Bloodthirst and Whirlwind for 8 s; fine, but Night Elf's and Orc's cooldowns last longer |
| **Protection** | **3/5**{ .stars .s3 } | Eureka! makes Shield Slam and Revenge cheaper on the pull. No passive mitigation |

**Engineering pairing:** Mining and Engineering gives you nets, bombs and gadgets for PvP, and the racial reduces failures. Blacksmithing remains the PvE choice.

### Leveling checklist

- [ ] Defensive Stance quest at **10**, Berserker Stance quest at **30**
- [ ] Cooking and *The Great Outdoors* for camping by level 5–6
- [ ] First Aid trained early: it now makes health potions as well as bandages
- [ ] Collect class library books for the quests at 20, 35 and 45
- [ ] **Mechanostrider** and Apprentice Riding at 40 (100g); plate and dual spec (50g) at the same time
- [ ] Warrior Legacy Points at 25, 45 and 60 (account-wide, first Warrior only)

## Verdict

A fun and genuinely viable PvP Warrior thanks to Escape Artist and Eureka!. In PvE it falls a little behind Human and Dwarf, which get passive crit or hit. Pick it if you like the look, or if you want to pair it with Engineering.
