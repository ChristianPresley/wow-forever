## Why this combo

In the past, players chose Gnome Warriors as a joke. Forever gives them a real damage cooldown: for 8 s, **Eureka!** makes non-periodic abilities cost 10% less and do 10% more damage. With **Escape Artist** against snares, the Gnome is a good PvP race with a small burst window.

- **Best at:** PvP (Escape Artist), burst windows with Mortal Strike or Bloodthirst.
- **Status:** available in Classic. Not a new combination.

## Starting zone walkthrough (1–12)

Gnomes start in **Coldridge Valley**, **Dun Morogh**, with Dwarves. *The quest and NPC names are from the Classic era. Make sure in the game.*

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **1–5**{ .lvl } **Coldridge Valley.** Speak to the Warrior trainer at Anvilmar. Kill wolves and troggs. Gnome refugees from Gnomeregan give more quests. **Charge** and **Rend** at 4.
2.  **~5**{ .lvl } **The Great Outdoors.** Camping intro and Cooking.
3.  **5–10**{ .lvl } **Kharanos and Brewnall.** Do the Kharanos inn quests and the Gnomeregan refugee chain. **Hamstring** at 8.
4.  **10**{ .lvl } **Defensive Stance** quest from your trainer. If you plan to be an engineer, train Engineering in Tinker Town, Ironforge.
5.  **First talent at 10.** Protection: **Shield Specialization**. Arms: **Improved Rend**.
6.  **10–13.** Complete Dun Morogh. Queue for **[Hall of Thanes](../../content/dungeons/hall-of-thanes.md)** in Ironforge.

</div>

## Faction-specific notes

- **Mount:** Mechanostrider, from Steelgrill's Depot in Dun Morogh. Your home reputation is the **Gnomeregan Exiles**. The Stormwind and Ironforge Honored discounts apply to Riding. Check if the Gnomeregan Exiles discount also applies (*unconfirmed*).
- **Engineering:** Gnome Warriors are the natural engineers. Nets, bombs and the Gnomish gadgets are good for PvP, and the racial decreases failures.
- **Size:** the small model makes it more difficult for enemies to see and target you in battlegrounds. It is a cosmetic advantage, but it is real.

## Spec fit

| Spec | Fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Arms** | **4/5**{ .stars .s4 } | Eureka! plus Mortal Strike is a real kill window in PvP. Escape Artist keeps you on your target |
| **Fury** | **3/5**{ .stars .s3 } | Eureka! increases Bloodthirst and Whirlwind for 8 s. Good, but the Night Elf and Orc cooldowns last longer |
| **Protection** | **3/5**{ .stars .s3 } | Eureka! makes Shield Slam and Revenge cheaper on the pull. No passive mitigation |

**Engineering pairing:** Mining and Engineering give you nets, bombs and gadgets for PvP, and the racial decreases failures. Blacksmithing is still the PvE choice.

### Leveling checklist

- [ ] Defensive Stance quest at **10**, Berserker Stance quest at **30**
- [ ] Cooking and *The Great Outdoors* for camping by level 5–6
- [ ] Train First Aid early: it now makes health potions and bandages
- [ ] Collect class library books for the quests at 20, 35 and 45
- [ ] **Mechanostrider** and Apprentice Riding at 40 (100g). Plate and dual spec (50g) at the same level
- [ ] Warrior Legacy Points at 25, 45 and 60 (account-wide, first Warrior only)

## Verdict

<div class="verdict" markdown>

Because of Escape Artist and Eureka!, it is a fun and really viable PvP Warrior. In PvE, it is a little weaker than Human and Dwarf, which get passive crit or hit. Choose it if you like the look, or if you want to use it with Engineering.

</div>
