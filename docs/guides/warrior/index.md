## Before level 1: Make your Warrior { #start }

A Warrior fights in melee range and uses rage. You get rage when you hit an enemy and when an enemy hits you. Rage starts at 0 and decreases out of combat, so each fight starts slowly.

The class has 3 stances. Battle Stance is the first stance. You get Defensive Stance at level 10 and Berserker Stance at level 30. Each stance permits different abilities. Forever makes stance changes cheaper than Classic, and Protection can stay in 1 stance.

A Warrior can do 2 jobs. Protection uses a shield and tanks for a group. Arms uses a two-hand weapon. Fury uses 2 weapons. In Forever, Protection is also a good solo leveling tree, because blocks give rage and Revenge does damage.

Your weapon is more important than for other classes. Method estimates that almost all your leveling damage comes from the weapon. Thus each equipment set on this page starts with the weapon.

Players in the beta reported the Warrior as one of the 2 slowest classes to level. Elite quests and dungeon quests give much more XP in Forever. Thus a group helps a Warrior more than it helps other classes.

You make 3 decisions before level 1: your race, your talent path and your equipment set. You can change the talents and the equipment later. You cannot change the race.

## Levels 1–9: Charge, Rend and your first weapons { #l1 }

You start in Battle Stance with Heroic Strike and Battle Shout. Keep Battle Shout on you. Heroic Strike replaces your next swing and costs rage, so use it only with rage that you do not need.

Level 4 gives Charge and Rend. Charge is your pull: it moves you to the enemy and gives rage. Rend is a Bleed that scales with attack power in Forever. A Bleed does its damage during some seconds. Use Charge, then Rend. After that, your weapon swings do most of the damage.

A solo kill of a usual enemy takes 10 to 15 seconds. You have no heal. Fight 1 enemy at a time, and eat when your health is low. Hamstring at level 8 slows an enemy that runs away.

Take each weapon reward. When you get a better weapon, sell the old one. Look at the Auction House for a weapon at each level. The game no longer adds new abilities to your action bars. After each visit to the trainer, move the new abilities from the spellbook to a bar.

Learn Cooking and do the quest *The Great Outdoors* at about level 5. It unlocks [camping](../../systems/camping.md), and the campfire gives rested XP. Learn First Aid also, because it now makes healing potions and bandages.

| Level | You train | Why it is important |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Heroic Strike, Battle Shout** | Your first attack and your first buff. You start in Battle Stance |
| 4 | **Charge, Rend** | Charge starts each fight with rage. Rend scales with attack power in Forever |
| 6 | **Thunder Clap** | Damage to all enemies near you. Classic-era level |
| 8 | **Hamstring** | A slow for enemies that run away, and for PvP |

!!! tip "Tip"
    Use Charge to start each fight. In Classic it works only out of combat, so do not start a fight with a ranged pull.

!!! hardcore "Hardcore"
    You cannot heal and you cannot escape. Do not fight 2 enemies at these levels. Run before your health is below 50%.

## Levels 10–19: Defensive Stance and your first dungeon { #l10 }

At level 10 your trainer gives the Defensive Stance quest. Do it immediately. It gives Defensive Stance, Sunder Armor and Taunt. The next quest in the chain gives a one-hand weapon. You also get your first talent point, and then 1 point at each level.

Now you have 2 stances, and each stance has different abilities. Charge needs Battle Stance. Revenge and Taunt need Defensive Stance. A stance change removes your rage until level 14. Then Tactical Mastery lets you keep 10 rage.

Overpower at 12 and Revenge at 14 are reactive abilities. Overpower becomes available when the enemy dodges. Revenge becomes available when you block, dodge or parry. They are cheap and strong, so put them on keys that you can reach quickly.

Your first dungeons open at level 13. Quests give much more XP than dungeon enemies, so do each dungeon for its quests. Meeting Stones cannot summon, so you must walk to the entrance.

A group expects a Warrior to tank, which means that the enemies attack you. An enemy attacks the player with the most threat. Use Defensive Stance and a shield, because Defensive Stance gives 80% more threat. Thus 1 target is easy to hold. On a group of enemies, use Thunder Clap and Demoralizing Shout. Then use Sunder Armor on each enemy in turn.

At level 14, start the scavenger hunt for the Cozy Sleeping Bag. It gives an XP buff and rested XP. See [Leveling](../../characters/leveling.md).

| Level | You train | Why it is important |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | **Defensive Stance, Sunder Armor, Taunt** | From the class quest. The tank stance and your first threat tools |
| 10 | **Bloodrage** | Rage for a small cost of health. Classic-era level |
| 12 | **Overpower** | Available after the enemy dodges. The main Arms ability for many levels |
| 12 | **Shield Bash** | Stops a cast. It needs a shield. Classic-era level |
| 14 | **Revenge, Demoralizing Shout** | Revenge is available after a block, a dodge or a parry |
| 14 | **Tactical Mastery** | Baseline in Forever. You keep 10 rage when you change stance |
| 16 | **Shield Block** | More blocks for a short time. Each block makes Revenge available. Classic-era level |

## Levels 20–29: Victory Rush, Dual Wield and polearms { #l20 }

Level 20 gives you Victory Rush. It is baseline in Forever, has a 30 second cooldown and heals 10% of your health. Use it on each kill. It is the first heal of the class, and it decreases the time that you eat.

You also train Retaliation at 20. It no longer shares a cooldown with Shield Wall and Recklessness. From level 20 a weapon master can teach polearms, and the Arms talent Weaponmaster gives crit to polearms. Warcraft Tavern says that you train Dual Wield from your class trainer at level 20.

Your talents now start to change how you fight. Each talent path gets the talent that defines it in these levels, and the Talents add-on gives the details. Execute at level 24 is the same for each path: use it on an enemy below 20% health.

At 20, turn in 10 library books to unlock the first book quest. Timesaver.gg lists the quest *Friend of the Library* with the necklace Erudite's Amulet. Warcraft Tavern lists class quests for mail armor at level 20. Timesaver.gg lists that armor only from level 30. Keep each class book that drops, because the later book quests need 20 and 25 books.

Level 25 gives the *Warrior: Novice* Legacy Point. Only the first Warrior on your account gets it. See [Legacy](../../characters/legacy.md).

| Level | You train | Why it is important |
|---|---|---|
| 20 | **Victory Rush** | Baseline in Forever. A 10% heal on a 30 second cooldown |
| 20 | **Retaliation** | An emergency cooldown against melee enemies. 15 minute cooldown |
| 20 | **Dual Wield** | Warcraft Tavern gives this level. No second source confirms it |
| 20 | **Cleave** | Your swing hits 2 enemies. Classic-era level |
| 24 | **Execute** | High damage to a target below 20% health. Classic-era level |
| 28 | **Shield Wall** | Your largest defensive cooldown. Classic-era level |

## Levels 30–39: Berserker Stance and the Whirlwind weapon { #l30 }

At level 30 your trainer sends you to Fray Island near Ratchet. The quest *The Affray* gives Berserker Stance, your third stance. Warcraft Tavern says that it also gives Intercept. Intercept is a second Charge that works in combat.

The quest chain continues in the Alterac Mountains with Bath'rah the Windwatcher. At the end you fight Cyclonian and select 1 of 3 two-hand weapons: Whirlwind Axe, Whirlwind Sword or Whirlwind Warhammer. Timesaver.gg still lists the Whirlwind Axe as the best two-hander at level 40. Do the chain on each path, because you can use the weapon in a second spec later. Method says that the chain is very hard at level 30. Take a friend, or buy the reagents from the Auction House.

Whirlwind at level 36 hits all enemies near you. It needs Berserker Stance. Arms and Fury now change stance in a fight: Battle Stance for Charge and Overpower, Berserker Stance for Whirlwind and Intercept.

The new battleground Battle for the Darkspear Islands opens at level 30. It has level brackets of 10 levels, so you fight players of levels 30 to 39.

Level 35 gives 3 more steps. The second book quest needs 20 library books. Artisan profession training opens. The new zone Riverglades opens at about this level, and it has more than 150 quests.

Start to save gold now. At level 40 you need 100g for riding, and dual spec costs 50g.

## Levels 40–49: Plate, a mount and a second spec { #l40 }

Level 40 is the largest step of the progression. Your class trainer teaches plate armor. You can buy riding for 100g, and the price includes your first mount. You can buy dual spec for 50g from your class trainer. Dual spec is a second set of talents, and you can change between the 2 sets.

Each talent path also gets its main ability at level 40: Shield Slam, Mortal Strike or Bloodthirst. Each costs rage and has a 6 second cooldown. From now on, you plan each fight around that cooldown.

Dual spec has much value for a Warrior. Keep Protection in 1 spec for dungeons and for safe solo play. Use the second spec for Arms or Fury. A tank finds a group quickly. The price of a respec decreases to 1g with time.

Replace your mail with plate step by step. Do not give up a better weapon for a plate item. Crafted Mithril plate from Blacksmithing fills the first slots.

At 45, the third book quest needs 25 library books. Level 45 also gives the *Warrior: Experienced* Legacy Point.

| Level | You train | Why it is important |
|---|---|---|
| 40 | **Plate armor** | From your class trainer. Replace mail step by step |

## Levels 50–59: Recklessness, hit and the last dungeons { #l50 }

The last 10 levels have the most dungeons. Reportedly, each boss in the new dungeons drops a blue item. Run Sunken Temple, Blackrock Depths and Lower Blackrock Spire for equipment while you level. Tank each dungeon that you can, because a tank finds a group quickly.

The talent points of these levels do not change how you fight. They add crit, rage or defense to the abilities that you have. The Talents add-on gives the order.

Hit becomes important now. Dungeon bosses have a higher level than you, and a miss gives no damage and no rage. Method says that items with hit are rare before level 50.

Recklessness is the third large cooldown of the class. It does not share a cooldown with Retaliation and Shield Wall, so you can use all 3 in 1 fight.

As a damage dealer in a group, let the tank get threat for 2 or 3 seconds. Death Wish increases the damage that you take. Do not use it on a pull that is not safe.

| Level | You train | Why it is important |

| 50 | **Recklessness** | Your damage cooldown for boss fights. Classic-era level |

## Level 60: The Drakefire Amulet, Defense and hit { #l60 }

At level 60 you get the *Warrior: Master* Legacy Point. Mount Hyjal opens through Darkwhisper Gorge in Winterspring. The fast mount costs 1,000g.

All 3 launch raids open on 9 December 2026. The raids have 1 difficulty. Use the time before that date for 4 tasks. Respec to a level 60 build, which means that you learn your talents again. Get a weapon. Collect consumables. Do the attunement for Onyxia's Lair, which is the quest chain that lets you enter the raid.

The attunement gives the Drakefire Amulet. Its quest chain ends in Upper Blackrock Spire. The amulet must be in your bags, and it is not account-wide.

No source has a tested item list or a stat ranking for level 60. A tank needs 440 Defense to be immune to crits from raid bosses (Warcraft Tavern). A damage dealer needs hit: 8% with 1 weapon and 29% with 2 weapons (Method.gg). Warcraft Tavern gives 9% for 1 weapon.

The consumables are health potions from First Aid, weapon stones from Blacksmithing, Rage potions, elixirs and food. The Legacy talent Gourmand makes food buffs last longer. World buffs from the Chronoboon Displacer do not work in dungeons and raids.

Rewards from reputation and Honor fill the slots that the dungeons do not fill. Blue PvP items can be good first raid equipment.

## Raids and PvP: Tank or damage dealer in the 3 raids { #raid }

**The Barrow Deeps** has 10 players. A group probably takes 1 tank Warrior and 1 or 2 Warriors for damage. In a small raid, a second tank that also does damage has much value. See [The Barrow Deeps](../../content/raids/barrow-deeps.md).

**Hyjal Summit** has 20 players and 13 bosses on routes that branch. Expect 2 or 3 tanks. If the raid divides, each group needs a tank. See [Hyjal Summit](../../content/raids/hyjal-summit.md).

**Onyxia's Lair** has 40 players. The main tank holds Onyxia, and fire resistance helps. Onyxia is Dragonkin, so Spearing Strike does 75% more damage to her. See [Onyxia's Lair](../../content/raids/onyxias-lair.md).

In PvP, Arms is the usual spec. Mortal Strike decreases healing by 50%. Hamstring keeps you near the target. Spearing Strike dismounts riders and does 75% more damage to them. Victory Rush heals you after each kill.

The new battleground is the Battle for the Darkspear Islands. It has 15 players on each side, 4 bases and a flag in the center. A Warrior is a good flag carrier and base defender there, and Spearing Strike hits the players that ride between the bases. Fan sites say that Warsong Gulch, Arathi Basin and Alterac Valley are also in the game. See [Battlegrounds](../../content/pvp/battlegrounds.md).

PvP has seasons. You start each season at Rank 1 and can go up to Rank 14, and Honor buys blue and epic sets. On a PvP realm, other players can attack you almost everywhere. Charge and Intercept win fights against casters that do not look for you. See [PvP](../../content/pvp/index.md).

The table gives Classic-era advice. No source has tested these fights in Forever.

| Enemy in PvP | What you do |
|---|---|
| Mage | The hardest enemy. Keep Intercept for the time after Blink. Use a trinket or a racial against Polymorph and Frost Nova |
| Rogue | Use Mortal Strike and Hamstring. Retaliation and your racial stop the burst |
| Hunter | Get to melee range quickly. Use Intercept after Concussive Shot. Stay out of the line of sight of the pet |
| Priest, Shaman, Druid | Use Mortal Strike against the heals. Stay near them through Fear and roots |
| Paladin | A long fight. Keep your burst for the time when Divine Shield is on cooldown |
| Warrior | The Warrior that hits first with Mortal Strike and Hamstring usually wins |

!!! hardcore "Hardcore"
    [Hardcore](../../about/hardcore.md) comes in winter 2026/27. Level with Protection and a shield. You kill more slowly, but you are much safer. Carry bandages and potions, and use Victory Rush to restore health.
    
    Select a race with a survival racial. For the Alliance: Will to Survive (Human), Stoneform (Dwarf), Escape Artist (Gnome) or Shadowmeld (Night Elf). For the Horde: War Stomp (Tauren) or Will of the Forsaken (Undead).
    
    Legacy Points that you get on Hardcore apply to the other rulesets. Legacy Points from other rulesets do not apply to Hardcore.

## Common mistakes

- You keep a quest weapon for too long. Look at the Auction House at each level.
- You use Heroic Strike when you do not have extra rage.
- You do not learn First Aid. It now makes the healing potions.
- You forget the stance quests at level 10 and level 30.
- You tank without a shield. Defiance, Bastion and Master of Defense need a shield.
- You use 2 weapons at a low level. Each miss gives no rage.
- You forget that the game no longer adds new abilities to your action bars.

## FAQ

??? question "Why do I have no rage?"
    At low levels, the cause is usually your weapon. A slow weapon with high damage gives more rage for each swing. Use Bloodrage, and take Shield Specialization or Improved Overpower. Do not use rage on enemies that are almost dead.

??? question "Is Protection good for leveling?"
    In Forever, yes. Shield Specialization gives rage, Revenge does damage, Thunder Clap works in Defensive Stance, and Vanguard permits Charge in Defensive Stance. Method recommends it for solo play. It is slow before level 25.

??? question "Must I still change stances?"
    Less than in Classic. Tactical Mastery is baseline and keeps 10 rage. Arms changes to Battle Stance for Overpower. Protection with Vanguard almost never changes stance.

??? question "Bloodthrill or Weaponmaster?"
    You can take the 2 talents. Warcraft Tavern takes Bloodthrill for more Overpower uses, and Method takes the 2 talents. A full Arms build has the points for 2/2 Bloodthrill and 5/5 Weaponmaster. When you level, take Bloodthrill first.

??? question "What does Reckless Swings do?"
    In the launch client, your next 4 melee attacks give 5 more rage, with a 15 second cooldown. The heal that some sources describe is a different talent, Gore Drinker. The client does not show a clear cost.

??? question "Do the Arms guides agree on the leveling build?"
    No. Warcraft Tavern stays in the Arms tree. Method puts points into Fury for Unbridled Wrath and Enrage after Mortal Strike. The Arms path on this page follows Method.

??? question "Do the Protection guides agree on the leveling build?"
    Almost. Warcraft Tavern takes Anticipation and Defiance earlier than Method. The 2 builds agree on the order Shield Specialization, Master of Defense, Vanguard. The Protection path on this page follows Method.

??? question "Is Skyborne a good Warrior race?"
    It is good for leveling, because of haste, a glide and a speed or regeneration cooldown. Warcraft Tavern rates it weak for Warriors in comparison with Orc, Human, Troll and Tauren. Select it for its appearance and its story.
