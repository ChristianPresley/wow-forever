# Night Elf Warrior

!!! tip "Add-on"
    Read with the [Warrior guide](index.md). This page covers only what changes for a Night Elf.

## Why this combo

Forever gives Night Elves a proper damage cooldown: **Elune's Light**, +10% crit for 15 s. Crits generate +75% rage in Forever, so it's a rage cooldown too. **Shadowmeld** doubles as a threat drop in groups, and **Quickness** adds dodge for tanks.

- **Best at:** burst damage, and tanking with a little extra avoidance.
- **Status:** available in Classic; not a new combination.

## Racials in practice

| Racial | Type | Rating | PvE | PvP |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Elune's Light** *(new)* | Active | ★★★★★ | +10% crit for 15 s. Use it with Death Wish or Recklessness; tanks can use it for a rage and threat spike on a pull | A burst window for Mortal Strike |
| **Shadowmeld** | Active | ★★★☆☆ | Drop out of combat to drink or eat, or dump threat as DPS *(Classic behavior; check in game)* | Ambush flag carriers; break targeting |
| **Quickness** | Passive | ★★★☆☆ | +1% dodge (tanks) and +2% run speed | The speed helps you stay on targets |
| **Wisp Spirit** | Passive | ★★☆☆☆ | Faster corpse runs | Same |

## Starting zone walkthrough (1–12)

Night Elves start in **Shadowglen**, **Teldrassil**. Blizzard says Shadowglen got the same refresh as the Valley of Trials, with new NPCs, quests and profession hooks. *Quest and NPC names are Classic-era; check in game.*

1. **Shadowglen (1–5).** Speak to the Warrior trainer near Aldrassil (Classic: Alyissia). Clear grells, nightsabers and the Webwood spiders. **Charge** and **Rend** at 4.
2. **The Great Outdoors (~5).** Camping intro and Cooking.
3. **Dolanaar (5–10).** Do the Dolanaar quests around the furbolgs and Ban'ethil Barrow Den. There's a Warrior trainer in Dolanaar. **Hamstring** at 8.
4. **Level 10.** **Defensive Stance** quest from your trainer (it may send you to Darnassus).
5. **First talent at 10.** Protection: **Shield Specialization**. Arms: **Improved Rend**.
6. **10–12.** Take the portal from Darnassus to Rut'theran and the boat to **Auberdine** (Darkshore).

## Route to 60 from Darnassus

| Levels | Zones | Flight paths / notes |
|---|---|---|
| 10–20 | Darkshore → Loch Modan or Westfall → Redridge | Auberdine. The new **Stormwind harbor** boat links Auberdine to Stormwind |
| 20–30 | Ashenvale → Wetlands → Duskwood | Astranaar, Menethil. Blackfathom Deeps (Ashenvale) |
| 30–40 | Stonetalon → Hillsbrad → Arathi → Stranglethorn → Desolace | Stonetalon Peak, Nijel's Point |
| 35–45 | **[Riverglades](../../world/zones.md#riverglades)** or Dustwallow Marsh → Feralas | The boat loop Auberdine–Menethil–Southshore is handy for crossing over |
| 45–55 | Feralas → Tanaris → Azshara → Un'Goro | Feathermoon, Talrendis Point |
| 55–60 | Felwood → Winterspring → Silithus → Mount Hyjal | Talonbranch Glade, Everlook. Hyjal is a Night Elf story zone |

**Class trainers:** the **Warrior's Terrace** in Darnassus.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Mount:** Frostsaber, from Rut'theran Village. **Honored with Darnassus** gives 10% off. Darnassus also has a new barbershop.
- **Travel:** the new Stormwind harbor boat and the Menethil–Southshore–Auberdine loop make Night Elf travel far less painful than in Classic.
- **Lore:** Mount Hyjal, the Barrow Deeps and Hyjal Summit are Night Elf history. Your character has a direct stake in the endgame story.

## Spec fit

| Spec | Fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Fury** | ★★★★★ | Elune's Light's +10% crit feeds Flurry, Enrage and rage for 15 s: the strongest Alliance burst cooldown |
| **Arms** | ★★★★☆ | Big Mortal Strike crits; Shadowmeld ambushes in battlegrounds |
| **Protection** | ★★★★☆ | Quickness adds dodge; Elune's Light on the pull gives a fast threat lead |

**Group tip:** if Shadowmeld drops threat in Forever (as in Classic), use it as a DPS when you pull aggro off the tank. Test it on a trash mob first.

### Leveling checklist

- [ ] Defensive Stance quest at **10**, Berserker Stance quest at **30**
- [ ] Cooking and *The Great Outdoors* for camping by level 5–6
- [ ] First Aid trained early: it now makes health potions as well as bandages
- [ ] Collect class library books for the quests at 20, 35 and 45
- [ ] **Frostsaber** and Apprentice Riding at 40 (100g); plate and dual spec (50g) at the same time
- [ ] Warrior Legacy Points at 25, 45 and 60 (account-wide, first Warrior only)

## Verdict

Strong in PvE thanks to Elune's Light, decent as a tank thanks to Quickness, and fun in PvP with Shadowmeld. It's a very good choice if you want a cooldown-heavy damage race on the Alliance side.
