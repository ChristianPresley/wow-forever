## Why this combo

**NEW in Forever.** The High Order descend from Highborne magisters, and most of them are mages. But their militias (the Peacekeepers and Empyrean Blades) also need fighters. A Skyborne Warrior gets a unique starting zone, a passive haste bonus and a glide. Warcraft Tavern rates Skyborne **weak for Warriors** compared with Human, Dwarf or Night Elf. Thus, choose it for the look and the story.

- **Best at:** leveling convenience and mobility.
- **Base stats (Warrior):** 20 Str, 24 Agi, 24 Int, 21 Sta, 17 Spi *(per the wiki's research notes)*. Blizzard corrected Skyborne base stats before launch.

## Starting zone walkthrough (1–12)

Every Skyborne starts in **Thendal Village** on **[Zephras Isle](../../world/zephras-isle.md)**, a floating island in Skywall. The zone takes you to about level 12–14. Both factions share it, but they are in different phases.

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **1–5**{ .lvl } **Thendal Village.** *Coming of Age*, *Harmony in Balance*, *The Cirrusfly Queen* and the Ursera bear chain. *Harvesting Windstones* gives a voucher for a gathering profession. Your Alliance opener is *Reading the Leylines*. Learn **Charge** and **Rend** at 4 from the Warrior trainer in the village (*exact location unconfirmed*).
2.  **5–8**{ .lvl } **Road to Shen'dar Village.** Fight Al'Aketh thugs. *Blessing of Zephras* tornadoes give +40% speed. **The Great Outdoors** (Raan Wildwind) teaches camping. Shen'dar Village has bandit, hippogryph and Prideclaw quests, and also the "Badwind" Bennic and Vulgara wanted posters. **Hamstring** at 8.
3.  **8–10**{ .lvl } **Falaath Village.** Infiltrate the cult in disguise, then kill its commanders.
4.  **10**{ .lvl } **Valanaar.** Hand the signet to Talaanis Shadowsong and meet **Elaadrin Evengale**. Valanaar has trainers and a bank. Do your **Defensive Stance** quest here (*details unconfirmed for Skyborne*). First talent: **Shield Specialization** (Prot) or **Improved Rend** (Arms).
5.  **10–12**{ .lvl } **Gustberry Lowlands and Shadowgale Forest.** The High Order chain *The Missing Scholar* and the Index Esoteria chain. `/sing` at the frogs for a secret pet.
6.  **12–14**{ .lvl } **The confrontation.** Assault the Shrine of Akir and face Lorthuna and Rohash. *What Comes Next* sends you away from the island.

</div>

## Route to 60 from Dalaran

The High Order airship lands in north **Dalaran** in the Alterac Mountains, a level 30–40 zone. Do not quest there yet.

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **12–15**{ .lvl } Walk south to **Southshore**. Take the new pier boat to Menethil (Wetlands), then continue to Ironforge or Stormwind

    <span class="step-label">Flight paths / notes</span> Get all flight paths on the way

2.  **15–20**{ .lvl } Loch Modan → Westfall → Redridge

    <span class="step-label">Flight paths / notes</span> [Hall of Thanes](../../content/dungeons/hall-of-thanes.md), Deadmines

3.  **20–30**{ .lvl } Duskwood → Wetlands → Ashenvale

    <span class="step-label">Flight paths / notes</span> [Excavation Site](../../content/dungeons/excavation-site.md)

4.  **30–40**{ .lvl } Hillsbrad → **Alterac Mountains (Dalaran)** → Arathi → Stranglethorn

    <span class="step-label">Flight paths / notes</span> [City of Dalaran](../../content/dungeons/city-of-dalaran.md). Kirin Tor reputation is Alliance-only

5.  **35–45**{ .lvl } **[Riverglades](../../world/zones.md#riverglades)** (Farholde Keep) → Dustwallow Marsh

6.  **45–60**{ .lvl } Tanaris → Feralas → Azshara → Un'Goro → Plaguelands → Winterspring → Mount Hyjal

</div>

**Class trainers:** Valanaar on Zephras Isle, and the Warrior trainers in Stormwind, Ironforge and Darnassus.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Mount:** the **Galestrider**, at **Fairweather Stables** on Zephras Isle (53.8, 81.3), in Umber, Stormy and Empyrean. High Order reputation gives 5/10/15/20% off, from Friendly to Exalted.
- **Reputation:** High Order (Zephras quests and cloth turn-ins in Dalaran), and the **Kirin Tor** in Dalaran. Only the Alliance can earn Kirin Tor reputation. Nightclaw druid reputation comes from a repeatable quest on Zephras.
- **Customization:** HD models only at launch. SD models are expected in early 2027. Feathers and tattoos are unique to the race.
- **Language:** Darnassian and Common. You can speak Darnassian with Horde Skyborne and Night Elves.

## Spec fit

| Spec | Fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Fury** | **3/5**{ .stars .s3 } | Wind Blessed's haste gives more swings and more Flurry. There is no burst cooldown |
| **Arms** | **3/5**{ .stars .s3 } | Good for leveling. Walk on Air helps you escape in PvP, but it does not help you kill |
| **Protection** | **2/5**{ .stars .s2 } | No mitigation or health racial |

**Leveling tip:** With Walk on Air, you can glide from cliffs directly to your next quest, mainly in Alterac, Hillsbrad and Stonetalon. Read Ley Line decreases your eating time after hard pulls.

### Leveling checklist

- [ ] Defensive Stance quest at **10**, Berserker Stance quest at **30**
- [ ] Cooking and *The Great Outdoors* for camping by level 5–6
- [ ] First Aid trained early: it now makes health potions as well as bandages
- [ ] Collect class library books for the quests at 20, 35 and 45
- [ ] **Galestrider** and Apprentice Riding at 40 (100g), and plate and dual spec (50g) at the same time
- [ ] Warrior Legacy Points at 25, 45 and 60 (account-wide, first Warrior only)

## Verdict

<div class="verdict" markdown>

A good-looking, comfortable leveler with a unique starting zone. For a Warrior, its racials are weaker than Human, Dwarf or Night Elf racials. Most of them give utility, not damage or survival. If you play for the Skyborne story, choose it. If you min-max, choose a different race.

</div>
