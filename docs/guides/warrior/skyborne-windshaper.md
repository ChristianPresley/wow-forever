# Skyborne (Windshaper) Warrior

!!! tip "Add-on"
    Read with the [Warrior guide](index.md). This page covers only what changes for a Horde Skyborne.

!!! warning "Paid race"
    Skyborne require the **Skyborne Heroic Pack** or a higher edition. See [Editions](../../about/editions.md) and [Skyborne](../../characters/races.md#skyborne).

## Why this combo

**NEW in Forever.** The Windshapers descend from ordinary kaldorei citizens and follow a shamanic path, but their militias fight too. A Windshaper Warrior gets Zephras Isle, a glide, passive haste and **Skysight**, a speed burst that a Warrior can actually use to close gaps. Warcraft Tavern rates Skyborne **weak for Warriors** compared with Orc, Troll or Tauren.

- **Best at:** mobility and leveling convenience.
- **Base stats (Warrior):** 20 Str, 24 Agi, 24 Int, 21 Sta, 17 Spi *(per the wiki's research notes)*. Blizzard corrected Skyborne base stats before launch.

## Racials in practice

| Racial | Type | Rating | PvE | PvP |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Walk on Air** | Active | **3/5**{ .stars .s3 } | A 10 s controlled glide. Skip paths, survive falls | Escape off ledges in battlegrounds |
| **Skysight** | Active | **3/5**{ .stars .s3 } | +15% speed for 15 s (+30% while *Charged* at an elemental convergence). Faster travel between quests | A gap closer when Charge and Intercept are down, or a way to catch a flag carrier |
| **Wind Blessed** | Passive | **3/5**{ .stars .s3 } | +haste (2% in the latest notes). More swings, more rage | Same |
| **Elemental Insight** | Passive | **2/5**{ .stars .s2 } | +5% damage vs Elementals | None |

!!! conflict "Conflicts"
    Wind Blessed is listed as 1%, 2% or 3% haste; the latest official notes say **2%**. Walk on Air's cooldown is given as 1.5 or 2 minutes.

## Starting zone walkthrough (1–12)

Every Skyborne starts in **Thendal Village** on **[Zephras Isle](../../world/zephras-isle.md)**. The zone takes you to about 12–14, phased apart from the Alliance.

1. **Thendal Village (1–5).** *Coming of Age*, *Harmony in Balance*, *The Cirrusfly Queen* and the Ursera bear chain. *Harvesting Windstones* gives a gathering-profession voucher. Your Horde opener is *The Gift of Skysight*. **Charge** and **Rend** at 4 from the village's Warrior trainer (*exact location unconfirmed*).
2. **Road to Shen'dar Village (5–8).** Al'Aketh thugs, *Blessing of Zephras* tornadoes (+40% speed) and **The Great Outdoors** (Raan Wildwind) for camping. Shen'dar Village's bandit and Prideclaw quests. **Hamstring** at 8.
3. **Falaath Village (8–10).** Infiltrate the cult and kill its commanders.
4. **Valanaar (10).** Meet Talaanis Shadowsong and **Grand Skyseer Ayessa Dawnsinger**. Do the **Defensive Stance** quest here (*details unconfirmed for Skyborne*). First talent: **Shield Specialization** (Prot) or **Improved Rend** (Arms).
5. **Gustberry Lowlands and Shadowgale Forest (10–12).** Windshaper chains *The Broken Construct*, then *In Service of Zephras* → *Blood for Blood*. Nightclaw druid quests for reputation.
6. **The confrontation (12–14).** Shrine of Akir, Lorthuna and Rohash. *What Comes Next* sends you off the island.

## Route to 60 from Thunder Bluff

The Windshaper airship lands at **Skywatcher Plateau** in northwest Mulgore, just outside **Thunder Bluff**. That's an easy start for the Horde.

| Levels | Zones | Flight paths / notes |
|---|---|---|
| 12–20 | Mulgore (catch up on quests if under-leveled) → the Barrens → Stonetalon | Thunder Bluff, Crossroads. Wailing Caverns, Ragefire Chasm |
| 20–30 | Stonetalon → Ashenvale → Thousand Needles → Hillsbrad | Razorfen Kraul, Shadowfang Keep, [Ruins of Lordaeron](../../content/dungeons/ruins-of-lordaeron.md) |
| 30–40 | Desolace → Arathi → Stranglethorn | Avoid Dalaran: hostile to the Horde. [The Drowned City](../../content/dungeons/drowned-city.md) |
| 35–45 | **[Riverglades](../../world/zones.md#riverglades)** (Rog'mar) → Dustwallow Marsh | Shen'dralas, reached from southern Desolace, has ties to Skyborne history |
| 45–60 | Tanaris → Feralas → Azshara → Un'Goro → Plaguelands → Winterspring → Mount Hyjal | |

**Class trainers:** Valanaar on Zephras Isle, then **Hunter Rise** in Thunder Bluff (closest to Skywatcher Plateau) or Orgrimmar's Valley of Honor.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Mount:** the **Galestrider**, at **Fairweather Stables** on Zephras Isle (53.8, 81.3), in Umber, Stormy and Empyrean. Windshaper reputation gives 5/10/15/20% off from Friendly to Exalted.
- **Reputation:** Windshapers (Zephras quests and cloth turn-ins in Mulgore) and Nightclaw. The Windshapers are allied with the **Earthen Ring**, which is why Muln Earthfury shows up in the finale.
- **Customization:** HD models only at launch; SD models are expected in early 2027.
- **Language:** Darnassian and Orcish. You can speak Darnassian with Alliance Skyborne and Night Elves.

## Spec fit

| Spec | Fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Arms** | **3/5**{ .stars .s3 } | Skysight is a useful extra gap closer in PvP |
| **Fury** | **3/5**{ .stars .s3 } | Wind Blessed haste means more swings and Flurry procs |
| **Protection** | **2/5**{ .stars .s2 } | No mitigation or health racial |

**Leveling tip:** chain Skysight and the Zephras tornadoes early, and use Walk on Air to cut corners in Thousand Needles and Stonetalon.

### Leveling checklist

- [ ] Defensive Stance quest at **10**, Berserker Stance quest at **30**
- [ ] Cooking and *The Great Outdoors* for camping by level 5–6
- [ ] First Aid trained early: it now makes health potions as well as bandages
- [ ] Collect class library books for the quests at 20, 35 and 45
- [ ] **Galestrider** and Apprentice Riding at 40 (100g); plate and dual spec (50g) at the same time
- [ ] Warrior Legacy Points at 25, 45 and 60 (account-wide, first Warrior only)

## Verdict

A comfortable leveler that starts right next to Thunder Bluff. Skysight gives it a little more PvP use than the High Order version. Even so, Orc, Troll, Tauren and Undead all have stronger Warrior racials. Pick Windshaper for the story and the look.
