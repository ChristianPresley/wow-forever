## Why this combo

**NEW in Forever.** The Windshapers descend from ordinary kaldorei citizens and follow a shamanic path. But their militias also fight. A Windshaper Warrior gets Zephras Isle, a glide, passive haste and **Skysight**. Skysight is a speed burst that a Warrior can really use to close gaps. Warcraft Tavern rates Skyborne **weak for Warriors** compared with Orc, Troll or Tauren.

- **Best at:** mobility and leveling convenience.
- **Base stats (Warrior):** 20 Str, 24 Agi, 24 Int, 21 Sta, 17 Spi *(per the wiki's research notes)*. Blizzard corrected Skyborne base stats before launch.

## Starting zone walkthrough (1–12)

Every Skyborne starts in **Thendal Village** on **[Zephras Isle](../../world/zephras-isle.md)**. The zone takes you to about level 12–14. You are in a different phase from the Alliance.

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **1–5**{ .lvl } **Thendal Village.** *Coming of Age*, *Harmony in Balance*, *The Cirrusfly Queen* and the Ursera bear chain. *Harvesting Windstones* gives a voucher for a gathering profession. Your Horde opener is *The Gift of Skysight*. Learn **Charge** and **Rend** at 4 from the village's Warrior trainer (*exact location unconfirmed*).
2.  **5–8**{ .lvl } **Road to Shen'dar Village.** Fight Al'Aketh thugs. *Blessing of Zephras* tornadoes give +40% speed. **The Great Outdoors** (Raan Wildwind) teaches camping. Do Shen'dar Village's bandit and Prideclaw quests. **Hamstring** at 8.
3.  **8–10**{ .lvl } **Falaath Village.** Infiltrate the cult and kill its commanders.
4.  **10**{ .lvl } **Valanaar.** Meet Talaanis Shadowsong and **Grand Skyseer Ayessa Dawnsinger**. Do the **Defensive Stance** quest here (*details unconfirmed for Skyborne*). First talent: **Shield Specialization** (Prot) or **Improved Rend** (Arms).
5.  **10–12**{ .lvl } **Gustberry Lowlands and Shadowgale Forest.** Do the Windshaper chains *The Broken Construct*, then *In Service of Zephras* → *Blood for Blood*. Do the Nightclaw druid quests for reputation.
6.  **12–14**{ .lvl } **The confrontation.** Attack the Shrine of Akir and fight Lorthuna and Rohash. *What Comes Next* sends you away from the island.

</div>

## Route to 60 from Thunder Bluff

The Windshaper airship lands at **Skywatcher Plateau** in northwest Mulgore, near **Thunder Bluff**. This is an easy start for the Horde.

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **12–20**{ .lvl } Mulgore (do the quests there if your level is low) → the Barrens → Stonetalon

    <span class="step-label">Flight paths / notes</span> Thunder Bluff, Crossroads. Wailing Caverns, Ragefire Chasm

2.  **20–30**{ .lvl } Stonetalon → Ashenvale → Thousand Needles → Hillsbrad

    <span class="step-label">Flight paths / notes</span> Razorfen Kraul, Shadowfang Keep, [Ruins of Lordaeron](../../content/dungeons/ruins-of-lordaeron.md)

3.  **30–40**{ .lvl } Desolace → Arathi → Stranglethorn

    <span class="step-label">Flight paths / notes</span> Do not go to Dalaran: it is hostile to the Horde. [The Drowned City](../../content/dungeons/drowned-city.md)

4.  **35–45**{ .lvl } **[Riverglades](../../world/zones.md#riverglades)** (Rog'mar) → Dustwallow Marsh

    <span class="step-label">Flight paths / notes</span> Shen'dralas has a connection to Skyborne history. You go in from southern Desolace

5.  **45–60**{ .lvl } Tanaris → Feralas → Azshara → Un'Goro → Plaguelands → Winterspring → Mount Hyjal

</div>

**Class trainers:** Valanaar on Zephras Isle, then **Hunter Rise** in Thunder Bluff (closest to Skywatcher Plateau) or Orgrimmar's Valley of Honor.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Mount:** the **Galestrider**, at **Fairweather Stables** on Zephras Isle (53.8, 81.3), in Umber, Stormy and Empyrean. Windshaper reputation gives 5/10/15/20% off, from Friendly to Exalted.
- **Reputation:** Windshapers (Zephras quests and cloth turn-ins in Mulgore) and Nightclaw. The Windshapers are allies of the **Earthen Ring**. Thus Muln Earthfury comes in the finale.
- **Customization:** HD models only at launch. SD models are expected in early 2027.
- **Language:** Darnassian and Orcish. You can speak Darnassian with Alliance Skyborne and Night Elves.

## Spec fit

| Spec | Fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Arms** | **3/5**{ .stars .s3 } | Skysight is a useful extra gap closer in PvP |
| **Fury** | **3/5**{ .stars .s3 } | Wind Blessed haste gives more swings and Flurry procs |
| **Protection** | **2/5**{ .stars .s2 } | No mitigation or health racial |

**Leveling tip:** at low levels, use Skysight and the Zephras tornadoes one after the other. Use Walk on Air to take shorter routes in Thousand Needles and Stonetalon.

### Leveling checklist

- [ ] Defensive Stance quest at **10**, Berserker Stance quest at **30**
- [ ] Cooking and *The Great Outdoors* for camping by level 5–6
- [ ] First Aid trained early: it now makes health potions as well as bandages
- [ ] Collect class library books for the quests at 20, 35 and 45
- [ ] **Galestrider** and Apprentice Riding at 40 (100g), and plate and dual spec (50g) at the same time
- [ ] Warrior Legacy Points at 25, 45 and 60 (account-wide, first Warrior only)

## Verdict

<div class="verdict" markdown>

A comfortable leveler that starts near Thunder Bluff. Skysight gives it a little more PvP use than the High Order version. Even so, Orc, Troll, Tauren and Undead all have stronger Warrior racials. Choose Windshaper for the story and the look.

</div>
