# Tauren Warrior

!!! tip "Add-on"
    Read with the [Warrior guide](index.md). This page covers only what changes for a Tauren.

## Why this combo

Tauren are the biggest Warriors in the game. Before launch, Blizzard raised Tauren Warrior starting Strength to **+5 over Humans**. **Endurance** (+5% health) and **War Stomp** (an area stun) make Tauren the Horde's natural tank. The new **Plainsrunning** speeds up getting around while leveling.

- **Best at:** tanking, leveling on foot, and PvP crowd control.
- **Status:** available in Classic; not a new combination.

## Racials in practice

| Racial | Type | Rating | PvE | PvP |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **War Stomp** | Active | ★★★★☆ | Area stun around you. Interrupts casters in a pack, or buys a Shield Slam | Stun, then Mortal Strike or Hamstring. Also interrupts heals |
| **Cultivation** *(new)* | Active | ★★☆☆☆ | Grows an herb anyone can pick, with bonus yield for Herbalists | None |
| **Plainsrunning** *(new)* | Passive | ★★★☆☆ | +2% speed per stack while moving, up to +60%, not while mounted. Great before level 40 | Stacking was blocked in battlegrounds by a 5 Nov hotfix |
| **Endurance** | Passive | ★★★★☆ | +5% health: tanks love it | Harder to burst |

!!! note "Nature Resistance"
    The old Tauren nature resistance racial was removed.

## Starting zone walkthrough (1–12)

Tauren start at **Camp Narache** in **Mulgore**. Mulgore gained **Skywatcher Plateau** in the northwest (the Horde Skyborne airship landing). *Quest and NPC names are Classic-era; check in game.*

1. **Camp Narache (1–5).** Warrior trainer in camp (Classic: Harutt Thunderhorn). Plainstriders, mountain cougars, the Battleboar camp and the quilboar. **Charge** and **Rend** at 4.
2. **The Great Outdoors (~5).** Camping intro and Cooking.
3. **Bloodhoof Village (5–10).** The Venture Co. and Palemane gnoll quests and the wells. Warrior trainer in Bloodhoof. **Hamstring** at 8. Plainsrunning makes Mulgore's long walks shorter.
4. **Level 10.** **Defensive Stance** quest from your trainer.
5. **First talent at 10.** Protection: **Shield Specialization**. Arms: **Improved Rend**.
6. **10–12.** Visit **Thunder Bluff**, then head east into **the Barrens**.

## Faction-specific notes

- **Mount:** Kodo, from Bloodhoof Village. **Honored with Thunder Bluff** gives 10% off. Thunder Bluff has a new barbershop.
- **Skywatcher Plateau:** the Windshaper airship to Zephras Isle lands beside Thunder Bluff. Any Horde player can visit the island; cloth turn-ins to Windshapers in Mulgore earn their reputation, which leads to a **Galestrider** at Exalted.
- **Shen'dralas:** the new zone between Mulgore and Desolace is reached through the Valley of Bones. Its level range and quest content are still unclear.

## Spec fit

| Spec | Fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Protection** | ★★★★★ | +5% health, top Strength and an area interrupt for caster packs |
| **Arms** | ★★★★☆ | +5 Strength and War Stomp into Mortal Strike; a strong PvP choice |
| **Fury** | ★★★☆☆ | No damage cooldown; the extra Strength helps a little |

**Leveling tip:** Plainsrunning stacks only while you keep moving and doesn't work mounted. Don't stop between mobs before level 40.

### Leveling checklist

- [ ] Defensive Stance quest at **10**, Berserker Stance quest at **30**
- [ ] Cooking and *The Great Outdoors* for camping by level 5–6
- [ ] First Aid trained early: it now makes health potions as well as bandages
- [ ] Collect class library books for the quests at 20, 35 and 45
- [ ] **Kodo** and Apprentice Riding at 40 (100g); plate and dual spec (50g) at the same time
- [ ] Warrior Legacy Points at 25, 45 and 60 (account-wide, first Warrior only)

## Verdict

The Horde's best main-tank race: more health, more Strength and an area stun. War Stomp also makes Tauren dangerous in PvP. Orc beats it for pure damage, but a Tauren Warrior is never a wrong pick.
