## Why this combo

Tauren are the biggest Warriors in the game. Before launch, Blizzard raised Tauren Warrior starting Strength to **+5 over Humans**. **Endurance** (+5% health) and **War Stomp** (an area stun) make Tauren the Horde's natural tank. The new **Plainsrunning** makes you move faster when you level.

- **Best at:** tanking, leveling on foot, and PvP crowd control.
- **Status:** available in Classic, not a new combination.

## Starting zone walkthrough (1–12)

Tauren start at **Camp Narache** in **Mulgore**. Mulgore gained **Skywatcher Plateau** in the northwest (the Horde Skyborne airship landing). *Quest and NPC names are Classic-era. Check in game.*

<div class="steps" markdown>

1.  **1–5**{ .lvl } **Camp Narache.** The Warrior trainer is in the camp (Classic: Harutt Thunderhorn). Kill plainstriders, mountain cougars, the Battleboar camp and the quilboar. **Charge** and **Rend** at 4.
2.  **~5**{ .lvl } **The Great Outdoors.** Camping intro and Cooking.
3.  **5–10**{ .lvl } **Bloodhoof Village.** Do the Venture Co. quests, the Palemane gnoll quests and the wells. There is a Warrior trainer in Bloodhoof. **Hamstring** at 8. Plainsrunning makes Mulgore's long walks shorter.
4.  **10**{ .lvl } **Defensive Stance** quest from your trainer.
5.  **First talent at 10.** Protection: **Shield Specialization**. Arms: **Improved Rend**.
6.  **10–12.** Visit **Thunder Bluff**, then go east into **the Barrens**.

</div>

## Faction-specific notes

- **Mount:** Kodo, from Bloodhoof Village. **Honored with Thunder Bluff** gives 10% off. Thunder Bluff has a new barbershop.
- **Skywatcher Plateau:** the Windshaper airship to Zephras Isle lands beside Thunder Bluff. All Horde players can visit the island. Cloth turn-ins to Windshapers in Mulgore give their reputation. At Exalted, this reputation gives a **Galestrider**.
- **Shen'dralas:** the new zone between Mulgore and Desolace. You go in through the Valley of Bones. Its level range and quest content are still not clear.

## Spec fit

| Spec | Fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Protection** | **5/5**{ .stars .s5 } | +5% health, top Strength and an area interrupt for caster packs |
| **Arms** | **4/5**{ .stars .s4 } | +5 Strength and War Stomp into Mortal Strike. A strong PvP choice |
| **Fury** | **3/5**{ .stars .s3 } | No damage cooldown. The extra Strength helps a little |

**Leveling tip:** Plainsrunning stacks only while you continue to move. It does not work when you are mounted. Before level 40, do not stop between mobs.

### Leveling checklist

- [ ] Defensive Stance quest at **10**, Berserker Stance quest at **30**
- [ ] Cooking and *The Great Outdoors* for camping by level 5–6
- [ ] First Aid trained early: it now makes health potions as well as bandages
- [ ] Collect class library books for the quests at 20, 35 and 45
- [ ] **Kodo** and Apprentice Riding at 40 (100g), and plate and dual spec (50g) at the same time
- [ ] Warrior Legacy Points at 25, 45 and 60 (account-wide, first Warrior only)

## Verdict

<div class="verdict" markdown>

The Horde's best main-tank race: more health, more Strength and an area stun. War Stomp also makes Tauren dangerous in PvP. Orc is better for pure damage, but a Tauren Warrior is never a wrong choice.

</div>
