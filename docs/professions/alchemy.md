Alchemy changes herbs and vials into potions, elixirs, flasks, weapon oils and draughts. It also transmutes metals and elemental materials. In Forever, the Classic healing potions are First Aid recipes. Alchemy has new lines in their place: **Discolored** healing potions, **Mender's**, **Spellblasting** and **Frenzy** potions, many new elixirs, and dyes.

## New in Forever

- **The 6 Classic healing potions are gone from Alchemy.** They are First Aid recipes now. Elixir of Poison Resistance, Cowardly Flight Potion and Purification Potion are also not in Forever.
- **Discolored healing potions:** 6 ranks from Minor to Major. Each needs a vial, 1 herb and 1 Murloc Eye. A trainer teaches 4 of them. 5 ranks are new. The Minor rank makes the Classic Discolored Healing Potion.
- **3 new potion lines:** Mender's, Spellblasting and Frenzy. Each line has 6 ranks from Minor to Major. The client data does not give their effects.
- **Many new elixirs**, for example the Mageblood and Cleric's lines, Elixir of Cunning, Elixir of Ferocity and Elixir of the Owl. Merchant's Favor buys most of the high ranks.
- **Many Classic elixirs have new names.** For example, Elixir of Greater Strength makes the Classic item of Elixir of Giants. The data marks these recipes as changed, so read the tooltip in the game.
- **28 recipes cost Merchant's Favor.** Nina Surefire at Three Corners sells them. All 28 recipe items bind when you pick them up. The wiki says that high-end recipes also need reputation.
- **Dyes and acid:** Cerulean Dye, Magenta Dye, Viridian Dye and Sulfuric Acid are new trainer recipes under Reagents. Recipes of 5 other professions use them.
- **Draughts** are a new heading. The Classic detection and water elixirs have new names, for example Draught of Detect Undead and Draught of Water Breathing.
- **4 new flasks** with the name Flask of Natural. They use the new materials Death Lotus, Demonsage and Marefoil. The names suggest herbs.
- **Transmute: Legionite** is new. It uses 1 Heavy Thorium Bar and 1 Fel Crystal.
- **6 recipes make 2 for each craft:** Fire Oil, Stonescale Oil, Shadow Oil, Frost Oil, Goblin Rocket Fuel and Ghost Dye. Blackmouth Oil makes 1.
- **3 camp features:** Mana Well, Fermenter and Alchemy Laboratory.

## Level 1 to 300

This path uses trainer recipes to skill 285. Only the last step needs a recipe item. The client does not hold the skill that the trainer asks for. The path uses the Classic-era trainer skill of the same recipe, and it marks each value. Vials come from a vendor in Classic: Empty Vial, Leaded Vial, Crystal Vial and Imbued Vial. The path does not use Imbued Vial.

The *Crafts* column is an estimate. A recipe always gives a skill point while it is orange. The chance decreases when the recipe is yellow and green, and a grey recipe gives no points. The estimate uses the Classic-era rule, which is not confirmed for Forever.

## Where recipes come from

A trainer teaches the basic recipes of each heading. The client does not hold the skill that the trainer asks for, so the path uses Classic-era values. Recipe items teach most of the other recipes. The client does not say where these items come from. Many recipe items bind when you pick them up, so you cannot buy them from a different player.

Nina Surefire at Three Corners in the Redridge Mountains sells 28 recipes for Merchant's Favor. On the beta, 8 cost 45 Favor, 5 cost 120, 13 cost 180 and 2 cost 240. You get Merchant's Favor when you turn in crates. The wiki says that high-end recipes need Merchant's Favor and reputation with the crate faction. The data does not give the reputation rank for each recipe. Three Corners is the Alliance camp of the Azeroth Commerce Authority. The Horde camp of Durotar Supply and Logistics is west of the Crossroads in the Barrens. The data does not name the Horde merchant.

The client also holds 15 Alchemy recipes from Season of Discovery under a separate heading. No merchant sells them, so this guide does not list them.

## Tips

- Learn Herbalism with Alchemy. The path uses herbs in each step.
- Keep Murloc Eye when you find it. Each Discolored healing potion needs 1.
- Buy vials before you start a step. Each craft uses 1 vial.
- The new potion ranks become grey 10 points after you learn them. Use them for the item, not for skill points.
- Make Fire Oil and Stonescale Oil when you have the fish. Each craft makes 2.
- The flasks, Refined Scale of Onyxia, Gurubashi Mojo Madness and Alchemist's Stone are yellow above skill 300 in the client.
- Revealing Draught makes an item that has no name in the launch client.
- Elixir of Sages and Elixir of the Sages are 2 different recipes. Elixir of Sages is new and costs Merchant's Favor. Elixir of the Sages is the Classic recipe.
- 11 recipes need an Imbued Vial. In Classic, alchemy supply vendors sell it. This is not confirmed for Forever.
- Recipes of Tailoring, Blacksmithing, Leatherworking, Enchanting and Engineering use the dyes and Sulfuric Acid. Make them for the crafters of these professions.

## FAQ

??? question "Does Alchemy still make healing potions?"
    Not the Classic ones. The 6 Classic healing potions are First Aid recipes. Alchemy makes the Discolored healing potions, and the client data does not give their effect.

??? question "Do I need Merchant's Favor to get to skill 300?"
    No. The path uses trainer recipes to 285 and 1 recipe item after that. The Favor recipes become grey quickly.

??? question "Are there Alchemy specializations?"
    The client data shows no Alchemy specializations.

??? question "At what skill do I learn each trainer recipe?"
    The client does not hold the trainer skill. The path uses the Classic-era values, and it marks them as Classic values.

??? question "How many herbs do I need for 1 to 300?"
    The materials table gives the estimate for each bracket. The real number changes, because yellow and green recipes do not always give a point.

??? question "Where do I get Death Lotus, Demonsage and Marefoil?"
    The client data does not say. The names suggest herbs, and they are new in Forever. In Alchemy, only the 4 new flasks use them.
