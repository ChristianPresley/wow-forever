## New in Forever

- **197 new recipes.** 193 of them come from plans that bind when you pick them up.
- **The skill colours moved down.** 186 Classic recipes have different values. Most recipes turn yellow and grey at a lower skill than in Classic.
- **61 Merchant's Favor plans.** They include 5 low-level mail sets, 5 high-level sets, 3 Enriched Thorium pieces, Thorium Cestus, Thorium Greatmace and Legionite Glaive.
- **New reagents:** Heavy Thorium Bar, Legionite Bar, Azerothium Bar, Malleable Essence of Nature, Pristine Leather, Sandpaper and Sulfuric Acid. Most new recipes at skill 300 and the Favor sets use them.
- **New armor lines:** Skyforged, Sterling Silver, Hard Gold, Shining Mithril and Blessed Plate, and the Justice and Glory epics.
- **2 Rare plate lines near skill 290:** Clutchlord's and Goregasher. Each line has gloves, a belt and boots. The plans bind when you pick them up.
- **3 Rare copper boots at skill 35:** Gemmed Copper Boots, Glowing Copper Boots and Strange Copper Boots.
- **More fist weapons, polearms and thrown weapons.** There are 5 new fist weapons and 4 new polearms. The 3 thrown Blacksmith Hammers are a new weapon type for the profession.
- **Copper Rod is a Blacksmithing recipe.** The 5 Inert Enchanting Rods have their own heading.
- **3 camp features:** Sharpening Wheel, Anvil and Master Forge.
- **6 Classic plans moved to Leatherworking:** Wildthorn Mail, Heavy Timbermaw Belt, Storm Gauntlets, Heavy Timbermaw Boots, Helm of the Great Chief and Invulnerable Mail. They are not Blacksmithing recipes in Forever.

## Level 1 to 300

This path uses trainer recipes to skill 240. The client does not hold the skill that the trainer asks for. The path uses the Classic-era value when that value is not above the yellow skill. For 5 mithril and steel recipes it uses the yellow skill as an estimate. Because the colours moved down, it is possible that the trainer teaches these recipes earlier. Go to the trainer each time you get 5 points. A recipe that you learn earlier stays orange longer. If the trainer does not teach a recipe at the skill in the table, continue with the recipe from the step before. Steel Breastplate and Heavy Mithril Axe are other trainer options. Their Classic trainer skills are 200 and 210.

The *Crafts* column is an estimate. A recipe always gives a skill point while it is orange. The chance decreases when the recipe is yellow and green, and a grey recipe gives no points. The estimate uses the Classic-era rule, which is not confirmed for Forever.

## Where recipes come from

A trainer teaches 83 of the 441 recipes. For 66 of them, the client does not hold the skill that the trainer asks for. Plans teach the other 358 recipes, and 262 of these plans bind when you pick them up. The client does not say where the plans come from. On the beta, Stondry Darkhammer sold 61 plans for Merchant's Favor at Three Corners in Redridge Mountains. This is the Alliance camp of the Azeroth Commerce Authority. The Horde camp of Durotar Supply and Logistics is west of the Crossroads in the Barrens. The data does not name the Horde merchant. You get Merchant's Favor when you turn in crates. The wiki says that high-end recipes need Favor and reputation. The data does not give the reputation rank for each plan. The Favor prices in this guide come from the beta, not from the launch client.

The Blacksmithing data does not show where the new reagents come from. The data of other professions shows some sources. Sulfuric Acid is an Alchemy trainer recipe. Sandpaper is an Engineering trainer recipe. Mining has the recipes Smelt Heavy Thorium and Smelt Azerothium. Alchemy has the recipe Transmute: Legionite. The data does not show the source of Pristine Leather, Malleable Essence of Nature, Torbernite, Death Lotus or Golem Heart.

## Specializations

The client holds 5 specialization abilities. It does not say which recipes need a specialization, or how you get one. In Classic, you select Armorsmith or Weaponsmith at skill 200 and level 40 with a quest. A Weaponsmith can then select 1 master specialization at skill 250 and level 50. These rules are not confirmed for Forever.

- **Armorsmith:** The client says that an Armorsmith can make special armors. In Classic, Lionheart Helm and Stronghold Gauntlets need Armorsmith.
- **Weaponsmith:** The client says that a Weaponsmith can make special weapons. In Classic, you need it before you can select a master specialization.
- **Master Axesmith:** The client says that a Master Axesmith can make special axes. In Classic, Arcanite Reaper needs it.
- **Master Hammersmith:** The client says that a Master Hammersmith can make special maces and hammers. In Classic, Hammer of the Titans and Masterwork Stormhammer need it.
- **Master Swordsmith:** The client says that a Master Swordsmith can make special swords. In Classic, Arcanite Champion and Frostguard need it.

## Tips

- Learn Mining with Blacksmithing. The path needs many bars and stones, and a Miner gets them without the auction house.
- Make Copper Rod for the first 10 points if you have Copper Bars. It is orange until 10.
- Keep all stones when you mine. Grinding stones are reagents for many recipes.
- Make the Sharpening Wheel when the trainer teaches it. It turns yellow at 20 and grey at 25.
- Offer Inert Enchanting Rods and skeleton keys to other players. Most need only 1 bar and 1 or 2 grinding stones. Arcanite Rod needs 3 Arcanite Bars.
- Do not use Coarse Sharpening Stone or Heavy Sharpening Stone for skill points. Each is grey at the skill where you learn it.
- The client shows 88 recipes that turn yellow above skill 300. Make them for the item, not for skill points.
- Before you buy a Favor plan, read the tooltip of the item. The client data does not give the stats.
- Before you buy a Favor set plan, make sure that you can get Sandpaper, Sulfuric Acid and Pristine Leather.
- Blacksmithing gives 1 Legacy Point each at skill 150, 225 and 300.

## FAQ

??? question "Which specialization is best?"
    The client does not say which recipes need a specialization. In Classic, Armorsmith makes special armor and Weaponsmith makes special weapons. Read the recipes at your trainer first.

??? question "Can I level with sharpening stones and grinding stones as in Classic?"
    Only at the start. In Forever, most stones turn grey much earlier. Dense Sharpening Stone is grey at 235.

??? question "At what skill does the trainer teach each recipe?"
    The client does not hold the trainer skill for 66 recipes. The path uses Classic-era values or estimates, and it marks them.

??? question "Where do the new plans come from?"
    The client does not say. It shows only that 262 plans bind when you pick them up. On the beta, Stondry Darkhammer sold 61 plans for Merchant's Favor at the Alliance camp.

??? question "Which Merchant's Favor plans do I buy first?"
    For a mail character that levels, buy the Chain Belt and Gloves of 1 low-level set. At level 60, compare the Enriched Thorium pieces and the weapons in the game. The wiki says that high-end recipes also need reputation.

??? question "Are the Season of Discovery recipes in Forever?"
    The client holds 65 Blacksmithing recipes from Season of Discovery under a separate heading. No merchant sells them, and the client does not say if a trainer teaches them. This guide does not include them.

??? question "How many bars do I need for 1 to 300?"
    The materials table gives the estimate for each bracket. The real number changes, because yellow and green recipes do not always give a point.
