Cooking is a secondary skill, so each character can learn it with 2 primary professions. It changes meat, eggs and fish into food. In Forever it also makes the **campfires** of the camping system, and 2 new groups of drinks. The client sorts most food below a heading that names a stat, for example Stamina Food and Strength Food.

## New in Forever

- **Campfires are Cooking recipes.** The Camping heading has 5 new recipes: Basic Campfire, Journeyman Campfire, Cookie's Feast, Expert Campfire and Iron Oven. The 3 campfires use wood. Cookie's Feast and Iron Oven use food reagents.
- **Headings by stat.** The client puts most food below a heading such as Stamina Food, Strength Food, Agility Food, Intellect Food or Spell Damage Food.
- **New drinks:** 6 teas (Soothing Drinks) and 6 smoothies (Delightful Drinks). The client data does not give their effects. Read the tooltip in the game.
- **44 new recipes in total.** A recipe item teaches 42 of them, and 33 of these recipe items bind when you pick them up. A trainer teaches the other 2: Basic Campfire and Spinefin Halibut.
- **Spinefin Halibut** is a new trainer recipe below the Fisherman's Delights heading. It uses 1 Raw Spinefin Halibut. The client does not hold its trainer skill, and it is grey at 220.
- **New food at skill 275:** 10 recipes, for example Bear Bruscitti, Swiftstrike Steak and Bat Hachee. Each uses 3 pieces of 1 main reagent and Soothing Spices.
- **21 Classic recipes changed.** The client data does not say what changed in each recipe. 13 of them make more than 1 food for each craft. Goldthorn Tea makes 4.
- **Simple Flour** is a new reagent. Skywall Souffle, Pincer Bites, Critter Cookie and Iron Oven use it.
- **Removed:** Crystal Throat Lozenge is not in Forever.
- 60 Classic recipes are not changed.

## Level 1 to 300

This path uses trainer recipes for most skill points. Only skill 240 to 285 uses recipe items, because no trainer recipe is orange there. In Classic, vendors sell spices and Refreshing Spring Water. The meat comes from beasts, so collect it when you level.

The *Crafts* column is an estimate. A recipe always gives a skill point while it is orange. The chance decreases when the recipe is yellow and green, and a grey recipe gives no points. The estimate uses the Classic-era rule, which is not confirmed for Forever.

## Where recipes come from

A trainer teaches 13 of the 125 recipes. The client does not hold the skill that the trainer asks for, so the path uses Classic-era values. Recipe items teach the other 112 recipes. The client does not say where these items come from. 47 of the recipe items bind when you pick them up, and 33 of these teach new recipes. Other players cannot sell you a recipe item that binds when you pick it up. You must get it yourself. No Cooking recipe is on the list of a Merchant's Favor merchant.

The client also holds 7 Cooking recipes from Season of Discovery under a separate heading. No merchant sells them, so this guide does not list them.

## Tips

- Keep low-level meat. The new recipes at skill 275 use 3 pieces of meat such as Meaty Bat Wing, Raptor Flesh, Big Bear Meat, Stag Meat or Tiger Meat.
- Keep 2 Mystery Meat and 1 Heavy Kodo Meat for Cookie's Feast.
- Count your meat before you buy spices. The new recipes at skill 275 use 1 to 4 Soothing Spices for each craft.
- The Legacy talent Master Chef gives a 10% chance of an extra cooking result. Working Overtime increases the skill-up chance by 4% for each rank.
- Level Fishing with Cooking. Fish recipes with 1 reagent are cheap skill points.
- Goldthorn Tea makes 4 for each craft, so 1 stack of Goldthorn gives many drinks.
- You learn Raging Raptor Ribs at skill 180, but it is yellow at 280 in the client data. If this is correct, it is orange for 100 points.
- The recipe item of Raging Raptor Ribs binds when you pick it up, and the client does not say where it drops. The path does not use it.
- Gingerbread Cookie and Egg Nog are below the Winter Veil Treats heading. They use Holiday Spices, which is a Winter Veil reagent in Classic.
- Iron Oven is yellow, green and grey at 300 in the client data. It gives no skill points.
- Cleansing Stew has no skill values and no item name in the client data. It is possible that you cannot make it at this time.
- The item that Critter Cookie makes has no name in the client data. The recipe is yellow at 80 and grey at 85.

## FAQ

??? question "Do I need Cooking for the camping system?"
    Cooking makes the campfires, and a trainer teaches Basic Campfire at skill 1. The quest The Great Outdoors tells you to learn Cooking. The client data does not say if a campfire binds to the player who makes it. Other players can add their camp features to your campfire.

??? question "At what skill do I learn each trainer recipe?"
    The client does not hold the trainer skill. The path uses the Classic-era values, and it marks them as Classic values.

??? question "What do the new teas and smoothies do?"
    The client data does not give their effects. Read the tooltip in the game.

??? question "Can I get to 300 without Fishing?"
    Yes. Continue Monster Omelet from 265 to 285, where it is yellow. Then make Smoked Desert Dumplings to 300.

??? question "Does a Merchant's Favor merchant sell Cooking recipes?"
    No. The beta stock shows recipes for 6 crafting professions. Cooking is not one of them.

??? question "Does Cooking give Legacy Points?"
    No. Legacy Points come from skill 150, 225 and 300 in the 6 crafting professions.
