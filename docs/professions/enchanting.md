In Forever it also makes wands, staves, off-hand items, relics, weapon oils and 3 camp features. Most recipes are enchants that make no item, so the recipe name is the only data on the effect.

## New in Forever

- **Copper Rod is now a Blacksmithing recipe.** Each Runed rod needs an inert rod: Copper Rod, Silver Rod, Golden Rod, Truesilver Rod or Arcanite Rod. Blacksmithing makes all 5 under the heading *Inert Enchanting Rods*. Only Copper Rod is new there.
- **Dust to Motes** is a new trainer recipe. It changes 1 Strange Dust into 3 Mote of Magic. The first rod, the first bracer enchant and the first wand use motes.
- **Cheaper early enchants.** 116 of the 225 recipes are changed from Classic Era. Many early enchants now use 1 or 2 dusts or essences.
- **Stamina and Intellect names.** The list has no enchants with Health or Mana in the name. The Stamina and Intellect chest enchants have the skill colours of the Classic Health and Mana chest enchants. The client data thus shows a rename. The names suggest Stamina and Intellect in place of Health and Mana.
- **New enchant slots:** 5 necklace enchants and 3 off-hand enchants. The bracer, glove, boots, shield, cloak and weapon lists also have new enchants.
- **New item lines:** 4 staves, 4 off-hand items, 14 relics, 7 new wands and 3 new Heart of the Mountain curios.
- **39 recipes cost Merchant's Favor.** The Alliance merchant Alynsia at Three Corners in Redridge Mountains sells them. The Horde camp is west of the Crossroads in the Barrens.
- **3 camp features:** Enchanted Lute, Arcane Salvager and Arcane Forge.
- The client lists 1 Classic recipe as removed: Charged Scale of Onyxia.

## Level 1 to 300

This path uses trainer recipes until skill 270. Each step uses 1 or 2 materials from Disenchant, so the enchants of the path use no shards. The materials table does not include the rods. You put the enchants on your own gear, and a new enchant replaces the old one.

The client does not hold the skill that a trainer asks for. For a recipe with the same name as in Classic Era, the path uses the Classic-era value. For a renamed recipe, the path gives an estimate. The estimate is the Classic-era value of the recipe with the same skill colours.

The *Crafts* column is an estimate. A recipe always gives a skill point while it is orange. The chance decreases when the recipe is yellow and green, and a grey recipe gives no points. The estimate uses the Classic-era rule, which is not confirmed for Forever.

## Where recipes come from

A trainer teaches 65 of the 225 recipes. The client holds the learn skill for only 5 of them, and all 5 are skill 1 recipes. For the other 60, the server holds the skill that the trainer asks for.

Recipe items teach the other 160 recipes. The client does not say where these items come from. 94 of the recipe items bind when you pick them up, so you cannot buy them from other players.

39 of these 94 recipes cost [Merchant's Favor](../systems/economy.md). The Alliance merchant Alynsia sells them at Three Corners in Redridge Mountains, the Alliance camp of the Azeroth Commerce Authority. The Horde camp of Durotar Supply and Logistics is west of the Crossroads in the Barrens. The data does not name the Horde merchant. You get Merchant's Favor when you turn in crates. The wiki says that high-end recipes also need reputation.

The client also holds 37 Enchanting recipes from Season of Discovery under a separate heading. No merchant sells them, so this guide does not list them.

## Tips

- Disenchant each green item that you do not use. The path needs many dusts, and few essences.
- Put the path enchants on your own bracers, chest, boots, cloak and gloves. A new enchant replaces the old one.
- Find a Blacksmith for the inert rods before you start. You cannot enchant without a runed rod.
- Keep the Mote of Magic from Dust to Motes. The Runed Copper Rod and the Novice's Practice Wand also use motes.
- Simple Wood and Star Wood are reagents for the wands, the staves and the Enchanted Lute. In Classic, trade supplies vendors sell Simple Wood and Star Wood. This is not confirmed for Forever.
- Many recipes have learn skill 300. They give no skill points, because the skill cap is 300.
- Do not buy Enchant 2H Weapon - Strength or Enchant Gloves - Lotus Claw for skill points. They turn grey at skill 300, and they use shards.
- Dusts, essences and shards come from Disenchant, not from a gathering profession. Tailoring makes items to disenchant.
- The Merchant's Favor recipes use reagents from other professions, for example dyes, Sulfuric Acid, Pyrite and scrolls. Read the reagents before you buy a recipe.
- Skill 150, 225 and 300 in Enchanting each count for the Tradeskills points of [Legacy](../characters/legacy.md).

## FAQ

??? question "Where do I get an enchanting rod?"
    You make the Runed rod from an inert rod and enchanting materials. Blacksmithing makes the inert rods. Copper Rod is new as a Blacksmithing recipe. A trainer teaches the first 4 Runed rods. A recipe item teaches the Runed Arcanite Rod.

??? question "What do the new enchants do?"
    The client data does not give the effect of an enchant. The recipe name gives the slot and the statistic. Read the tooltip in the game for the number.

??? question "Where are the Health and Mana enchants?"
    They are not in the recipe list. The Stamina and Intellect chest enchants have the skill colours of the Classic Health and Mana chest enchants. This is a comparison with Classic-era values. The names suggest Stamina and Intellect in place of Health and Mana.

??? question "At what skill does the trainer teach each enchant?"
    The client does not hold the trainer skill. The path uses Classic-era values for recipes with a Classic name, and it marks the other values as estimates.

??? question "Can I get to 300 with trainer recipes only?"
    Yes, but it is slow. From skill 290, each trainer recipe is green or grey, and a green recipe gives few points. The path uses 2 recipe items for the last 30 points.

??? question "How many materials do I need for 1 to 300?"
    The materials table gives the estimate for each bracket. The real number changes, because yellow recipes do not always give a point.
