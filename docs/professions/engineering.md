Engineering changes metal bars, stone and cloth into explosives, guns, scopes, ammunition, goggles and devices. In Forever it also makes 3 camp features and 2 new lines of parts with the names **SAF-T** and **EZ-Thro**. Most Classic explosives and all ammunition now make more items for each craft. The profession uses many bars and stones, so Mining is the usual second profession.

## New in Forever

- **More items for each craft.** Rough Dynamite, Coarse Dynamite and Solid Dynamite make 2. Heavy Dynamite, Large Copper Bomb, Big Bronze Bomb and Iron Grenade make 3. Hi-Explosive Bomb makes 4.
- **Ammunition makes 200 for each craft.** Explosive Arrow makes 100 from 100 Razor Arrows.
- **Silver Contact makes 5 and Gold Power Core makes 3** from 1 bar.
- **SAF-T and EZ-Thro lines.** New parts, such as SAF-T Tabs, EZ-Thro Wrap, SAF-T Casing and EZ-Thro Shell, go into new versions of the Classic explosives. The client data does not give their effects.
- **Satchels of bombs.** 4 new recipes each use 20 bombs of 1 type. The client puts them under the Ranged Weapons heading.
- **Field devices at skill 300.** 6 new consumable devices use a transporter or a "Schematic: Tinker" item as a reagent, together with SAF-T Tube or EZ-Thro Shell.
- **New items at skill 300:** Hyper Deluxe Sniper Rifle Mk XVII, SAF-T Ultra Precision Scope, Swiftfeather Arrow, Swiftstrike Shot, Dimensional Transporter - Mt. Hyjal and Gnomish Weather Machine.
- **New materials:** Legionite Bar, Azerothium Bar, Heavy Thorium Bar, Bauxite, Pyrite, Pitchblende, Torbernite and Death Lotus are reagents in the new recipes.
- **New headgear.** Merchant's Favor schematics teach 4 goggles, 1 for each armor type. They also teach Clanking Cord, Gizmo Girdle and Whimsical Waistwrap. The names suggest belts.
- **New devices from recipe items that bind:** Harvester Override Signalcaster, 9-60 Battery Pack, Hoof-Shaped Foot Pedal and Centaur Banner Deployment Device. Shredder Autosalvage Unit is also new, and its recipe item does not bind. The client data does not give their effects or their sources.
- **New Merchant's Favor gadgets:** Compact Critter Carrier, Emergency Field Cloak and SAF-T Disposable Parachute. The client data does not give their effects.
- **3 camp features:** Reagent Bot, Repair Bot and Anarchist's Workbench.
- **Purple, white and yellow fireworks** are new for each rocket size.
- **Recipe items at high skill.** The client data shows recipe items for Thorium Widget, Thorium Grenade and Thorium Shells. In Classic, a trainer also teaches them. This is not confirmed for Forever.
- The client data removes no Classic recipe. Forever does not change 120 recipes.

## Level 1 to 300

This path uses trainer recipes to skill 260. Above 260, the client data shows recipe items for the 2 best recipes. In Classic, a trainer also teaches them, so ask the trainer first. This is not confirmed for Forever. The trainer skills are Classic-era values, because the client does not hold them.

The *Crafts* column is an estimate. A recipe always gives a skill point while it is orange. The chance decreases when the recipe is yellow and green, and a grey recipe gives no points. The estimate uses the Classic-era rule, which is not confirmed for Forever.

## Where recipes come from

A trainer teaches 71 of the 247 recipes. The client does not hold the skill that the trainer asks for, so the guide uses Classic-era values. Recipe items teach the other 176 recipes, and the client does not say where these items come from. 97 of the recipe items bind when you get them, so you cannot buy them from other players.

Fritz Fizzle at Three Corners in Redridge Mountains sells 28 schematics for Merchant's Favor. Three Corners is the Alliance camp of the Azeroth Commerce Authority. The Horde camp of Durotar Supply and Logistics is west of the Crossroads in the Barrens. The data does not name the Horde merchant. You get Merchant's Favor when you turn in crates. The wiki says that high-end recipes also need reputation. See the Economy page. In the beta, the schematics cost 45, 180, 270 or 360 Favor. The price increases with the skill of the recipe.

8 of the 28 Favor schematics do not bind, so you can buy them from other players. These include SAF-T Tabs, EZ-Thro Wrap, No Slip SAF-T Padding, SAF-T Bell and EZ-Thro Fireproof Fuse.

The client also holds 28 Engineering recipes from Season of Discovery. No merchant sells them in the beta data, so this guide does not list them.

## Specializations

The client has 2 specializations. It does not say which recipes each one needs, or how you select one. In Classic, you select one at skill 200 with a quest, and the recipes with Gnomish or Goblin in the name follow that selection. This is not confirmed for Forever.

- **Gnomish Engineer:** The client text says that it lets an engineer make "fantastic, yet unreliable gnomish devices". The Gnomish recipes are gadgets, such as Gnomish Shrink Ray, Gnomish Cloaking Device, Gnomish Mind Control Cap and Gnomish Battle Chicken. In Classic, Ultrasafe Transporter - Gadgetzan is also Gnomish. New recipes with Gnomish in the name are Gnomish Army Knife, Gnomish Poultryizer and Gnomish Weather Machine. The client does not say that they need the specialization.
- **Goblin Engineer:** The client text says that it lets an engineer make "lethal goblin devices". The Goblin recipes are mostly explosives, such as Goblin Sapper Charge, The Big One, Goblin Mortar and Goblin Bomb Dispenser. In Classic, Dimensional Ripper - Everlook is also Goblin. Ultralight Goblin Glider is a new recipe with Goblin in the name. The client does not say that it needs the specialization.

## Tips

- Mine and smelt your own bars. The path uses bars of each tier, and stone for all blasting powder.
- Keep the stone that you mine. Each tier of blasting powder uses a different stone.
- Make the parts for a step before you start it. Explosive Sheep and Hi-Explosive Bomb each use 3 different parts.
- Keep spare Silver Contacts and Gold Power Cores. 1 bar now makes many of them.
- Gnomes have Engineering Specialization. It gives 20% fewer device failures.
- Engineering gives Legacy Points at skill 150, 225 and 300.
- Many recipes need skill 300 and turn yellow at 320. The skill cap is 300, so they give no skill point that you can use.
- The Mortar: Reloaded has no skill colours in the client. The client data does not show if it gives skill points.
- Goblin Jumper Cables asks for skill 165, but it is yellow at 160. It is never orange.
- Serrated Strap makes an item that has no name in the launch client.

## FAQ

??? question "Do I need Mining for Engineering?"
    No, but Mining saves much gold. Most recipes use bars, and all blasting powder uses stone from mining nodes.

??? question "Where do I get the Thorium Widget and Thorium Shells recipes?"
    Ask the trainer first. In Classic, a trainer teaches the 2 recipes, but this is not confirmed for Forever. The client data shows recipe items for them, and it does not say where the items come from. The 2 recipe items do not bind, so you can buy them from other players.

??? question "What do the SAF-T and EZ-Thro items do?"
    The client data does not give the effects. Each item is a Classic explosive or device with a new part added. Read the tooltip in the game.

??? question "Gnomish or Goblin Engineering?"
    In Classic, Gnomish gives gadgets and Goblin gives explosives, such as Goblin Sapper Charge. The client does not confirm that Forever uses the same rules.

??? question "At what skill do I learn each trainer recipe?"
    The client does not hold the trainer skill. The path uses the Classic-era values, and it marks them as Classic values.

??? question "Can I level to 300 with trainer recipes only?"
    The client data does not confirm it. The trainer recipes above 260 are Salt Shaker, the 2 transporters and Tranquil Mechanical Yeti. Salt Shaker is orange to 270 and yellow to 280. In Classic, each transporter needs a different specialization. All of these recipes use many costly reagents, so the path uses Thorium Widget and Thorium Shells.
