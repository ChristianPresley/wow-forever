In Forever it also makes the **healing potions** that Alchemy made in Classic, and a new group of **curatives**. Each class gets value from it, and classes without a heal get the most.

## New in Forever

- **Healing potions are First Aid recipes.** The 6 potions from Minor Healing Potion to Major Healing Potion moved here from Alchemy. Each needs a vial, spices and 1 herb.
- **New curatives:** 4 poultices and 3 tourniquets. The client data does not give their effects. Read the tooltip in the game.
- **More Anti-Venom ranks:** Potent Anti-Venom is new. Anti-Venom and Strong Anti-Venom now make 3 for each craft.
- **Dense Runecloth Bandage** is a new bandage at skill 300. It uses 2 Heavy Runecloth Bandages.
- **3 camp features.** The First Aid Kit gives a Stamina buff at a camp.
- The 10 Classic bandages from Linen to Heavy Runecloth are not changed.

## Level 1 to 300

## Tips

- Keep 2 stacks of cloth of each type when you level. It is cheaper than to buy cloth later.
- If you are a Tailor, keep 2 stacks of each cloth for First Aid. Tailoring uses much more cloth.
- Use a bandage after a fight, not during it. Damage stops the bandage.
- Crystal Infused Bandage lists a reagent with the name DEPRECATED ITEM in the client. It is possible that you cannot make it.

## FAQ

??? question "Do healing potions still come from Alchemy?"
    No. In the launch client, the 6 healing potions are First Aid recipes. Alchemy has a different group of potions with the name Discolored Healing Potion.

??? question "How much cloth do I need for 1 to 300?"
    The materials table gives the estimate for each bracket. The real number changes, because yellow and green recipes do not always give a point.

??? question "At what skill do I learn each bandage?"
    The client does not hold the trainer skill. The path uses the Classic-era values, and it marks them as Classic values.

??? question "Does First Aid give Legacy Points?"
    No. Legacy Points come from skill 150, 225 and 300 in the 6 crafting professions.
