Fishing is a secondary skill, so each character can learn it with 2 primary professions. You catch fish in open water and in fish pools. Cooking and Alchemy use the fish. In Forever, Fishing also has its first recipes: 3 **camp features**. The Fish Bowl gives +8% to stats at a camp.

## New in Forever

- **Fishing has recipes.** Classic Fishing had none. The launch client has 3, and all 3 are camp features.
- **Fish Bowl** is the first feature. It gives +8% to stats at a camp, and it does not stack with Blessing of Kings.
- **Fishing Rack** and **Fishing Hut** are the features at skill 140 and skill 300. The client data does not give their effects.
- **Bait and Tackle** is the ability that lets you add Fishing features to a camp.
- The Legacy talent **Luremaster** gives a 25% chance of an extra fish while a lure is active.
- The Cooking data shows 2 fish recipes as new: **Spinefin Halibut** (trainer) and **Plated Armorfish** (a recipe item that binds on pickup). The client data does not say where you catch Raw Plated Armorfish.
- **Savory Whimsyfin Delight** is also a new Cooking recipe, and it uses 1 Whimsyfin. The name suggests a fish, but the client data does not confirm it.

## Level 1 to 300

You level Fishing with catches, not with crafts. The Fish Bowl and the Fishing Hut are grey when you learn them. The Fishing Rack is yellow at 140 and grey at 145, so it gives a few points at most. The client data does not hold the fishing skill of a zone, so all zone values below are Classic-era values. They are not confirmed for Forever. In Classic, each catch counts for skill in all zones, and each point needs more catches as your skill increases.

The *Crafts* column is an estimate. A recipe always gives a skill point while it is orange. The chance decreases when the recipe is yellow and green, and a grey recipe gives no points. The estimate uses the Classic-era rule, which is not confirmed for Forever.

## Where recipes come from

A trainer teaches the Fish Bowl. The client does not hold the skill that the trainer asks for. Recipe items teach the Fishing Rack at skill 140 and the Fishing Hut at skill 300. These are the values in the launch client. Tier 2 and Tier 3 blueprints drop from dungeon and raid bosses. The Fishing Rack blueprint drops from Gelihast in Blackfathom Deeps. The source of the Fishing Hut blueprint is not known. Merchant's Favor has no Fishing merchant, and no Fishing recipe item binds when you pick it up.

You need a campfire from Cooking first. After you make a Basic Campfire with Cooking, each profession gives a "Camping 101" quest for its Tier 1 feature. For Fishing, that feature is the Fish Bowl. A Basic Campfire holds 3 features. The Fish Bowl gives the same type of buff as Blessing of Kings, so it has the most value in a group without a Paladin. If your group has Blessing of Kings, the Fish Bowl buff does not stack with it. Use a feature from a different profession in that group. Sit or craft at the camp for 60 seconds to get the buff for 1 hour. The Legacy talent Reagent Economy removes the reagents of Tier 1 features.

## Tips

- Use a lure at all times. In Classic, a lure adds fishing skill, and fewer fish get away.
- Classic-era lures: Shiny Bauble (+25, skill 1), Nightcrawlers (+50, skill 50), Bright Baubles (+75, skill 100), Aquadynamic Fish Attractor (+100, skill 100). These values are not confirmed for Forever.
- If you have the Luremaster Legacy talent, a lure also gives a 25% chance of an extra fish.
- Level Cooking at the same time. Cooking uses the fish that you catch, and it makes the campfire that the Fish Bowl needs.
- Keep some Raw Brilliant Smallfish and Raw Mithril Head Trout. The Fish Bowl and the Fishing Rack need them.
- Keep Oily Blackmouth, Firefin Snapper and Stonescale Eel for an Alchemist. Blackmouth Oil, Fire Oil and Stonescale Oil use them.
- Keep Raw Spinefin Halibut. The Cooking trainer teaches a recipe for it.
- Find Fish shows fishing nodes on the minimap. Use it to find fish pools.
- In Classic, vendors sell Empty Vials and Crystal Vials. In Classic, trade supplies vendors sell Simple Wood and Star Wood. This is not confirmed for Forever.
- The Fishing Hut needs 2 Bolt of Runecloth. A Tailor makes each bolt from 5 Runecloth, or you buy the bolts at the Auction House.

## FAQ

??? question "Does Fishing have recipes in Forever?"
    Yes. The launch client has 3 Fishing recipes. They are the camp features Fish Bowl, Fishing Rack and Fishing Hut.

??? question "Can I level Fishing with the recipes?"
    No. The Fish Bowl is grey at skill 20, and the Fishing Hut is grey at 300. The Fishing Rack is yellow at 140 and grey at 145, so it gives a few points at most. You level with catches.

??? question "What skill does each zone need?"
    The client data does not hold zone skills. The path uses the Classic-era values, and it marks them as Classic values.

??? question "What does the Fish Bowl do?"
    It gives +8% to stats at a camp. It does not stack with Blessing of Kings.

??? question "Where do Seasonal Fish Steaks come from?"
    The client data does not say. The Fishing Hut needs 1 for each craft.

??? question "Does Fishing give Legacy Points?"
    No. Legacy Points come from skill 150, 225 and 300 in the 6 crafting professions.
