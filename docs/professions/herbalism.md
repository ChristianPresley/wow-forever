## New in Forever

- **Gardening is a new ability.** It lets the herbalist add features to a wilderness camp.
- **3 new recipes, all camp features:** Incense Candle, Greenhouse and Seed Hybridizer. Classic Era Herbalism has no recipes.
- **Incense Candle** gives an Intellect buff at a camp. The buff does not stack with Arcane Intellect.
- **Greenhouse** and **Seed Hybridizer** come from recipe items at skill 140 and skill 300. The client data does not give their effects.
- **More professions use herbs.** The First Aid healing potions, the new Cooking teas and the new Blacksmithing Sentinel's armor all list herbs as reagents.
- **New reagents with plant names.** Alchemy recipes use Death Lotus, Demonsage, Marefoil, Frilled Lichen, Rosecap and Stranglevine. The names suggest herbs. The client data does not say how you get them.
- A starter quest lets you select a gathering profession, and it gives +2 skill.
- The client data removes no Classic recipe, because Classic Herbalism has none.
- The client data does not hold herb nodes. The guide uses Classic-era node skills, and they are not confirmed for Forever.

## Level 1 to 300

You level Herbalism with herbs, not with recipes. Each herb needs a minimum skill, and the path gives that skill as a Classic-era value. In Classic, a node gives a point each time for the first 25 points above its skill. Then the chance decreases, and the node is grey 100 points above its skill. The training skills and character levels in the notes are Classic-era values too. These rules and values are not confirmed for Forever.

The *Crafts* column is an estimate. A recipe always gives a skill point while it is orange. The chance decreases when the recipe is yellow and green, and a grey recipe gives no points. The estimate uses the Classic-era rule, which is not confirmed for Forever.

## Recipes to get

Herbalism has 3 recipes, and each one makes a camp feature. Each player can add 1 feature to a camp. A Basic Campfire holds 3 features.

### Camp features

The wiki gives the effect of the Incense Candle only. Read the tooltip in the game for the other 2 features. Higher tiers include the features of the lower tiers.

## Where recipes come from

A trainer teaches Incense Candle. The client does not hold the skill that the trainer asks for. The wiki says that camp utility starts at skill 20, and the recipe is yellow at 20. The wiki also says that each profession gives a Camping 101 quest for its Tier-1 feature. You unlock camping first with the quest The Great Outdoors. Recipe items teach Greenhouse at skill 140 and Seed Hybridizer at skill 300. The wiki says that the Greenhouse blueprint drops from the Relic Guardian in Excavation Site. The wiki says that Tier 3 blueprints drop from dungeon and raid bosses. It does not name the boss for the Seed Hybridizer. No Herbalism recipe uses Merchant's Favor. The data shows no Herbalism recipe item that binds on pickup.

## Camp features

Herbalism makes these features for a [camp](../systems/camping.md). The camp page gives the effect of each feature.

| Feature | Learn | Reagents | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Incense Candle** | — | 1 Peacebloom, 1 Silverleaf | Trainer |
| **Greenhouse** | 140 | 2 Simple Wood, 1 Arthas' Tears, 1 Purple Lotus | Recipe item |
| **Seed Hybridizer** | 300 | 4 Mountain Silversage, 1 Crystal Vial, 2 Runecloth | Recipe item |

To unlock camping, do the quest The Great Outdoors at about level 5. The quest asks you to learn Cooking and make a Basic Campfire. The camp buff lasts 1 hour. You get it when you sit or craft at a camp for 60 seconds. The Greenhouse lists Arthas' Tears and Purple Lotus as reagents. In Classic, these herbs need skill 220 and 210, but the recipe needs only skill 140. Buy these herbs, or make the Greenhouse later. The Greenhouse also needs Simple Wood. The Seed Hybridizer needs Mountain Silversage, a Crystal Vial and Runecloth. In Classic, trade supplies vendors sell Simple Wood and Star Wood. This is not confirmed for Forever. In Classic, vendors sell Crystal Vials, and humanoids of level 51 and above drop Runecloth. These sources are not confirmed for Forever.

## Tips

- Keep Find Herbs on. You can track only 1 type of thing at a time.
- Mining uses Find Minerals. If you have Mining too, switch between Find Herbs and Find Minerals.
- Pick each node that gives a point, even if you do not need the herb. Alchemy uses most herbs.
- The Incense Candle does not stack with Arcane Intellect. If your group has a Mage, a different feature gives more value.
- Each player adds 1 feature to a camp. If you also have Alchemy, choose between the Incense Candle and the Mana Well.
- Tauren have Cultivation. It grows an herb that all players can pick, and Herbalists get a bonus yield.
- The Legacy talent Bountiful Harvest gives +20% Scarce materials from Mining, Herbalism and Skinning.
- The Legacy talent Reagent Economy removes the reagents from Tier-1 camp features. The Incense Candle is a Tier-1 feature.
- The 6 First Aid healing potions use Peacebloom, Briarthorn, Bruiseweed, Liferoot, Sungrass and Golden Sansam.
- The 6 new Cooking teas use Peacebloom, Mageroyal, Liferoot, Fadeleaf, Sungrass and Mountain Silversage.
- The 8 new Blacksmithing Sentinel's recipes and 2 new Enchanting recipes use Purple Lotus.
- Keep the herbs that you use, and sell the others. The client data gives no prices.

## FAQ

??? question "Does Herbalism have recipes in Forever?"
    Yes. The launch client has 3 recipes, and all 3 make camp features. Classic Herbalism has no recipes.

??? question "What skill does each herb need?"
    The client data does not hold node skills. The path uses Classic-era values, and they are not confirmed for Forever.

??? question "Do the new zones have herbs?"
    The wiki has no herb data for Zephras Isle, Riverglades, Shen'dralas or Mount Hyjal. The path uses Classic zones only.

??? question "Can I level with the camp features?"
    Not in practice. Incense Candle is yellow at 20 and grey at 25, and the client does not hold its trainer skill. Greenhouse is grey at 145, and Seed Hybridizer is grey at 300. Herb nodes are faster.

??? question "Is there a Merchant's Favor merchant for Herbalism?"
    The merchant data lists recipes for 6 crafting professions only. It lists no Herbalism recipe.

??? question "Does Herbalism give Legacy Points?"
    No. Legacy Points come from skill 150, 225 and 300 in the 6 crafting professions.
