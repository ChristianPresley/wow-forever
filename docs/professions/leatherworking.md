Leatherworking changes leather, hides and scales into leather armor, mail armor, armor kits, quivers and bags. It is the largest profession in Forever, with 512 recipes in the launch client. Druids, Rogues, Hunters and Shamans wear most of its armor. Skinning supplies almost all of its materials.

## New in Forever

- **277 of the 512 recipes are new.** 199 Classic recipes changed, and 36 are the same as in Classic Era. The client removed no Classic recipe.
- **Lower skill values.** Most Classic recipes need less skill than in Classic Era. Example: Wicked Leather Bracers needs skill 240. The Classic-era value is 265.
- **81 Merchant's Favor recipes.** On the beta, Daniel Stitchsong sold 6 sets for skill 60 to 125 and 7 sets for skill 175 to 250. All of the armor pieces are Rare. The 2 armor kits are Uncommon.
- **More pieces for the Favor sets.** Each set has 3 more pieces from recipe items that are not Favor recipes. Examples: Brawler's Leather Armor, Mender's Leather Belt, Stalker's Mail Gauntlets.
- **4 new sets from BoP recipe items:** Azure Gustwoven, Azure Windraveled, Cloudy Gustwoven and Cloudy Windraveled. Most pieces need skill 50 to 60. The helmets and shoulders need skill 175.
- **New sets for level 60:** Tooled Leather and Blue Suede for leather, Supple Scorpid and Charged Scorpid for mail. The Wicked Leather set has 2 new pieces.
- **New Epic armor at skill 300:** the Grovekeeper, Grimstitch, Spiritcaller and Wildstalker's pieces. They use Fel Leather and Malleable Essence of Nature.
- **Forceful and Mystic armor kits.** Each uses a standard armor kit, enchanting dust and an elixir or a mana potion.
- **6 recipes moved from Blacksmithing:** Wildthorn Mail, Storm Gauntlets, Helm of the Great Chief, Invulnerable Mail, Heavy Timbermaw Belt and Heavy Timbermaw Boots.
- **Light Leather Reagent Bag** is a new bag for the reagent bag slot. A BoP recipe item teaches it at skill 30.
- **3 camp features.** The Camp Tent gives rested XP at a camp, up to 5% of a level.

## Level 1 to 300

The path uses trainer recipes to skill 235. After that, the trainer has no cheap recipe, so the path uses 3 recipe items from vendors. The client does not hold the skill that a trainer asks for. Each learn value of a trainer recipe in this path is a Codex estimate. The estimate is 20 to 30 points below the yellow skill. Many Classic recipe items in the client have this interval. If the trainer does not offer a recipe at the start of a step, continue with the recipe of the step before. It gives points until it is grey. The sources list 2 published Forever guides. For each step, at least 1 of them uses the recipe at the same skill or earlier. The Heavy Armor Kit step is the exception. It comes from the client data only.

The *Crafts* column is an estimate. A recipe always gives a skill point while it is orange. The chance decreases when the recipe is yellow and green, and a grey recipe gives no points. The estimate uses the Classic-era rule, which is not confirmed for Forever.

## Where recipes come from

A trainer teaches 76 of the 512 recipes. The client does not hold the skill that the trainer asks for, so the path gives estimates. Recipe items teach the other 436 recipes, and 238 of these recipe items bind when you pick them up. The client does not say where a recipe item comes from.

81 recipes are Merchant's Favor recipes. On the beta, Daniel Stitchsong sold them at Three Corners in Redridge Mountains, and each one binds when you pick it up. Three Corners is the Alliance camp of the Azeroth Commerce Authority. The Horde camp of Durotar Supply and Logistics is west of the Crossroads in the Barrens. The data does not name the Horde merchant. The stock and prices come from the beta, not from the launch client. You get Merchant's Favor when you turn in crates. The wiki says that high-end recipes also need reputation. The client data does not say where Pristine Leather, Pristine Hide and Pristine Scale come from. The pieces of the Favor sets that are not on the merchant are the Armor, Pants, Helm, Belt, Shoes, Mail Boots and Gauntlets recipes.

The client also holds 80 Leatherworking recipes from Season of Discovery under a separate heading. No merchant sells them, so this guide does not list them.

## Specializations

Leatherworking has 3 specializations. The client data gives their names and 1 sentence for each. wow-professions.com says that you select 1 specialization at skill 225 and level 40. The client does not show which recipes each specialization unlocks.

- **Dragonscale Leatherworking:** The client says that it lets you make special dragonscale armors. All dragonscale armor is mail. In Classic, Hunters and Shamans select it.
- **Elemental Leatherworking:** The client says that it lets you make special elemental armors. In Classic, these are the Stormshroud, Volcanic and Living recipes, and Rogues select it.
- **Tribal Leatherworking:** The client says that it lets you make special tribal armors. In Classic, these are the Ironfeather, Warbear and Wolfshead recipes, and Druids select it.

## Tips

- Learn Skinning as your second profession. It supplies the leather and the hides for almost every recipe.
- Keep each hide that you skin. Cured hides need a hide and Salt, and many recipes use them.
- The trainer recipes Medium Leather, Heavy Leather, Thick Leather and Rugged Leather change a lower leather into a higher one. Use them when the lower leather is cheap.
- Do not sell the Fine Leather Belts from the path. The Dark Leather Belt uses 1 for each craft.
- Before you buy a Favor recipe, find a source of Pristine Leather. Each Favor armor recipe needs it, and the client data does not give its source.
- Winter Boots has no skill colours in the client. It is possible that it gives no skill points.
- The 4 Favor mail bracers (Mender's, Skirmisher's, Skycaller's and Stalker's) and Heavy Scorpid Bracers are yellow before their learn skill in the client. They are never orange.
- Tanning Rack is yellow at its learn skill and grey 5 points later, so it gives few points. Sewing Machine is grey at its learn skill and gives none.
- Leatherworking gives 1 Legacy Point each at skill 150, 225 and 300.

## FAQ

??? question "Do I need Skinning for Leatherworking?"
    No, but it is the best pair. Without Skinning, you must buy all leather and hides.

??? question "At what skill do I learn each trainer recipe?"
    The client does not hold the trainer skill. The path gives a Codex estimate for each trainer recipe, and it marks it as an estimate.

??? question "Why are the skill values different from Classic guides?"
    Forever decreased the skill values of most Classic recipes. A Classic guide sends you to a recipe that is green or grey in Forever.

??? question "Which specialization is best?"
    The client data does not show which recipes each specialization unlocks. In Classic, the class decides: Dragonscale for mail, Elemental for Rogues, Tribal for Druids.

??? question "Where do I get the Merchant's Favor recipes?"
    On the beta, Daniel Stitchsong sold them at Three Corners in Redridge Mountains, the Alliance camp. The beta prices were 30, 60 or 90 Merchant's Favor. The data does not name the Horde merchant.

??? question "How much leather do I need for 1 to 300?"
    The materials table gives the estimate for each bracket. The real number changes, because yellow recipes do not always give a point.

??? question "Can I get skill points from the recipes at skill 300?"
    No. Almost all of these recipes are orange or yellow at 300, but the skill cap is 300.
