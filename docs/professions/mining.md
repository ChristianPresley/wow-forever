## New in Forever

- **3 new smelting recipes:** Smelt Azerothium, Smelt Heavy Thorium and Smelt Obsidian-Infused Thorium Bar. Blacksmithing and Engineering use Azerothium Bars and Heavy Thorium Bars in new recipes for skill 250 and above.
- **New ore names in the reagents:** Pitchblende and Heavy Thorium Ore. The client data does not say where you get them.
- Recipes of other professions also use Bauxite, Pyrite and Torbernite. The names suggest minerals. The client data does not say where you get them.
- A starter quest lets you select a gathering profession, and it gives +2 skill.
- **3 camp features.** The Lodestone gives a melee attack power buff at a camp. It does not stack with Blessing of Might. The wiki has no effect data for Rock Garden and Molten Foundry.
- **Smelt Bronze** has the status changed in the client data. The data does not show what changed. It makes 2 bars from 1 Copper Bar and 1 Tin Bar.
- The other 11 Classic smelting recipes are not changed. Forever removed no Classic recipe.
- Gathering gives **Scarce materials** in Forever. The Legacy talent Bountiful Harvest increases them.

## Level 1 to 300

You get most Mining skill from ore veins. Smelting gives skill mainly at low skill, so the path uses only 2 smelting recipes, Smelt Bronze and Smelt Iron. The skill for each vein is the Classic-era value. It is not confirmed for Forever. The zones are Classic zones with their Classic ore.

The *Crafts* column is an estimate. A recipe always gives a skill point while it is orange. The chance decreases when the recipe is yellow and green, and a grey recipe gives no points. The estimate uses the Classic-era rule, which is not confirmed for Forever.

## Tips

- Smelt Copper is grey at 70, Smelt Tin at 75, Smelt Bronze at 115 and Smelt Iron at 140. Smelt your ore before these values to get skill from it. You can smelt grey recipes later, but they give no skill.
- Keep Find Minerals on when you level your character. You then get skill during quests.
- Mine each vein until it is empty. In Classic, each hit can give a skill point.
- Keep stone for the camp features. Lodestone needs 1 Rough Stone, Rock Garden needs 2 Heavy Stone, and Molten Foundry needs 2 Dense Stone.
- Keep some Pitchblende as ore. The Engineering recipe Ultrasafe Rechargeable Battery uses 1 Pitchblende.
- Smelt Elementium is yellow at 310 and Smelt Obsidian-Infused Thorium Bar at 315. The skill cap is 300, so they stay orange.

## FAQ

??? question "Can I get to 300 with smelting only?"
    Not with the recipes that are confirmed. Smelt Gold is grey at 185. Smelt Heavy Thorium is orange until 300, but the client data does not give its trainer skill or the source of its ore.

??? question "What are the new bars for?"
    Blacksmithing and Engineering use Heavy Thorium Bars and Azerothium Bars in new high-skill recipes. No crafting recipe in the client data uses the Obsidian-Infused Thorium Bar.

??? question "What skill do I need for each vein?"
    The client data does not hold vein skills. The path uses the Classic-era values, and it marks them as Classic values.

??? question "Where do I get Pitchblende and Heavy Thorium Ore?"
    The client data does not say. It only shows them as reagents of Smelt Azerothium and Smelt Heavy Thorium.

??? question "Does Mining give Legacy points?"
    No. Only the 6 crafting professions give Legacy points for skill 150, 225 and 300.

??? question "Can I buy Mining recipes with Merchant's Favor?"
    No. The client data lists no Mining recipe for Merchant's Favor. The merchants sell recipes for the 6 crafting professions. Some of the Blacksmithing and Engineering recipes use Heavy Thorium Bars and Azerothium Bars.
