Skinning is a primary gathering profession. You skin dead beasts and get leather and hides. It has no nodes, so you level it while you kill creatures for quests. In Forever it also has **Tanning**, an ability that makes 3 camp features. Skinning gives the materials for Leatherworking.

## New in Forever

- **Tanning is new.** The ability lets a skinner add features to a wilderness camp. All 3 Skinning recipes are camp features, and all 3 are new.
- **Camp Chair** is the Tier 1 feature. A trainer teaches it. It gives +2% critical strike chance at a camp, and it does not stack with Moonkin Aura.
- **Field Guide** is the Tier 2 feature at skill 140. The Camping page lists Track Beasts for it. The client data does not give the exact effect.
- The Field Guide camp feature is not the Legacy talent with the same name.
- **Trapper's Workbench** is the Tier 3 feature at skill 300. The client data does not give its effect.
- **Scarce materials** come from gathering. The Legacy talent Bountiful Harvest gives +20% Scarce materials from Skinning.
- A starter quest lets you select a gathering profession, and it gives +2 skill.
- The client has no removed Skinning recipes. Classic Skinning had no recipes at all.

## Level 1 to 300

You level Skinning with creatures, not with recipes. The client data does not give the skill for each creature level. This path uses the Classic-era rule. Skill 1 lets you skin creatures up to level 10. Each level from 11 to 20 needs 10 more skill. From skill 100, your skill divided by 5 is the highest creature level. The zone lists are Classic-era zone levels. The rule and the zone levels are not confirmed for Forever.

The *Crafts* column is an estimate. A recipe always gives a skill point while it is orange. The chance decreases when the recipe is yellow and green, and a grey recipe gives no points. The estimate uses the Classic-era rule, which is not confirmed for Forever.

## Where recipes come from

A trainer teaches the Camp Chair. The client does not hold the skill that the trainer asks for. The recipe turns yellow at skill 20, and camp utility starts at skill 20. Each profession gives a "Camping 101" quest for its Tier 1 feature. First do the quest The Great Outdoors and make a Basic Campfire with Cooking. Recipe items teach the Field Guide at skill 140 and the Trapper's Workbench at skill 300. Tier 2 and Tier 3 blueprints drop from dungeon and raid bosses. The known blueprint drops do not include the 2 Skinning blueprints. No Skinning recipe needs Merchant's Favor, and no Merchant's Favor merchant sells Skinning recipes. No recipe item binds when you pick it up.

These sources are Classic-era values. They are not confirmed for Forever. The Legacy talent Reagent Economy removes the reagents of Tier 1 camp features.

## Tips

- A camp accepts 1 feature from each player. Higher tiers include the features of the lower tiers.
- If you cannot skin a creature, use the Classic rule. From skill 100, your skill divided by 5 is the highest creature level.
- A creature that is far below your skill gives no skill point. This is the Classic-era rule.
- In Classic, you can skin a beast that a different player killed and looted. This is not confirmed for Forever.
- Keep Light Leather, Medium Leather and Heavy Leather for the 3 camp features.
- The Camp Chair is grey at skill 25 and the Field Guide is grey at skill 145. Do not use them to level.
- The Trapper's Workbench is yellow, green and grey at skill 300 in the client. It gives no skill points.
- Bountiful Harvest in the Legacy tree gives +20% Scarce materials from Skinning.

## FAQ

??? question "What creature level can I skin?"
    The client data does not give this. In Classic, from skill 100, divide your skill by 5. Below skill 100, skill 1 lets you skin up to level 10. Each level above 10 needs 10 more skill. This is not confirmed for Forever.

??? question "Do I need a tool?"
    Yes. In Classic you need a Skinning Knife in your bags. Trade supplies vendors sell it. This is not confirmed for Forever.

??? question "What is Tanning?"
    Tanning is the Skinning ability that makes camp features. It has 3 recipes: Camp Chair, Field Guide and Trapper's Workbench.

??? question "Is the Field Guide camp feature the same as the Field Guide Legacy talent?"
    No. The Legacy talent Field Guide decreases camp cooldowns. The Skinning Field Guide is a Tier 2 camp feature.

??? question "Which leather comes from which creatures?"
    The client data does not give this. In Classic, the leather tier follows the creature level, from Light Leather to Rugged Leather. In Classic, creatures also give a hide of the same tier at a low rate.

??? question "Does Skinning give Legacy Points?"
    No. Legacy Points come from skill 150, 225 and 300 in the 6 crafting professions. Gathering professions do not count.

??? question "Where do Pristine Leather and Fel Leather come from?"
    The client data does not say. Leatherworking, Blacksmithing and Tailoring recipes use Pristine Leather, and Leatherworking recipes use Fel Leather. The wiki says that gathering gives Scarce materials, but it does not name them.
