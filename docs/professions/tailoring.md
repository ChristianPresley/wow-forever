Tailoring changes cloth into bolts, and bolts into bags and cloth armor. It needs no gathering profession, because cloth drops from humanoids. In Forever it also makes the bags for the new **reagent bag** slot. It has 183 new recipes, and most of them are cloth armor.

## New in Forever

- **Reagent bags.** Forever has a reagent bag slot. Tailoring makes 7 bags for it, from the Linen Reagent Bag at skill 5 to the Bottomless Reagent Bag at skill 300.
- **183 new recipes.** Almost all of them are cloth armor. Recipe items teach them, and a trainer teaches only the 2 Faction Banners.
- **67 Merchant's Favor patterns.** Mivin Shadowweave sells them at Three Corners, the Alliance camp. They include 6 color sets for skill 60 to 125 and 2 reagent bags.
- **16 Stormsewn patterns** (Azure and Cloudy). They make uncommon armor from skill 50, and the recipe items bind when you pick them up.
- **18 Filigreed patterns** make rare gowns, leggings and circlets at skill 75, 100 and 125.
- **New level 60 epics.** The Conviction, Manaflare and Demonheart patterns and 5 other epics use a new reagent, Malleable Essence of Nature.
- **Changed recipes.** 209 Classic recipes changed. The client data does not say what changed in each recipe. Runecloth Belt, for example, turns yellow at 225, and its Classic-era value is 270.
- **Mooncloth** has learn skill 230 and stays orange until 270. It needs 2 Felcloth, as in Classic. Its recipe item binds when you pick it up. The client data does not give a cooldown.
- **3 camp features.** The Faction Banner gives a Spirit buff at a camp.
- No Classic recipe is gone. 25 recipes are the same as in Classic.

## Level 1 to 300

The path uses trainer recipes to skill 240. After that, the trainer has no recipe that gives points at a low cost, so the path uses 3 recipe items.

The client does not hold the skill that the trainer asks for. Each learn skill with the label *estimate* is the yellow skill minus the Classic-era difference between learn and yellow for that recipe. The Warcraft Wiki pattern list gives the same numbers for the bolts, Woolen Cape, Black Mageweave Leggings and Runecloth Belt. If the trainer does not offer a recipe, continue the step before it.

Make bolts only when you need them. Bolts of each cloth also give skill points for a short range, and each step note shows that range.

The *Crafts* column is an estimate. A recipe always gives a skill point while it is orange. The chance decreases when the recipe is yellow and green, and a grey recipe gives no points. The estimate uses the Classic-era rule, which is not confirmed for Forever.

## Where recipes come from

A trainer teaches 85 of the 417 recipes. The client does not hold the skill that the trainer asks for. Recipe items teach the other recipes, and the client does not say where these items come from.

The recipe items for 161 recipes bind when you pick them up. Mivin Shadowweave sells 67 of them for Merchant's Favor at Three Corners in Redridge Mountains. Three Corners is the Alliance camp of the Azeroth Commerce Authority. The Horde camp of Durotar Supply and Logistics is west of the Crossroads in the Barrens. The data does not name the Horde merchant. You get Merchant's Favor when you turn in crates. The wiki says that high-end recipes also need reputation. The other 94 include the Stormsewn, Swarmtender's, Venomspew, Conviction, Manaflare and Demonheart patterns and Mooncloth.

The Warcraft Wiki pattern list gives a vendor as the source of the Stormsewn, Swarmtender's, Venomspew, Conviction, Manaflare and Demonheart patterns. It gives drops as the source of the Filigreed patterns. These sources are not in the client data.

The client data shows no recipe item for Mooncloth Boots and Gordok Ogre Suit. In Classic, quests teach these 2 recipes. This is not confirmed for Forever.

The client also holds 54 Tailoring recipes from Season of Discovery. No merchant sells them, so this guide does not list them.

## Tips

- Keep 2 stacks of each cloth for First Aid. Tailoring uses much more cloth than First Aid.
- Do not sell bolts. Almost all recipes use bolts, not cloth.
- In Classic, a trade supplies vendor sells thread, Bleach and the basic dyes. Cerulean Dye, Magenta Dye and Viridian Dye are new Alchemy trainer recipes. Buy them from an Alchemist.
- Collect Wool Cloth early. In Classic, Wool Cloth has the shortest supply of all cloth. This is not confirmed for Forever.
- Make the trainer bag of each tier for your characters: Woolen Bag, Small Silk Pack and Mageweave Bag. The client data does not give prices.
- Boots of Darkness is yellow when you learn it, and Felcloth Bag is green. Do not use them for skill points.
- If you have Enchanting, disenchant the uncommon armor that you make.
- Tailoring gives 1 Legacy point each at skill 150, 225 and 300.
- Before you buy a Merchant's Favor pattern, read its reagents. Some pieces need 8 or more of a rare material.

## FAQ

??? question "Does Tailoring need a gathering profession?"
    No. Cloth drops from humanoids. Enchanting is the usual second profession. A gathering profession is a second option.

??? question "At what skill do I learn each trainer recipe?"
    The client does not hold the trainer skill. The path gives estimates from the Classic-era values and marks them as estimates.

??? question "Why does the path use recipe items after skill 240?"
    Runecloth Belt is green at 240 and grey at 255. Above that skill, the data shows only 2 other recipes without a recipe item, Mooncloth Boots and Gordok Ogre Suit. In Classic, quests teach them, and they need rare materials.

??? question "Does Mooncloth have a cooldown?"
    The client data does not say. In Classic, Mooncloth has a cooldown of 4 days and you make it at a moonwell. It is not confirmed for Forever.

??? question "Which Merchant's Favor patterns do I buy first?"
    Buy the Mageweave Reagent Bag and the Runecloth Reagent Bag first. Then buy 1 color set for your class.

??? question "How much cloth do I need for 1 to 300?"
    The materials table gives the estimate for each bracket. The real number changes, because yellow and green recipes do not always give a point.
