# Economy

## Structure

!!! forever "No token, no boosts"
    **No WoW Token and no paid boosts.** Clay Stone: "the journey is the game".

- **A realmless economy.** Each ruleset is one regional mega-realm. Thus players share the auction house, mail and trade **in a ruleset and faction**. Blizzard has not said if anything goes from one ruleset to a different ruleset. Characters cannot move between rulesets.
- **Gold sources:** quests (1,000+ new) and vendor sales.
- **Gold sinks:** training (~206g for levels 1–60), riding (100g and 1,000g), consumables, repairs, dual spec (50g), respecs, transmog outfit slots.
- **Black Market NPCs** sell certain materials at fixed prices.
- You can buy high-end recipes with **Merchant's Favor** from profession crates. See [Professions](../characters/professions.md#crates-merchants-favor).
- **Hidden item stats** make early AH prices difficult. Items that nobody has found possibly show no stats. See [Items](items.md#hidden-items).

## GDKP

- **Banned in NA and EU**, as in the Season of Discovery and Anniversary policy (Josh Greenfield, 17 Sep). Blizzard's reason: GDKP usually becomes the format of all groups.
- **Allowed in Korea and other regions** (Clay Stone to 4Gamers, 8 Oct). Some early Korean reports said that there was a full ban. The 8 Oct statement made the policy clear.

## Anti-RMT measures

| When | Measure |
|---|---|
| 17 Sep | GDKP ban announced |
| Beta | **~2,100 gold-buying** and **~1,600 gold-selling or botting** accounts closed (headlines said "nearly 4,000"). Greenfield admitted that Blizzard was "probably too lenient" on buyers |
| 1 Oct | Blue post *Actions Taken Against Botting and RMT*. Penalties: gold removal, suspension or permanent closure. A warning on trades from strangers |
| 13/14 Oct | **Temporary Economy Holds**: During an investigation of possible RMT, Blizzard can freeze **trade, mail, guild bank and AH**. No permanent strike unless RMT is proven. Possibly also for other WoW versions in the future |
| Pre-launch | Streamer **Payo** banned because he told viewers to buy gold |
