# Economy

## Structure

!!! forever "No token, no boosts"
    **No WoW Token and no paid boosts.** Clay Stone: "the journey is the game".

- **A realmless economy.** Each ruleset is one regional mega-realm, so the auction house, mail and trade are shared **within a ruleset and faction**. Whether anything crosses rulesets has not been stated, and characters can't move between them.
- **Gold sources:** quests (1,000+ new) and vendor sales.
- **Gold sinks:** training (~206g for levels 1–60), riding (100g and 1,000g), consumables, repairs, dual spec (50g), respecs, transmog outfit slots.
- **Black Market NPCs** sell certain materials at fixed prices.
- **Merchant's Favor** from profession crates buys high-end recipes. See [Professions](../characters/professions.md#crates-merchants-favor).
- **Hidden item stats** make early AH pricing tricky; undiscovered items may show no stats. See [Items](items.md#hidden-items).

## GDKP

- **Banned in NA and EU**, continuing the Season of Discovery and Anniversary policy (Josh Greenfield, 17 Sep). Blizzard's reason is that GDKP tends to take over every group.
- **Allowed in Korea and other regions** (Clay Stone to 4Gamers, 8 Oct). Some early Korean coverage reported a blanket ban, but the 8 Oct statement clarified it.

## Anti-RMT measures

| When | Measure |
|---|---|
| 17 Sep | GDKP ban announced |
| Beta | **~2,100 gold-buying** and **~1,600 gold-selling or botting** accounts closed (headlined as "nearly 4,000"). Greenfield admitted Blizzard had been "probably too lenient" on buyers |
| 1 Oct | Blue post *Actions Taken Against Botting and RMT*. Penalties: gold removal, suspension or permanent closure. A warning on trades from strangers |
| 13/14 Oct | **Temporary Economy Holds**: Blizzard can freeze **trade, mail, guild bank and AH** for suspected RMT during an investigation. No permanent strike unless RMT is proven. Could extend to other WoW versions |
| Pre-launch | Streamer **Payo** banned for encouraging viewers to buy gold |
