# Items & Collectibles

## Legendaries

- **Taldramir, the Whispering Wood**, Forever's **first legendary**, is a staff. Blizzard showed it on the Warcraft Twitch stream about 1 hour before launch. Blizzard made a physical prop of it with **Wētā Workshop**. Players probably get it from the raids that open on 9 Dec *unconfirmed*{ .unconfirmed }.
- Blizzard gave a hint about a **legendary connected to Hyjal Summit**. It is possibly Taldramir.
- A **new legendary questline** is on the spring 2027 roadmap.
- Classic legendaries (Sulfuras, Thunderfury and others) dropped only during the one-time [End of Beta event](../world/events.md#end-of-beta-event-2223-oct-2026).

## Hidden items

!!! forever "Hidden by design"
    Blizzard **encrypts the item database**.

What that means:

- About **12,594 items (~40%)** shipped without readable stats. Players found **4,419** of them during beta.
- When a player finds the item, its stats unlock **for the full region**.
- **No PTRs** for new raids. Blizzard says that it disabled "thousands of secrets" during beta.
- Josh Greenfield expects launch week to be "the Wild West" of item discovery. AtlasLoot and Wowhead will not have stats until players find the items.
- Some loot is not documented, even inside Blizzard. Developers say that they do not know Herod's drop.

## Dungeon loot design

- Unique stats themed to class, spec, biome or enemy type.
- Each boss in a new dungeon reportedly always drops a **blue** item.
- Some new dungeon items have **special effects** designed with Hardcore in mind.
- Group loot unlocks transmog for **all players** who were eligible.
