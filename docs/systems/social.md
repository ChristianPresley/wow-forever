# Social Systems

## Rulesets replace realms

You pick a ruleset instead of a realm. Grouping, dungeons, raids, guilds and trade are limited to your **ruleset and faction**. Battlegrounds mix rulesets, except Hardcore. See [Rulesets & Megaserver](../about/rulesets.md).

- **The PvP ruleset faction lock:** an account can make characters for only one faction there. Players complained it splits guilds and friends ("Horde PvP Faction Lock is preventing guilds and friends from playing together").
- **Population balancing:** at launch, **PvP Horde creation was locked** in NA and EU, and some race/class combinations (e.g. Undead Paladin, Tauren Shaman) were **throttled**.
- **Preferred Online Language:** an account-wide setting with a 24-hour cooldown.

## Layering

Layering (several copies of the world inside one mega-realm) is confirmed, and it was **the top beta complaint**: it splits groups and interrupts quests and world PvP. Clay Stone said layering won't be removed but promised fixes. At launch some zones felt empty, and a 6 Nov hotfix improved long-range player visibility.

## Names & guild identity

- Names have two parts and are unique per region. There is a "My Secondary Name" nameplate option.
- With no realm reputation, guilds recruit across the whole ruleset and faction. GuildOrder advises storing **full names** in rosters and DKP logs, since first names repeat.
- An in-game guild finder at launch is **unconfirmed**. Guild banks exist.

## Grouping

- A Classic-style **LFG listing** only: no auto-queue and no teleport. Massively OP reported a "playstyle" filter in it.
- **Meeting Stones don't summon**; a Warlock's Ritual of Summoning needs the Warlock plus 2 party members.
- **Campfires** are explicitly social hubs. See [Camping](camping.md).

## Chat & Discord

- **Discord account linking** under *Options → Social → Enable Discord Functionality*.
- **Guild chat mirroring:** officers link a channel under *Guild & Communities (J) → Roster → Guild Settings → Guild Control → Discord Settings*. Discord messages go to `/guild` or a dedicated `/discord` channel. Players who haven't linked Discord aren't mirrored. This works in both Forever and retail.
- Blizzard promotes the **"WoW Portal Room"** Discord for meeting players.
- Whispers get their own tab by default.

## Rules of conduct

- **GDKP** is banned in NA and EU. See [Economy](economy.md#gdkp).
- RP rulesets enforce naming and behavior rules.
- Blizzard suspended beta accounts over offensive names.
