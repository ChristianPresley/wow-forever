# Social Systems

## Rulesets replace realms

You choose a ruleset, not a realm. Groups, dungeons, raids, guilds and trade are limited to your **ruleset and faction**. Battlegrounds mix rulesets, but not Hardcore. See [Rulesets & Megaserver](../about/rulesets.md).

- **The PvP ruleset faction lock:** an account can make characters for only one faction there. Players complained that it separates guilds and friends ("Horde PvP Faction Lock is preventing guilds and friends from playing together").
- **Population balancing:** at launch, **PvP Horde creation was locked** in NA and EU, and Blizzard **throttled** some race/class combinations (for example Undead Paladin, Tauren Shaman).
- **Preferred Online Language:** an account-wide setting with a 24-hour cooldown.

## Layering

Layering (many copies of the world in one mega-realm) is confirmed. It was **the top beta complaint**: it separates groups and interrupts quests and world PvP. Clay Stone said that Blizzard will not remove layering, but he promised fixes. At launch, some zones seemed empty. A 6 Nov hotfix improved the visibility of players at long range.

## Names & guild identity

- Names have 2 parts and are unique in each region. There is a "My Secondary Name" nameplate option.
- There is no realm reputation, so guilds recruit across the full ruleset and faction. GuildOrder recommends that you keep **full names** in rosters and DKP logs, because first names repeat.
- An in-game guild finder at launch is *unconfirmed*{ .unconfirmed }. Guild banks exist.

## Grouping

- Only a Classic-style **LFG list**: no auto-queue and no teleport. Massively OP reported that it has a "playstyle" filter.
- **Meeting Stones do not summon**. A Warlock's Ritual of Summoning needs the Warlock and 2 party members.
- Blizzard clearly designed **campfires** as social hubs. See [Camping](camping.md).

## Chat & Discord

- **Discord account linking** under *Options → Social → Enable Discord Functionality*.
- **Guild chat mirroring:** officers link a channel under *Guild & Communities (J) → Roster → Guild Settings → Guild Control → Discord Settings*. Discord messages go to `/guild` or a dedicated `/discord` channel. The game does not mirror messages from players who have not linked Discord. This works in both Forever and retail.
- Blizzard recommends the **"WoW Portal Room"** Discord as a place to meet players.
- Whispers get their own tab by default.

## Rules of conduct

- **GDKP** is banned in NA and EU. See [Economy](economy.md#gdkp).
- RP rulesets enforce naming and behavior rules.
- Blizzard suspended beta accounts because of offensive names.
