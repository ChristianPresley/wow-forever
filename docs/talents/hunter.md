Each Hunter tree is a damage spec in Forever. **Beast Mastery** is the pet tree and the tree for leveling. **Marksmanship** is the ranged tree that players expect in raids, and it can play with no pet. **Survival** is the melee and trap tree, and it is strong in PvP. All level 60 builds on this page are theory until the raids open.

## How to read this page

The Hunter has 51 talents in 3 trees. You get 1 talent point for each level from 10 to 60, for a total of 51 points.

- **Opens at:** the number of points that you must spend in the same tree before the row opens. Each row needs 5 more points.
- **Max:** the maximum rank of the talent.
- **Leveling:** the value of the talent when you quest and do dungeons from level 10 to 59.
- **PvE:** the value at level 60 in dungeons and raids. This is damage, healing or tank value, for the role of the tree.
- **PvP:** the value in battlegrounds and world PvP.

**S**{ .tier .tier-s } Core. Take it in each build of that type. **A**{ .tier .tier-a } Strong. Take it when you can. **B**{ .tier .tier-b } Good. Use it to fill points. **C**{ .tier .tier-c } Situational or a personal preference. **D**{ .tier .tier-d } Do not take it.

=== "Marksmanship (raid)"

    **Raid DPS**{ .pill .pill--info } **5/46/0**{ .pill .pill--outline }

    The 5/46/0 split that Warcraft Tavern gives for raids, built from the launch client. It takes all damage talents in Marksmanship and plays with no pet for Lone Wolf. It has no hit talent, so get hit from gear.

    - **Beast Mastery (5):** Deadly Aspects 5/5
    - **Marksmanship (46):** Hawk Eye 3/3 · Lethal Attacks 5/5 · Improved Stings 3/3 · Careful Aim 5/5 · Rapid Killing 2/2 · Improved Arcane Shot 5/5 · Lone Wolf 1/1 · Trueshot Aura 1/1 · Mortal Shots 5/5 · Improved Serpent Sting 5/5 · Rapid Recuperation 2/2 · Barrage 3/3 · Ranged Weapon Specialization 5/5 · Sniper Shot 1/1

    Key choices:

    - **Improved Stings:** 20% Serpent Sting damage
    - **Rapid Killing:** A shorter Rapid Fire cooldown. Rapid Recuperation needs it
    - **Lone Wolf:** 20% damage with no pet. If you keep the pet, move this point to Improved Tracking
    - **Rapid Recuperation:** Your mana talent. Keep Serpent Sting on the boss
    - **Improved Serpent Sting:** The weakest 5 points of the build. You can move them to Efficiency if you have no mana
    - **Hawk Eye:** A personal preference. You can move these points to Efficiency
    - **Deadly Aspects:** 30% ranged attack speed procs

    - It is not confirmed how much damage a pet does in raids. If the pet does more than 20% of your damage, a build with a pet is better.
    - Keep a pet in the stable for fights where its utility helps.

## FAQ

??? question "Is it a good idea to level with Lone Wolf?"
    No. Warcraft Tavern and Method recommend Beast Mastery for leveling, because the pet tanks for you. Lone Wolf opens at 10 Marksmanship points and is a raid talent.

??? question "Where is Trueshot Aura in the tree?"
    In the launch client, Trueshot Aura is in Marksmanship row 4 and needs 15 points. Beta-era information called it a 21-point talent. It gives 20 Ranged Attack Power, and Sniper Shot needs it.

??? question "How does Rapid Recuperation work?"
    In the launch client, it starts when you apply Serpent Sting and lasts 15 s. During that time, 25/50% of your mana regeneration continues while you cast. The value is 50/100% after you use the Rapid Killing bonus. Beta-era information gave a constant 50% while Serpent Sting is on the target.

??? question "Does Improved Arcane Shot make Summon Hawk faster?"
    No. Summon Hawk and Arcane Shot share a cooldown, but the tooltip says that Improved Arcane Shot does not change abilities that share the cooldown. Thus, Beast Mastery builds do not take it.

??? question "What does Deadly Aspects do?"
    It does not increase the Attack Power of your aspects. In the launch client it gives a 2–10% chance for 30% attack speed for 12 s. Auto Shot procs it with Aspect of the Hawk, and melee auto attacks proc it with Aspect of the Beast.
