Each Rogue tree is a damage spec in Forever. **Combat** is the easy tree for leveling and the default tree for raids. **Assassination** is the poison and crit tree, and it gets **Mutilate** at 30 points. **Subtlety** is the tree for openers, bleeds and PvP. The new ability **Shank** has a talent in each tree, so most builds put points in 2 or 3 trees.

## How to read this page

The Rogue has 54 talents in 3 trees. You get 1 talent point for each level from 10 to 60, for a total of 51 points.

- **Opens at:** the number of points that you must spend in the same tree before the row opens. Each row needs 5 more points.
- **Max:** the maximum rank of the talent.
- **Leveling:** the value of the talent when you quest and do dungeons from level 10 to 59.
- **PvE:** the value at level 60 in dungeons and raids. This is damage, healing or tank value, for the role of the tree.
- **PvP:** the value in battlegrounds and world PvP.

**S**{ .tier .tier-s } Core. Take it in each build of that type. **A**{ .tier .tier-a } Strong. Take it when you can. **B**{ .tier .tier-b } Good. Use it to fill points. **C**{ .tier .tier-c } Situational or a personal preference. **D**{ .tier .tier-d } Do not take it.

## Level 60 builds

Dual spec becomes available at level 40 for 50g. Thus, you can keep 2 of these builds at the same time.

=== "Combat (raid)"

    **Raid DPS**{ .pill .pill--info } **16/35/0**{ .pill .pill--outline }

    The default build for dungeons and raids. It works with swords, axes, maces and fist weapons. It takes all the damage talents of Combat and the first 16 points of Assassination for crit.

    - **Assassination (16):** Malice 5/5 · Ruthlessness 3/3 · Improved Slice and Dice 3/3 · Vigor 2/2 · Lethality 2/2 · Cold Blood 1/1
    - **Combat (35):** Coup de Grace 3/3 · Efficient Strikes 2/2 · Lightning Reflexes 3/5 · Precision 3/3 · Endurance 2/2 · Composure 2/2 · Flawless Execution 1/1 · Weapon Expertise 2/2 · Aggression 3/3 · Dual Wield Specialization 2/2 · Blade Flurry 1/1 · Hack and Slash 5/5 · Flash of Steel 5/5 · Adrenaline Rush 1/1

    Key choices:

    - **Lightning Reflexes:** Filler to open row 3. You can use Improved Sprint
    - **Endurance:** Filler to open row 4. For dungeons, you can move these points to Deflection and Riposte
    - **Hack and Slash:** The bonus applies to the weapon that makes the attack. No source has tested the best weapon type
    - **Cold Blood:** You can move this point to Blood Splatter if you use Rupture

    - With daggers and Backstab, move the points from Efficient Strikes, Aggression and Composure into Puncturing Wounds and Opportunity.
    - If your raid needs Expose Armor from you, move 2 points from Vigor to Improved Expose Armor.

=== "Assassination (Mutilate)"

    **Raid DPS**{ .pill .pill--info } **35/11/5**{ .pill .pill--outline }

    A deep Assassination build with a dagger in each hand. Mutilate crits give 3 Combo Points with Seal Fate. Venom, Vile Poisons and Improved Poisons make poisons a large part of your damage. It takes Opportunity and Puncturing Wounds, because they include Mutilate in Forever.

    - **Assassination (35):** Malice 5/5 · Ruthlessness 3/3 · Improved Slice and Dice 3/3 · Vigor 2/2 · Lethality 2/2 · Vile Poisons 4/5 · Cold Blood 1/1 · Improved Poisons 5/5 · Relentless Strikes 5/5 · Venom 1/1 · Seal Fate 3/3 · Mutilate 1/1
    - **Combat (11):** Coup de Grace 3/3 · Efficient Strikes 2/2 · Puncturing Wounds 3/3 · Precision 3/3
    - **Subtlety (5):** Opportunity 5/5

    Key choices:

    - **Vile Poisons:** 4/5 leaves 1 point for Improved Slice and Dice. You can move it
    - **Opportunity:** 20% more Mutilate damage for 5 points
    - **Efficient Strikes:** For the Sinister Strike that adds the last Combo Point. You can use Lightning Reflexes
    - **Puncturing Wounds:** 15% more Mutilate crit, thus more Seal Fate procs

    - Use Shank each time it is ready. It starts Venom.
    - The build has no cooldown for 2 targets. For dungeons, the Combat build is better.

    [Open this build in the talent calculator](https://wowforevertalents.com/rogue/?t=h00530320241551031-320303-005)

=== "Subtlety (bleeds)"

    **Raid DPS**{ .pill .pill--info } **16/4/31**{ .pill .pill--outline }

    A bleed build for players who want to try Subtlety in raids. Hemorrhage increases your Rupture and Garrote damage, and Ghostly Strike lets you apply Garrote again. Death Mark stores bleed damage at 2 times the rate. Icy Veins says that Subtlety can possibly become the strongest spec, but no source has tested it at level 60.

    - **Assassination (16):** Malice 5/5 · Ruthlessness 3/3 · Improved Slice and Dice 2/3 · Vigor 2/2 · Blood Splatter 1/1 · Lethality 2/2 · Cold Blood 1/1
    - **Combat (4):** Coup de Grace 2/3 · Efficient Strikes 2/2
    - **Subtlety (31):** Opportunity 5/5 · Dirty Deeds 2/2 · Camouflage 3/3 · Improved Ambush 3/3 · Initiative 3/3 · Premeditation 1/1 · Serrated Blades 3/3 · Elusiveness 2/2 · Ghostly Strike 1/1 · Preparation 1/1 · Hemorrhage 1/1 · Boot Knife 5/5 · Death Mark 1/1

    Key choices:

    - **Opportunity:** It includes Garrote
    - **Dirty Deeds:** Garrote from the front, for 20 less Energy
    - **Improved Ambush:** Filler. Use Ambush after Ghostly Strike when Garrote is active
    - **Initiative:** Each Garrote gives 2 Combo Points
    - **Elusiveness:** Filler. A shorter Vanish cooldown gives more openers
    - **Camouflage:** Filler to open row 6
    - **Blood Splatter:** Rupture lasts 6 s longer and Garrote 9 s longer
    - **Efficient Strikes:** Hemorrhage costs 5 less Energy
    - **Coup de Grace:** More damage and a faster storage rate for Death Mark

    - It is not confirmed that raid bosses can bleed in each fight.
    - The damage cap of Death Mark limits its value. Read the tooltip for your cap.

    [Open this build in the talent calculator](https://wowforevertalents.com/rogue/?t=h00530221201-22-005233031321001151)

## FAQ

??? question "Where are Mutilate and Venom in the tree?"
    In the launch client, Venom is in Assassination row 5 and needs 20 points. Mutilate is the last talent, needs 30 points and needs Venom. Some information from the beta gave Mutilate at 21 points and Venom as the last talent.

??? question "Is Venom a finisher or a passive talent?"
    It is a passive talent. Each Shank gives +30% Poison damage and +20% chance to apply Poisons for 6 s. Blizzard's deep dive from the beta described a 25-Energy finisher.

??? question "Is the last Subtlety talent Thousand Cuts or Death Mark?"
    It is Death Mark. The launch client has no Thousand Cuts, Quietus, Cutthroat, Improved Distract or Murder. Ghostly Strike now has the effect that lets you use Ambush or Garrote without Stealth.

??? question "Which weapon type is best for Hack and Slash?"
    It is not known. Axes and swords get extra attacks, daggers and fist weapons get crit, and maces ignore armor. No source has published simulations, thus use the best weapon that you have.

??? question "Why do builds take talents for Shank?"
    Shank has a talent in each tree. Venom makes poisons stronger, Flash of Steel gives 15 more Energy, and Boot Knife gives 2 Combo Points. Use Shank each time it is ready.
