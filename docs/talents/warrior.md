The Warrior has 2 damage trees and 1 tank tree in Forever. **Protection** is the tank tree and the safe tree for solo leveling. **Arms** is the two-hander tree for groups and PvP. **Fury** is the dual-wield tree for raids. Most damage builds put points in Arms and Fury, because Cruelty, Impale and Deep Wounds help both specs.

## How to read this page

The Warrior has 53 talents in 3 trees. You get 1 talent point for each level from 10 to 60, for a total of 51 points.

- **Opens at:** the number of points that you must spend in the same tree before the row opens. Each row needs 5 more points.
- **Max:** the maximum rank of the talent.
- **Leveling:** the value of the talent when you quest and do dungeons from level 10 to 59.
- **PvE:** the value at level 60 in dungeons and raids. This is damage, healing or tank value, for the role of the tree.
- **PvP:** the value in battlegrounds and world PvP.

**S**{ .tier .tier-s } Core. Take it in each build of that type. **A**{ .tier .tier-a } Strong. Take it when you can. **B**{ .tier .tier-b } Good. Use it to fill points. **C**{ .tier .tier-c } Situational or a personal preference. **D**{ .tier .tier-d } Do not take it.

## Level 60 builds

Dual spec becomes available at level 40 for 50g. Thus, you can keep 2 of these builds at the same time.

=== "Protection (tank)"

    **Tank**{ .pill .pill--info } **5/5/41**{ .pill .pill--outline }

    A deep Protection build for dungeons and raids. It takes each threat and survival talent in the tree. The last 10 points go into Deflection for parry and Cruelty for threat and rage.

    - **Arms (5):** Deflection 5/5
    - **Fury (5):** Cruelty 5/5
    - **Protection (41):** Improved Bloodrage 2/2 · Shield Specialization 5/5 · Anticipation 5/5 · Improved Revenge 3/3 · Improved Thunder Clap 2/3 · Last Stand 1/1 · Master of Defense 2/2 · Defiance 3/3 · Improved Sunder Armor 3/3 · Vanguard 1/1 · Improved Shield Bash 2/2 · Improved Shield Wall 2/2 · Concussion Blow 1/1 · Focused Rage 3/3 · Bastion 5/5 · Shield Slam 1/1

    Key choices:

    - **Improved Thunder Clap:** For threat on packs. You can move these points to Iron Will
    - **Improved Shield Bash:** A silence for caster mobs in dungeons
    - **Bastion:** More damage gives more threat
    - **Deflection:** 5% parry. Master of Defense gives rage for each parry
    - **Cruelty:** Crit for threat and rage. If threat is not a problem, move these points to Iron Will

    - Defiance, Bastion and Master of Defense need a shield.
    - No source has published a level 60 tank build. This build follows the direction of the class guide.

    [Open this build in the talent calculator](https://wowforevertalents.com/warrior/?t=h05-05-250532120331221351)

    Key choices:

    - **Improved Rend:** Deep Wounds needs 3/3
    - **Improved Tactical Mastery:** Anger Management needs 3/3
    - **Improved Charge:** Filler to open the next rows

    - **Improved Berserker Rage:** 10 Rage each time. You can move these points to Booming Voice

    The two-hander raid build. It takes all the Arms damage talents, then Cruelty, Unbridled Wrath and Enrage from Fury. The class guide says that Arms is probably competitive in the 10- and 20-player raids.

## FAQ

??? question "Where are Raging Blows, Boundless Rage and Precision?"
    The launch client does not have them. Guides from the beta list them in the Fury tree. The launch Fury tree has Furious Precision, which gives hit chance only to off-hand attacks.

??? question "What does Reckless Swings do?"
    In the launch client, your next 4 melee attacks give 5 more Rage, with a 15 s cooldown. The heal that some sources describe is a different talent, Gore Drinker. The client shows the cost of Reckless Swings as 25% of base power, so the real cost is not confirmed.

??? question "Do the signature talents need 16 points?"
    No. Spearing Strike, Reckless Swings and Vanguard are in row 4, which opens at 15 points in the tree. The talent is your 16th point, so the earliest level is 25.

??? question "Is Iron Will in the Arms tree?"
    No. In the launch client, Iron Will is in Protection row 1. It decreases the duration of Stun and Fear effects by 3–15%. One-Handed Weapon Specialization is also not gone: it is now Bastion in Protection row 6.

??? question "Bloodthrill or Weaponmaster?"
    You can take the 2 talents. They are in the same row, and a full Arms build has the points for 2/2 Bloodthrill and 5/5 Weaponmaster. When you level, take Bloodthrill first.
