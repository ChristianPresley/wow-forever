# Lore & Story

## When and where

- **Year 1 of World of Warcraft, 25 ADP.** That is after *Warcraft III: Reforged – Forsaken Kingdom*, "before Molten Core", before Kazzak reopens the Dark Portal, and before Northrend.
- **A separate continuity.** Forever lives in its own **"time bubble"** and does **not** share continuity with retail. Blizzard says that this lets it tell new stories, and the foundational stories stay the same.
- **It stays there.** There will be no Burning Crusade or later expansion. The world becomes larger, not later in time.

## How Forever differs from the original first year

    - The **Skyborne** have arrived from Skywall.

## Story philosophy: "found photographs"

From Blizzard's BlizzCon *Found Photos* panel: "found photographs" is **Chris Metzen**'s term from Season of Discovery. It means new stories that seem to be always part of Classic. Blizzard prefers local mysteries, realistic conflicts and characters that were not used much. New content has 3 categories:

<div class="compare" markdown>

| Bucket | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Anticipated** | Places that players asked for during a long time | Mount Hyjal |
| **New but familiar** | New stories rooted in existing history | Riverglades |
| **Unexpected** | Used only a little | Zephras Isle and the Skyborne |

</div>

There is **no single "big bad"**. This agrees with the stated pillar that *not every story is about saving the world*. It also agrees with the Warcraft Wiki pillar of *a dangerous Azeroth*. The tone is realistic: everyday people and the effect of the wars on them. BlizzardWatch said that the panel did not explain how the parts of the lore connect.

## Companion story: *Forsaken Kingdom*

*Warcraft III: Reforged – Forsaken Kingdom* came out at BlizzCon 2026. It tells the story of the fall of Lordaeron and the founding of the Undercity. It leads directly into Forever's setting. It is included in the [Warcraft Forever Collection](../about/editions.md).
