# Travel

!!! forever "No flying, by design"

## New overland paths

- Redridge, the Badlands, the Swamp of Sorrows and the Burning Steppes all connect to the **Riverglades**.
- Desolace (Valley of Bones) connects to **Shen'dralas**.
- Mulgore (Skywatcher Plateau) has a path toward Desolace.
- Winterspring (Darkwhisper Gorge) leads to **Mount Hyjal**.
- Stonetalon and Thousand Needles have widened eastern paths *fan source*{ .fan-source }.

## Other notes

- **Mages** of both factions reportedly learn *Teleport: Dalaran* at level 50 *fan source*{ .fan-source }.
- **Meeting Stones don't summon.** Only a Warlock's Ritual of Summoning does.
- Gamepad players can end a flight early at the next flight point.
- The **Chronoboon Displacer** (from Season of Discovery) works in the open world only.
