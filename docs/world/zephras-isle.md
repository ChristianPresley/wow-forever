## Lore

The **Skyborne** call themselves the ***shen'dorei***, "hidden people". They descend from the **Shen'dralar**, Highborne rebels of **Eldre'Thalas** (Dire Maul) who opposed **Prince Tortheldrin**'s plan to summon a demon. They fled by sea after the War of the Ancients. Wind spirits hid them in Skywall (Warcraft Tavern calls it a pact with the wind elementals), where they have lived across several floating islands for about **10,000 years**.

**The crisis:** the air elementals who shared the island vanished years ago. The magic and pylons that keep Zephras hidden and anchored are failing. Contact with the sister islands **Anvilas** and **Siroccas** has been lost, and refugees from them are arriving on a Zephras that is starting to break apart.

**Ruler:** High Elder **Talaanis Shadowsong**, who lives atop Valanaar's central tree. The island was previously ruled by **the Shal'nan**.

## Story walkthrough

??? abstract "Chapter 1: Thendal Village"
    Every Skyborne starts in the northwest. Quests: *Coming of Age*, *Harmony in Balance*, *The Cirrusfly Queen*, *Harvesting Windstones* (grants a gathering-profession voucher), *Falling With Style*, *The Anchors of Zephras*. The Ursera bear chain ends with Urs'anah. Faction openers: *The Gift of Skysight* (Windshaper) and *Reading the Leylines* (High Order).

??? abstract "Chapter 2: The road to Shen'dar Village"
    You fight Al'Aketh thugs under Malduko Cloudcrash. *Blessing of Zephras* tornadoes give a speed buff. **The Great Outdoors** (Raan Wildwind) teaches the [camping](../systems/camping.md) system. Shen'dar Village (Constable Aonda) has bandit, hippogryph and Prideclaw quests, the bandit boss "Badwind" Bennic, and *WANTED: Vulgara the Insatiable*. The two factions skirmish at the Windsong Standing Stones.

??? abstract "Chapter 3: Secret Agent Elf"
    Wearing the Mark of Akir, you infiltrate the cult-held **Falaath Village** and overhear Lorthuna and Skypriest Aanders. You kill Commander Cyclas, and Commander Aanders at the Western Watch. *A Last Request* recovers the Shadowsong Family Signet.

??? abstract "Chapter 4: Valanaar"
    You give the signet to Talaanis Shadowsong and meet your faction leader (*The Grand Skyseer* / *The Supreme Magister*).

??? abstract "Chapter 5: Gustberry Lowlands"
    Windshaper chain *The Broken Construct*. High Order chain *The Missing Scholar* (Shriekling Den). The Windfield Orchard chain with Aamelia Windfield. **Secret:** `/sing` at the frogs to get the *Musical Gustjumper* pet.

??? abstract "Chapter 6: Shadowgale Forest"
    Windshaper chain *In Service of Zephras* → *Blood for Blood*. The High Order chain takes you to Index Esoteria at the Overlook Standing Stones. A gnome hermit (*The Strange Hermit*) trains Engineering. Nightclaw druids: *Mercy Falls on Deaf Ears* is repeatable for **Nightclaw reputation**, and *The Ties That Bind* and *Wounds of Betrayal* lead to Commander Haalien. Recording crystals in **Nightclaw Cavern** tell the Skyborne origin story.

??? abstract "Chapter 7: The confrontation"
    At the East Pylon Watchtower you kill Commander Belguilos, and a turncoat reveals *The Cult's True Plans*. *Desperate Times* unites Ayessa and Elaadrin. You assault the **Shrine of Akir** and *Confront Lorthuna* through a portal to a Vortex Pinnacle-like area with a wind-bridge event. Lorthuna summons **Rohash, Lord of the East Wind**, and Horde and Alliance reinforcements arrive under Runeweaver and Earthfury. Closing quests: *The Fate of Zephras* and *What Comes Next*. Your faction leader sends you off the island.

## Leaving the island

- **High Order:** airship from the east Skydock to north **Dalaran** (Alterac Mountains).
- **Windshapers:** airship from the west Skydock to **Skywatcher Plateau** (Mulgore), just outside Thunder Bluff.
- Skyborne can leave at any level.
