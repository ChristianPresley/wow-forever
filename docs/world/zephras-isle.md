The **Skyborne** call themselves the ***shen'dorei***, "hidden people". They descend from the **Shen'dralar**, Highborne rebels of **Eldre'Thalas** (Dire Maul). These rebels opposed the plan of **Prince Tortheldrin** to summon a demon. They escaped by sea after the War of the Ancients. Wind spirits hid them in Skywall (Warcraft Tavern calls it a pact with the wind elementals). They have lived on a number of floating islands there for about **10,000 years**.

**The crisis:** the air elementals who shared the island disappeared some years ago. The magic and pylons that keep Zephras hidden and anchored now fail. The Skyborne have no more contact with the sister islands **Anvilas** and **Siroccas**. Refugees from these islands now arrive on Zephras, and Zephras starts to break apart.

**Ruler:** High Elder **Talaanis Shadowsong**, who lives on top of Valanaar's central tree. Before, **the Shal'nan** ruled the island.

## Story walkthrough

??? abstract "Chapter 1: Thendal Village"
    All Skyborne start in the northwest. Quests: *Coming of Age*, *Harmony in Balance*, *The Cirrusfly Queen*, *Harvesting Windstones* (gives a voucher for a gathering profession), *Falling With Style*, *The Anchors of Zephras*. The Ursera bear chain ends with Urs'anah. Faction openers: *The Gift of Skysight* (Windshaper) and *Reading the Leylines* (High Order).

??? abstract "Chapter 2: The road to Shen'dar Village"
    You fight Al'Aketh thugs under Malduko Cloudcrash. *Blessing of Zephras* tornadoes give a speed buff. **The Great Outdoors** (Raan Wildwind) teaches the [camping](../systems/camping.md) system. Shen'dar Village (Constable Aonda) has bandit, hippogryph and Prideclaw quests, the bandit boss "Badwind" Bennic, and *WANTED: Vulgara the Insatiable*. The 2 factions fight small battles at the Windsong Standing Stones.

??? abstract "Chapter 3: Secret Agent Elf"
    You wear the Mark of Akir and infiltrate **Falaath Village**, which the cult holds. There you hear Lorthuna and Skypriest Aanders. You kill Commander Cyclas, and Commander Aanders at the Western Watch. In *A Last Request*, you recover the Shadowsong Family Signet.

??? abstract "Chapter 4: Valanaar"
    You give the signet to Talaanis Shadowsong and meet your faction leader (*The Grand Skyseer* / *The Supreme Magister*).

??? abstract "Chapter 5: Gustberry Lowlands"
    Windshaper chain: *The Broken Construct*. High Order chain: *The Missing Scholar* (Shriekling Den). The Windfield Orchard chain with Aamelia Windfield. **Secret:** `/sing` at the frogs to get the *Musical Gustjumper* pet.

??? abstract "Chapter 6: Shadowgale Forest"
    Windshaper chain: *In Service of Zephras* → *Blood for Blood*. The High Order chain takes you to Index Esoteria at the Overlook Standing Stones. A gnome hermit (*The Strange Hermit*) trains Engineering. Nightclaw druids: you can repeat *Mercy Falls on Deaf Ears* for **Nightclaw reputation**. *The Ties That Bind* and *Wounds of Betrayal* lead to Commander Haalien. Recording crystals in **Nightclaw Cavern** tell the Skyborne origin story.

??? abstract "Chapter 7: The confrontation"
    At the East Pylon Watchtower, you kill Commander Belguilos. A traitor then tells you *The Cult's True Plans*. In *Desperate Times*, Ayessa and Elaadrin join together. You attack the **Shrine of Akir**. You *Confront Lorthuna* through a portal to an area similar to the Vortex Pinnacle, with a wind-bridge event. Lorthuna summons **Rohash, Lord of the East Wind**. Then Horde and Alliance reinforcements arrive, with Runeweaver and Earthfury as leaders. Closing quests: *The Fate of Zephras* and *What Comes Next*. Your faction leader sends you away from the island.

## Leaving the island

- **High Order:** airship from the east Skydock to north **Dalaran** (Alterac Mountains).
- **Windshapers:** airship from the west Skydock to **Skywatcher Plateau** (Mulgore), near Thunder Bluff.
- Skyborne can leave at any level.
