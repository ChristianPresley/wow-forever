## Riverglades

<aside class="infobox" markdown>
<p class="infobox-title">Riverglades</p>

| | |
|---|---|

| **Size** | About Stranglethorn Vale |
| **Quests** | 150+ (launch copy) or nearly 200 (BlizzCon) |
| **Hubs** | Farholde Keep (A), Rog'mar (H), Bolder'ok, Powderfuse Port |
| **Instance** | [Krol'dok Stronghold](../content/dungeons/kroldok-stronghold.md) |

</aside>

*Levels 35–45 (Wowhead estimates 36–44). Contested.*

Built from a previously inaccessible part of **southeastern Khaz Modan**: east of the Badlands and Burning Steppes, north of the Swamp of Sorrows, east of Redridge. Blizzard says it is about the size of Stranglethorn Vale, with **150+ quests** (launch copy) or **nearly 200** (BlizzCon panel). It is the only WoW area with **oxbow lakes**, three of them along its central river.

**Factions:** Brotherhood of the Horse (Alliance), Twilight's Hammer (hostile), Powderfuse goblins (Steamwheedle), Bolder'ok ogres (Horde-aligned).
**Threats:** gnolls, ogres, Twilight's Hammer cultists, pirates.

| Hub | Faction | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Farholde Keep** | Alliance | A fortified island keep of the Brotherhood of the Horse, led by **Knight Commander Demar Brant**. Inn, forge, stables, and a memorial to fallen knights. Gryphon master Gretchen Mayberry |
| **Rog'mar** | Horde | The Horde starting hub. Its leader is unknown |
| **Bolder'ok** | Horde-aligned ogres | Ogres who broke from the Twilight's Hammer and allied with the Horde |
| **Powderfuse Port** | Neutral (goblin) | Run by **Melbin Powderfuse**. Ferry to Steamwheedle Port (Tanaris). The arena of Jazzle Cheapshot is "not open yet" |

**Other locations:** Meadowsbrook, Windstead, Wheeler's Grange, **Sunnyglade** (ruined), Twilight's Shroud, Ashwood's Fall, Bristle Hills, Eastwind Shore, Elbrim's Farm, Forlorn Pit, Southern Watch, Terral's Watch, Turner's Logging Camp.

### History

- **First War:** the Old Horde sacked the Stormwind town of **Sunnyglade**. Survivors founded Meadowsbrook and Farholde, and Stormwind later abandoned the region.
- **After the Second War:** Twilight's Hammer ogres and Stormreaver orcs fled here from the Tomb of Sargeras. There were unconfirmed rumors of Cho'gall.
- **Siege of Farholde:** Fern Crimsonleaf assassinated the Horde leadership, and Sir Howard Armstrong's dawn charge broke the siege. The Horde fell back to **Krol'dok Stronghold**.
- **In Forever (25 ADP)** Sunnyglade stays in ruins, unlike the rebuilt version shown in Warcraft I.

!!! tip "Not Twilight Highlands"
    The zone resembles the Twilight Highlands but lies farther south and has no Wildhammer. It is not a pre-Cataclysm version of that zone.

**Getting there:** on foot from Redridge (Blackrock or Redridge Pass), the Badlands, the Swamp of Sorrows and the Burning Steppes, or by ferry from Tanaris.

</aside>

*Level range unknown (beta maps suggest 38–40, and one guide says 40+). Contested.*

Blizzard describes a hidden mystery between **Mulgore and Desolace**, tied to the **Shen'dralar** Highborne of Eldre'Thalas (Dire Maul) and the local centaur tribes. You reach it on foot from southern Desolace through the **Valley of Bones**, roughly where retail's Lake Dumont is.

- **Peoples:** Shen'dralar Highborne, the **Magram** centaur clan, the **Bristleback** quilboar.
- **Subzones:** Shen'dralas (capital), Bristleback Retreat, Evenshade Overlook, Outcast Hideaway, Magram Front, Valley of Bones.
- The Skyborne descend from Shen'dralar rebels. Blizzard hasn't confirmed any direct story crossover.

!!! warning "Thin information"
    The Warcraft Wiki page is a stub with no quests, NPCs or levels. It is unclear whether Shen'dralas is fully populated with quests at launch.

## Mount Hyjal

**Also called:** Hyjal
{: .fc-aka }

<aside class="infobox" markdown>
<p class="infobox-title">Mount Hyjal</p>
<p class="infobox-roles" markdown>**New**{ .pill .pill--new } **60**{ .lvl } **Contested**{ .badge }</p>

| | |
|---|---|
| **Location** | Northern Kalimdor |
| **Access** | Darkwhisper Gorge, Winterspring |
| **Hubs** | Tainted Foothills, Summit of Eternity |
| **Raids** | [Barrow Deeps](../content/raids/barrow-deeps.md) (10), [Hyjal Summit](../content/raids/hyjal-summit.md) (20) |

</aside>

*Level 60 (some beta reports say 55–60). Contested.*

Set in the aftermath of **Archimonde's defeat**. Malfurion sealed Hyjal and Nordrassil behind dense growth after the war. You enter through **Darkwhisper Gorge** in Winterspring, help "shape the future" of a recovering land, and meet emerging factions.

- **Subzones:** Cradle of Tranquility, Dae'gun, Elderwild, Felblood Scar, Malorne's Retreat, Mourning's Rest, Shrine of Aviana, Summit of Eternity, Tainted Foothills.
- **Raids:** [Barrow Deeps](../content/raids/barrow-deeps.md) (10) and [Hyjal Summit](../content/raids/hyjal-summit.md) (20), both opening 9 Dec.
- **Factions:** *Guardians of Hyjal* (a Cenarion Circle splinter) and a "Barkskin" reputation. Fan sources only, *unconfirmed*{ .unconfirmed }.
- **Flight paths:** Tainted Foothills (to Everlook) and Summit of Eternity. *fan source*{ .fan-source }
- A questline reportedly starts with a "Curious Crow" investigating warlock activity around the "Wild King". *unconfirmed*{ .unconfirmed }

!!! warning "Don't mix up your Hyjals"
    The Warcraft Wiki's *Mount Hyjal quests* page covers **Cataclysm**, and its *Darkwhisper Gorge* quest is from Season of Discovery. Neither is Forever content.
