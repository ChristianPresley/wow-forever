## Riverglades

<aside class="infobox" markdown>
<p class="infobox-title">Riverglades</p>
<p class="infobox-roles" markdown>**New**{ .pill .pill--new } **35–45**{ .lvl } **Contested**{ .badge }</p>

| | |
|---|---|
| **Location** | Southeastern Khaz Modan |
| **Size** | About Stranglethorn Vale |
| **Quests** | 150+ (launch copy) or nearly 200 (BlizzCon) |
| **Hubs** | Farholde Keep (A), Rog'mar (H), Bolder'ok, Powderfuse Port |
| **Instance** | [Krol'dok Stronghold](../content/dungeons/kroldok-stronghold.md) |

</aside>

*Levels 35–45 (Wowhead estimates 36–44). Contested.*

Blizzard made it from a part of **southeastern Khaz Modan** that was closed to players before. It is east of the Badlands and Burning Steppes, north of the Swamp of Sorrows, and east of Redridge. Blizzard says that it is about the size of Stranglethorn Vale, with **150+ quests** (launch copy) or **nearly 200** (BlizzCon panel). It is the only WoW area with **oxbow lakes**. It has 3 of them along its central river.

**Factions:** Brotherhood of the Horse (Alliance), Twilight's Hammer (hostile), Powderfuse goblins (Steamwheedle), Bolder'ok ogres (Horde-aligned).
**Threats:** gnolls, ogres, Twilight's Hammer cultists, pirates.

| Hub | Faction | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Farholde Keep** | **Alliance**{ .pill .pill--alliance } | A fortified island keep of the Brotherhood of the Horse. Its leader is **Knight Commander Demar Brant**. Inn, forge, stables, and a memorial to dead knights. Gryphon master Gretchen Mayberry |
| **Rog'mar** | **Horde**{ .pill .pill--horde } | The Horde starting hub. Its leader is unknown |
| **Bolder'ok** | Horde-aligned ogres | Ogres who left the Twilight's Hammer and became allies of the Horde |
| **Powderfuse Port** | Neutral (goblin) | **Melbin Powderfuse** controls it. Ferry to Steamwheedle Port (Tanaris). The arena of Jazzle Cheapshot is "not open yet" |

**Other locations:** Meadowsbrook, Windstead, Wheeler's Grange, **Sunnyglade** (ruined), Twilight's Shroud, Ashwood's Fall, Bristle Hills, Eastwind Shore, Elbrim's Farm, Forlorn Pit, Southern Watch, Terral's Watch, Turner's Logging Camp.

### History

- **First War:** the Old Horde destroyed the Stormwind town of **Sunnyglade**. Survivors founded Meadowsbrook and Farholde. Later, Stormwind abandoned the region.
- **After the Second War:** Twilight's Hammer ogres and Stormreaver orcs escaped to this area from the Tomb of Sargeras. There were unconfirmed rumors of Cho'gall.
- **Siege of Farholde:** Fern Crimsonleaf killed the Horde leaders. Then Sir Howard Armstrong's charge at dawn stopped the siege. The Horde retreated to **Krol'dok Stronghold**.
- **In Forever (25 ADP)** Sunnyglade stays in ruins. This is different from the rebuilt version in Warcraft I.

!!! tip "Not Twilight Highlands"
    The zone is similar to the Twilight Highlands, but it is farther south and has no Wildhammer. It is not a pre-Cataclysm version of that zone.

**Getting there:** walk from Redridge (Blackrock or Redridge Pass), the Badlands, the Swamp of Sorrows and the Burning Steppes, or by ferry from Tanaris.

<p class="infobox-title">Shen'dralas</p>

| **Access** | On foot via the Valley of Bones |
| **Peoples** | Shen'dralar Highborne, Magram centaurs, Bristleback quilboar |
| **Instance** | — |

</aside>

*Level range unknown (beta maps show possibly 38–40, and 1 guide says 40+). Contested.*

Blizzard describes a hidden mystery between **Mulgore and Desolace**. It has a connection to the **Shen'dralar** Highborne of Eldre'Thalas (Dire Maul) and to the local centaur tribes. You walk there from southern Desolace through the **Valley of Bones**. This is approximately where Lake Dumont is in retail.

- **Peoples:** Shen'dralar Highborne, the **Magram** centaur clan, the **Bristleback** quilboar.
- **Subzones:** Shen'dralas (capital), Bristleback Retreat, Evenshade Overlook, Outcast Hideaway, Magram Front, Valley of Bones.
- The Skyborne descend from Shen'dralar rebels. Blizzard has not confirmed a direct story connection.

!!! warning "Thin information"
    The Warcraft Wiki page is a stub with no quests, NPCs or levels. It is not clear if Shen'dralas has its full set of quests at launch.

## Mount Hyjal

**Also called:** Hyjal
{: .fc-aka }

<aside class="infobox" markdown>
<p class="infobox-title">Mount Hyjal</p>
<p class="infobox-roles" markdown>**New**{ .pill .pill--new } **60**{ .lvl } **Contested**{ .badge }</p>

| | |
|---|---|
| **Location** | Northern Kalimdor |
| **Access** | Darkwhisper Gorge, Winterspring |
| **Hubs** | Tainted Foothills, Summit of Eternity |
| **Raids** | [Barrow Deeps](../content/raids/barrow-deeps.md) (10), [Hyjal Summit](../content/raids/hyjal-summit.md) (20) |

</aside>

*Level 60 (some beta reports say 55–60). Contested.*

The zone is after the **defeat of Archimonde**. After the war, Malfurion sealed Hyjal and Nordrassil behind dense plants. You go in through **Darkwhisper Gorge** in Winterspring. You help "shape the future" of a land that recovers, and you meet new factions.

- **Subzones:** Cradle of Tranquility, Dae'gun, Elderwild, Felblood Scar, Malorne's Retreat, Mourning's Rest, Shrine of Aviana, Summit of Eternity, Tainted Foothills.
- **Raids:** [Barrow Deeps](../content/raids/barrow-deeps.md) (10) and [Hyjal Summit](../content/raids/hyjal-summit.md) (20), both open on 9 Dec.
- **Factions:** *Guardians of Hyjal* (a Cenarion Circle splinter) and a "Barkskin" reputation. Fan sources only, *unconfirmed*{ .unconfirmed }.
- **Flight paths:** Tainted Foothills (to Everlook) and Summit of Eternity. *fan source*{ .fan-source }
- A questline reportedly starts with a "Curious Crow" that investigates warlock activity near the "Wild King". *unconfirmed*{ .unconfirmed }

!!! warning "Do not confuse the Hyjals"
    The Warcraft Wiki's *Mount Hyjal quests* page is about **Cataclysm**. Its *Darkwhisper Gorge* quest is from Season of Discovery. Neither is Forever content.
